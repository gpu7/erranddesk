Warren, Michigan document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Warren, Michigan, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Warren, the Warren City Clerk, the Warren Public Library, Macomb County, the Macomb County Clerk / Register of Deeds, the 16th Judicial Circuit Court, the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services, the Michigan Department of State, the Michigan Legislature, the State of Michigan, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Warren Public Library notaries are free with a valid Warren library card and $5 per document for everyone else, up to $20 for 4 or more documents; faxes and scans are $1.00 for the first page and $.25 for each additional page [1]. If you were born or died in Warren, the City Clerk's application forms list $7.50 for one certified certificate and $3.00 for each additional one [2][3]. Macomb County charges $15 for one certified birth or death record and $5 for each additional copy of the same record [4][5]. A Macomb County marriage license is $20 for Michigan residents [6].
One county, offices in Mount Clemens: the Census places Warren city entirely in Macomb County [7]. The Macomb County Clerk / Register of Deeds offices on this page are at 120 N. Main St. and 40 N. Main St. in Mount Clemens, which the Census Geocoder places in Mount Clemens city, not Warren [8][9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Warren Public Library

  • Branches: the library has 4 branches: Civic Center Library, One City Square, Suite 100, (586) 751-0770; Arthur J. Miller Branch, 5460 Arden, Suite 303, (586) 751-5377; Dorothy Busch Branch, 23333 Ryan, (586) 353-0580; and Maybelle Burnette Branch, 23345 Van Dyke, (586) 353-0579 [10]. The Census Geocoder places the Miller, Busch and Burnette addresses in Warren city; it didn't match One City Square [9].
  • Upcoming closures: the library says all Warren Public Libraries will be closed Tuesday, November 3, 2026 for Election Day and Wednesday, November 11, 2026 for Veterans Day [11].
  • Printing and copying: mobile printing is available at all branches [1], with black-and-white and color options for each branch [12]. The Civic Center Library lists color printing and a black-and-white and color copy machine [11]. Tax forms can be printed for $.15 per page [1]. We didn't find the library's general per-page printing or copy prices.
  • Fax and scanning (pages differ on where): the library's FAQ (updated 8.17.15) says all branches can fax and scan, at $1.00 for the first page and $.25 for each additional page, and the library doesn't provide cover sheets [1]. The City of Warren's 311 answer (updated 7/29/2011) says fax is at the Civic Center Branch only, at $1 for the first page and 25 cents for each additional page [13]. Call your branch to confirm before you go.
  • Library card: to apply, bring a completed application and a valid Michigan Driver's License or State ID with your current address [1].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 2 locations in Warren: 13700 12 Mile Rd, (586) 486-5004, and 4005 E Eleven Mile Rd, (586) 619-9470. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [14]. Prices aren't listed online: its 13700 12 Mile Rd store page shows no prices and says products, services, pricing and hours of operation may vary by location [15], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 1, 2026). The Census Geocoder places both stores in Warren city [14][9].

Notary in Warren

  • Warren Public Library: notary services are free with a valid Warren library card and $5.00 per document for all other patrons, with a maximum charge of $20.00 for 4 or more documents. Notaries may not be available at all branches (FAQ updated 4/7/26) [1]. Call ahead.
  • Warren City Clerk: we didn't find notary service for the public on the City Clerk's page [16].
  • Macomb County Clerk: the clerk's notary page covers becoming a notary: a $10,000 surety bond, which it says generally costs $50.00 to $100.00, and a $10 bond filing fee [17]. We didn't find whether the clerk's office notarizes documents for the public.
  • The UPS Store: its Warren page lists notarizing [14]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store [15].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Michigan Department of State commissions notaries. Applicants file a $10,000 surety bond with the county clerk and take the oath of office there. A commission lasts 6 to 7 years and ends on the notary's birthday; there's no renewal, so notaries apply for a new commission each time [18][19]. Once commissioned, a Michigan notary can notarize anywhere in Michigan, but not outside the state [18].
  • Fees (statewide law): a Michigan notary may charge "not more than $10.00 for any individual transaction or notarial act" and must post a sign or tell you the fee before notarizing. A separate travel fee can be agreed before the notary travels. A notary may refuse to perform a notarial act [20].
  • No true copies (statewide): a Michigan notary can't certify that a copy is a true copy of a document [21][22]. A county clerk can certify true copies of documents kept in the clerk's office [19]. Otherwise, only the issuer or the person named on the document can make a true copy statement; a notary can notarize that person's signature [22].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: City of Warren or Macomb County

  • Warren City Clerk (born or died in Warren): the clerk's application forms (2018 versions) are for births in the City of Warren and deaths in the City of Warren. They list $7.50 for one certificate and $3.00 for each additional certificate, per record, per transaction. Submit in person or by mail with payment made payable to City of Warren; don't e-mail the form. The office is at Suite 205, One City Square, Warren, MI 48093-2393; (586) 574-4557 [2][3]. For a birth certificate by mail, include a copy of your government-issued ID [2].
  • Macomb County Clerk (born or died in Macomb County): $15 for one certified copy and $5 for each additional certified copy of the same record. It accepts cash, check, VISA, Mastercard and Discover, but not American Express; make checks payable to "Macomb County Clerk" [4][5]. Records are for Macomb County births and deaths only [4][5].
  • Where and how (county): by mail, online or in person [4][5]; in person needs an appointment at the Vital Records Department, 120 N Main in Mount Clemens [5]. Register of Deeds / Vital Records hours are Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 4:15 pm [8]. Birth records phone: 586-469-5205 [4].
  • Who can order (county): birth records can be obtained only by the individual, parents listed on the record, a legal guardian, or an heir if the individual is deceased and the relationship is shown with a court order. Birth records over 100 years old are public. Bring valid photo identification (driver's license) or three pieces of identification, one with a photo; people under 15 can't request their own birth record [4]. Death records may be purchased by anyone; give the name of the decedent and the date and place of death [5].

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) lists a $34 search fee for a standard record, or $42 for an apostille or exemplified record for use outside the U.S. Online orders add a $14 processing fee [27]. State law sets $34.00 for a search with 1 certified copy, $16.00 for each additional identical copy ordered at the same time, $12.00 for each additional year searched and $12.00 for an expedited search [28]. If you're 65 or older and ordering your own birth record, the state charges $14.00 for one certified copy [27][28]. Search fees aren't refunded [27].
  • Local fees (statewide law): a local government may set local registrar fees at or below the state amounts. A charter county with more than 1,500,000 people may set higher fees, but not more than the cost of the service [28]. County fees on this page can differ from the state's.
  • How to order from the state (statewide): by mail, online or by phone at 866-443-9897 through VitalChek (the state's only authorized online provider, with an extra fee), at the drop box in the main lobby of 333 S. Grand Ave., Lansing (Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m.–5:30 p.m.), or in person by appointment on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Will Call orders placed through VitalChek are processed within 2–5 business days for pickup in Lansing. The state takes checks or money orders payable to the State of Michigan, credit cards and cash. Questions: 517-335-8666 [27].
  • Processing times (statewide): MDHHS currently lists 8–10 weeks for expedited and 10–12 weeks for regular birth, death, marriage and divorce orders, and 8–10 weeks (expedited) or 12–16 weeks (regular) for authenticated or apostilled records [27].
  • Who can order a birth record (statewide): the person named on the record, parents named on it, a legal heir, a court-appointed guardian, someone with a power of attorney that specifically grants access to vital records, or a licensed attorney for the person or parents. Records more than 100 years old aren't restricted [27].

Marriage licenses (Macomb County Clerk)

  • Marriage license fee (statewide law): Michigan law sets a $20.00 license fee, plus $10.00 more if both of you live outside Michigan. A charter county with more than 1,500,000 people may set a different fee by ordinance [29]. Check the county fee below.
  • Waiting period (statewide law): the license can't be handed over within 3 days, counting the day you apply. The county clerk may release it right away for good cause, for a fee set by the county board. The license is void if you don't marry within 33 days after you apply [30].
  • Macomb County fees: $20 application fee for Michigan residents and $30 for non-residents of Michigan. It accepts cash, check, VISA, Mastercard and Discover [6].
  • Who can apply here: at least one applicant needs to be a Macomb County resident with proof of residency (current driver's license or State ID) [6].
  • How: apply online 30 days or less before your wedding date, then make an appointment at the Clerk's Office for notarization of the affidavit, fee payment and receipt of the license. You don't pay online [6]. Have both parties' Social Security numbers [6].
  • Timing: you must wait three days from the day of application before the license is valid; waiving the wait costs a $10 waiver fee on top of the $20 license fee. The license is valid anywhere in Michigan for 33 days from the date of application [6].
  • Errors: if the license has to be redone because of an error in information you provided, there is a $20 re-print or re-type fee [6].
  • Copies: certified marriage records are $15 for the first copy and $5 for each additional copy of the same record, with no added fee for ordering online. Marriage certificates are public records, so you don't need to show ID. By mail, write to Macomb County Clerk, Attn: Marriage Records, 120 North Main, Mount Clemens, MI 48043 [31].

Recorded documents (Macomb County Register of Deeds)

  • Fees: effective October 1, 2016, recording fees are $30 per document regardless of the number of pages, plus $3 for each instrument reference after the first. Real estate searches are a $5.00 minimum plus a $1 copy fee per page, and certification of a copy is $5.00 [32].
  • Certified copies: requests for certified copies of recorded documents are accepted by U.S. Mail [33].
  • Where: 120 N. Main St., Mount Clemens; Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-4:15 p.m.; 586-469-7953 [33].

Court records (16th Judicial Circuit Court, Macomb County Clerk)

  • Copy fees: regular copies of pleadings are $2 per page. Certified copies are $10 for the certification plus $2 per page, and the whole document must be copied. Name searches are $2 per name, and a Register of Actions printout is $2 per page [34].
  • How to order: online, by fax or mail, or in person at the Macomb County Circuit Court Building [35]. The Circuit Court Section is at 40 North Main (1st Floor), Mount Clemens; counter service closes at 4:15 p.m. on regular business days [35]. The Court Section's listed hours are Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 4:30 pm; 586-469-5351 [8].

Apostilles and authentications (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $1 per document from the Michigan Department of State's Office of the Great Seal, paid by check or money order payable to the State of Michigan. Don't send cash [21][22]. The request form lists the fee as $1.00 per authentication, not per page, and asks for the country where the document will be used (not the USA) [36].
  • What it can authenticate (statewide): documents notarized by a Michigan notary, and certified copies of birth, marriage, divorce or death records from the State Registrar or a Michigan county clerk. Documents from court clerks, or sent by fax, aren't accepted [21]. Court and register of deeds documents must be notarized first [22]. City clerk records: the service page accepts them from city clerks in Wayne, Macomb and Oakland counties [21], and one FAQ answer agrees, but another FAQ answer says city registrars in Wayne County only [22]. If yours is from a city clerk, confirm with the Office of the Great Seal.
  • Where and how (statewide): by mail to the Office of the Great Seal, 7064 Crowner Drive, Lansing, MI 48918, with the request form, the fee and a self-addressed stamped envelope; or in person by appointment on Mondays or Wednesdays, 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Phone (888) 767-6424 [21]. The office is in the Richard H. Austin Building, 430 W. Allegan, 1st floor, Lansing [37].
  • How long (statewide, sources differ): the service page and FAQs say turnaround is 1–2 weeks [21][22], but the request form says to allow 4–6 weeks after the office receives it [36]. There's no expedited service [22][36]. Confirm with the office if you have a deadline.
  • Warren note: the Office of the Great Seal accepts certified vital records from Michigan county clerks [21], which includes Macomb County Clerk copies. Court documents must be notarized before they can be authenticated [22]. We didn't find whether it accepts City of Warren certificates; ask the Office of the Great Seal.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [38]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Warren?

If you were born in Warren, the City Clerk's form lists $7.50 for one certificate and $3.00 for each additional one [2]. For any Macomb County birth, the Macomb County Clerk charges $15 for one certified copy and $5 for each additional copy of the same record [4].

How much is a marriage license in Warren?

Macomb County charges $20 for Michigan residents and $30 for non-residents, and at least one applicant must be a Macomb County resident. The license is valid three days after you apply, unless you pay a $10 waiver fee [6].

Can I get something notarized for free in Warren?

Yes, at the Warren Public Library if you have a valid Warren library card; others pay $5.00 per document, up to $20.00. Notaries may not be at every branch [1].

Can I send a fax at the Warren Public Library?

Yes. It's $1.00 for the first page and $.25 for each additional page. The library's FAQ says all branches fax [1], while an older City 311 answer says the Civic Center Branch only [13], so call first.
