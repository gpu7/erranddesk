Paterson, New Jersey document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Paterson, New Jersey, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Paterson, Passaic County, the Paterson Public Library, the New Jersey Department of Health, the New Jersey Department of the Treasury, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the Passaic County Clerk is the one passport office the locator lists in Paterson, and appointments are required [1][2]. Paterson's Vital Statistics office charges $25 for a birth certificate and takes no cash [3]. New Jersey notaries may charge $2.50 per act [4].
Who does what: Paterson is in Passaic County [5][6], and the county seat is here. The City of Paterson Division of Health's Vital Statistics office issues birth, death and marriage certificates for events in Paterson [3]. The state Office of Vital Statistics and Registry in Trenton also issues them [7]. Deeds are recorded by the Passaic County Clerk's Registry Division [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Paterson Public Library (Danforth Main Library, 250 Broadway): 973-321-1223. Open Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–6 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–2 p.m. The library system is closed Sundays [9].
  • Library printing prices: the library's online-printing page showed a bot check, so we couldn't read its prices. It needs a browser read (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [10]. We didn't find a library fax service.
  • The UPS Store: its Paterson page lists 3 locations, 2 marked Coming Soon. The open store at 448 Chamberlain Ave. offers printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [11], and the Census Geocoder places it inside Paterson [12]. Prices aren't listed online.

Notary in Paterson

  • The UPS Store: its Paterson page lists notarizing [11]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [13].
  • Library notary: we didn't find a notary service at the Paterson Public Library (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Jersey's Division of Revenue says a notary may charge $2.50 each for an oath or affirmation, a jurat, a proof of a deed or an acknowledgment [4]. The state Notary Public Manual covers the real-estate closing fees [14].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Paterson Vital Statistics (City Division of Health)

  • Where: 176 Broadway, Paterson. Order in person, by mail (7–10 business days) or online through VitalChek, which charges an extra fee [3].
  • Hours, the page differs: the page header says Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–4 p.m., and its body says 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Monday to Friday [3]. Call 973-321-1277 before you go.
  • Fees: birth, marriage and death certificates are $25 for the first copy and $5 for each extra copy of the same record on the same day. Pay by money order or card. No cash [3].
  • Marriage licenses (statewide rule): apply in the municipality where either of you lives. The fee is $28 and there's a 72-hour wait [17]. We didn't find Paterson's marriage-license hours on its page (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Birth, death and marriage records from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the New Jersey Office of Vital Statistics and Registry charges $25 for the search and one certified copy (or a certification, or a no-record statement), and $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. VitalChek adds a processing fee of $6 by mail or $12.95 by phone [18].
  • Certified copy or certification: certified copies have a raised seal and are legal documents. Certifications are on plain paper, can't be used for identity or legal purposes, and are mainly useful for genealogy [19].
  • Walk-in (statewide, in Trenton): the walk-in center at 140 E. Front Street, Trenton offers same-day service. It has not taken cash since June 18, 2026 [7]. Online orders go through VitalChek [7][20].
  • Local copies: municipal local registrars also issue copies, and their fees vary by municipality [18].

Deeds and land records (Passaic County)

  • Passaic County Clerk, Registry Division: a certified copy is $10 for the first page and $2 for each additional page [8].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (Department of the Treasury), for documents signed by a New Jersey notary or selected public officials such as county clerks and the State Registrar. It gives an apostille for Hague Convention countries and a certification for other countries [21].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 per certificate, or $5 per certificate for documents for a child's adoption. The fee table also lists 2-hour and 1-hour expedite fees for walk-in requests, but we couldn't match those amounts to their rows (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [22].
  • Vital records: order a certified copy from the state Office of Vital Statistics, and say on the application that it needs an apostille [19].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Paterson?

At the Passaic County Clerk, 401 Grand St., Room 130, by appointment [1][2].

How much is a Paterson birth certificate?

$25 for the first copy and $5 for each extra copy of the same record on the same day [3].

How much does a New Jersey notary charge?

$2.50 per oath, jurat, proof or acknowledgment [4].
