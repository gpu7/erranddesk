Omaha, Nebraska document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Omaha, Nebraska, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Nebraska rules differ from Colorado's, Wyoming's and Utah's in several places, and we point those out.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Omaha: this page is about Omaha, Nebraska, which is in Douglas County. Its library system is Omaha Public Library, whose Central Library is at 7205 Dodge St and Downtown Branch at 1401 Jones St [4][5]. The Douglas County Clerk of the District Court (passports and court records) is in the Hall of Justice, 1701 Farnam St [7][8]. The Douglas County Health Department issues birth and death certificates for Douglas County events at the Omaha-Douglas Civic Center, 1819 Farnam Street, Room H-01 [10]. The state's Office of Vital Records is in Lincoln [11]. Douglas County's own website, including the County Clerk's and Register of Deeds' pages, blocked our automated reads, so for marriage licenses and recording we show the statewide fees set by Nebraska law.
Short version: every Omaha Public Library location has a public fax machine with scanning, and "This service is available at no cost." Printing is $0.10 a page for black and white and $0.25 for color, but the library doesn't have a notary on staff [1][2][3]. The Douglas County Clerk of the District Court takes passport applications Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–4 p.m. by appointment only [7]. Nebraska law caps notary fees at $5 for an acknowledgment and $2 for an oath or affidavit (statewide) [13]. A birth certificate is $17.00 at the county health department or the state office [10][12].

Notary in Omaha

  • Omaha Public Library: "No, Omaha Public Library does not have a notary on staff" [3].
  • Douglas County Clerk of the District Court: the passport locator data says "Notary and photocopies available" at the clerk's office, 1701 Farnam Street, Suite 300 [6]. We didn't find a notary price or hours for walk-in notary service on the court's pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: the 321 S 17th St store lists notary service, available 7 days [21]. We didn't find its price.
  • Nebraska fee limits (statewide): under Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-133, a notary may charge $5 for taking an acknowledgment, $5 for each certificate and seal, $2 for taking an affidavit and seal, and $2 for administering an oath or affirmation, plus mileage for serving notice. A state or local government employee may not charge these fees if the employer paid the notary's commission and bond fees [13]. Nebraska's caps are much lower than Utah's $10 per signature, Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document.
  • Relatives (statewide): the Secretary of State says a Nebraska notary may not notarize a document signed by their parents, grandparents, children, grandchildren, spouse, brothers or sisters, including in-laws, step and half relatives [15].
  • Commissions (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions Nebraska notaries. Its online filing process for notary forms took effect November 20, 2023 [14].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [18]. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Douglas County Health Department

  • Who issues them: the Douglas County Health Department issues certificates for births and deaths that happened in Douglas County. It doesn't issue them for adopted persons [10]. Like Colorado and Utah, and unlike Wyoming, Nebraska has a local counter as well as the state office.
  • In person in Omaha: Omaha-Douglas Civic Center, 1819 Farnam Street, Room H-01, 402-444-7204. Hours are 8 a.m.–4 p.m., Monday through Friday. If you need help with an amendment, start by 3 p.m. [10].
  • Fees: a birth certificate is $17.00 and a death certificate is $16.00. The department also takes online orders. By mail, send a check or money order payable to DCHD, a copy of your ID and a self-addressed stamped envelope [10].

Nebraska Office of Vital Records (statewide)

  • Where: the office has moved to the Nebraska State Office Building, 301 Centennial Mall South, First Floor, Lincoln, NE. Phone (402) 471-2871, DHHS.VitalRecords@nebraska.gov [11][12].
  • Fees: $17.00 for each birth certificate and $16.00 for each death, marriage or divorce certificate [12].
  • How long: walk-in requests take a minimum of 30 minutes, regular mail a minimum of 15 days in-office processing, and online applications a minimum of 7 days. Expedited service is offered only on mail-in orders, with 1 to 2 days of in-office processing [12].
  • How to pay: online applications may be charged to a Visa or MasterCard. Mail orders need a check or money order made out to "Vital Records" (no credit cards), sent to P.O. Box 95065, Lincoln, NE 68509-5065 [11][12].
  • Rules: the office doesn't accept faxed or emailed applications. If a certificate isn't found, the search fee isn't refunded [11].
  • Voter ID: if you don't have a current Nebraska driver's license or state ID card, you can ask for a no-fee certified birth certificate to get a state ID for voting. Say so on the application, apply in person or by mail, and bring proof of identity [11].

Court records: Douglas County Clerk of the District Court

  • Copies of court documents (for example, a divorce decree) are requested by calling 402-444-7619. A regular copy is free by email or $10.00 mailed. A certified copy is $1.00 by email or $20.00 mailed. An authenticated copy is $20.00, paper only. Payment processing fees are extra and depend on how you pay [9].
  • Timing: emailed copies take up to 3 business days. Mailed copies take up to 5 business days plus delivery, counted from when payment is received [9].

Marriage records and recorded documents (statewide fees)

  • Marriage licenses (statewide): under Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-110, as amended by Laws 2026, LB596 (effective July 18, 2026), the county clerk's fee is $50 for the entire proceedings of issuing a marriage license, administering the oaths and recording the marriage certificate. Each certified copy of a marriage record is $16 [16]. The Douglas County Clerk's pages blocked our automated reads, so we didn't confirm the office's address, hours or how it applies the new fee. Call before you go.
  • Recording (statewide): under Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-109, recording a deed, mortgage or other instrument costs $10 for the first page and $6 for each additional page. A certified copy of a recorded instrument is $1.50 per page [17]. In Nebraska these fees are set by state law, not by each county. The Douglas County Register of Deeds' pages also blocked our automated reads, so we didn't confirm its address, hours or card fees.
  • State copies (statewide): the Office of Vital Records also issues marriage and divorce certificates for $16.00 each [12].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. We didn't find an Omaha government office that sells money orders. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I fax for free in Omaha?

Yes. Public fax machines at all Omaha Public Library locations are free to use and also scan [2].

Is there a notary at the Omaha library?

No. Omaha Public Library doesn't have a notary on staff [3]. The locator data lists notary service at the Douglas County Clerk of the District Court [6], and The UPS Store at 321 S 17th St lists notary service [21]. Nebraska's legal limit is $5 for an acknowledgment (statewide) [13].

Can I renew my passport at the Douglas County courthouse?

No. The Clerk of the District Court doesn't handle adult renewals, and applications there are by appointment only [7]. See the passport checklist for renewing by mail.

Where can I get a birth certificate in Omaha?

For a birth in Douglas County, at the Douglas County Health Department, 1819 Farnam Street, Room H-01, for $17.00 [10]. For any Nebraska birth, from the state Office of Vital Records in Lincoln, by mail or online [11][12].
