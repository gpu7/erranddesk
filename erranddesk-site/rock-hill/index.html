Rock Hill, South Carolina document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Rock Hill, South Carolina, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: the York County Library charges 20 cents a page for black-and-white prints and copies and 60 cents for color, and has self-service fax kiosks at $1.75 for the first page [1][2]. The State Department's locator lists 1 passport office in Rock Hill, a York County Clerk of Court office that says it has notary and photocopy on site [3]. The state's York County vital records office has a Rock Hill address; state birth certificates are $12 by mail or $17 in person, with one certified copy [4][5]. A York County marriage license is $50 by cash, check or money order, by appointment at the Probate Court in York [6].
City and county: this page covers the place the Census Bureau calls "Rock Hill city" [7]. It lies entirely in York County [8]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [9]. York County's page lists its Clerk of Court, who is also the Register of Deeds, at 6 S Congress Street in York, not Rock Hill [10].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

York County Library

  • Prices (all locations): printers at all library locations print, copy and scan. Black-and-white prints and copies are $0.20 a page and color $0.60 a page [1]. The library's pages put an asterisk on these prices, but we didn't find the note it refers to. We didn't find a scan price.
  • Mobile printing: send documents from a laptop, tablet or phone by website, email or the Princh app, at the same prices. Pay for and pick up prints within 24 hours at the checkout desk, or at the Computer Lab at the Main Library in Rock Hill [11].
  • Fax (self-service): FaxScan24 kiosks are at all library locations [12]. Faxes to the U.S., Canada and the Caribbean cost $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page; international faxes cost $3.95 and $3.45. You pay at the kiosk with a credit or debit card; no cash. The kiosks can't receive faxes, and they shut down 30 minutes before the library closes [2].
  • Main Library: 138 East Black St., Rock Hill, SC 29730, 803-981-5858. Hours are Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–7 p.m. and Friday–Saturday 9 a.m.–6 p.m. [13]. The Census Geocoder places it in Rock Hill city [9]. Microfilm readers and printers are only at the Main Library [12].
  • Closings: all locations are closed Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026 (staff in-service day) and Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026 (Veterans Day) [13].
  • The UPS Store: its Rock Hill page lists 5 locations, one marked "Coming Soon" (4859 Old York Rd.), and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [14]. The Census Geocoder places 2 of the 4 open stores in Rock Hill city: 572 John Ross Pkwy. and 1015 Charlotte Ave. It places 2736 Celanese Rd. outside the city and didn't match 1735 Heckle Blvd. [9]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Rock Hill

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the York County Library's technology, mobile printing, fax, FAQ or hours pages [1][12][13].
  • York County Clerk of Court (passport office): the State Department's locator entry for the York County Clerk of Court at 933 Heckle Blvd. says "We have notary and photocopy on site" [3]. We didn't find a fee or hours for this notary service.
  • Filing a notary commission (for new notaries): York County's Common Pleas page says that once you receive your notary certificate, you have 15 days to hand-carry it to the Common Pleas Office with photo ID and a $10 fee. That office is at 1675 York Hwy., York, phone 803-684-8507, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [15].
  • The UPS Store: its Rock Hill page lists notarizing [14]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [16].
  • How notaries are appointed (statewide): the Governor appoints South Carolina notaries for a ten-year term, and applicants must be registered voters [17]. The Secretary of State commissions notaries and keeps their oaths on file. You apply on a paper form mailed to your county legislative delegation office, or to the House of Representatives if your county has none [18]. The fee is $25.00, payable to the Secretary of State, plus a $1.43 SC.GOV service fee if you pay by card [19]. A notary must report a name or address change within 45 days [18].
  • Fees (statewide; two laws differ): under § 26-1-100 (2014), a notary can charge at most $5 per signature for an acknowledgment, jurat or signature witnessing, $5 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature, and $5 per certificate for a verification of fact. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, may charge a travel fee only if you agree on it in advance, and doesn't have to charge at all [17]. The Secretary of State's manual also says up to $5.00 [20]. An older section, § 8-21-140 (1898), still lists notary fees of 50 cents for every notarial certificate with seal and 25 cents for administering an oath for an affidavit [21]. We didn't find an official note on how the two fit together. The $5 limit is the one the Secretary of State cites.
  • No certified photocopies (statewide): a South Carolina notary can't certify or authenticate a photograph or photocopy [17]. Notaries also can't advertise as a "notario publico" [20].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide): the South Carolina Electronic Notary Public Act was signed May 18, 2021. Electronic notarization (IPEN) still requires the notary and the signer to be in each other's presence [18].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (state vital records)

  • Where: the state's York County Vital Records office is at 1070 Heckle Blvd., Suite 3800, Rock Hill, SC 29731, phone (803) 909-7307 [4]. It has a Rock Hill mailing address, but the Census Geocoder places it just outside the city limits [9]. There's no separate city or county fee; state fees apply.

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): a records search is $12 and includes one certified copy; an expedited search is $17, because a $5 expedite fee has applied since October 1, 2023 to services that take 10 business days or less. Additional copies of the same record ordered at the same time are $3 each, and the special filing fee for an amended certificate is $15 [25].
  • In person (statewide): birth and death certificates are usually ready in 30–45 minutes at the $17 expedited fee, paid with exact cash, a money order or a cashier's check [5][26]. The fee page also lists credit and debit cards and cash (no bills over $50) for on-site service [25]. Regional offices issue long-form birth certificates [5] and death certificates from 2005 on [26]. Since January 1, 2026, all Vital Records offices are open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. and Wednesday 9:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [4].
  • By mail or drop-off (statewide): mail takes about 4 weeks at the $12 fee, to S.C. Department of Public Health, Vital Records Section, P.O. Box 2046, West Columbia, SC 29171, paid by money order or cashier's check. Drop-offs are accepted only at the state office in Richland County (2600 Bull St., Columbia): $12 for about 4 weeks or $17 for 5 business days or less [5][26][25].
  • Online or by phone (statewide): through GoCertificates, $17 plus $3 for each additional copy and an $8.70 processing fee, or VitalChek online, $17 plus an $8.75 processing fee; by phone, VitalChek charges $12.85. Processing takes 5–7 business days [5][26].
  • Amendments (statewide): since Nov. 17, 2025, the Richland County office (formerly called the State Office) no longer does same-day amendments; most in-person amendment requests take 1–2 business days [25].
  • Divorce reports (statewide): DPH has certified copies of the Report of Divorce for divorces from July 1962 to December 2025, by mail only for now: in-person requests are currently unavailable [27]. The divorce decree itself comes from the county Clerk of Court [28].

Marriage licenses (York County Probate Court)

  • State law (statewide): you file a written application with the probate judge at least 24 hours before the license is issued (in Darlington and Georgetown counties, with the clerk of court instead) [29]. A $20 fee for the Domestic Violence Fund is added to each license, and couples who finish a qualifying premarital preparation course can get a one-time $50 state income tax credit [29].
  • Older copies from the state (statewide; the page gives two date ranges): DPH says its marriage records cover July 1950 to December 2025, and in-person requests are currently unavailable. The same page later says the Columbia office has July 1950 to December 2018, and that records before July 1950 or after December 2018 come from the probate office in the county that issued the license [30]. Mail requests are $12 with one certified copy and $3 for each additional copy [30].
  • York County fee and hours: the fee is $50, paid by cash, check or money order. You apply by appointment, Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; call 803-684-8513 or email the Probate Court. The license can be used only for ceremonies in South Carolina [6].
  • Where: the Historical Courthouse at 2 S. Congress St. in York is closed for renovations, and the Probate Court is now on the second floor of the Moss Justice Center, 1675 York Highway, York. Office hours are Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [6]. The Census Geocoder places the Moss Justice Center in York County, outside Rock Hill city [9].
  • Wait and mailing: the court's FAQ says there's a 24-hour wait before the license can be issued. If both of you were there when you applied and filled out the mailing request form, the license can be mailed for $1 [31].
  • Online or in person (the county pages differ): the Probate Court page says "marriage licenses are also available online," but its marriage section describes applying by appointment [6]. The court's FAQ says all applicants must apply in person and pay the $50 fee then (cash or check) [31]. Ask the court when you book (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Copies: you don't need an appointment for a certified copy of a marriage license. By mail it's $5 a copy with a self-addressed stamped envelope; you can also request one online through southcarolinaprobate.net [6].

Recorded documents (York County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide, uniform since 2019): under § 8-21-310, registers of deeds collect $15 to record a deed and $25 for a mortgage, plat, lease, easement, power of attorney or other document affecting real estate. A power of attorney is free with deployment orders to a combat zone. Separate acknowledgements or affidavits not attached to another document are $10 [21]. The county's posted fees follow; if they differ from the statute, ask the office which applies.
  • York County: the Clerk of Court also serves as the Register of Deeds and records property transactions [10]. The office is at the Government Center, 6 South Congress St., York, SC 29745, phone 803-684-8510. Hours are Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., and since March 2, 2026 the record room closes at 4:45 p.m. Walk-in recordings after 4:30 p.m. are processed the next business day. The office takes cash, check or money order only, not debit or credit cards [32].
  • York County fees: certified true copies of recorded documents are a flat $10.00 per document [32]. The fee sheet (South Carolina recording fees as of Aug. 1, 2019) lists $15.00 to record a deed plus deed tax ($1.30 state and $0.55 county per $500), $25.00 for a mortgage, $25.00 for a plat of any size and $10.00 for a certified copy [33]. The office also makes photocopies for a fee [32], but we didn't find a plain copy price on the fee sheet (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Court record copies (York County Clerk of Court)

  • State law (statewide): § 8-21-310 sets uniform clerk fees, including $10 for an official certificate under the seal of the court and $10 for filing a notary commission. It sets $100 for filing a first complaint [21], and § 14-1-204(B)(1) adds a $50 fee to that, so the total is $150 [21][34]. The county's posted fees follow; if they differ from the statute, ask the Clerk which applies.
  • York County: the Clerk of Court serves the Circuit Court and Family Court and keeps their records [10]. Common Pleas (civil): 1675 York Hwy., York, phone 803-684-8507, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [15]. Family Court: 1555 West Main St., Rock Hill, phone 803-909-7100, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [35]; the Census Geocoder places it in Rock Hill city [9]. We didn't find copy or certification fees for court records on these pages (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the South Carolina Secretary of State, in Columbia. It certifies documents signed by South Carolina officials and documents notarized by a commissioned South Carolina notary, but only documents that will go to a foreign country. The Secretary of State has a separate page for countries outside the Hague Convention [36].
  • Fee (statewide): $5.00 per document, not per page, by cash, personal, business or cashier's check, or money order [37]. Checks and money orders go to the Secretary of State's Office [36].
  • How to send (statewide): the Authentication Cover Letter, a self-addressed stamped return envelope, payment and the documents, by mail, UPS or FedEx, or by hand between 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m., to SC Secretary of State's Office, Attn: Authentications, 1205 Pendleton Street, Suite 525, Columbia, SC 29201 [36]. Phone (803) 734-2512 [37]. Foreign-language documents need an English translation signed by the translator, with the translator's signature notarized [36].
  • Vital records (statewide): photocopies aren't accepted; records must be certified by the issuing agency. Birth and death certificates must come from the state Vital Records office; marriage licenses from the county Probate Court or the state; divorce decrees from the county Clerk of Court or the state [28]. That page still calls the state office DHEC; vital records are now run by the South Carolina Department of Public Health (DPH) [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [38]. Other sellers: money order guide.

The page's title says October 9, 2026, but its details say "Friday, October 9, 2025" [39]. Either way, it is past. The Census Geocoder places the address in Rock Hill city [9].

Both are past. The next dates are March 6 and Oct. 2, 2027, 8 a.m.–noon, at the same address [42]. The Census Geocoder places the site in York County, outside Rock Hill city [9].

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Rock Hill?

At the state's York County Vital Records office, 1070 Heckle Blvd., Suite 3800 [4]. In person it's $17 with one certified copy; by mail it's $12 and takes about 4 weeks [5].

How much does it cost to print at the Rock Hill library?

$0.20 a page for black-and-white and $0.60 for color at York County Library locations [1].

Can I send a fax at the library?

Yes. FaxScan24 kiosks cost $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page within the U.S., Canada and the Caribbean, by card only [2].

How much is a marriage license in Rock Hill?

York County's Probate Court charges $50, by cash, check or money order, and you apply by appointment at the Moss Justice Center in York [6]. There's a 24-hour wait [31].

Where can I apply for a passport in Rock Hill?

The State Department's locator lists the York County Clerk of Court, 933 Heckle Blvd., Suite 101 [3]. The county charges a $35 execution fee and $10 for a photo [22].
