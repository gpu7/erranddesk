Castle Rock, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport, get certified copies and shred documents in Castle Rock, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the Town of Castle Rock, Douglas County, Douglas County Libraries, USPS, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Castle Rock: The Town of Castle Rock is at 100 N. Wilcox St., 720-896-8696 [14]. The Douglas County Libraries branch is at 100 S. Wilcox Street [2], and the Douglas County Recording Office, which also takes passport applications, is at 301 Wilcox Street [9]. Douglas County's in-person birth and death certificate office is not in Castle Rock; the county lists it in Englewood [10]. Passport search results for "Castle Rock" also include Castle Rock, Washington. That's a different town, so check that a result says Castle Rock, CO [7].
Short version: The Castle Rock library prints black and white for 10 cents a page and color for 25 cents [1]. The Douglas County Recording Office takes new passport applications by appointment only, Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m., and can take your passport photo for $13 [8]. Recording a document costs $43 [11].

Notary in Castle Rock

  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [5].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [6]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 200 S Wilcox St, Castle Rock, CO 80104 says it has "a commissioned notary public available" and is "Open 7 Days," with appointments available online. Phone (303) 660-2244. Its page doesn't post a price [4]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [5].
  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Douglas County Libraries pages we checked [1][2].

Passports in Castle Rock

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator lists one acceptance facility with a Castle Rock, Colorado address [7]:

FacilityAddress and phonePassport hours listedPhotos
Douglas County Clerk and Recorder301 Wilcox Street, Castle Rock, CO 80104. 303-660-7446Mon–Wed and Fri 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., closed 1–2 p.m.; Thu 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.; by appointmentListed as on site
  • County hours and rules: the Recording Office takes NEW passport applications only, by appointment only, Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Appointments can be scheduled 28 days in advance; "If there are no openings, please try again the following morning." Every applicant, including children, must be present [8].
  • Renewals: adult passports issued within the past 15 years are renewed online or by mail with the State Department, not at this office [8].
  • Fees: the $35 acceptance fee and the $13 passport photo can be paid by cash, check, money order or credit card to Douglas County Clerk. The State Department fee (national) must be a personal check, money order or cashier's check payable to the U.S. Department of State; the county lists $130 for a first adult passport book [8].
  • Urgent travel: the office can't process urgent or emergency requests; call the State Department at 1-877-487-2778 [8].
  • Post offices: we didn't find a post office with a Castle Rock, Colorado address in the locator data we used. The only other "Castle Rock" result is a post office in Castle Rock, WA, which we left out [7]. USPS fees are on its passports page [15].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Douglas County Health Department

  • In person (Englewood, not Castle Rock): 11045 E. Lansing Circle, 3rd Floor, Suite 337, Englewood, CO 80112. Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., excluding holidays. No appointment is needed, and records are available for births and deaths in any Colorado county. Phone 720-643-2480 [10]. The same page also gives Suite 300 for this address, so check before you go [10].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 for the first copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same certificate ordered at the same time. The county notes the State of Colorado raised these fees effective January 1, 2026 [10].
  • Payment: cash, check and money orders have no convenience fee. Credit and debit cards carry a 2.5% fee with a $2.00 minimum [10].
  • Online orders: processing takes 5 business days, with an extra charge [10].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Douglas County Recording Office, Castle Rock

  • Office: 301 Wilcox Street, Castle Rock, CO 80104, 303-660-7446. Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [9].
  • Recording fees: $43 per document; death certificates and military discharge (DD-214) have no charge; land survey plats are $50 [11].
  • Copy fees: recorded documents $.25 per printed page; certification $1.00 per document; mailing $1–$7 depending on pages. Recorded documents can also be viewed online through LandmarkWeb. The office doesn't keep birth or death certificates [11].
  • Marriage or civil union license: $30, cash or credit card only. Apply online first, then both parties come to 301 Wilcox Street by appointment, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. A certified copy is $1.25 [12].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [13]. The county accepts money orders for passport fees and certificates [8][10]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

We didn't find a Town of Castle Rock or Douglas County shred event on the official pages we checked. Some businesses hold free shred days; check the host's own page for dates and limits. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Castle Rock, Colorado?

The Douglas County Recording Office at 301 Wilcox Street, for new applications by appointment only, Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. [8].

How much does printing cost at the Castle Rock library?

10 cents per black-and-white page and 25 cents per color page, letter size only. Pay staff with Visa or MasterCard ($1 minimum) [1].

Can I get a birth certificate in Castle Rock?

Douglas County's in-person vital records office is listed at 11045 E. Lansing Circle in Englewood. You can also order online, by email or by mail [10].

How much does it cost to record a document in Douglas County?

$43 per document. Recording a death certificate or DD-214 has no charge [11].
