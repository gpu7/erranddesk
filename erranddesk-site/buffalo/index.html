Buffalo, New York document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Buffalo, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Buffalo, Erie County, the Buffalo & Erie County Public Library, New York State, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: 6 passport acceptance facilities are inside the city, including the Erie County Clerk [1][2]. Buffalo birth certificates are $10 from the City Clerk [3]. A marriage license is $40 at City Hall [4]. New York notaries may charge $2.00 per act (statewide) [5]. The City's free shred day is Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026 [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Buffalo & Erie County Public Library, Kenilworth Branch (318 Montrose Avenue): network printing is 10¢ per black-and-white page and 25¢ in color. A self-service public fax is $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page, and two of its public computers have scanners [7]. Other branches may differ; we read only this branch page.
  • Central Library, Grosvenor Room (special collections): photocopies are 15¢ for black-and-white letter size and 50¢ in color; microfilm and database printouts are 10¢ a page, black and white only [8].
  • The UPS Store: its Buffalo page lists 3 locations (345 Broadway, 2316 Delaware Ave and 266 Elmwood Ave) offering printing, shredding, notarizing and faxing [9]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Buffalo

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the library pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Buffalo page lists notarizing [9]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a New York notary may charge $2.00 for an oath or affirmation, or for taking an acknowledgment or proof of execution, and unless another law allows more, no more than that. That is the wording on the Westchester County Clerk's notary FAQ [5]; the Department of State's own FAQ refused our request [11].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Birth certificates (Buffalo City Clerk, local registrar)

  • Who and where: the City Clerk's vital records office, City Hall Room 1302, issues certificates for births in the city of Buffalo; its records start in 1878 [3].
  • Fee: $10 per certificate in person or by mail (money order to the City Clerk). A wallet-sized photo birth certificate is $25, in person only. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds a fee. Mail orders take 7–10 days [3].
  • State alternative: the New York State Department of Health also issues certificates for births outside New York City, for $30 by mail (statewide) [14].
  • Death certificates: we didn't find a Buffalo death certificate fee on the pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Marriage licenses (Buffalo City Clerk)

  • Where and when: City Hall Room 1301, Monday–Friday, 9 a.m. to no later than 3:30 p.m. Both people must be present, and you must be 18 or older [4].
  • Fee and wait: $40. The license is issued immediately, but the ceremony can't be within 24 hours of the time it was issued. It's valid for 60 days, starting the day after it's issued [4].
  • Compared with New York City: the statewide fee outside NYC is $40 [15]; the NYC City Clerk charges $35 [16].

Deeds and land records (Erie County Clerk)

  • Copy fees: copies are $1.30 for the first 2 pages and 65¢ for each additional page. Certified copies are $5.00 for the first 4 pages and $1.25 for each additional page. Either kind is capped at $40.00 per document; a 5-page record is $3.25, or $6.25 certified [17].
  • Fee schedule: the Clerk's fee schedule lists the same copy minimums and the $40.00 maximum [18].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New York State Department of State. The state Department of Health can forward a state-issued certificate to it if you order by mail with expedited processing ($45.00 per copy) and enclose a separate $10.00 check or money order per certificate, payable to the Department of State [19]. The Department of State's own apostille page refused our request [20].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Upcoming (City of Buffalo): Free Shredding Event & Clothing Recycling Donation Drive, Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026, 9 a.m.–noon, Shoshone Park, 445 Beard Ave [6].
  • Past event: the City's spring shred day was April 11, 2026, at 363 Clinton St. The City holds the event twice a year, in April and November, for city residents [22].
  • The UPS Store: its Buffalo page lists shredding [9].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Buffalo?

At 6 facilities in the locator data: the Erie County Clerk and 5 post offices [1][2].

How much is a Buffalo birth certificate?

$10 from the City Clerk, or $25 for a wallet-sized photo certificate in person [3].

How much is a marriage license in Buffalo?

$40, with a 24-hour wait before the ceremony [4].

How much does a New York notary charge?

$2.00 per oath, affirmation or acknowledgment (statewide) [5].
