Huntington, West Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Huntington, West Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Errand Desk isn't affiliated with Cabell County, the Cabell County Clerk or Circuit Clerk, Cabell County Public Library, the West Virginia Secretary of State, the West Virginia Health Statistics Center, the State of West Virginia, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: Cabell County Public Library charges 15 cents a page to print (first page free) or copy, $1.00 for the first fax page and notarizes for free [1]. The Cabell County Clerk lists a $57 marriage license, or $37 with premarital counseling [2]. The only passport acceptance facility in the locator's Huntington list is the Main Post Office [3].
Two counties, two clerks: Huntington is mostly in Cabell County, with part in Wayne County [4][5]; this page covers the Cabell County offices at the courthouse, 750 5th Avenue. The county clerk handles land, birth, death and marriage records and wills [6]; the circuit clerk handles court records [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Cabell County Public Library (455 9th Street)

  • Printing: black and white $0.15 a page, first page free; color $0.50 a page where available [1].
  • Copies: $0.15 a page, color $0.50 [1].
  • Fax: sending is $1.00 for the first page and $0.50 for each additional page; receiving is $0.25 a page [1].
  • Scanning: we didn't find a scanning price on the library page (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Hours: Monday 9 a.m.–8:30 p.m., Tuesday–Friday 9 a.m.–6 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; 304-528-5700 [1]. The Census Geocoder places it in Huntington city [8].
  • The UPS Store: its Huntington page lists 1 location, 729 9th Ave, (304) 529-1776 [9]. The Census Geocoder places it in Huntington city [8]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Huntington

  • Library (free): free notary service during regular business hours at the Reference Desk on the library's second floor. Photo ID is required; don't sign until you're in front of the notary. Branch times vary, so call ahead [1].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules vary by state; call the Huntington store to confirm [9][10].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State commissions notaries. The application or recommission fee is $52, waived for state and local government notaries; a commission is good for five years [11][12]. No fee is charged to add e-notarization authorization [11].
  • Fees (statewide): under W. Va. Code §39-4-30, a notary may charge up to $10.00 for each signature notarized, up to $10.00 per 8½ × 11 page for certifying a copy (the notary keeps a copy on file), and up to $10.00 for any other notarial act [13].
  • Copy certification (statewide): if no other office can certify a document, a West Virginia notary can certify a true copy after comparing it with the original and keeping a copy [14][13]. Vital records, court orders and recorded deeds still come from the office that holds them [14].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Cabell County Clerk: the clerk keeps copies of Cabell County birth and death records; the originals are with the State Vital Statistics Office. Only certain family members can get a certificate, and online orders go through VitalDirector [2]. We didn't find a county birth or death certificate fee online; state law sets $5 for a county clerk's certified copy [17].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $12 per certified copy from the state Vital Registration Office; the fee includes a nonrefundable search. If the record isn't found, staff search the year given plus the year before and after; more searches are $12 per three-year period [18].
  • In person (statewide): same-day walk-in service at 350 Capitol Street, Room 165, Charleston, WV 25301-3701. Cash, check and money order are accepted; cards at the walk-up counter add a 4% surcharge. Expect a wait of at least 15 minutes, longer from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. [18].
  • By mail or online (statewide): mail the form with a color copy of your ID and a check or money order (no cards by mail). Online orders go through third-party vendors such as VitalChek; the state gets $12 a copy whatever the vendor charges [18].
  • County clerk copies (statewide law): W. Va. Code lets the county clerk charge $5 for a certified copy of a birth certificate, death certificate or marriage license it holds [17]. Check with the county clerk which records it has.
  • Divorce records (statewide): a divorce decree comes from the circuit clerk of the court that issued it, not from Vital Registration [14].

Marriage licenses and records (Cabell County Clerk)

  • Cabell County fees: $57 without premarital counseling, $37 with proof of counseling dated within a year [2]. These are $2 more than the $55 and $35 set in state code [17]; we show both and haven't confirmed what accounts for the difference. Pay the county's posted fee.
  • How: both parties apply together, no appointment; applications stop at 4 p.m. Bring ID with proof of age and Social Security numbers. Licenses are valid for 60 days [2].
  • Certified marriage copy: $5.00 [2].
  • State law (statewide): every West Virginia marriage needs a license from a county clerk (the clerk of the county commission) [19]. The age of consent is 18; 16- and 17-year-olds need written consent under the statute's conditions [20]. The statutory fee is $55, or $35 if you show a premarital education course completion certificate [17].

Recorded documents (Cabell County Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in West Virginia, the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) records deeds; the circuit clerk handles court records [17][14]. Recording a deed or trust deed is $30; most other documents are $10; documents over five pages add $1 per extra page. Copies are $1.50 (plus $1 per page over two), and attaching the clerk's seal is $1 (§59-1-10) [17].
  • Cabell County: deeds can be brought in, mailed or e-recorded. Every deed needs a Sales Listing Form, and transfer tax is $5.50 per $1,000 unless exempt, plus a $20.00 WV Housing Development Fund fee when transfer tax is paid [21]. Record Room: (304) 526-9820 [22].

Court records and divorce decrees (Cabell County Circuit Clerk)

  • Circuit clerk fees (statewide): W. Va. Code §59-1-11 sets $1 a page for a copy the circuit clerk makes to go out of the office, $135 to file for divorce, separate maintenance or annulment, and $200 for most other civil filings [23] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Cabell County: 750 5th Avenue, Room 114, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., 304-526-8622 [7]. We didn't find a local copy-price list (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State, at the State Capitol Building in Charleston. It issues Apostille Certificates for countries in the Hague Apostille Convention and Authentication Certificates for other countries [14].
  • What it certifies (statewide): only documents attested by a proper West Virginia officer, such as a notary public, county clerk or the State Registrar. Recorded deeds and marriage certificates need the county clerk's certified copy; court orders and divorce decrees need the circuit clerk's [14].
  • Fee and hours (statewide): $10 per certification; if several documents are certified by the same official, the first is $10 and each additional one is $5. In-person service runs 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday–Friday [14].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.
