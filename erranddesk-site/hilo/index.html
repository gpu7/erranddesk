Hilo, Hawaii document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Hilo CDP, Hawaii, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Counties and recording in Hawaii (statewide): Vital records and marriage licenses are issued by the state Department of Health (and licensed marriage agents), not by city clerks. Land records are recorded at the state Bureau of Conveyances in Honolulu under a statewide fee schedule — Hawaii does not use county recorders for deeds [1][2][3].
Short version: the passport locator dump has 0 facilities with a Hilo city-name match [4]. Marriage agents include Hilo District Health Office (75 Aupuni St Ste 201) [5][6]. No FedEx Office in Hilo — OfficeMax/Depot + Ship Center [7]. UPS /hi/hilo returned 404; Pahoa is on the Hawaii index [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Hawaii State Public Library System: a print donation of 15¢ per page is appreciated [9]. Photocopies: an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page; color and special paper may cost more — fees are displayed at each library [10].
  • FedEx: Hilo has no FedEx Office — listings show FedEx at OfficeMax/Office Depot (311 E Makaala St) and a FedEx Ship Center (43 Akahana) [7]. Office Depot national document-printing starting prices: $0.18 B&W / $0.70 color per page — confirm at the Hilo OfficeMax counter (store 6472) [11][12].
  • The UPS Store: /hi/hilo returned 404; Hilo is not on the Hawaii index (24 stores) [8]. The Big Island listing includes Pahoa at 15-2714 Pahoa Village Rd Ste H1, (808) 769-4877 — that store is in Pahoa CDP, not Hilo [13].

See printing prices.

Notary in Hilo, Hawaii

  • County Clerk: Office of the County Clerk (Jon Henricks), 25 Aupuni Street Suite 1402, Hilo, (808) 961-8255, clerk-council@hawaiicounty.gov — elections/council duties. We didn't find a published public notary service or customer fee on that page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [14].
  • The UPS Store: no Hilo listing on the Hawaii index [8][15].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Hawaii commissions notaries through the Attorney General. Application $20, examination $10, commission $100; renewal $120; four-year term; $1,000 surety bond [16][17]. Under HRS §456-17, notaries may charge at most $5 for an acknowledgment (per party signing) or for administration of an oath; remote online notarial acts are capped at $25 [18][19]. A notary may charge less [19].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): certified birth and marriage certificates are $10 for the first copy and $4 for each additional copy of the same record, plus a $2.50 portal/administration fee per increment of up to five copies [23]. Death certificates use the same $10 / $4 copy fees plus the $2.50 fee [24].
    • Oahu Vital Records office: 201 Merchant St., Suite 100, City Financial Tower, Honolulu. Phone (808) 586-4539 (certificates). Hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (issuance/marriage); closed state holidays [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $3.00 per document [25].

    Marriage licenses

    • State rules (statewide): a marriage or civil union license costs $65.00 ($60 application fee + $5 portal fee), non-refundable. Apply online at emrs.ehawaii.gov, then both applicants meet a licensed agent together in person no more than 30 days before the ceremony [26][6].
    • Where in Hilo: District Health Office, 75 Aupuni St., Suite 201, Hilo — Letisha (808) 974-6008; private agent Rose Delfin also listed. Apply online first [5][6][26].

    Land records (Bureau of Conveyances)

    • Bureau of Conveyances office: Kalanimoku Building, 1151 Punchbowl Street, Honolulu. Office hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recordation windows about 8:01 a.m.–3:29 p.m. (confirm before you go) [2][27].
    • Recording fees (statewide): Bureau of Conveyances Regular System: $41 per document up to 50 pages, or $106 for 51 pages or more. Land Court: $36 (≤50 pages) or $101 (51+); Certificate of Title issuance $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [25].
    • Fee (statewide): $3.00 per document (apostille or certification) [25].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [28]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Shredding

    • Hawaii County: we didn't find a county-run household paper-shredding event (last checked Nov 5, 2026). A Hilo HHW collection (e.g. Feb 7, 2026) is hazardous waste, not paper shredding.

    For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

    Is there a UPS Store in Hilo?

    No — /hi/hilo returned 404. Pahoa (15-2714 Pahoa Village Rd) is on the Hawaii index but is Pahoa CDP, not Hilo [13][8].

    Where do I get a marriage license in Hilo?

    Apply online ($65), then meet an agent at the Hilo District Health Office (75 Aupuni Ste 201) or a listed private agent [5][26].

    Where can I apply for a passport in Hilo?

    Facilities.json and the Census Geocoder: 0 passport acceptance facilities inside Hilo CDP [4][20]. The live passport locator was Cloudflare-blocked during research (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Check passportfacilitylocator.travel.state.gov for current island options — nearby CDP matches are not Hilo.

    How much does printing cost at the Hilo library?

    Hawaii State Public Library System asks for a print donation of 15¢ per page. Photocopies are an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page; color and special paper may cost more, and fees are displayed at each library [9][10].
