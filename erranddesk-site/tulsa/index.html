Tulsa, Oklahoma document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Tulsa, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the Tulsa City-County Library, Tulsa County, the Tulsa County Court Clerk, the Tulsa County Clerk, the Tulsa Health Department, the Oklahoma State Department of Health, the Oklahoma Secretary of State, the State of Oklahoma, USPS, The UPS Store, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Tulsa: the public library is the Tulsa City-County Library [1]. The Tulsa County Court Clerk, in the Tulsa County Courthouse at 500 S. Denver Ave., issues marriage licenses and accepts passport applications [2][3]; the Tulsa County Clerk records deeds and sells copies of them [4]. Birth and death certificates come from the Oklahoma State Department of Health, which has a Will Call pickup in Tulsa [5], and apostilles from the Oklahoma Secretary of State in Oklahoma City [6]. Most of Tulsa is in Tulsa County; this guide covers Tulsa County offices.
Short version: Tulsa City-County Library copies and prints are 10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color, faxes are 50 cents a page and scanning is free; every location has a Business Center [1]. Libraries are cashless, and cardholders get $3 a month of free printing, copying or faxing [7]. The passport locator lists 4 Tulsa facilities: 3 post offices and the Tulsa County Court Clerk, which takes walk-ins [8][3]. Marriage licenses are $50, or $5 with a premarital counseling certificate [2]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [9], and apostilles are $25.00 at the Secretary of State [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Tulsa City-County Library

  • Business Center: available at all TCCL locations to copy, fax, print and scan. Copies and prints are 10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color; faxes are 50 cents a page; scanning to email or a USB drive is free, and USB drives are $8 [1].
  • Paying: all TCCL locations are cashless; the card readers take credit, debit and prepaid cards and Apple and Google Pay. Every TCCL cardholder gets a $3 allowance each month for free printing, copying and faxing, which doesn't carry over [7].
  • Wireless printing: send a job from your own device or from home, then release it at a print station at any location within 24 hours. A guest name or library card number is needed [10].
  • Central Library: 400 Civic Center, 918-549-7323. Hours: Sunday 1–5 p.m., Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–9 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–6 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Its page lists a Business Center, wireless printing and public fax, scan and copy [11]. Library customers parking in the Central Library garage get 2 hours of free parking [7].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 9 locations in Tulsa; see its city page for addresses and phone numbers. That page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [12]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [13].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Tulsa

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Tulsa City-County Library's library services page [14]. Call 918-549-7323 to ask.
  • Tulsa County Clerk: its fee schedule lists a $5.00 notary fee [4]. We didn't find hours or details for notary service; call 918-596-5801 before you go.
  • The UPS Store: its Tulsa page lists notarizing [12]. We didn't find store notary prices; Oklahoma's legal maximum is below.
  • Fee cap (statewide): under 49 O.S. § 5, the most an Oklahoma notary may charge is $5.00 for each notarial act, and notarizing an official absentee ballot affidavit is free [9]. The Secretary of State's Notary Public Guide (dated 12/2025) gives the same $5.00 maximum and the same absentee ballot rule [15].
  • Remote online notarization (statewide): 49 O.S. § 209 lets a notary charge up to $25.00 for a remote online notarial act [16].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Oklahoma State Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees: OSDH Vital Records charges $15.00 per certified birth or death certificate. Phone and online orders add a $5.00 convenience fee for Oklahoma residents or $12.95 for non-residents [19]; the FAQ puts phone and online orders at $20.00 for the first copy and $15 for each additional copy [19].
  • No walk-ins: the Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins. Online orders (through VitalChek) and phone orders at (405) 426-8880 can be picked up at "Will Call" in Oklahoma City, Tulsa or McAlester, usually in about 2 business days; bring a photo ID and the order confirmation [20][21]. Will Call hours are 12:00–4:45 p.m., Monday–Friday [19].
  • By mail: send the application, a photo ID and a check or money order payable to OSDH to Vital Records Service, Oklahoma State Department of Health, PO Box 248964, Oklahoma City, OK 73124-8964 [19][22]. OSDH lists about 4–6 weeks for mail orders [20], and its death certificate page says mail orders may take 6 weeks [21].
  • Marriage and divorce records: OSDH points these to the county court clerk where the license or divorce was filed [20]; the county block below has the local office.
  • Will Call in Tulsa: the Oklahoma State Department of Health's Tulsa Division of Vital Records offers Will Call pickup only, at the James O. Goodwin Health Center, 5051 S. 129th East Ave., Tulsa, OK 74134, 12:00–4:45 p.m. Monday–Friday except state holidays. Records must be ordered in advance online or by phone; same-day service is no longer available. Inquiries: (405) 426-8880 [5].
  • Tulsa Health Department: doesn't take online or phone orders itself and points you to VitalChek [5].

Marriage licenses: Tulsa County Court Clerk

  • Fee: $50.00, reduced to $5.00 if you bring a certificate showing at least 4 hours of premarital counseling, as described in 43 O.S. § 5.1. At the Courthouse office you can pay with cash or a credit or debit card; the Broken Arrow satellite takes only credit or debit cards [2].
  • How: both applicants must be present, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday. Bring proof of age, such as a state-issued ID, a birth certificate plus photo ID, or a passport. There's no residency requirement, but the marriage must be performed in Oklahoma. There's no waiting period unless an applicant is under 18 (then 72 hours) [2].
  • Where: 2nd floor, Tulsa County Courthouse, 500 S. Denver Ave., Tulsa, 918-596-5478, or the satellite office at 123 N. Main Street, Broken Arrow, 918-596-8991 [2]. The Broken Arrow satellite is also open Saturday 9 a.m.–1 p.m. [23].
  • Copies: for a copy of a marriage license or divorce decree, visit the Court Clerk's Office 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday; call 918-596-5478 for marriage licenses or 918-596-5454 for divorce decrees [2]. The Broken Arrow satellite lists a $5.00 search fee and $1.50 for each certified copy [23].

Recorded documents: Tulsa County Clerk

  • Recording fees: $18.00 for the first page of a deed or other recorded instrument and $2.00 for each additional page; a cover sheet is $2.00 [4].
  • Copies: $1.00 a page; a certified copy of an instrument is $1.00 plus $1.00 for each additional page; plat copies are $5.00. Filing or copying a military discharge (DD214) is free [4].
  • Where and how: Tulsa County Headquarters, 218 W. 6th St., 7th Floor, Tulsa, 918-596-5801. Documents are accepted for recording Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [4]. Credit cards are accepted with a 2½ percent convenience fee [24].
  • County clerk fees (statewide): 28 O.S. § 32 sets county clerk recording fees of $8.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, plus a $10.00 preservation fee per instrument, and $1.00 a page for copies and $1.00 a page to certify them [25].

Court clerk copy fees (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): under 28 O.S. § 31, a court clerk charges $1.00 for the first page of a copy of a record and $0.50 for each additional page, $0.50 to certify an instrument and $5.00 to authenticate court records. Recording a marriage license is $5.00 with a premarital counseling certificate (43 O.S. § 5.1) or $50.00 without one [26]. A judge or justice may also collect a $10.00 marriage ceremony fee on top of that (28 O.S. § 56) [27].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Oklahoma apostilles and authentications come from the Secretary of State, not the county. They can be attached to an original notarized document or a certified copy of a recordable document, such as an Oklahoma birth certificate, marriage license or divorce decree [6].
  • Fee: $25.00 per apostille (per notarial act or certified document), or $10.00 per apostille for international adoption proceedings; an authentication is $20.00. Credit card payments add a 4% convenience fee [6].
  • In person: 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, (405) 521-4211, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. In person you can pay by cash, cashier's check, money order, credit card or business check [6].
  • By mail: include a cover letter naming the country, a phone number or email address and the return mailing address, and pay by certified cashier's check, money order or business check payable to Oklahoma Secretary of State [6]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [28]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I fax at the library in Tulsa?

Yes. Every Tulsa City-County Library location has a Business Center, and faxes are 50 cents a page [1]. Payment is by card; locations are cashless [7].

How much does printing cost at the library?

10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color, and cardholders get $3 a month free [1][7].

Where can I get something notarized in Tulsa?

The UPS Store's Tulsa page lists notarizing [12], and the Tulsa County Clerk's fee schedule lists a $5.00 notary fee [4]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [9].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the Tulsa County Court Clerk?

No. It's first come, first served, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday, and payment is by check or money order [3].

How much is a marriage license in Tulsa County?

$50, or $5 with a premarital counseling certificate, at the Court Clerk [2].

Can I walk in for a birth certificate in Tulsa?

No. Order online or by phone first, then pick up at Will Call at the James O. Goodwin Health Center, 12:00–4:45 p.m. weekdays [5].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Oklahoma Secretary of State, 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, in person or by mail, for $25.00 per apostille (statewide) [6].
