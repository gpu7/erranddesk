New Haven, Connecticut document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in New Haven, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of New Haven, the New Haven Free Public Library, the State of Connecticut, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 5 passport acceptance facilities in New Haven: 4 post offices and the Ives Main Library [1]. The library doesn't offer notary service [2]. Birth certificates are $20 at the city's Office of Vital Statistics, and a marriage license is $50 with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [3].
No county clerk in Connecticut (statewide): Connecticut abolished county government effective Oct. 1, 1960, and counties remain only as geographic areas [4][5]. Town clerks keep land records, and each town's registrar of vital statistics issues birth, death and marriage records.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • New Haven Free Public Library printing: 25¢ a page black and white, 50¢ color, at every location. Card payments carry a temporary $10 hold. Wi-Fi printing uses Princh [6].
  • Library fax: Fax24 machines at every location: $1.75 for the first page and $1 for each extra page. Pay by card at the machine, or buy a prepaid card at the desk for 1–6 pages [6].
  • FedEx: FedEx's New Haven page lists 3 locations: a FedEx Office Ship Center at 30 Whitney Ave. and 2 Walgreens counters [7].
  • The UPS Store: 1 New Haven store, at The Shops at Yale, 33 Dixwell Ave. [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in New Haven

  • Library: the library says it doesn't offer notary service. It points to The UPS Store at 33 Dixwell Ave., weekdays 9–6, at $5 per document. That answer was last updated May 14, 2024, so the $5 may be out of date. The state maximum is now $10 (see below) [2].
  • City Clerk: the clerk's fee list includes $5.00 to certify a notary's signature and $20.00 for a notary certificate. These are clerk services for notaries, not notarizations [9].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): since July 1, 2026, a Connecticut notary may charge up to $10.00 per notarial act, plus travel at no more than the IRS business mileage rate. Public Act 26-81 raised the fee from $5.00 [11][12].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): the General Assembly's compiled statute page for §3-95, revised to January 1, 2026, still shows the old cap of $5.00 plus 35¢ a mile [13]. The Secretary of the State's pages give the $10.00 fee in effect since July 1, 2026 [11][12]. We show the newer figure.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

New Haven Office of Vital Statistics

  • Where: City Hall, 165 Church St., Suite 154, 1st floor. Phone 203-946-7931 [18].
  • Hours conflict: the Vital Statistics page says Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. [18]. The records page says Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [3]. Both say 9 a.m.–2 p.m. on the first Wednesday of the month.
  • Birth certificates: $20 full size, $15 wallet size, $65 for an adopted person's original certificate. Pay by cash, card or money order; no personal checks. Mail requests go to a processing address in El Paso, Texas. Online orders go through VitalChek, which charges an extra fee [3].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Where (statewide): from the vital records office of the town where the event happened, or from the State Vital Records Office. Town requests are generally faster [19].
  • Fees (statewide): town-issued birth, death, marriage and civil union certificates are $20 per copy. State-issued birth certificates are $30 per copy [19][20].
  • Paying a town (statewide): by mail, towns take a money order payable to the town; personal checks aren't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds fees [19].

Marriage licenses (Office of Vital Statistics)

  • Fee: $50.00, by cash, card or money order. Valid 65 days [3].
  • 48-hour wait (conflict on the same page): the page says a 48-hour wait applies from Oct. 1, 2026. Further down, it still says New Haven residents can walk in for same-day service [3]. Under the new state rule, plan on the 48-hour wait [21].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at the vital records office of the town where the wedding will be. The fee is $50.00. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the town can't issue the license until 48 hours after the second person signs the application. The license is good for 65 days [21][22].

Land records (City Clerk, 200 Orange St.)

  • New Haven's posted fees (effective July 1, 2025): recording $70.00 for the first page and $5.00 for each extra page; copies $1.00 a page; certified copies $2.00. Recording hours are 9 a.m.–4 p.m. The clerk accepts cash, check or money order [9].
  • Copy fees (statewide law): CGS §7-34a sets town clerk copies at $1 per page and $2 to certify a copy. Recording is $10 for the first page and $5 for each extra page, plus $10 and $50 per-document state surcharges [23].
  • How they compare: New Haven's posted copy and certification fees match the statute [9][23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Connecticut Secretary of the State. All orders must be placed online at sots.service.ct.gov/apostille; paper order forms are no longer accepted [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $40 per document, or $15 for documents for a child adoption. Expedited service is $50 more per document [25][13]. These figures come from the SOTS order form and the statute; the online-ordering page we read doesn't list fees.
  • Delivery (statewide): paper documents go by courier to 165 Capitol Avenue, Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06106, or by mail to P.O. Box 150470, Hartford, CT 06115-0470. E-apostilles began July 6, 2026, but vital records still need a paper apostille. E-apostille and expedited orders are processed within 24 hours [24].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Hometown Recycling Day (past, Sept. 26, 2026): the New Haven Solid Waste & Recycling Authority's flyer listed on-site document shredding, up to 3 file boxes, on Saturday, Sept. 26, 2026, 10 a.m.–1 p.m. at 114 Farnham Ave. (SCSU campus), for New Haven residents only. Info: 203-691-5374 [27]. We didn't find a later 2026 shredding event (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: the New Haven store page lists shredding among its services [8]; we didn't find prices online.

Questions

Can I apply for a passport at the New Haven library?

Yes, at the Ives Main Library, 133 Elm St. The library charges a $35 fee; check its hours, because they differ from the locator's [15][1].

How much is a birth certificate in New Haven?

$20 full size or $15 wallet size [3].

Does the New Haven library notarize documents?

No [2].

How much does a Connecticut notary charge?

Up to $10.00 per act since July 1, 2026, plus mileage (statewide) [11][12].

How much does printing cost at the New Haven library?

New Haven Free Public Library printing is 25¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color, at every location. Card payments carry a temporary $10 hold [6].
