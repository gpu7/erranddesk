Cheyenne, Wyoming document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Cheyenne, Wyoming, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Wyoming rules differ from Colorado's in several places, and we point those out.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Cheyenne: Cheyenne is the capital of Wyoming and the seat of Laramie County. This page covers the Laramie County Library System, whose main Cheyenne Library is at 2200 Pioneer Ave., Cheyenne, WY 82001, 307.634.3561 [3]; the Laramie County Clerk, 309 W. 20th St., which handles recording and marriage licenses [6][7]; and Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, the state office that issues birth and death certificates, which is also in Cheyenne [8][9].
Short version: Black and white printing at the Cheyenne Library is $0.10 a side after up to $1.00 of free printing a day [1]. The main library offers free notary service during regular hours [4]. The Cheyenne Main Post Office takes passport applications by appointment [5]. Wyoming birth certificates are $25 each from the state office at 2300 Capitol Avenue [10]. The county charges $12 for the first page of a recorded document and $3 for each additional page [6].

Notary in Cheyenne

  • Library: the main library in Cheyenne "offers free notary services during regular library hours." Bring a government-issued photo ID. If you don't have one, you may bring two people with valid ID who can verify who you are. Call 307-634-3561 before you go to make sure a notary is on duty [4].
  • Wyoming fee limit (statewide): under W.S. 32-3-126, a notarial officer may charge "not more than ten dollars ($10.00) per notarial act," may charge less or waive the fee, and may add a technology fee for electronic notarization or a travel fee (up to the IRS standard mileage rate) only if you agree to it in advance [12]. The Wyoming Secretary of State's notary page links to this law [11]. This differs from Colorado, where the limit is $15 per document.
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • Credit union (example): WyHy Federal Credit Union says "Notary services are free" [14]. Its Cheyenne branch, 1715 Stillwater Ave, Cheyenne, WY 82009, 800.442.2392, lists notary services. Branch hours: Mon, Tue and Thu 8 a.m.–5 p.m., Wed 9 a.m.–5 p.m., Fri 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m., Sat 9 a.m.–noon [15].
  • The UPS Store (example): its Cheyenne page lists notarizing among its services, without prices [19]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $10 per notarial act [12].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Wyoming Vital Statistics Services (state office)

  • Who issues them (statewide): in Wyoming, certified birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates come from the state's Vital Statistics Services, part of the Wyoming Department of Health. Its office is in Cheyenne [8][9]. Unlike the Colorado cities we cover, we didn't find a county vital records counter.
  • In person in Cheyenne: 2300 Capitol Avenue, 1st Floor of the Hathaway Building. Public hours Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; Friday by appointment only, (307) 777-7591 [9].
  • Fees (statewide, from the mail form): birth, marriage, divorce or stillbirth certificate $25.00 each. Death certificate $25.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy per request. A photocopy of an original certificate is $30.00. The 2026 form's footer reads "Form 1a - 2024" [10].
  • Closed records (statewide): "Wyoming is a closed record state." Birth records are closed to the public for 100 years and death, marriage and divorce records for 50 years, so only entitled people (such as the person named, a parent, or immediate family for a death record) can get copies. You need a government-issued photo ID, or a notarized signature on the form [10].
  • By mail: send the form with ID, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Checks or money orders go to "Vital Statistics Services"; cash is accepted but not recommended [10].
  • Online (statewide): the How to order page's Order Online link goes to the state's own VSS Portal at wyomingvitalrecords.wyo.gov [8]. You need an account, and you must attach an image of a valid government-issued ID. Online orders are paid by credit card only, with a 2.4% convenience fee ($1.00 minimum) on every order. For delivery other than USPS First Class Mail, you can attach a prepaid label you buy from UPS, FedEx or USPS [21]. Per-certificate online fees, processing times and shipping options are behind the portal sign-in; we didn't find them.
  • Payment (statewide): the state FAQ lists money orders, a personal check from the person applying, credit card (online or in-person orders only) and cash (in-person orders only). The office can't take payment over the phone [22].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Laramie County Clerk

  • Office: Laramie County Clerk, 309 W. 20th St., Cheyenne, WY 82001. Marriage licenses and recording are both in Suite 1600, 307-633-4350. Office hours Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [7].
  • Recording: $12.00 for the first page and $3.00 for each additional page, plus small per-item charges for long name or legal-description lists [6]. This differs from Colorado's flat $43 per document.
  • Copies: certification of a recorded document is $5.00, plus copy fees of $0.50 per printed page or $1.00 per page by email. You can research and view recorded records in the office during business hours at no charge [6].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. Wyoming Vital Statistics Services takes money orders for mailed certificate requests [10]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • WyHy Federal Credit Union (example): its free Fall Shred Day in Cheyenne was October 3, 2026, 9–11 a.m., with up to two large boxes of paper per person [16]. That date has passed. Its spring 2026 Cheyenne event was at 1715 Stillwater Ave [20]. We didn't find a 2027 date.

We didn't find a City of Cheyenne or Laramie County shred event on the pages we could read. The UPS Store lists shredding at its Cheyenne location, without prices [19]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Cheyenne?

At the Cheyenne Main Post Office, 4800 Converse Ave., by appointment at usps.com/scheduler [5].

Is there a free notary in Cheyenne?

Yes. The main Cheyenne Library at 2200 Pioneer Ave. offers free notary services during regular library hours. Call 307-634-3561 first [4].

How much does printing cost at the library in Cheyenne?

Up to $1.00 of printing a day is free, then black and white is $0.10 per side and color $0.50 per side [1].

Where can I get a Wyoming birth certificate in Cheyenne?

At Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, 2300 Capitol Avenue, Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m. Each copy is $25 [9][10].
