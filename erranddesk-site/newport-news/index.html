Newport News, Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Newport News, Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: Newport News Public Library prints for 20 cents a page in black and white [1][2]. The Public Law Library notarizes for free, up to 3 documents a visit [3]. Main Street Library takes passport applications by appointment [4]. A marriage license from the Circuit Court Clerk is $30 [5]. Virginia notaries can charge up to $10 per act (statewide) [6].
An independent city, no county: Newport News isn't part of any county. The Census Bureau lists "Newport News city" [7] as its own county-level area [8], so the city's own Circuit Court Clerk handles marriage licenses and records [5]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Newport News Public Library

  • Printing: 20 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents in color, at the Grissom, Main Street and Pearl Bailey branches [1].
  • Mobile printing: send jobs from your phone or home computer at the same prices and pick them up at any of the three main branches within 24 hours [2].
  • Copies: the Public Law Library has a photocopier [3]. We didn't find a copy price.
  • Fax and scanning: the Grissom, Main Street and Pearl Bailey branches each list a Copy, Scan & Fax Station [10][11][12]. We didn't find fax or scan prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Newport News page lists 1 location, 11712 Jefferson Ave, Ste C, (757) 873-0384, and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [13]. The Census Geocoder places it in Newport News city [9]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Newport News

  • Public Law Library (free): 2501 Washington Avenue, 757-926-8678. Up to 3 documents a visit; the notary is scheduled 8:30–11:30 and 1:30–4:30 Monday–Friday, so call to check availability [3]. The Census Geocoder places it in Newport News city [9].
  • Passport Office: notary service there is only for new passport applications [4].
  • The UPS Store: its Newport News page lists notarizing [13]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [14].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth commissions Virginia notaries. The application or renewal fee is $45, nonrefundable [15]. The new notary then takes the oath before the clerk of the circuit court the commission was sent to, within 60 days [16][15]; the clerk's fee for qualifying a notary is $10 [17].
  • Fees (statewide): a Virginia notary may charge up to $10 for an acknowledgment, oath, affidavit or a true-copy certification, and up to $25 for the same acts on an electronic document. Travel costs can be added only if you agree and the notary goes somewhere other than their usual place of business [6].
  • Copy certification (statewide): unlike some states, Virginia law lists "certifying that a copy of a document is a true copy" among the acts a notary may charge for [6]. Some documents, such as vital records, still have to come from the office that holds them.
  • Electronic notaries (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth also commissions electronic notaries and has a search for current notaries [15].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Newport News: we didn't find a city office that issues birth or death certificates; see the state details below.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): since July 1, 2026, the Virginia Department of Health charges $15 for each certified copy, or for each search when no record is found [21].
  • Who can order (statewide): only immediate family (mother, father, spouse, child, brother, sister and grandparents) with valid ID; a legal guardian with proof of custody can get a birth record [21].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): walk in Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–4 p.m. at 8701 Park Central Drive, Suite 100, Richmond. Drop-off requests for same-day pickup must be in by 2 p.m. Mail goes to VDH, Office of Vital Records, P.O. Box 1000, Richmond, VA 23218-1000. The customer care line is (804) 662-6200 [21].

Marriage licenses (Newport News Circuit Court Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the minimum age to marry is 18 [22]. A license is good for 60 days from the day it's issued [23]. The clerk's statutory fee for issuing it is $10 [17]; the clerk's own fee list (below) may show a different total.
  • Newport News: $30 by cash, check or credit card (2% convenience fee). Both of you must come together. Photo ID isn't required, but card payment needs matching ID [5].
  • How it works: submit the online pre-application to get an appointment. The license is valid for 60 days with no waiting period, and the ceremony must be in Virginia. The clerk doesn't offer officiants or Justice of the Peace services. Certified copies are $2.50 [5].
  • Office: 2500 Washington Ave [5]. The Census Geocoder places it in Newport News city [9].

Recorded documents and court copies (Newport News Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in Virginia, deeds are recorded by the clerk of the circuit court, and the same statewide clerk fees apply. Recording costs $18 for 10 or fewer pages, $32 for 11 to 30 pages and $52 for 31 or more (§ 17.1-275 A.2) [17].
  • Clerk copy fees (statewide): under Va. Code § 17.1-275, circuit court clerks charge $0.50 a page for copies not priced elsewhere in the law (A.8), and $2 to attach the court's seal and clerk's certificate, plus $0.50 for a judge's certificate (A.9) [17] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Newport News: copies of court documents are $0.50 a page, plus $2.00 per document to certify. Get them in person 8 a.m.–4:15 p.m. Monday–Friday, or by mail with a check or money order payable to Newport News Circuit Court and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Land Records is open 8 a.m.–4 p.m., 757-926-8355 [24] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth's Authentication Division. Depending on the country, it issues a Great Seal or an Apostille. It authenticates documents from a Virginia notary, Virginia clerk of court or Virginia deputy state registrar; it doesn't notarize documents [25].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document (not per page). Several documents signed by the same official on the same date for the same country are $10.00 for the first and $5.00 for each additional one [26].
  • How to send (statewide): USPS mail goes to Secretary of the Commonwealth, Authentication Division, 1111 East Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219; FedEx, UPS and DHL go to 400 East Cary Street, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone 804-692-0114 [25][26]. In-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Thursday; appointments for the next week open Fridays at 10 a.m., up to 10 documents per customer or family [26].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Newport News Public Library?

20 cents a page for black and white and 50 cents for color [1].

Is there a free notary in Newport News?

Yes, at the Public Law Library, up to 3 documents a visit [3].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the library?

Yes, applications at Main Street Library are by appointment only [4].

How much is a marriage license in Newport News?

$30, plus a 2% fee if you pay by card [5].

Where do I get a Virginia birth certificate?

From the Virginia Department of Health, $15 per copy (statewide) [21].
