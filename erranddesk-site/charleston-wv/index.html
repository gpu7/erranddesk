Charleston, West Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Charleston, West Virginia, the state capital, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: Kanawha County Public Library's Main Library charges 20 cents a page to print, copy or fax, scans for free and notarizes for free [1][2]. A marriage license from the county clerk is $55 by state law, or $35 with a premarital education certificate [3]. The only passport acceptance facility in the locator's Charleston list is the Main Post Office [4].
Two clerks: Charleston is in Kanawha County [5][6]. In West Virginia the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) issues marriage licenses and records deeds, liens and births, marriages and deaths [7][8]; the circuit clerk keeps circuit court, family court and mental hygiene records, including divorces [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Kanawha County Public Library, Main Library (123 Capitol Street)

  • Printing and copies: 20 cents a page to print from a library PC or make copies, on the 3rd floor. The card minimum for prints is $5.00 [1].
  • Fax: 20 cents a page, and the fax machine needs a $10 hold on a credit or debit card [1].
  • Scanning: free to your own storage device [1].
  • Phone: (304) 343-4646 [1]. The Census Geocoder places the library in Charleston city [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Charleston page lists 1 location, 3501 MacCorkle Ave SE, (304) 720-8777, and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. The Census Geocoder places it in Charleston city [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Charleston

  • Library (free): the Main Library notarizes for free at the 3rd-floor Reference desk when notary staff are available. Come at least an hour before closing, bring valid photo ID and don't sign the document beforehand. If you need witnesses, the library can't promise them [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Charleston page lists notarizing [11]. Notary rules vary by state [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State commissions notaries. The application or recommission fee is $52, waived for state and local government notaries; a commission is good for five years [13][14]. No fee is charged to add e-notarization authorization [13].
  • Fees (statewide): under W. Va. Code §39-4-30, a notary may charge up to $10.00 for each signature notarized, up to $10.00 per 8½ × 11 page for certifying a copy (the notary keeps a copy on file), and up to $10.00 for any other notarial act [15].
  • Copy certification (statewide): if no other office can certify a document, a West Virginia notary can certify a true copy after comparing it with the original and keeping a copy [16][15]. Vital records, court orders and recorded deeds still come from the office that holds them [16].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • State office in Charleston: the state Vital Registration Office is in Charleston, at 350 Capitol Street; details are below (statewide) [19].
  • Kanawha County Clerk: the clerk records births, marriages and deaths [7]. We didn't find a county price list online; state law sets $5 for a county clerk's certified copy [3].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $12 per certified copy from the state Vital Registration Office; the fee includes a nonrefundable search. If the record isn't found, staff search the year given plus the year before and after; more searches are $12 per three-year period [19].
  • In person (statewide): same-day walk-in service at 350 Capitol Street, Room 165, Charleston, WV 25301-3701. Cash, check and money order are accepted; cards at the walk-up counter add a 4% surcharge. Expect a wait of at least 15 minutes, longer from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. [19].
  • By mail or online (statewide): mail the form with a color copy of your ID and a check or money order (no cards by mail). Online orders go through third-party vendors such as VitalChek; the state gets $12 a copy whatever the vendor charges [19].
  • County clerk copies (statewide law): W. Va. Code lets the county clerk charge $5 for a certified copy of a birth certificate, death certificate or marriage license it holds [3]. Check with the county clerk which records it has.
  • Divorce records (statewide): a divorce decree comes from the circuit clerk of the court that issued it, not from Vital Registration [16].

Marriage licenses (Kanawha County Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): every West Virginia marriage needs a license from a county clerk (the clerk of the county commission) [8]. The age of consent is 18; 16- and 17-year-olds need written consent under the statute's conditions [20]. The statutory fee is $55, or $35 if you show a premarital education course completion certificate [3].
  • Kanawha County: the County Clerk's Office is at 409 Virginia Street East, open Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., (304) 357-0130 [7]. We didn't find a separate county marriage page or a county fee list (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Recorded documents (Kanawha County Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in West Virginia, the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) records deeds; the circuit clerk handles court records [3][16]. Recording a deed or trust deed is $30; most other documents are $10; documents over five pages add $1 per extra page. Copies are $1.50 (plus $1 per page over two), and attaching the clerk's seal is $1 (§59-1-10) [3].
  • Kanawha County: the County Clerk records deeds and liens and oversees wills and judgments [7]. The Census Geocoder places 409 Virginia Street East in Charleston city [10].

Court records and divorce decrees (Kanawha County Circuit Clerk)

  • Circuit clerk fees (statewide): W. Va. Code §59-1-11 sets $1 a page for a copy the circuit clerk makes to go out of the office, $135 to file for divorce, separate maintenance or annulment, and $200 for most other civil filings [21] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Kanawha County: the Circuit Clerk is at 111 Court Street, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., (304) 357-0440 [9]. We didn't find a local copy-price list (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State, at the State Capitol Building in Charleston. It issues Apostille Certificates for countries in the Hague Apostille Convention and Authentication Certificates for other countries [16].
  • What it certifies (statewide): only documents attested by a proper West Virginia officer, such as a notary public, county clerk or the State Registrar. Recorded deeds and marriage certificates need the county clerk's certified copy; court orders and divorce decrees need the circuit clerk's [16].
  • Fee and hours (statewide): $10 per certification; if several documents are certified by the same official, the first is $10 and each additional one is $5. In-person service runs 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday–Friday [16].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [22]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Charleston page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.
  • Free events: we found no upcoming free shred event in Charleston as of Nov 3, 2026.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Kanawha County Public Library?

20 cents a page at the Main Library, with a $5.00 card minimum [1].

Is there a free notary in Charleston, WV?

Yes, at the Main Library's 3rd-floor Reference desk, when notary staff are available [2].

How much is a marriage license in Kanawha County?

$55 under state law, or $35 with a premarital education certificate [3].

Where do I apply for a passport in Charleston, WV?

The locator lists the Main Post Office at 1002 Lee Street East [4][10].

Where do I get a West Virginia birth certificate?

From the state Vital Registration Office in Charleston, $12 per copy (statewide) [19].
