Reno, Nevada document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Reno, Nevada, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Reno, Washoe County, Northern Nevada Public Health, the Nevada courts, the State of Nevada, USPS, The UPS Store, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Reno: Reno and Sparks share Washoe County's offices. The Washoe County Clerk issues marriage licenses and takes passport applications [1][2], the Washoe County Recorder records documents and sells marriage certificates [3], and Northern Nevada Public Health sells Washoe County birth and death certificates [4]. Court records come from the Second Judicial District Court [5]. Nevada apostilles come from the Secretary of State [6].
Short version: Washoe County libraries charge 25 cents a page to print or copy and fax free to local and toll-free numbers [7][8]. The Washoe County Clerk takes passport applications on weekdays 9 a.m.–3 p.m., walk-ins accepted, and sells passport photos for $15 [2][9]. Nevada notaries may charge up to $15 for the first signature on an acknowledgment (statewide) [6]. A Washoe County marriage license is $85 [10]. Birth certificates are $25 from Northern Nevada Public Health [4].
Nevada Day (Friday, Oct. 30, 2026): the Washoe County Recorder's office is closed and reopens Monday, Nov. 2 [3].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Washoe County Library System

  • Printing and copying: 25 cents a page, which also covers printing faxes you receive [7].
  • Fax: free public faxing to local and toll-free numbers only, at the Downtown Reno, Incline Village, North Valleys, Northwest Reno, Senior Center, Sierra View, South Valleys, Spanish Springs and Sparks libraries [8].
  • Scanning: at those branches plus Duncan/Traner [8].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 9 locations in Reno. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. We didn't find prices on the store pages.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Reno

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Washoe County Library System pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: its Reno page lists notarizing [11]. We didn't find store notary prices; Nevada's legal maximums are below.
  • Fees (statewide): Nevada law sets the most a notary may charge: $15.00 for taking an acknowledgment, for the first signature of each signer, and $7.50 for each additional signature of each signer; $7.50 for an oath or affirmation; $7.50 for a certified copy; and $15.00 for each signature on a jurat. Fees are payable in advance if demanded [6].
  • Travel fee (statewide): a notary may add a travel fee only if you asked for the travel, the notary explains it's extra and not required by law, and you agree to the hourly rate in advance. The cap is $15 an hour from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. and $30 an hour from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., with a 2-hour minimum allowed [6].
  • Posted fee table (statewide): a notary who charges must post a table of fees in the office, in type no smaller than 1/2 inch, and can't charge without one [6]. Nevada's $15 acknowledgment fee is higher than Montana's and Wyoming's $10 caps.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Northern Nevada Public Health (Washoe County)

  • Office: 1001 E. Ninth Street, Building B, first floor (east side), Reno, (775) 328-2455. The order window is open Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–12:45 p.m. and 2:00–4:00 p.m. [4]. (The state calls this office the Washoe County health district [16].)
  • Fees: $25 for each certified birth certificate and $25 for each death certificate; a search or verification is $10 per name. In person it takes checks, money orders, cash, MasterCard and Visa. Bring original photo ID and proof of need [4].
  • By mail or online: mail takes 3–4 weeks, to NNPH Vital Statistics, 1001 East Ninth Street, Bldg B, Reno, NV 89512. Online orders go only through VitalChek, which adds a fee. Marriage certificates come from the Washoe County Recorder, (775) 328-3660 [4].

Birth and death certificates: Nevada Office of Vital Records (statewide)

  • State office: 4150 Technology Way, Suite 104, Carson City, NV 89706, (775) 684-4242, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., excluding state holidays. It no longer accepts faxed documents [16][17].
  • State price: $25 for each certified birth certificate. A certified death certificate is $25 if the death happened in Carson City or Clark, Douglas, Lyon, Mineral or Washoe County, and $22 for any other Nevada county. A record search is $10 for each name; corrections cost $45.00 [17].
  • Local offices: the state says you can also get records from the Southern Nevada Health District (Clark County) or the Washoe County health district, and that "Pricing may vary" there [16]. The state's only online vendor is VitalChek (vitalchek.com, 877-456-5410) [17].

Marriage licenses: Washoe County Clerk

  • Reno office: 1001 E. 9th Street, Building A, Reno, NV 89512, (775) 784-7287. Open 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., 365 days a year, with holiday hours of 8 a.m.–4 p.m. After 5:30 p.m. on weekdays and on weekends and holidays, use the 9th Street entrance of Building A [1].
  • Fee and rules: $85.00, by cash, credit card, money order or cashier's check. Both parties must be present, and the license is valid for up to one year before the ceremony [10].
  • Incline Village: the clerk says marriage licenses stopped being issued there May 29, 2026 [1].

Recorded documents: Washoe County Recorder

  • Fees: recording $43.00 per document; copies $1.00 a page and certification $4.00; a certified marriage record $15.00 and a plain marriage copy $1.00 [18].
  • Office: 1001 E. Ninth St., Building A, Rooms 140 and 150, Reno, NV 89512. Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific; (775) 328-3660 for marriages and copies [3].
  • Nevada Day: closed Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, reopening Monday, Nov. 2 [3].
  • Statewide law: county recorders charge $1 a page for copies, $4 to certify a document, and $10 plus $5 (for the state's Account for Aid for Victims of Domestic or Sexual Violence) for a certified copy or abstract of a marriage certificate [19].

Court records: Second Judicial District Court (Washoe County)

  • Fees: 50 cents a page plus $3 per document to certify; for example, 5 certified pages cost $5.50. Certified divorce decrees are a flat $6.00 each [5].
  • Where: in person at the Resource Center, 1 S. Sierra Street, Reno, NV 89501 (cash, card with photo ID, cashier's check or money order). The Filing Office is on the 3rd floor, (775) 328-3110, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. By mail: 75 Court Street, Reno, NV 89501; email Records@washoecourts.us [5].
  • Fees (statewide law): the county clerk or clerk of the court charges 50 cents a page for a copy of any record (unless waived), $3.00 for each certificate under seal, $5.00 to examine and certify a copy you prepared, and 50 cents for each year searched [20].

Apostilles (statewide)

Nevada apostilles come from the Secretary of State, not the county. State law sets the fee at $20 to authenticate a notary's signature on a document for use abroad, as an apostille for Hague Convention countries or a certification for others [6]. The Secretary of State's request form lists 401 North Carson Street, Carson City, NV 89701, 775-684-5708, offers regular, expedited, 24-hour, 4-hour, 2-hour and 1-hour service, and says all fees are per apostille or certification and listed on its fee schedule [21]. We couldn't open the Secretary of State's apostille page or fee schedule from our checker (its firewall blocked us), so we didn't verify the expedite fees or the rules for other documents, such as vital records: nvsos.gov/sos/businesses/apostille. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [22]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I fax for free in Reno?

Washoe County libraries, including Downtown Reno, North Valleys, Northwest Reno, Sierra View and South Valleys, offer free fax to local and toll-free numbers [8]. Printing received faxes costs 25 cents a page [7].

Where can I apply for a passport in Reno?

At the Washoe County Clerk, 1001 E. 9th Street, or at the Main, Steamboat and Peavine Station post offices [12][2].

What does a notary cost in Nevada?

At most $15 for the first signature of each signer on an acknowledgment, $7.50 for each additional signature, and $15 for each signature on a jurat (statewide) [6].

Where do I get a marriage license?

From the Washoe County Clerk at 1001 E. 9th Street, open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day of the year; the license is $85 [1][10].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Reno?

From Northern Nevada Public Health, $25 a copy, or from the state office in Carson City, also $25 [4][17].
