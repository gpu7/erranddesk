Thornton, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Which offices serve Thornton: The City of Thornton's Historic Preservation Plan describes the city's planning area as "located largely in Adams County," with a portion in Weld County to the north [17]. This page covers the Anythink Libraries branches in Thornton, the Thornton City Clerk's Office, the Adams County Clerk and Recorder and the Adams County Health Department (vital records). The Adams County Health Department issues birth certificates for any Colorado county [11].

Notary in Thornton

  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [13].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [14]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 871 Thornton Pkwy., Thornton, CO 80229 (Lambertson Lakes Center) says it has a commissioned notary public on staff. It lists notary hours as "Available 7 Days." Phone (303) 457-4877. Its page doesn't post a price [15]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [13].
  • Anythink, the City of Thornton and Adams County: we didn't find a public notary service on the Anythink, Thornton City Clerk or Adams County Clerk and Recorder pages we checked as of Oct 28, 2026.

  • Thornton City Clerk's Office: you don't need to live in Thornton to apply there. New applications on Form DS-11 need an appointment. The office can't offer times that aren't shown on its online calendar. Two separate payments are required. The State Department fee is check or money order only. The City of Thornton's fee can be paid by Visa, Mastercard, check or money order. Passport photos are $10 per person. The page doesn't list the city's fee amount [7].
  • Thornton courtesy services, no appointment needed: the office will review a DS-82 renewal form before you mail it, help assemble your materials, give you a free pre-addressed envelope and take passport photos ($10 each) [7].
  • Adams County Clerk and Recorder: applications are by appointment only. Hours are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 8:15 a.m.–3:30 p.m., and Wednesday 8:45 a.m.–3:30 p.m., at the Adams County Government Center. The county's $35 execution fee is cash or credit/debit card only. Photos are $14, and a retake is $7. The State Department fee must be a check or money order [8].
  • Processing times: Thornton's page lists routine processing at 4–6 weeks and expedited processing at 2–3 weeks, for an extra $60 per application. Mailing time isn't included [7].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Adams County Health Department

  • Any Colorado county: the department "issues birth and death certificates for any Colorado county" [11].
  • Where: Vital Records office, 7190 Colorado Blvd., Suite 170, Commerce City, CO 80022, 720.200.1401. Open Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., closed for lunch 12–1 p.m. [12].
  • In person: by appointment. Cash, check, money order or credit card are accepted. If the record is available, certificates are issued the same day [11].
  • Other ways to order: by email at VitalRecords@adamscountyco.gov or by mail. Online orders through GoCerts add $9, and through VitalChek add $13.95 [11].
  • Fee (statewide): the department's page doesn't list the certificate fee. Mesa County Public Health says the Colorado Office of the State Registrar of Vital Statistics sets the fee schedule statewide: $25 for a first copy and $20 for additional copies in 2026 [16].

Recorded documents: Adams County Clerk and Recorder

  • Recording: $43.00 per document. Death certificates or verifications of death carry no recording fee [10].
  • Copies: regular copies are $0.25 per page. Certified copies are $0.25 per page plus $1.00 per document for certification, and they can't be sent electronically. Certified marriage or civil union license copies are $0.25 per page plus $1.00 per document. A credit card fee of 3% applies [10].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. The Thornton City Clerk's Office accepts money orders for passport payments [7]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

The City of Thornton's most recent listed free Shred-A-Thon was on Saturday, Aug. 1, 2026, at the Thornton Active Adult Center, 11181 Colorado Blvd., with a limit of two boxes [19]. That date has passed. We didn't find an upcoming free shred event as of Oct 28, 2026. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Is faxing free at the library in Thornton?

Yes. Anythink says faxing is free for all Anythink customers [1].

How much does printing cost at Anythink?

10 cents per page, black and white or color. Every customer gets a $5 printing credit each day. Résumés and job applications are free to print [2].

Where can I apply for a passport in Thornton?

The Thornton City Clerk's Office at City Hall, 9500 Civic Center Dr., by appointment. You don't have to live in Thornton to apply there [7][9].

Where can I get a birth certificate near Thornton?

The Adams County Health Department's Vital Records office in Commerce City issues certificates for any Colorado county, by appointment in person or by email, mail or online [11][12].
