Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Prices: black-and-white printing is $.10 a page and color printing is $.50 a page at all branches. Wireless printing from your phone, tablet or laptop is available at all branches, and each branch has at least one flatbed scanner [4][10].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax service on the library's computer, technology, locations or FAQ pages [4][10][3][11].
  • Paula A. Lewis Branch: 2950 SW Rosser Boulevard, 772-871-5470. Hours: Monday 9 am–5:30 pm, Tuesday 9 am–8 pm, Wednesday 9 am–5:30 pm, Thursday 9 am–8 pm, Friday and Saturday 9 am–5 pm [3].
  • Morningside Branch: 2410 SE Morningside Boulevard, 772-337-5632. Hours: Tuesday 9 am–8 pm, Wednesday 9 am–5:30 pm, Thursday 9 am–8 pm, Friday and Saturday 9 am–5 pm [3]. It has public computers and color and black-and-white printing [2]. The Census Geocoder places both branches in Port St. Lucie city [9].

  • St. Lucie County Clerk and Comptroller (countywide): copies of public records up to 14" x 8.5" are $1.00 a page, and larger pages are $5.00. Faxing or emailing court records is $1.00 a page, and non-court records are $0.15 a page [1].

Notary in Port St. Lucie

  • Clerk and Comptroller: we didn't find a notary service on the Clerk's fee, locations, passport FAQ or marriage pages. Its fee schedule lists "Oath, administering, attesting, & sealing" at $3.50 [1].
  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the library's computer, technology, locations, Morningside or FAQ pages [4][10][3][2][11].
  • The UPS Store: its Port St. Lucie pages list notarizing [13][14]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store [15]. Notary rules vary by state [16].
  • How notaries are appointed (statewide law): Florida notaries are appointed by the Governor for 4 years. They must be at least 18 and legal residents of Florida, and can act only while they are within the state [17].
  • Fees (statewide law): a Florida notary may charge no more than $10 per notarial act [18]. For an online notarization, the limit is $25 per act; fees for other services, including a remote online notarization (RON) service provider's fees, aren't covered by that limit [19]. Witnessing a vote-by-mail ballot is free [18].
  • ID (statewide law): a notary may rely on an ID that is current or issued within the past 5 years and has a serial or other ID number, such as a Florida ID card or driver's license or a U.S. passport. The law lists other accepted IDs too [18].
  • Check a notary (statewide): the Florida Department of State has an online Notary Search of commissioned notaries [20]. Its Notary Section is at P.O. Box 6327, Tallahassee, FL 32314-6327 [20]; phone 850.245.6975 [21].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (Florida Department of Health in St. Lucie County)

  • Birth certificate fees: $17 for a birth certificate and $17 for each additional certificate. Rush service is $10, a protective carton folder is $3 and shipping is $1 [5].
  • Folder price (sources differ): the birth certificate page lists a $3 protective carton folder [5]. The office's application form (DH 1960, 04/2016) lists a $5.00 protective sheath, and a $2.00 search fee for each extra year searched when the exact year isn't known [26].
  • Payment: cash, credit/debit card (Visa, Discover, American Express, MasterCard) or money order payable to St. Lucie County Health Department [26].
  • Death certificate fees: $17 for a death certificate and $17 for each additional certificate, $10 rush, $3 folder and $1 shipping, plus $2 a year to search when the exact year isn't known, up to $50 [27]. This office has Florida deaths from 2009 to the present; for earlier deaths, use the state Bureau of Vital Statistics in Jacksonville or VitalChek. Only a family member or legal representative can request the cause of death [27].
  • In person: the main office, 5150 NW Milner Drive, Port St. Lucie, is open Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and closes for lunch from noon to 12:30 p.m. Vital Statistics: 772-873-4932, fax 772-873-8592 [5]. The Census Geocoder places it in Port St. Lucie city [9].
  • Tax Collector: we didn't find an official St. Lucie County Tax Collector page selling birth certificates.
  • Older records: Florida births before 1930 must be requested from the state Bureau of Vital Statistics in Jacksonville [5]. Records of Florida marriages before June 6, 1927 come from the Clerk of Court in the county that issued the license [28].

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State birth certificate fees (statewide): the Florida Bureau of Vital Statistics charges $9 for the first computer-generated birth certification, which includes a non-refundable $9 search fee, and $4 for each additional copy. A photocopy certificate is $14 for the first copy and $4 for each additional copy. Rush service is $10 more, and shipping is $1 [29].
  • Who can get a birth certificate (statewide): births less than 125 years old are confidential. Only the person named (if 18 or older), a parent on the record, a legal guardian, a legal representative or someone with a court order can get one, with a valid photo ID [29].
  • State death certificate fees (statewide): $5 for a one-year search and one certified copy (the $5 search fee isn't refundable), $4 for each additional copy, $2 for each extra year searched (up to $50), $10 for rush service and $1 for shipping. Records before 2009 need the $10 rush fee for same-day walk-in service, when available [30].
  • Who can get a death certificate (statewide): anyone 18 or older can get a certified copy without the cause of death. Records less than 50 years old that show the cause of death are confidential and limited to family and others the department lists [30].
  • State office walk-in (statewide): 1217 N Pearl St., Jacksonville, FL 32202; lobby hours Monday through Friday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Pay by cash, check, money order, Visa or Mastercard; contactless and tap-to-pay aren't accepted [29][30].
  • By mail (statewide): Florida Department of Health, Vital Statistics, P.O. Box 210, Jacksonville, FL 32231-0042. Computer-generated birth certificates normally take 3–5 business days plus shipping. Mark the envelope RUSH and add $10 for priority processing [29].
  • Online (statewide): VitalChek is the only vendor the state contracts with. A birth certificate through VitalChek is $19 for the first copy (including the $9 search fee and $10 rush fee), plus a $7 VitalChek processing fee [29].
  • County health departments (statewide): they issue Florida birth records from 1917 to the present, and the state says fees vary by county [29]. Compare the county fee above.

Marriage licenses (St. Lucie County Clerk and Comptroller)

  • Who issues licenses (statewide law): a county court judge or the clerk of the circuit court [31].
  • Waiting period (statewide law): if you don't give the clerk certificates showing you finished a premarital preparation course, the license takes effect 3 days after you apply. The clerk must make exceptions for people who don't live in Florida and for couples who claim hardship, and a county judge may waive the wait for Florida residents with good cause [32].
  • Course discount (state law has two figures): the course must be at least 4 hours, and section 741.0305 says it cuts your license fee by $32.50 [33]. Section 741.01 says the fee is reduced by $25 for couples with course certificates [31]. Check the county fee below for what you'd actually pay.
  • Validity (statewide law): a Florida marriage license is valid for 60 days after it's issued [34].
  • St. Lucie County fee: $86.00, which includes two certified copies, or $61.00 with a premarital preparation course certificate [1]. Couples who attend counseling get the $25 discount whether or not they live in Florida [35].
  • Where: online, through a 30-minute Zoom video appointment, or in person at the Fort Pierce Main Office or St. Lucie West, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. A license is valid for 60 days in any Florida county [35].
  • Waiting period: 3 days, waived if both applicants attend counseling. There's no waiting period for people who don't live in Florida [35].
  • Ceremonies: $30, at the Main Office or the South County Annex, 250 NW Country Club Dr, (772) 462-6900. A photographer is $50.00 and a keepsake certificate is $10.70. You don't need an appointment unless you want the photographer [35][1].
  • Copies: St. Lucie County marriage licenses can be viewed online in the Official Records. E-certified copies can be bought through Clerk E-Certify, and printed certified copies come from the Research Department [35][36]. An additional certified copy is $9.00, a certified copy of a license from before 1972 is $7.00, and a marriage license search is $6.00 [1].

Recorded documents (St. Lucie County Clerk and Comptroller)

  • State base fees (statewide law): state law lists recording fees of $5.00 for the first page and $4.00 for each additional page, and requires additional service charges on top [37]. The county total below is what you pay.
  • St. Lucie County recording fees: $10.00 for the first page and $8.50 for each additional page, plus $1.00 per name for documents with more than 4 names [1].
  • Taxes: the fee schedule lists documentary stamps of $0.70 per $100 for deeds and $0.35 per $100 for mortgages, and intangible tax of $0.20 per $100 [1].
  • Where: the Recording Department, 201 South Indian River Drive, 4th Floor, Fort Pierce, Monday–Friday 8 A.M.–5 P.M. [38]. The locations page lists recording at the Fort Pierce main office, not the Port St. Lucie annex [8]. Bring a self-addressed, stamped envelope for the return of your documents [38].
  • Online: official records from 1980 on are online. For older records or deeper searches, call the Research Department at 772-462-6930 [38].

Court records (St. Lucie County Clerk and Comptroller)

  • State fee limits (statewide law): Florida law sets clerk charges of $2.00 for certifying a copy of a court record and $1.00 a page for copies up to 14 by 8½ inches [37].
  • St. Lucie County: copies are $1.00 a page, certifying a copy is $2.00 per document, and searching records is $2.00 for each year searched [1].
  • Port St. Lucie annex: it handles county civil, small claims and traffic filings, payments and copies [8].

Office hours (sources differ)

  • The Clerk's locations page gives office hours as Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [8]. Its passport pages say applications are accepted 8 A.M.–4 P.M. [6], and its marriage page says licenses are issued 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [35].

Apostilles and notarial certifications (statewide)

  • What it is (statewide): the Florida Secretary of State is the only authority in Florida that issues apostilles and notarial certifications [39]. Countries that aren't in the 1961 Hague Convention get a notarial certification instead of an apostille [40].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document, or $20.00 per document certified by any Florida county Clerk of the Court ($10 for the apostille and $10 for a certificate of incumbency) [39]. State law caps the apostille fee at $10 per apostille [41].
  • Payment (statewide): a check or money order payable to the Florida Department of State, in U.S. currency from a U.S. bank. Cash and credit cards aren't accepted [39].
  • By mail (statewide): send the request form, the document (an original certified copy, not a photocopy) and a self-addressed stamped envelope or prepaid airbill [39] to Department of State, Division of Corporations, Apostilles Section, P.O. Box 6800, Tallahassee, FL 32314; phone 850.245.6053 [21]. Requests are processed in the order received, and there's no expedited service [40].
  • Walk-in (statewide): 2415 N. Monroe Street, Suite 810, Tallahassee, Monday through Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., except state holidays. Most requests can be completed while you wait [40].
  • Birth, death and marriage records (statewide): birth and death certificates must be signed by the State Registrar, whether issued by the Bureau of Vital Statistics or a county health department. Marriage and divorce certificates must be signed by the State Registrar or the Clerk of the County Courts [42].
  • Elsewhere (statewide): federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State, and documents from another state go to that state [39]. The office doesn't translate, and the translator can't be the notary [40].

Money orders

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Port St. Lucie?

At the Florida Department of Health in St. Lucie County, 5150 NW Milner Drive, for $17, and $17 for each additional copy [5]. The state Bureau of Vital Statistics in Jacksonville charges $9 for a computer-generated birth certification [29].

How much is a marriage license in Port St. Lucie?

$86.00 at the St. Lucie County Clerk and Comptroller, including two certified copies, or $61.00 with a premarital course [1].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the Clerk?

No. The Clerk says appointments aren't needed; applications are accepted Monday–Friday, 8 A.M.–4 P.M. [6].

Can I renew my passport at the St. Lucie County Clerk?

No. Renewals must be submitted by mail [6].

How much does a Florida notary charge?

No more than $10 per notarial act under state law, or $25 for an online notarization [18][19].
