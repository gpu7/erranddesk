Littleton, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Littleton, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Birth certificates are $25 for a first copy at Arapahoe County Public Health [8]. The county charges a flat $43 to record a document [7].

Notary in Littleton

  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • Credit union (example): Colorado Credit Union's Littleton branch, 10026 W. San Juan Way, Littleton, CO 80127, lists "Notary Services (free to CCU members)." Lobby hours are Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. and Sat 9 a.m.–noon. Phone 303.978.2274 [11].
  • The UPS Store (example): its Littleton page lists notarizing among its services, without prices [16]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [12].
  • Library and city: we didn't find a notary service on the Bemis Public Library pages we checked [1][2][3]. The City of Littleton City Clerk's office is at Littleton Center, 2255 W. Berry Avenue, Littleton, CO 80120, 303-795-3780, open Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Its page doesn't mention notary or passport services, so we didn't find either offered by the city [17].

Passports in Littleton

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator lists two acceptance facilities with a Littleton, Colorado address [4]:

  • County appointments: passport appointments are "extremely limited," and in-person service is by appointment only. Book through the county's online scheduler, because "Appointments cannot be made by phone." Appointments are released 21 days ahead and on a daily basis, so the county suggests checking early in the morning [5].
  • County fees and photos: the county's processing fee is "exactly $35 per application," by check or money order payable to "Arapahoe County Clerk." The Littleton office has a self-service photo booth that issues two photos for $14.99 plus tax; the county doesn't recommend it for infants [5].
  • County hours: the passport page's contact box lists 5334 S. Prince St., 303-795-4200, Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–4 p.m. [5]. The county's Locations and Hours page says that since January 1, 2026, all Clerk and Recorder branches open at 8 a.m. and close at 4 p.m. [6]. Check your appointment time.
  • Renewals: the county notes that applicants who meet certain requirements can renew online, and that others may be eligible to renew by mail [5].
  • Post office fees (national): a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [14].
  • Wrong-city results: the locator data also has post offices in Littleton, NH and Littleton, MA, which we left out [4].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Arapahoe County Public Health

  • Fee (statewide): "The statewide fee for vital records certificates is $25 for the first copy, and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time" [8].
  • Any Colorado county: the office provides birth and death certificates "that occurred in any Colorado county" and doesn't charge extra service or credit card fees [8].
  • In person (Centennial or Aurora, not Littleton): appointments at Centennial—Lima Plaza, 6964 S. Lima Street, or Aurora—Altura Plaza. Call 303-734-4300 to schedule [9]. Hours are 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. on weekdays, closed daily 12–1 p.m. In-person payment can be cash, check, money order or credit card, and certificates are "typically issued within 20 minutes" [8]. We didn't find a vital records counter in Littleton.

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Arapahoe County Clerk and Recorder

  • Littleton branch: 5334 S. Prince St., Littleton, CO 80120, (303) 795-4500. Red 24-hour document drop boxes are outside the county's Motor Vehicle offices [6].
  • Recording: a flat $43 per document under Colorado HB 24-1269 (a statewide change, effective July 1, 2025). DD214 recording with 2 certified copies, and death certificate recording and verification of death, are listed as "No Charge" [7].
  • Copies: $0.25 per page non-certified; add $1 per document for a certified copy. Marriage or civil union license: $30 [7].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [15]. The county takes a check or money order for its passport processing fee [5]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office: its annual Shred-A-Thon was September 19, 2026, from 8 a.m. to noon, limited to three clean trash bags or bankers' boxes per vehicle, at 13101 E. Broncos Parkway, Centennial [10]. That date has passed.
  • Colorado Credit Union (example): its Littleton branch says it "hosts a free shred day annually" and to check its events page for dates [11]. We didn't find a 2026 date on the branch page.

We didn't find a City of Littleton shred event on the pages we could read. The UPS Store lists shredding at its Littleton locations, without prices [16]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Littleton?

At the Arapahoe County Clerk and Recorder, 5334 S. Prince St., by appointment booked online [5], or at the Highlands Ranch Post Office, 9609 South University Blvd, which has a Littleton address, by appointment [4].

Is printing free at the library in Littleton?

Black and white printing and copying at Bemis Public Library is free up to 100 pages. Color is $0.25 per side [1].

Can I get a passport photo in Littleton?

The county's Littleton office has a self-service booth: two photos for $14.99 plus tax [5].

Where can I get a birth certificate near Littleton?

At Arapahoe County Public Health in Centennial (6964 S. Lima Street) or Aurora, by appointment at 303-734-4300. The first copy is $25 [8][9].
