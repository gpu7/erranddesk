Elizabeth, New Jersey document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Elizabeth, New Jersey, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Elizabeth, Union County, the Elizabeth Public Library, the New Jersey Department of Health, the New Jersey Department of the Treasury, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport offices in Elizabeth, the Union County Clerk and the main post office [1]. The city's Office of Vital Statistics charges $15 per certified copy, with same-day walk-in service [2]. The Main Library offers free basic notary service [3].
Who does what: Elizabeth is in Union County [4][5], and the county seat is here. The City of Elizabeth Office of Vital Statistics issues birth, death and marriage records [2]. The state Office of Vital Statistics and Registry in Trenton also issues them [6]. Deeds are recorded by the Union County Clerk.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Elizabeth Public Library: printing is 25¢ a page for black and white and 50¢ for color, and scanning is available through staff [3].
  • Fax: the library has no fax machines [3].
  • The UPS Store: its Elizabeth page lists 2 locations, at 162 Elmora Ave. and 801 Newark Ave. [7]. The Census Geocoder places both inside Elizabeth [8]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Elizabeth

  • Elizabeth Public Library (Main Library): free basic notary service, first come, first served, Monday–Friday 10 a.m.–4 p.m. Call 908-354-6060 first to confirm a notary is in. The limit is 3 documents per person per visit. It won't notarize wills, trusts, deeds, real estate documents, Form I-9s or birth, death and marriage certificates [3].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [9].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Jersey's Division of Revenue says a notary may charge $2.50 each for an oath or affirmation, a jurat, a proof of a deed or an acknowledgment [10]. The state Notary Public Manual covers the real-estate closing fees [11].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Elizabeth Office of Vital Statistics

  • Walk-in: 50 Winfield Scott Plaza, Room G12, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Same-day service for non-genealogical orders takes about 15 minutes. Pay by cash or money order [2].
  • Fee: $15 per certified copy. Mail requests usually take 1–2 weeks and are paid by check or money order [2].
  • Marriage licenses: the city requires an appointment to apply [16]. Statewide rule: apply in the municipality where either of you lives; the fee is $28 and there's a 72-hour wait [17].

Birth, death and marriage records from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the New Jersey Office of Vital Statistics and Registry charges $25 for the search and one certified copy (or a certification, or a no-record statement), and $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. VitalChek adds a processing fee of $6 by mail or $12.95 by phone [18].
  • Certified copy or certification: certified copies have a raised seal and are legal documents. Certifications are on plain paper, can't be used for identity or legal purposes, and are mainly useful for genealogy [19].
  • Walk-in (statewide, in Trenton): the walk-in center at 140 E. Front Street, Trenton offers same-day service. It has not taken cash since June 18, 2026 [6]. Online orders go through VitalChek [6][20].
  • Local copies: municipal local registrars also issue copies, and their fees vary by municipality [18].

Deeds and land records (Union County)

  • Union County Clerk: we didn't find a certified deed copy fee on the clerk's fee page (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [21].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (Department of the Treasury), for documents signed by a New Jersey notary or selected public officials such as county clerks and the State Registrar. It gives an apostille for Hague Convention countries and a certification for other countries [22].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 per certificate, or $5 per certificate for documents for a child's adoption. The fee table also lists 2-hour and 1-hour expedite fees for walk-in requests, but we couldn't match those amounts to their rows (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [23].
  • Vital records: order a certified copy from the state Office of Vital Statistics, and say on the application that it needs an apostille [19].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Elizabeth?

At the Union County Clerk, 2 Broad St., Room 114, by appointment, or at the Elizabeth Main Post Office, 310 N. Broad St. [1][12].

How much is an Elizabeth birth certificate?

$15 per certified copy from the city's Office of Vital Statistics [2].

Is there a free notary in Elizabeth?

Yes. The Main Library offers free basic notary service Monday–Friday 10 a.m.–4 p.m., up to 3 documents [3].
