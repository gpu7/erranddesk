Chesapeake, Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Chesapeake, Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Chesapeake, the Chesapeake Circuit Court Clerk, Chesapeake Public Library, the Virginia Secretary of the Commonwealth, the Virginia Department of Health, the Commonwealth of Virginia, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Chesapeake Public Library prints and copies for 20 cents a page in black and white, sends faxes for free and offers free notary service by appointment [1][2]. A marriage license from the Circuit Court Clerk is $30, by appointment [3]. The City Clerk takes passport applications by appointment, with a $35 acceptance fee [4]. Virginia notaries can charge up to $10 per act (statewide) [5].
An independent city, no county: Chesapeake isn't part of any county. The Census Bureau lists "Chesapeake city" [6] as its own county-level area [7], so the city's own Circuit Court Clerk handles marriage licenses, deeds and notary oaths [8]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Chesapeake Public Library

  • Printing and copies: printers and copiers are at all seven locations. Black and white is 20 cents a page and color 35 cents; pay by cash or debit/credit card [1].
  • Scanning: available at all seven locations; bring a flash drive [1].
  • Fax: sending is free. You can't send international faxes or receive faxes at the library [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Chesapeake page lists 5 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [10]. The Census Geocoder places all 5 in Chesapeake city [9]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Chesapeake

  • Library (free): notary service is free; bring a government-issued photo ID and book an appointment online. Library staff can't act as witnesses [1].
  • Library limits: up to three simple documents per person per day; no real estate transactions; the library provides no witnesses. Library notaries can certify true copies of documents, except vital records and court-issued reports [11].
  • Becoming a notary: you take the oath at the Circuit Court Clerk's Office, with no appointment, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. The clerk's page says renewals in good standing no longer need the courthouse oath (since July 1, 2024) and that a commission lasts four years [12].
  • The UPS Store: its Chesapeake page lists notarizing [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [13].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth commissions Virginia notaries. The application or renewal fee is $45, nonrefundable [14]. The new notary then takes the oath before the clerk of the circuit court the commission was sent to, within 60 days [15][14]; the clerk's fee for qualifying a notary is $10 [16].
  • Fees (statewide): a Virginia notary may charge up to $10 for an acknowledgment, oath, affidavit or a true-copy certification, and up to $25 for the same acts on an electronic document. Travel costs can be added only if you agree and the notary goes somewhere other than their usual place of business [5].
  • Copy certification (statewide): unlike some states, Virginia law lists "certifying that a copy of a document is a true copy" among the acts a notary may charge for [5]. Some documents, such as vital records, still have to come from the office that holds them.
  • Electronic notaries (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth also commissions electronic notaries and has a search for current notaries [14].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Local office: we didn't find a Chesapeake city office that issues birth or death certificates on the pages we checked (last checked Nov 3, 2026). The state office is below.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): since July 1, 2026, the Virginia Department of Health charges $15 for each certified copy, or for each search when no record is found [20].
  • Who can order (statewide): only immediate family (mother, father, spouse, child, brother, sister and grandparents) with valid ID; a legal guardian with proof of custody can get a birth record [20].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): walk in Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–4 p.m. at 8701 Park Central Drive, Suite 100, Richmond. Drop-off requests for same-day pickup must be in by 2 p.m. Mail goes to VDH, Office of Vital Records, P.O. Box 1000, Richmond, VA 23218-1000. The customer care line is (804) 662-6200 [20].

Marriage licenses (Chesapeake Circuit Court Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the minimum age to marry is 18 [21]. A license is good for 60 days from the day it's issued [22]. The clerk's statutory fee for issuing it is $10 [16]; the clerk's own fee list (below) may show a different total.
  • Chesapeake: apply online and schedule an appointment before you come in [3]. The license is $30; the statute's clerk fee is $10 [16], so expect to pay the clerk's $30.
  • Payment (the page differs): the same page says "$30 cash or credit card" in one place and "$30 cash only" in another [3]. Bring cash to be safe.
  • Ceremonies and copies: deputy clerks don't perform ceremonies; the page lists marriage commissioners. Certified copies are $2.50 after the license is returned [3].

Recorded documents and court copies (Chesapeake Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in Virginia, deeds are recorded by the clerk of the circuit court, and the same statewide clerk fees apply. Recording costs $18 for 10 or fewer pages, $32 for 11 to 30 pages and $52 for 31 or more (§ 17.1-275 A.2) [16].
  • Clerk copy fees (statewide): under Va. Code § 17.1-275, circuit court clerks charge $0.50 a page for copies not priced elsewhere in the law (A.8), and $2 to attach the court's seal and clerk's certificate, plus $0.50 for a judge's certificate (A.9) [16] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Chesapeake copies: 50 cents a page and $2 for certification, at 307 Albemarle Drive, Suite 300A (third floor). Remote land-records access is a $600 yearly subscription [23]. Court order copies are 50 cents a page plus a $2 seal and certification fee [8].
  • Property Notification: a free email alert when documents are recorded under your name [8].
  • Hours (sources differ): the clerk's main page lists office hours of 8:30 a.m.–3:45 p.m. (757-382-3000) [8]; the notary page says 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. for oaths [12]. The Census Geocoder places the office in Chesapeake city [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth's Authentication Division. Depending on the country, it issues a Great Seal or an Apostille. It authenticates documents from a Virginia notary, Virginia clerk of court or Virginia deputy state registrar; it doesn't notarize documents [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document (not per page). Several documents signed by the same official on the same date for the same country are $10.00 for the first and $5.00 for each additional one [25].
  • How to send (statewide): USPS mail goes to Secretary of the Commonwealth, Authentication Division, 1111 East Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219; FedEx, UPS and DHL go to 400 East Cary Street, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone 804-692-0114 [24][25]. In-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Thursday; appointments for the next week open Fridays at 10 a.m., up to 10 documents per customer or family [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Chesapeake Recycles Day (upcoming): Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, 9 a.m.–noon, at Tidewater Community College's Chesapeake Campus, 1428 Cedar Road. Shred-It is one of the partners, and sensitive documents are among the accepted items. It's free and open to Chesapeake residents [27][28].
  • Past event: the Oct. 3, 2026 Recycles Day at Greenbrier Sentara College of Health Sciences has passed [28] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Chesapeake page lists shredding [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at the Chesapeake library?

20 cents a page for black and white and 35 cents for color [1].

Can I fax at the Chesapeake library?

Yes, sending is free, but not internationally [2].

How much is a marriage license in Chesapeake?

$30 at the Circuit Court Clerk, by appointment [3].

Where do I apply for a passport in Chesapeake?

At the City Clerk's Office by appointment (757-382-6110) or the Chesapeake Post Office [4][17].

Where do I get a Virginia birth certificate?

From the Virginia Department of Health, $15 per copy (statewide) [20].
