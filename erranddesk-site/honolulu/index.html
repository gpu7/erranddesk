Honolulu, Hawaii document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Honolulu (Urban Honolulu CDP), Hawaii, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City and County of Honolulu, Hawaii Department of Health, Bureau of Conveyances, the Lieutenant Governor, the Attorney General, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Counties and recording in Hawaii (statewide): Vital records and marriage licenses are issued by the state Department of Health (and licensed marriage agents), not by city clerks. Land records are recorded at the state Bureau of Conveyances in Honolulu under a statewide fee schedule — Hawaii does not use county recorders for deeds [1][2][3].
Short version: the locator data lists 4 passport acceptance facilities with a Honolulu address [4]. Vital copies $10/$4 + $2.50 portal; marriage license $65 [5][6]. BOC recording Regular $41 (≤50 pages) [2]. HSPL print donation 15¢/page [7]. FedEx Office print from $0.24 B&W / $0.76 color [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Hawaii State Public Library System: a print donation of 15¢ per page is appreciated [7]. Photocopies: an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page; color and special paper may cost more — fees are displayed at each library [9].
  • FedEx Office: office-2801 (3109 Waialae Ave), office-2802 (1500 Kapiolani Blvd) and office-2803 (590 Queen St) publish print starting at $0.24 B&W / $0.76 color; 18×24 $2.70 B&W / $17.97 color; passport photos $15.95 for two; fax send local $2.19 / domestic $2.49; fax receive $1.59; shred $1.49/lb [8][10][11]. City listing: [12].
  • The UPS Store: 8 Honolulu locations; store pages advertise printing, faxing, scanning, shredding and notary [13].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Honolulu, Hawaii

  • City Clerk: Office of the City Clerk, 530 S King St Rm 100, (808) 768-3810, clerks@honolulu.gov — council/elections support. We didn't find a published public notary service or customer fee (the /clerk/notary/ path returned 404 "Page not found"; last checked Nov 5, 2026) [14].
  • The UPS Store: Honolulu locations advertise notary; fees vary and are subject to statewide caps [13][15].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Hawaii commissions notaries through the Attorney General. Application $20, examination $10, commission $100; renewal $120; four-year term; $1,000 surety bond [16][17]. Under HRS §456-17, notaries may charge at most $5 for an acknowledgment (per party signing) or for administration of an oath; remote online notarial acts are capped at $25 [18][19]. A notary may charge less [19].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): certified birth and marriage certificates are $10 for the first copy and $4 for each additional copy of the same record, plus a $2.50 portal/administration fee per increment of up to five copies [5]. Death certificates use the same $10 / $4 copy fees plus the $2.50 fee [23].
    • Oahu Vital Records office: 201 Merchant St., Suite 100, City Financial Tower, Honolulu. Phone (808) 586-4539 (certificates). Hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (issuance/marriage); closed state holidays [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $3.00 per document [24].

    Marriage licenses

    • State rules (statewide): a marriage or civil union license costs $65.00 ($60 application fee + $5 portal fee), non-refundable. Apply online at emrs.ehawaii.gov, then both applicants meet a licensed agent together in person no more than 30 days before the ceremony [6][25].
    • Where in Honolulu: State Department of Health Main Office marriage agents, City Financial Tower area / listed Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m., (808) 586-4544 — complete the online application first [6][25].

    Land records (Bureau of Conveyances)

    • Bureau of Conveyances office: Kalanimoku Building, 1151 Punchbowl Street, Honolulu. Office hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recordation windows about 8:01 a.m.–3:29 p.m. (confirm before you go) [2][26].
    • Recording fees (statewide): Bureau of Conveyances Regular System: $41 per document up to 50 pages, or $106 for 51 pages or more. Land Court: $36 (≤50 pages) or $101 (51+); Certificate of Title issuance $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [24].
    • Fee (statewide): $3.00 per document (apostille or certification) [24].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Shredding

    • FedEx Office: Honolulu Office pages list shredding at $1.49/lb [8].
    • The UPS Store: Honolulu store pages advertise shredding [13].
    • City and County of Honolulu ENV: we didn't find a city-run household paper-shredding event on upcoming-events (last checked Nov 5, 2026). ENV lists commercial collectors Access (808) 673-3200, Shred It (808) 841-6605, and Shredex (808) 848-7776 — not city-run events [28]. An HHW date (Nov 7, 2026) is hazardous waste, not paper shredding.

    For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

    Where can I get a notary in Honolulu, Hawaii?

    The UPS Store lists eight Honolulu locations that advertise notary [13]. We didn't find a published City Clerk notary fee [14]. Statewide, acknowledgments are capped at $5 per party under HRS §456-17 [18].

    How much is library printing in Honolulu?

    HSPL asks for a 15¢-per-page print donation; photocopies are an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page (color/special may cost more; fees displayed at each library) [7][9].

    Where do I record a deed in Hawaii?

    Bureau of Conveyances, 1151 Punchbowl Street, Honolulu. Regular System $41 up to 50 pages [2].
