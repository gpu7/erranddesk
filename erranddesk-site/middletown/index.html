Middletown, Delaware document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Middletown, Delaware, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the Town of Middletown, New Castle County, the Delaware Courts, the Delaware Solid Waste Authority, the Delaware Secretary of State, the Delaware Division of Public Health, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the Middletown Post Office on East Main Street accepts passport applications [1][2]. The UPS Store at 364 E Main St lists printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [3]. Delaware notaries may charge at most $5 per paper notarization [4]. Both 2026 DSWA shredding events in Middletown have passed [5].
Who does what: Middletown is a town in New Castle County [6][7]. In Delaware, each county's Clerk of the Peace (the Marriage Bureau) issues marriage licenses [8]. New Castle County's Superior Court Prothonotary office is in Wilmington, at the Leonard L. Williams Justice Center [9][2]. Birth and death certificates come from the state Office of Vital Statistics [10].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • The UPS Store: its Middletown page lists 1 location, The UPS Store Middletown, 364 E Main St, (302) 378-7778, offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [3]. The Census Geocoder places it inside Middletown town [2]. Prices aren't listed online.
  • Appoquinimink Community Library: 204 E. Main St., (302) 378-5588. Hours as of Sept 8: Monday 10 a.m.–9 p.m., Thursday closed, Friday 10 a.m.–1 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–5 p.m., Sunday 1–5 p.m.; check the library page for Tuesday and Wednesday [11]. We didn't find its print, copy, scan, fax or notary prices (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Delaware's public libraries each post their own prices, so prices from other Delaware cities don't carry over.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Middletown

  • The UPS Store: its Middletown page lists notarizing [3]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [12].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Delaware law caps a notary's fee at $5 per notarial act on a paper record and $25 per act on an electronic record, and a notary may waive the fee [4][13]. The Secretary of State's notary FAQ says Delaware law does not address travel fees [13].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Office of Vital Statistics charges $25 per certified birth certificate and $25 per certified death certificate; if the record isn't found, the fee is kept for the search [16][17]. Online orders through GoCertificates or VitalChek add those companies' own fees [10].
  • In person (statewide): three offices, open weekdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m. except holidays: New Castle County at 258 Chapman Road, Newark, 302-283-7130; Kent County (the central office) in Dover; and Sussex County at 546 S. Bedford St., Georgetown [10].
  • The sources conflict on two details: the office web page lists the Kent County central office at 805 River Road, Dover (James Williams State Service Center), 302-744-4549, and Georgetown at 302-515-3190 [10]; the state's application forms list the Dover office at the Jesse S. Cooper Building, 417 Federal Street, and Georgetown at 302-856-5495 [16]. Mail requests go to the Dover address printed on the form [10]. Call before you go.

Marriage licenses (New Castle County Clerk of the Peace)

  • The law (statewide): the Clerk of the Peace in each county issues marriage licenses. You need the license at least 24 hours before the ceremony, and it's good for 30 days [8].
  • Fee, statute vs county: the Delaware Code says clerks of the peace issue licenses for $10 each [8]. New Castle County's own fee schedule lists more, shown side by side (last checked Nov 4, 2026):
    ItemDelaware Code (statewide)New Castle County fee schedule
    Marriage license$10 [8]$70 if either of you is a Delaware resident, $120 if neither is; plus a $5 processing fee and a $20 background form [18]
    ExtensionsNot listed$25 for 30 days, $50 for 60 days, $75 for 90 days [18]
    Certified copy of a licenseNot listed$25 plus $5 processing [18]
  • How to apply: start online, then book an in-person QLess appointment. Both of you must come together, and the license can't be issued solely online. It's valid for 30 days, the ceremony must be in Delaware, and there's a 24-hour wait [19].
  • Office: New Castle County Clerk of the Peace, 800 N. French St., 2nd Floor, Wilmington 19801 (in Wilmington); 302-395-7780; Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m., closed noon–1 p.m. [20]. We didn't find how the license fee is paid at the office (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Certified copies by mail are paid by money order or certified check only [21].

Court records and land records

  • Superior Court copies (statewide fee schedule): a certified copy is $15 for up to 3 pages and an exemplified copy $25; extra copies are at most $2 a page, and fax copies at most $8 a page [22].
  • New Castle County Prothonotary (in Wilmington, not Middletown): Suite 500, Lower Level 1, Leonard L. Williams Justice Center, 500 North King Street, Wilmington, open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; main number 302-255-0800 [9][2].
  • New Castle County deeds (fee schedule revised 9/28/26): copies you print yourself are 50¢ a page, staff-printed copies $1 a page, and copies by mail, fax or email $2 a page. Certified copies are $3 a page if you supply the copies or $6 a page if the office makes them, plus a $1 certificate. A plat copy is $6 ($7 by mail), and a notary is $3 [23]. Office: 800 N. French St., 4th Floor, Wilmington; 302-395-7700; Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (windows close 3:45), Friday 8 a.m.–1 p.m. (windows close 12:45) [24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Delaware Division of Corporations (Secretary of State), John G. Townsend Building, 401 Federal Street, Suite 4, Dover, for documents signed by a Delaware notary or public official. Mail requests are typically processed the same business day; in-person drop-off is by appointment, 302-739-3077 [25].
  • Fee (statewide): for personal use, $30 for any number of documents presented together, with no expedite fees; commercial documents are $30 each, plus $50 same-day or $40 24-hour [25][26].
  • Not accepted: documents notarized remotely or electronically. The Division doesn't notarize [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Delaware Solid Waste Authority (statewide program): residents can bring up to two boxes of paper for free shredding [28].
  • Middletown events (past): DSWA's 2026 schedule listed Saturday collection events with document shredding at St. Anne's School, 211 Silver Lake Road, Middletown, on April 25 and September 12, 8 a.m.–2 p.m. Both have passed. The Census Geocoder places the school inside Middletown town [5][2].
  • Next listed event: Saturday, Nov 14, 2026, 8 a.m.–2 p.m., at the Newark Recycling Center, 470 Corporate Blvd, outside Middletown. DSWA says events can change [5].
  • The UPS Store: its Middletown page lists shredding [3].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Where can I get something notarized in Middletown?

The UPS Store at 364 E Main St lists notarizing [3]. Delaware caps paper notarization fees at $5 [4].

How much is a Delaware birth certificate?

$25 per copy from the Office of Vital Statistics [16].
