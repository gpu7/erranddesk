Erie, Pennsylvania document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Erie, Pennsylvania, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: Erie County's marriage license is $65 total, including one certified copy, under the county's current page [1]. Deed and civil court copies are 25 cents a page, plus $1 to certify [2][3]. The locator's two Erie passport post offices weren't confirmed inside the city limits [4][5].
Who does what: Erie is in Erie County [6][7]. The county Clerk of Records runs the Marriage Bureau, the Recorder of Deeds and the Prothonotary, all at the Erie County Courthouse, 140 West Sixth Street [1][8][3].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Erie County Public Library: its website returned a security-certificate error, and we didn't bypass it, so we didn't find its print or fax prices (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Erie page lists 3 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [9]. The Census Geocoder places 1 of them, 3411 Peach St, in Erie city [5]. Prices aren't listed online.

Notary in Erie

  • The UPS Store: its Erie page lists notarizing [9].
  • County offices: the Recorder of Deeds and Prothonotary fee schedules list notary appointment and certificate fees for notaries themselves, not notarizing for the public [2][3].
  • Notary fees (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State sets notary fees by regulation, and a notary can't charge more. The maximums are $5 per acknowledgment (plus $2 for each additional name on the same certificate), $5 per oath or affirmation, $5 per verification, $5 per signature witnessed, $5 per certified copy and $3 a page to note a protest. Electronic and remote notaries may add up to $20 per act [10].
  • Extra charges (statewide): a notary may waive the fee. Clerical or administrative fees, such as copying, postage or travel, aren't set by the Department; they must be reasonable, disclosed before the notarization and itemized on the receipt [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $20 per birth certificate and $20 per death certificate from the Pennsylvania Department of Health; online orders through VitalChek, the state's only authorized online vendor, add a $10 fee [13][14].
  • Who can order (statewide): birth certificate applicants must be at least 16 for in-person and mail requests; death certificate applicants must be at least 18 [13][14].
  • In person (statewide): the Department of Health has six Vital Records public offices: Erie, Harrisburg, New Castle, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Scranton. A request received before 2:30 p.m. may be filled the same day [15].

Marriage licenses (Marriage Bureau)

  • Fee, sources differ: the county's Marriage Licenses page says "Effective March 1, 2025, the fee is $65.00 total": $60 for the license and $5 for a certified copy. It accepts cash or credit card, with a $2 card fee [1]. The same page still says "$60 fee, cash only" in its checklist, and the linked fee schedule (marked effective March 1, 2019) lists $60, cash only [1][16].
  • How: call 814-451-6347 for an appointment; both applicants appear in person at the Erie County Courthouse, 140 West Sixth Street, Room 123. Pennsylvania has a three-day waiting period [1]; the fee schedule lists a $20 waiver of the 3-day wait [16].
  • Copies: in person or by mail [1]. The fee schedule lists $5 for a certified marriage record [16].

Deeds (Recorder of Deeds)

  • Copies: $0.25 a page by pickup, email or fax, plus $1.00 per document to certify (effective Dec 1, 2025) [2]. Room 121, 814-451-6246 [8].

Court records and divorce decrees (Prothonotary)

  • Fees (effective Jan 1, 2026): copies $0.25 a page; certified copies $1.00 plus $0.25 a page; gold-seal certified copies $5.50 plus $0.25 a page; a divorce, good-standing or notary certificate $6.00; genealogy or name-change searches $12.00; exemplification $12.00 [3].
  • Office: Erie County Courthouse, 140 West Sixth Street, Room 120, 814-451-6250 [17]. The page doesn't list hours; the county's contact page lists Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [18] (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State, North Office Building, Room 201, 401 North Street, Harrisburg. The fee is $15 per document (not per page). Walk-in service no longer takes cash; checks, money orders and credit cards are accepted. The building's front doors lock after 3 p.m. Mail requests typically take two to three weeks, not counting mailing time [19].
  • County documents (statewide): marriage certificates must be signed by the register of wills or clerk of the orphans' court, not only the officiant [19].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is a marriage license in Erie County?

$65 total under the county's current page, including one certified copy; an older fee schedule says $60 [1][16].

How much is a deed copy in Erie County?

$0.25 a page, plus $1 per document to certify [2].

Where do I apply for a passport in Erie?

The locator lists the Presque Isle Branch and Erie GMF post offices; neither is confirmed inside the city limits [4][5].
