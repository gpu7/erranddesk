Allentown, Pennsylvania document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Allentown, Pennsylvania, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: Allentown Public Library prints for 25 cents a black-and-white page and faxes for $1 a page [1][2]. Lehigh County's marriage license application is $100, including two certified copies [3]. The Clerk of Judicial Records at the courthouse accepts passports [4]. The city runs a paper-shredding drop-off [5].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Allentown Public Library (1210 Hamilton St.)

  • Printing: $0.25 black-and-white, $0.50 color. The vending device takes $1 and $5 bills, coins other than pennies, cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay [1].
  • Fax: outgoing only, $1.00 a page; incoming fax isn't available [2].
  • Hours: Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Allentown page lists 4 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [10], but the Census Geocoder places none of the 4 inside Allentown city; they have Allentown mailing addresses in Lehigh County [11]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Allentown

  • Clerk of Judicial Records: the passport locator notes "notary services available" at its courthouse office [4]. Its Civil Division fee schedule lists a "Notarizing Fee" of $7.00 under Notary Public [9]. That is more than the statewide $5 cap below; the schedule doesn't say what the fee covers, so ask before you go.
  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Allentown Public Library pages we checked.
  • Notary fees (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State sets notary fees by regulation, and a notary can't charge more. The maximums are $5 per acknowledgment (plus $2 for each additional name on the same certificate), $5 per oath or affirmation, $5 per verification, $5 per signature witnessed, $5 per certified copy and $3 a page to note a protest. Electronic and remote notaries may add up to $20 per act [12].
  • Extra charges (statewide): a notary may waive the fee. Clerical or administrative fees, such as copying, postage or travel, aren't set by the Department; they must be reasonable, disclosed before the notarization and itemized on the receipt [12].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $20 per birth certificate and $20 per death certificate from the Pennsylvania Department of Health; online orders through VitalChek, the state's only authorized online vendor, add a $10 fee [15][16].
  • Who can order (statewide): birth certificate applicants must be at least 16 for in-person and mail requests; death certificate applicants must be at least 18 [15][16].
  • In person (statewide): the Department of Health has six Vital Records public offices: Erie, Harrisburg, New Castle, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Scranton. A request received before 2:30 p.m. may be filled the same day [17].

Marriage licenses (Clerk of the Orphans' Court)

  • Fee: $100, including two certified copies, due at the in-person appointment. Cards are preferred and cash is allowed. Extra certified copies are $10 each when ordered with the application [3].
  • How: email the application and documents to schedule an in-person interview, 9 a.m.–3:30 p.m., Monday–Friday. A license is valid for 60 days [3].
  • Copies later: $10 per certified copy, with a 2.9% card fee; Room 123 of the Lehigh County Courthouse, 455 W. Hamilton Street [3][8].

Court records (Clerk of Judicial Records, Civil Division)

  • Fees (Jan 1, 2026–Dec 31, 2028): photocopies $0.50 a page, docket printout $3.50, general certification $7.00 [9].

Deeds (Recorder of Deeds)

  • We didn't find deed copy fees we could verify: Lehigh County's website didn't load for our checker (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Call the courthouse at (610) 782-3000 [8].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State, North Office Building, Room 201, 401 North Street, Harrisburg. The fee is $15 per document (not per page). Walk-in service no longer takes cash; checks, money orders and credit cards are accepted. The building's front doors lock after 3 p.m. Mail requests typically take two to three weeks, not counting mailing time [18].
  • County documents (statewide): marriage certificates must be signed by the register of wills or clerk of the orphans' court, not only the officiant [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • City drop-off: documents go into a locked container at the Recycling Drop-Off Center, 1400 Martin Luther King Jr Drive; an attendant unlocks it Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–3 p.m. and Saturday 9–10:30 a.m. [5]. The page doesn't list a fee.
  • Witnessed shredding: for Allentown residents and businesses, by appointment, Wednesdays 9–11 a.m. (April–November) at 1401 Oxford Dr., up to 8 banker's boxes [5].
  • The UPS Store: its Allentown page lists shredding [10].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Allentown Public Library?

$0.25 black-and-white, $0.50 color [1].

Can I fax at the Allentown library?

Yes, outgoing only, $1.00 a page [2].

How much is a marriage license in Lehigh County?

$100, including two certified copies [3].

Where do I apply for a passport in Allentown?

The Clerk of Judicial Records at 455 W Hamilton St or the Allentown Post Office at 442 Hamilton St [4].
