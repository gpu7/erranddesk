Dover, Delaware document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Dover, Delaware's capital, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Dover, Kent County Levy Court, the Delaware Courts, the Dover Public Library, the Delaware Solid Waste Authority, the Delaware Secretary of State, the Delaware Division of Public Health, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the Dover Public Library prints black-and-white pages for 25¢ and accepts passport applications, but has no public fax or notary [1][2][3]. A Kent County marriage license is $70 for Delaware residents, cash only [4]. Deed copies printed at the Recorder of Deeds office are $1 a page [5].
Who does what: Dover is in Kent County [6][7]. Kent County's Clerk of the Peace issues marriage licenses [4], and its Recorder of Deeds keeps land records [8]. The Superior Court's Prothonotary office is in the Kent County Courthouse [9]. Birth and death certificates come from the state Office of Vital Statistics [10].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Dover Public Library, 35 Loockerman Plaza (inside Dover city): since September 8, 2026, black-and-white prints and copies are 25¢ a page (up from 15¢) [2][11]. Color is 50¢ a page [1]. The library's FAQ still shows the old 15¢ price next to its notice of the increase [1]. You need a library card to print from the public computers but not from your own device. The print terminals take $1 or $5 bills or coins; card payments at the desk must be $5 or more [1]. Phone: 302-736-7030 [2].
  • Fax: the Dover Public Library says it has no public fax machine [1].
  • Scanning: we didn't find scanning details on the Dover Public Library pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Not one statewide price: Delaware's public libraries each post their own print and fax prices, so these are Dover Public Library prices only.
  • The UPS Store: its Dover page lists 1 location, at 73 Greentree Dr (inside Dover city), offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [12][11]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Dover

  • The UPS Store: its Dover page lists notarizing [12]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [13].
  • Library notary: the Dover Public Library says it doesn't offer notary services [1].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Delaware law caps a notary's fee at $5 per notarial act on a paper record and $25 per act on an electronic record, and a notary may waive the fee [14][15]. The Secretary of State's notary FAQ says Delaware law does not address travel fees [15].

Passports in Dover

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 2 facilities with a Dover address. The Census Geocoder places the Dover Public Library inside the city. Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Office of Vital Statistics charges $25 per certified birth certificate and $25 per certified death certificate; if the record isn't found, the fee is kept for the search [20][21]. Online orders through GoCertificates or VitalChek add those companies' own fees [10].
  • In person (statewide): three offices, open weekdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m. except holidays: New Castle County at 258 Chapman Road, Newark, 302-283-7130; Kent County (the central office) in Dover; and Sussex County at 546 S. Bedford St., Georgetown [10].
  • The sources conflict on two details: the office web page lists the Kent County central office at 805 River Road, Dover (James Williams State Service Center), 302-744-4549, and Georgetown at 302-515-3190 [10]; the state's application forms list the Dover office at the Jesse S. Cooper Building, 417 Federal Street, and Georgetown at 302-856-5495 [20]. Mail requests go to the Dover address printed on the form [10]. Call before you go.

Marriage licenses (Kent County Clerk of the Peace)

  • Fee: $70 total for Delaware residents and $120 for non-residents, cash only [4].
  • Side by side: the Delaware Code says clerks of the peace issue licenses for $10 each [22], while Kent County's page lists the $70 or $120 "total license fee" [4]. We report both; the county's figure is what it says you pay.
  • Rules: both of you must appear together, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:15 p.m. Apply at least 24 hours before the ceremony; the license is good for 30 days [4]. Questions: 302-744-2346 [4].

Court records and land records

  • Superior Court copies (statewide fee schedule): a certified copy is $15 for up to 3 pages and an exemplified copy $25; extra copies are at most $2 a page, and fax copies at most $8 a page [23].
  • Kent County Prothonotary: Room 140, 1st floor, Kent County Courthouse, 414 Federal Street (inside Dover city), open Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; main number 302-735-1900 [9][11].
  • Kent County Recorder of Deeds: office copies are $1 a page, certified copies $7 a page and mailed copies $2 a page [5]. Searching and viewing deeds online is free; a printable download without the watermark is $2 a page, or $75 for a month of unlimited use [8].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Delaware Division of Corporations (Secretary of State), John G. Townsend Building, 401 Federal Street, Suite 4, Dover, for documents signed by a Delaware notary or public official. Mail requests are typically processed the same business day; in-person drop-off is by appointment, 302-739-3077 [24].
  • Fee (statewide): for personal use, $30 for any number of documents presented together, with no expedite fees; commercial documents are $30 each, plus $50 same-day or $40 24-hour [24][25].
  • Not accepted: documents notarized remotely or electronically. The Division doesn't notarize [24].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Delaware Solid Waste Authority (statewide program): residents can bring up to two boxes of paper for free shredding [27]. The Kent County monthly session is at the Cheswold Collection Station, 54 Fork Branch Road, Cheswold (a Cheswold address, not Dover), on the 1st Friday of each month, 10 a.m.–2 p.m. [27].
  • Collection events: DSWA's 2026 Saturday events include document shredding. None is listed in Dover. The nearby Smyrna (June 13) and Milford (July 18) events have passed. DSWA says events can change [28].
  • The UPS Store: its Dover page lists shredding [12].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Can I send a fax at the Dover library?

No. The Dover Public Library says it has no public fax machine [1].

How much is a marriage license in Kent County?

$70 for Delaware residents and $120 for non-residents, cash only [4].

How much is a Delaware birth certificate?

$25 per copy from the Office of Vital Statistics [20].

Can I get something notarized at the Dover library?

No. The Dover Public Library says it doesn't offer notary services [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Dover?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 2 facilities with a Dover address. The Census Geocoder places the Dover Public Library inside the city. The Census Geocoder couldn't match the Dover Post Office's street address, but Delaware's FirstMap locator places 445 Bank Ln inside the FirstMap municipal boundary for Dover, and a Census Geocoder lookup of that point also returns Dover city, so both facilities are inside city limits [3][16][17][11].
