Jersey City, New Jersey document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Jersey City, New Jersey, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Jersey City, Hudson County, the Jersey City Free Public Library, the New Jersey Department of Health, the New Jersey Department of the Treasury, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the Hudson County Clerk (by appointment) and 3 post offices accept passport applications [1][2]. The City Clerk issues Jersey City birth certificates for $25 a copy [3]. New Jersey notaries may charge $2.50 per act [4]. Library printouts are 10¢ a page in black and white [5].
Who does what: Jersey City is in Hudson County [6][7]. The Office of the City Clerk, Division of Vital Records, issues local birth, marriage and death certificates and marriage licenses [3]. The state Office of Vital Statistics and Registry in Trenton also issues birth, death and marriage records [8]. The Hudson County Clerk handles passports, notary filings and county records [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Jersey City Free Public Library: printing from a computer or copier is 10¢ a page in black and white and 25¢ in color. Children 18 and younger with a library card can print up to 20 black-and-white pages free per day (policy dated Nov. 2025) [5].
  • Library wireless printing and fax: the library's wireless-printing page returned HTTP 403, even in a browser, so we couldn't read it. We didn't find a library fax service (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Jersey City page lists 9 locations, 2 marked Coming Soon. It offers printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [11]. The Census Geocoder places 6 of the 7 open stores inside Jersey City and couldn't match 123 Town Square Place [2]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Jersey City

  • The UPS Store: its Jersey City page lists notarizing [11]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [12].
  • Library notary: we didn't find a notary service at the Jersey City Free Public Library (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Jersey's Division of Revenue says a notary may charge $2.50 each for an oath or affirmation, a jurat, a proof of a deed or an acknowledgment [4]. The state Notary Public Manual covers the real-estate closing fees [13].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Jersey City Office of the City Clerk, Division of Vital Records

  • Where: City Hall, 280 Grove Street, Room 118. In-person requests are by appointment only: call 201-547-5150. You can also order by mail or online through VitalChek [3].
  • Fee: $25 per copy for birth, marriage and civil union certificates. Make money orders or checks out to the City of Jersey City [3].
  • Which births, the sources differ: the City Clerk's page says it issues certificates for people born in Jersey City after 1949 [3]. The state Health Department's page says after 1925, and notes those certificates are accepted for Real ID and passports (that state page was last reviewed March 10, 2022) [18]. Ask the clerk before you go.
  • Marriage licenses: apply in person by appointment, 201-547-5150, option 3 [3]. Statewide, you apply in the municipality where either of you lives, the fee is $28 and there's a 72-hour wait [19].

Birth, death and marriage records from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the New Jersey Office of Vital Statistics and Registry charges $25 for the search and one certified copy (or a certification, or a no-record statement), and $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. VitalChek adds a processing fee of $6 by mail or $12.95 by phone [20].
  • Certified copy or certification: certified copies have a raised seal and are legal documents. Certifications are on plain paper, can't be used for identity or legal purposes, and are mainly useful for genealogy [21].
  • Walk-in (statewide, in Trenton): the walk-in center at 140 E. Front Street, Trenton offers same-day service. It has not taken cash since June 18, 2026 [8]. Online orders go through VitalChek [8][22].
  • Local copies: municipal local registrars also issue copies, and their fees vary by municipality [20].

Deeds and land records (Hudson County)

  • Hudson County Register of Deeds and Mortgages: a certified copy is $10.00 for the first page and $1.50 for each additional page. Plain copies are 5¢ a page, and computer printouts are 7¢ a page (fee sheet dated Feb. 26, 2024). Make checks payable to the Hudson County Register [23][24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (Department of the Treasury), for documents signed by a New Jersey notary or selected public officials such as county clerks and the State Registrar. It gives an apostille for Hague Convention countries and a certification for other countries [25].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 per certificate, or $5 per certificate for documents for a child's adoption. The fee table also lists 2-hour and 1-hour expedite fees for walk-in requests, but we couldn't match those amounts to their rows (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [26].
  • Vital records: order a certified copy from the state Office of Vital Statistics, and say on the application that it needs an apostille [21].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past events: the Hudson County Improvement Authority's 2026 free shred days in Jersey City were May 30 and Oct. 17 at Lincoln Park (Duncan Ave. lot), 9 a.m.–2 p.m., for Hudson County residents only. Its last 2026 event was Oct. 31 in Harrison [28]. We didn't find 2027 dates (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Jersey City page lists shredding [11].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Jersey City?

At the Hudson County Clerk, 257 Cornelison Ave., 4th Floor (by appointment), or at the Main, Journal Square or Hudson City post offices [1][14].

How much is a Jersey City birth certificate?

$25 per copy from the City Clerk's Division of Vital Records [3].

How much does a New Jersey notary charge?

$2.50 per oath, jurat, proof or acknowledgment [4].
