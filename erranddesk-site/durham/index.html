Durham, North Carolina document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Durham, North Carolina, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Durham County Library charges 10 cents a side for black-and-white letter prints and copies, and faxing is free at every location [1][2]. Durham County birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each at the Register of Deeds [3]. A marriage license is $60, cash only [4]. North Carolina notaries can charge at most $10 per signature (statewide) [5]. The State Department's locator lists 3 passport offices in Durham city [6][7].
City and county: this page covers the city the Census Bureau calls "Durham city" [8], which is mostly in Durham County and also reaches into Orange and Wake counties [9]. This page uses Durham County offices. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [7]. A Durham mailing address doesn't always mean the city itself, so we note where the Geocoder disagrees.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Durham County Library

  • Printing: black-and-white letter prints are 10 cents a side and color 25 cents; legal size is 20 cents black-and-white and 50 cents color. Double-sided pages count as two. You can pay by card at all locations [1].
  • Copiers: copying is cash only at all locations (coins, $1 and $5 bills) [10].
  • Fax: fax machines are at all Durham County Library locations, and faxing is free [2]. The main library is at 300 N. Roxboro St. [2], which the Census Geocoder places in Durham city [7].
  • The UPS Store: its Durham page lists 7 locations, one marked "Coming Soon" (5431 Page Rd), and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. Of the 6 open stores, the Census Geocoder places 5 in Durham city; it didn't match 3307 Watkins Rd [7]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Durham

  • Library: Durham County Library says it does not offer notary services [12] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Register of Deeds: the office gives the $10 oath to new and renewing notaries [13][3]. Its online ordering page also lists "Notary (In-Office Only) $10.00 ea" [14]; call 919-560-0482 to ask what that covers.
  • The UPS Store: its Durham page lists notarizing [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [15].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions North Carolina notaries for a five-year term. A new applicant takes an approved classroom course of at least six hours and a written exam, pays a $50 nonrefundable application fee, and then takes the oath before the county Register of Deeds within 45 days [5]. The Register of Deeds fee for that oath is $10 [16].
  • Fees (statewide): at most $10 per notarized signature for an acknowledgment, jurat, verification or proof, and $10 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature. Remote notarization is at most $25 per signature. Travel can be charged only as actual mileage at the federal business rate, and only if you agree in writing before the trip. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, and doesn't have to charge at all [5].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide; two official pages differ): the statute lists $15 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment or jurat [5]. The Secretary of State's FAQ says $10.00 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment [17]. Ask the notary which fee applies before you start.
  • No certified copies (statewide): the Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy [17]. For a certified copy of a recorded or vital record, go to the office that holds it (below). The Secretary of State's site has a "Find a Notary Near You" search [18].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death and marriage certificates (Durham County Register of Deeds)

  • Durham County records: certified copies of birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each [3]. Uncertified copies are 25 cents in person and $1 by mail; pay by cash, certified check, money order or card [22].
  • Where: 200 E Main Street, Durham [22], which the Census Geocoder places in Durham city [7].
  • Other North Carolina births: the office issues certified copies for births elsewhere in North Carolina from 1971 on for $24 each, cash only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., with photo ID [22].
  • Online: online orders add a $4 service fee plus a card fee [14].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • County first (statewide): NC Vital Records says birth, death and marriage certificates can also be ordered from the Register of Deeds in the county where the event happened, usually the same day [23]. State law sets the Register of Deeds fee at $10 for a certified copy of a birth or death certificate or marriage license [16].
  • State fees (statewide): $24 for a search of one three-year period, including one copy if the record is found. The search fee isn't refunded if no record is found. Extra copies of the same certificate are $15 each, expedited processing is $15, and VitalChek adds a $13.95 processing fee [24].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): in-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m. (last appointment 2:30 p.m.), at the Cooper Memorial Health Building, 225 N. McDowell St., Raleigh. Book at 919-733-3000, option 3; the $15 expedited fee applies to in-person service. Mail goes to North Carolina Vital Records, 1903 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-1900 [25].
  • Divorce (statewide): the state has divorce certificates from 1958 on [26]; the divorce decree itself is kept by the Clerk of Court in the county where the divorce was filed [23].

Marriage licenses (Durham County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): unmarried people 18 or older may marry [27]. The Register of Deeds fee for a license is $60, and $10 for each certified copy [16]. The license form in the statute authorizes the marriage "at any time within 60 days" [27].
  • Durham County fee and hours: the license is $60, cash only. Licenses are issued Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–4 p.m., and an appointment is required. Applicants 18–21 also need a certified copy of their birth certificate. You must marry within 60 days [4].

Recorded documents (Durham County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): registers of deeds charge $5 for the first page of a certified copy of a recorded instrument and $2 for each additional page [16]. Uncertified copy prices are set by each office [16].
  • Durham County fees: certified copies are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page; the fee schedule says uncertified copies are "cost as posted" [3].

Court record copies (Durham County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • Court copy fees (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court charges $2.00 for the first page of each document copied and $0.25 for each additional page, $3.00 for a certificate under seal and $10.00 for exemplification of records. These are state fees under G.S. 7A-308, shown in the NC Judicial Branch costs chart in effect Jan. 1, 2026 [28][29][30] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Where and when: Durham County Courthouse, 510 South Dillard St., Durham, phone (919) 808-3000, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [31]. The Census Geocoder places it in Durham city [7].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the North Carolina Secretary of State's Authentication Office, for North Carolina documents used in foreign countries, such as birth and marriage certificates, statements of marital status, powers of attorney, diplomas and transcripts [32].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document; for adoptions, a duplicate original ordered at the same time is $5.00. Pay by check or money order to "North Carolina Secretary of State" [33][34].
  • How to send (statewide): mail a cover letter, payment, the original documents with all certifications attached and a prepaid return envelope or courier label [33] to North Carolina Secretary of State, Authentication Office, P.O. Box 29622, Raleigh, NC 27626-0622. The office is at 2 South Salisbury Street, Raleigh [32]. It no longer takes appointments; you can bring documents in without one [35].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [36]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • City events: the City of Durham says Solid Waste Management hosts citywide events such as e-waste collections and paper shredding [37]. We didn't find an upcoming city or county shredding event (last checked Nov 3, 2026). The latest city calendar listing we found was Jan. 18, 2020, which is past [38].
  • The UPS Store: its Durham page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Durham?

For a Durham County birth, at the Register of Deeds, 200 E Main Street, for $10 [3][22]. Births elsewhere in North Carolina from 1971 on are $24, cash only [22].

Can I fax at the Durham library?

Yes. Fax machines are at all locations, and faxing is free [2].

How much is a marriage license in Durham County?

$60, cash only, by appointment [4].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the Durham County Register of Deeds?

Yes. The county says passport services are by appointment only, Tuesday–Thursday [19].

Can a North Carolina notary certify a copy?

No. The Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy (statewide) [17].
