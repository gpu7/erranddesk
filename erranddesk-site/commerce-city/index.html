Commerce City, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Commerce City, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Which offices serve Commerce City: This page covers the City of Commerce City City Clerk's Office at the Civic Center, 7887 E. 60th Ave., Commerce City, CO 80022, 303-289-3611 [7]; the Anythink Libraries branches in Commerce City [3][4]; the Adams County Health Department Vital Records office, which is in Commerce City [14]; and the Adams County Clerk and Recorder [12][16].
Short version: Faxing is free at Anythink, and prints are 10 cents a page with a $5 daily printing credit [1][2]. The City Clerk's Office takes new passport applications by appointment, Monday–Thursday 8:30 a.m.–noon [6]. The city's fee directory lists public notary at $5.00 per stamp [8]. Birth certificates come from the Adams County Health Department at 7190 Colorado Blvd. in Commerce City [14].

Notary in Commerce City

  • City of Commerce City: the city's Directory of City Fees and Charges (as of Dec. 16, 2024) lists "Public Notary (per stamp)" at $5.00 [8]. The State Department locator's entry for the city also says "Notary Services provided" [10]. We didn't find notary hours or appointment rules on the city's pages we checked.
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [17].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [18]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example): its Commerce City page lists notarizing among its services, without prices [20]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [17].
  • Anythink and Adams County: we didn't find a public notary service on the Anythink or Adams County pages we checked.

Passports in Commerce City

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator lists one acceptance facility with a Commerce City address [10]:

FacilityAddress and phonePassport hours listedPhotos
City of Commerce City (City Clerk's Office)7887 E. 60th Ave., Commerce City, CO 80022. 303-289-3611Mon–Thu 8:30 a.m.–12 p.m., by appointmentListed as nearby, not on site
  • City Clerk's Office: services are by appointment only. New applications are accepted 8:30 a.m. to noon, Monday through Thursday [6]. The locator says to call 303-289-3600 for an appointment [10].
  • Fees listed by the city: the city's processing (execution) fee is $35 per application, by cash, check or charge. The State Department fee is a separate payment, by check or money order only: $130 for an adult book, $30 for an adult card, $100 for a youth book (15 and under) and $15 for a youth card [6].
  • Photos: passport photos aren't available at the city clerk's office or the Civic Center. Bring one 2 x 2 inch color photo taken within the last six months [6].
  • Adams County Clerk and Recorder (Brighton, nearby): applications are by appointment only at the Adams County Government Center. The county's $35 execution fee is cash or credit/debit card only. Photos are $14, and a retake is $7 [12].
  • Post Office fees (national): where USPS accepts applications, it charges a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [11]. We didn't find a post office with a Commerce City address in the locator data.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Adams County Health Department (in Commerce City)

  • Where: Vital Records office, 7190 Colorado Blvd., Suite 170, Commerce City, CO 80022, 720.200.1401. Open Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., closed for lunch 12–1 p.m. [14].
  • Any Colorado county: the department "issues birth and death certificates for any Colorado county" [13].
  • In person: by appointment. Cash, check, money order or credit card are accepted. If the record is available, certificates are issued the same day [13].
  • Other ways to order: by email at VitalRecords@adamscountyco.gov or by mail. Online orders through GoCerts add $9, and through VitalChek add $13.95 [13].
  • Fee (statewide): the department's page doesn't list the certificate fee. Mesa County Public Health says the Colorado Office of the State Registrar of Vital Statistics sets the fee schedule statewide: $25 for a first copy and $20 for additional copies in 2026 [15].

Recorded documents: Adams County Clerk and Recorder

  • Recording: $43.00 per document. Death certificates or verifications of death carry no recording fee [16].
  • Copies: regular copies are $0.25 per page. Certified copies are $0.25 per page plus $1.00 per document for certification. Certified marriage or civil union license copies are $0.25 per page plus $1.00 per document. A credit card fee of 3% applies [16].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. At the Commerce City clerk's office, the State Department passport fee must be paid by check or money order [6]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Commerce City?

At the City Clerk's Office in the Civic Center, 7887 E. 60th Ave., by appointment. New applications are taken Monday–Thursday 8:30 a.m.–noon, and you need to bring your own photo [6].

How much does a notary cost at Commerce City?

The city's fee directory lists public notary at $5.00 per stamp [8]. Statewide, the limit is $15 per document in person [17].

Is faxing free at the library in Commerce City?

Yes. Anythink says faxing is free for all Anythink customers [1].

Where can I get a birth certificate in Commerce City?

At the Adams County Health Department's Vital Records office, 7190 Colorado Blvd., Suite 170, by appointment, or by email, mail or online. It issues certificates for any Colorado county [13][14].
