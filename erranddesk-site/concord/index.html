Concord, New Hampshire document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Concord, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Concord, Concord Public Library, Merrimack County Registry of Deeds, the State of New Hampshire, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Concord (Concord Post Office) [1]. Concord Public Library prints are 15¢ B&W / 50¢ color; free scan; no public fax and no library notary [2][3]. City Clerk offers notary (call for details; we didn't find a published fee) [3]. Certified vital copies are $15 / $10 [4].
Counties in New Hampshire (statewide): New Hampshire has ten counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds. Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the state Division of Vital Records Administration (Department of State) [5][6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Concord Public Library (45 Green Street): black-and-white prints are 15¢/page; color 50¢/page; cash or coin only (no credit cards). Public scanner is free (USB, email or print). Wireless printing is available at the Main Library [2].
  • Fax: the library FAQ says CPL does not have a public fax machine; downtown copy shops offer fax (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [3].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Concord page lists 3 locations, including a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center at 10 Ft Eddy Rd, FedEx at Walgreens at 142 Loudon Rd, and FedEx at Dollar General at 197 Fisherville Rd [7].
  • The UPS Store: Concord Square Plaza, 75 S Main St ((603) 226-2848). Store page advertises printing, faxing, shredding and notarizing [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Concord

  • City Clerk (City Hall, 41 Green Street): the library FAQ says City Hall offers notary services at the City Clerk's office; call (603) 225-8500. We didn't find a published City Clerk notary fee on the City Clerk or library FAQ pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [3][9].
  • Library: Concord Public Library does not offer notary services [3].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state; NH's statewide cap is $10 per ordinary act [10][11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Hampshire caps ordinary notarial acts at $10 each (oath, witness, service, or certification). Remote notarial acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act. Depositions follow RSA 517:19 (SOS manual: $5 to $50, plus $0.20/mile to swear witnesses). No fee for administering oaths of office for town officers [11][12].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Concord City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 41 Green Street, Concord, NH 03301. Monday–Wednesday and Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Thursday 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Phone (603) 225-8500 [9][4].
  • Fees: $15 for one certified copy; $10 for each additional copy purchased at the same time. A nonrefundable $15 search fee is collected for each record requested [4][16].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): any of New Hampshire's 234 city and town clerks can issue certified copies when you have a direct and tangible interest; many clerks use the NHVRIN system for records since 1935 [5].
  • State Division of Vital Records Administration (statewide): 9 Ratification Way, Concord, NH 03301-2455. Search fee $15 for the first copy (kept whether or not a record is found); $10 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [5][16].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): request a notarized certified copy from a city/town clerk or DVRA, then take it to the Secretary of State (vital-record photocopies are not accepted) [17][5].

Marriage licenses (Concord City Clerk)

  • When: marriage licenses issued 8:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; both applicants must appear together in person [18].
  • Fee: state-set $50; cash or debit/credit card [18].
  • Validity: may be issued the same day if requirements are met; valid for 90 days anywhere in New Hampshire [18].
  • State rules (statewide): apply for a marriage license at any New Hampshire city or town clerk, regardless of where you live. There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in New Hampshire. Both parties must usually appear together. You must be 18 or older [5].

Land records (Merrimack County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 163 North Main Street, Suite 103, Concord, NH 03301. Office hours 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; copying and recording 8:00 a.m.–3:45 p.m. Phone (603) 228-0101. Concord is the county seat for Merrimack County [19][6].
  • Copies: $2.00 per page at the Registry or via internet account; certified copies add $5.00 per document (in-office only). Email copies by phone order are $10.00 per document [19].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): RSA 478:17-g sets $10 for the first recorded page plus $4 for each additional page for most documents; mortgage discharges and many lien releases are $15 first page plus $4 per additional page. An additional $25 LCHIP surcharge applies to each deed, mortgage, mortgage discharge, or plan [20].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the New Hampshire Secretary of State's Office, Room 204, State House, 107 North Main Street, Concord. Counter Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:15 p.m. [17].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per document; cash or check payable to the State of New Hampshire. Same-day service on 10 or more documents adds $25 per 10 documents [17].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, payment, and a self-addressed prepaid return envelope [17].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: Concord store listing advertises shredding among other services; confirm price with the store before you go [8].
  • We didn't find a City of Concord household paper-shredding event schedule on the City Clerk pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026).

Questions

Does Concord Public Library notarize?

No. CPL does not offer notary services. City Hall (41 Green Street) offers notary at the City Clerk's office; call (603) 225-8500 [3].

How much is a birth certificate in Concord?

$15 for the first certified copy and $10 for each additional copy purchased at the same time at the City Clerk [4].

How much can a New Hampshire notary charge?

Up to $10 per ordinary notarial act. Remote acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act (statewide) [11][12].

Can I get an apostille on a Concord birth certificate?

Request a notarized certified copy from the City Clerk or DVRA, then bring it to the Secretary of State in Concord. The apostille is $10 per document (statewide) [17].

Where do I record a deed for Concord property?

Merrimack County Registry of Deeds, 163 North Main Street, Suite 103, Concord. Recording fees follow RSA 478:17-g [19][20].

Where can I apply for a passport in Concord?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 1 New Hampshire facility with a Concord address [1]. The Census Geocoder places it inside Concord city [13].
