Syracuse, New York document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Syracuse, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide, countywide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Syracuse, Onondaga County, Onondaga County Public Libraries, OCRRA, New York State, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: 7 passport acceptance facilities are inside the city, including the Onondaga County Clerk [1][2]. Birth certificates are $30, and extra copies ordered at the same time are $15 [3]. A marriage license is $40 at City Hall, with no appointment needed [4]. New York notaries may charge $2 per act (statewide) [5]. The Central Library is closed for air-conditioning repairs and offers copies and faxes at the curb [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Central Library (447 S. Salina St.): the building is closed because of ongoing air-conditioning problems. Copies and faxes are offered curbside at the Salina Street community room entrance during regular hours [6].
  • OCPL fees (county law): 10¢ per side for black-and-white copies, 50¢ per side for color, and $1.00 per fax page, under a county law passed Oct. 8, 2024 [7]. OCPL's own faxing page says pricing varies by location, so call first [8].
  • Printing: OCPL says printing prices vary by library. The prices it lists are for suburban libraries, and we didn't find a price for the Syracuse city branches, so call the branch (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [9].
  • The UPS Store: its Syracuse page lists 2 locations offering printing, shredding, notarizing and faxing [10]. Prices aren't listed online.
  • FedEx Office: we didn't find a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center inside city limits. The Syracuse-area ones FedEx lists are at 3115 Erie Blvd E (in the village of East Syracuse) and 3528 W Genesee St, both outside the city [11][2].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Syracuse

  • Libraries: OCPL lists notaries on staff at libraries including the Hazard, Mundy, Petit and Soule branches. Call the library before you go [12][13]. We didn't find a fee on that page.
  • The UPS Store: its Syracuse page lists notarizing [10]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [14].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a New York notary may charge $2.00 for an oath or affirmation, or for taking an acknowledgment or proof of execution [15]. Executive Law §136 sets $2 for an acknowledgment by one person and $2 for each additional person, plus $2 for each witness sworn [16]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [15]. The Westchester County Clerk's notary FAQ gives the same $2.00 fee [17].
  • Notary certificate for an apostille: the Onondaga County Clerk charges $3.00 to authenticate a notary's signature [18].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Birth and death certificates (Onondaga County Office of Vital Statistics)

  • Where and when: 421 Montgomery St., Civic Center Suite 20, 9 a.m.–4 p.m. weekdays [3].
  • Fee: $30 for the first certificate and $15 for each extra copy of the same certificate ordered at the same time. Expedited mail adds $20 (Priority Mail) or $25 (next business day) [3][21].
  • State alternative: the New York State Department of Health also issues certificates for births outside New York City, for $30 by mail (statewide) [22].

Marriage licenses (Syracuse City Clerk)

  • Where: City Hall, 233 E. Washington St. Both people apply in person; no appointment is needed [4].
  • Fee and wait: $40, by cash, card, money order or check. Apply at least 24 hours before the ceremony; the license is valid for 60 days. A certified copy is $10 [4].
  • Compared with New York City: the statewide fee outside NYC is $40 [23]; the NYC City Clerk charges $35 [24].

Deeds and land records (Onondaga County Clerk, countywide)

  • Copies: 65¢ a page for regular copies. Certified copies are $5.00 for the first 4 pages, then $1.25 a page [18].
  • Recording a deed: $45.50 plus $5.00 a page, with transfer tax of $4 per $1,000 [25][18].
  • Cards: card payments carry a 2.65% service fee ($3 minimum) [18].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New York State Department of State, for $10 per document. You can apply by mail or walk in at its offices in New York City, Albany, Binghamton, Buffalo and Utica. Drop-off is offered only in Albany and New York City, and same-day walk-in service is limited to 10 documents [26].
  • By mail (statewide): NY Department of State, Division of Licensing Services, Apostille and Authentication Unit, PO Box 22001, Albany, NY 12201-2001. Express carriers use the physical address instead. Phone 518-474-4429 [26].
  • State vital records (statewide): the state Department of Health can forward a state-issued certificate to the Department of State if you order by mail with expedited processing ($45.00 per copy) and enclose a separate $10.00 check or money order per certificate, payable to the Department of State [27].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [28]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Free events: OCRRA, the county recycling agency, says local elected officials hold occasional drop-off events for confidential documents. We didn't find a dated upcoming shred event on its pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [29]; its events page lists only a pumpkin drop-off, Nov 2–21, 2026 [30].
  • Past change: Bodow Recycling, which offered OCRRA-partnered reduced-cost shredding, closed for good on June 29, 2026 [29].
  • Don't recycle shreds: OCRRA says shredded paper doesn't belong in home recycling bins [29].
  • The UPS Store: its Syracuse page lists shredding [10].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Syracuse?

At the Onondaga County Clerk, the F. Franklin Moon Library or 5 post offices inside the city [1][2].

How much is a birth certificate in Syracuse?

$30, plus $15 for each extra copy ordered at the same time [3].

How much is a marriage license in Syracuse?

$40; apply at least 24 hours before the ceremony [4].

How much does a New York notary charge?

$2 per oath, affirmation or acknowledgment, under Executive Law §136 (statewide) [5]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [15].
