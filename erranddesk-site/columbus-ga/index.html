Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Chattahoochee Valley Libraries

  • Prices: copy machines are at all locations; copies are black and white and cost 10 cents each. Prints are 10 cents a page for black and white and 50 cents for color [1].
  • Scanning: the copy machines scan in color at no cost; you save scans to your own drive through a USB Type-A port [1].
  • Computers and wireless printing: desktop computers are free with a library card or guest pass, and SmartALEC lets you send documents to print from anywhere [1].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax service on the library's services list [1].
  • Where: Columbus Public Library, 3000 Macon Rd, 706-243-2669. Hours are Mon–Thu 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Fri–Sat 10 a.m.–6 p.m. and Sun 1:30–6 p.m. It's closed Nov. 11 for Veterans Day and Nov. 26 for Thanksgiving, and closes at 6 p.m. on Nov. 25. The page also lists a Thanksgiving closure dated "November 27, 2025," so check with the library about Nov. 27 [13]. The Census Geocoder places the library in Columbus city [14].
  • Clerk of Superior & State Courts: court and real estate records can be faxed or emailed as standard copies for $2.50 for the first page and $1.00 for each page after [15].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 4 locations with Columbus, Georgia addresses. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [16]. The Census Geocoder places these 3 in Columbus city: 1639 Bradley Park Dr, Ste 500; 3201 Macon Rd, Ste 139; and 8132 Veterans Parkway, Ste 409. It didn't match the fourth, 6361 Talokas Ln, Ste C140 [16][14]. Prices aren't listed online: its 1639 Bradley Park Dr store page shows no prices and says products, services, pricing and hours of operation may vary by location [17], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Columbus

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the library's services list [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Columbus page lists notarizing [16]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store [17]. Notary rules vary by state [18].
  • Becoming a notary (Muscogee County): the Clerk's cost schedule lists a new notary certificate and a renewal at $55.00 each, and a certificate of authority at $2.00 [19]. The Clerk's notary and passport line is (706) 225-3208 [15]. We didn't find a fee on the Clerk's pages for notarizing your documents.
  • How notaries are appointed (statewide): Georgia notaries are commissioned for a four-year term by the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where they live; residents of neighboring states who work or own a business in Georgia can apply in that county. Applicants must be at least 18 and complete the notary training course. The appointment fee is $40.00–$55.00 depending on the county. A Georgia notary can act anywhere in the state [20].
  • Fees (statewide): the GSCCCA's summary of Georgia notary law says the authorized fee is $2.00 for each notarial act. On request, a notary may charge $2.00 more for a certificate from the Clerk of Superior Court showing the commission is in effect. A notary doesn't have to charge, but must tell you beforehand if there's a fee and the amount allowed by law [21].
  • Journal (statewide): since January 1, 2025, a notary must keep a written or electronic journal entry for each notarial act done for a self-filer [21].
  • The law itself (statewide): Georgia notary law is in Title 45, Article 17 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated [21]. The official online code needs JavaScript, so we didn't read the statute text; it needs a browser read [22].
  • Check a notary (statewide): the GSCCCA keeps the statewide notary database and has an online Notary Public Search Index. Its Notary Division phone is 404-327-6023 [20].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (West Central Health District)

  • Fee: $25.00 for one certified copy and $5.00 for each additional copy on the same person bought at the same time. Valid picture ID is required, fees aren't refundable, and personal checks haven't been accepted since April 1, 2011 [2]. That's the same as the state fees below.
  • Which records: certified birth copies are available whether you were born in Muscogee County or any other Georgia county [2].
  • Where: Vital Records in Columbus, 5601 Veterans Parkway, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. It closes at 3:30 p.m. on listed 2026 Mondays, including Nov. 9 and Dec. 14. The Vital Records Annex, next to the Department of Driver Services at 8397 Macon Rd., Midland, 706-530-4336, is open Tuesday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Saturday 9:00–11:30 a.m. [2]. The Census Geocoder places both in Columbus city [14].
  • By mail: orders are mailed by USPS; tracked UPS mailing costs extra. Phone 833-337-1749, option 1, option 1 [2].

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): birth and death certificates are $25.00 each, plus $5.00 for each additional copy. Marriage certificates are $10.00 plus $5.00 for additional copies, and amendments are $10.00 plus the cost of the certificate [26].
  • Payment (statewide): credit or debit cards at walk-in service or online, cash at walk-in service only, certified checks and money orders. Personal checks aren't accepted [26].
  • Who can get one (statewide): certified birth and death certificates go only to people with a direct and tangible interest, such as immediate family members or legal representatives of the family [27][28]. Georgia.gov says only the registrant, an immediate family member or a legal representative can request one [12].
  • Older births (statewide): the State Office of Vital Records has births from January 1919 on. For older births, contact the county where the birth happened or the Georgia Archives [27].
  • In person (statewide): for same-day service, visit the State Office of Vital Records, 1680 Phoenix Boulevard, Suite 100, Atlanta, GA 30349, 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Monday–Friday except state holidays, or any county vital records office [12]. The state works with 159 county offices [29]. Georgia Vital Records doesn't do same-day cruise rush requests [27].
  • By mail (statewide): mail the request form and payment to the State Office of Vital Records at the Phoenix Boulevard address. Georgia.gov gives a response time of 8–10 weeks [12], and the Department of Public Health says 8 to 10 weeks for mail-in death certificate requests [28].
  • Online (statewide; pages differ on timing): the state's ROVER service charges $25 per certified copy plus $8 for processing. FedEx shipping is $16 per order and speeds up delivery only; the Department of Public Health says standard processing takes up to 10 weeks [30]. Georgia.gov gives an online response time of 2–4 weeks, or up to 5 business days for expedited orders, and lists ROVER, GO Certificates and VitalChek [12]. Ask the office which timing applies before you order.
  • Divorce records (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where the divorce was granted holds the copies [12].

Marriage licenses (Muscogee County Probate Court)

  • Who issues licenses (statewide): county probate courts. You must be at least 18, or 17 if certain conditions are met. If at least one of you lives in Georgia, any county's probate court can issue the license; if neither does, you must apply in the county where the ceremony will be. Both of you must be present [11].
  • After the ceremony (statewide): the signed license should go back to the probate court within 30 days of the ceremony, and the certificate typically arrives by mail within 30 days [11].
  • Older copies (statewide): the State Office of Vital Records holds marriage applications and certificates from June 1952 to August 1996, with a non-refundable $10.00 search fee and $5.00 for each additional copy. Records outside those years are at the probate court of the county that issued the license [31].
  • Muscogee County fee: $56.00, or $16.00 with printed proof of at least 6 hours of premarital counseling. The fee doesn't include any certified copies [3].
  • When and payment: walk-in only; the court stops processing licenses at 4:00 p.m. You can start the application online. Pay by cash, money order or credit/debit card (3% fee added); American Express, personal checks and phone tap (Apple/Google Pay) aren't accepted. Phone 706-225-4750 [3]. The court is moving Nov. 6–9; see the note above [7].
  • After the ceremony: return the original license to the Probate Court in person or by U.S. Mail [3].
  • Copies: certified copies are $10.00 each once the license is returned and recorded. By mail, send the marriage date, both names, a return address and a $10 money order per copy to Probate Court, P.O. Box 1340, Columbus, GA 31902. Online requests are credit card only and take about 5–7 days by mail [3].
  • Divorce papers: the Probate Court says copies of Muscogee County divorce documents come from the Superior Court Clerk's office [3].

Recorded documents (Muscogee County Clerk of Superior & State Courts)

  • State law (statewide): we didn't read the state's recording fee law, because the official online Georgia code needs JavaScript; it needs a browser read [22]. The county fee schedule below is what you pay.
  • Muscogee County recording fees: the Clerk's cost schedule lists deeds and other real estate instruments at $25.00, cancellations, satisfactions, releases or assignments at $25.00 each, and plats, maps and condominium floor plans at $10.00. "All Other Recording" is $5.00 for the first page and $5.00 for the second [19].
  • E-filing and alerts: the Clerk takes real estate documents online through the GSCCCA e-file portal, and offers free FANS alerts on filings about your property. Recording Division: (706) 653-4358, option 2 [32].

Court and real estate record copies (Muscogee County Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the official online Georgia code needs JavaScript, so we didn't read the state's clerk fee law; it needs a browser read [22]. The county fee schedule below is what you pay.
  • Copy fees: in person, a standard copy is $1.00 a page and a certified or exemplified copy is $2.50 for the first page plus $0.50 for each page after. By mail, certified or exemplified copies are $10.00 for the first 25 pages per document plus $1.00 for each page after [15].
  • Pages differ: the Clerk's cost schedule lists uncertified copies at $0.50 a page without help or $1.00 a page with help [19], while its copy request page lists a standard copy at $1.00 a page [15]. Ask which applies.
  • Payment: cash, money order, business check, Apple and Google Pay, and American Express, Discover, Mastercard and Visa cards; a 3.5% fee is added to card and phone payments [15].
  • Where and when: the Government Center, 100 10th Street, 2nd floor Tower, with security screening; (706) 653-4358, option 1. Mailing address: P.O. Box 2145, Columbus, GA 31902 [15]. Office hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m., closed on City holidays [6]. The Census Geocoder places the Government Center in Columbus city [14].

Apostilles and other certifications (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA) is the only state agency that issues apostilles for Georgia documents, and Atlanta is its only location. For countries outside the Apostille Convention, the Georgia Secretary of State issues a Great Seal Certification instead (404-656-2871) [33].
  • Fees (statewide): $3.00 per document for an apostille. A certification of notary public, for documents used in the U.S., is $2.00 per document [33].
  • What it takes (statewide): birth, death and marriage certificates, divorce decrees and court documents must be official certified copies signed and sealed by the issuing Georgia official. Notarized documents don't need a county certification first. Out-of-state and federal documents go elsewhere [33].
  • Walk-in (statewide): 1875 Century Blvd., Ste. 100, Atlanta, GA 30345, 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Monday–Friday, no appointment needed; it typically takes less than 20 minutes. Pay by cash, a check with a pre-printed name and address payable to GSCCCA, a money order or credit card [33].
  • By mail (statewide): GSCCCA, Attn: Notary Division, at the same address. Include a cover letter naming the destination country and a prepaid, self-addressed envelope or airbill; processing normally takes one to two business days. Contact notary@gsccca.org, 404-327-6023 or 800-304-5175 [33].

The GSCCCA's apostille office at 1875 Century Blvd. is outside Columbus: the Census Geocoder places it in Chamblee city [14]. More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [34]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Columbus, Georgia?

At the West Central Health District's Vital Records office, 5601 Veterans Parkway, or its Midland annex, for $25 [2], or at the State Office of Vital Records, which offers same-day walk-in service [12].

How much is a marriage license in Columbus, Georgia?

$56.00 at the Muscogee County Probate Court, or $16.00 with printed proof of at least 6 hours of premarital counseling. Certified copies are $10.00 each and aren't included [3].

Which Columbus office is cheaper for passport photos?

The Probate Court lists photos at $15 [5]; the Clerk of Superior & State Courts lists $20.00 [4]. Both charge a $35 application fee [4][5].

How much does a Georgia notary charge?

$2.00 per notarial act, according to the GSCCCA's summary of Georgia notary law [21].
