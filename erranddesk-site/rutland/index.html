Rutland, Vermont document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Rutland, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Rutland, Rutland Free Library, the State of Vermont, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Rutland (Rutland Post Office) [1]. Rutland Free Library prints are 25¢ B&W / 50¢ color; the library says it does not have wireless printing (yet); we didn't find fax listed [2]. Certified vital and land copies are $10 at the City Clerk [3][4]. The UPS Store advertises notary; we didn't find a City Clerk notary page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [5][6].
Counties in Vermont (statewide): Vermont has 14 counties. Land records are kept by each town or city clerk — there is no county registry of deeds. Vital records are issued by town and city clerks and by the Vermont Department of Health Vital Records Office [7][8][9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Rutland Free Library (10 Court St): printing from public computers or USB is 25¢ per black-and-white page or 50¢ color. Staff can also print from email for patrons in the building. The library computers page says it does not have wireless printing (yet). We didn't find fax listed on that page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • The UPS Store: 188 Shopping Plaza Rd ((802) 773-7410). Store pages advertise printing, shipping, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailboxes [5][6].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Rutland page lists 1 location — FedEx at Walgreens, 10 Woodstock Ave — drop-off and hold for pickup of pre-packaged, pre-labeled shipments [10][11]. The same city page's marketing copy mentions "FedEx Office in Rutland" and passport photos at FedEx Office; the locations list does not name a staffed FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in Rutland (both cited; last checked Nov 5, 2026). FedEx's official locate search for Rutland, VT likewise lists no staffed FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in Rutland (Walgreens and drop boxes; no Office in the first ~28 mi) [12]. A nearby FedEx Authorized ShipCenter listed on locate is Woodstock Pack & Ship, 452 Woodstock Rd, Woodstock ((802) 457-3291)—not a FedEx Office [12].

Notary in Rutland

  • City Clerk: we didn't find a notary-services page or fee on the Clerk's Office pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Call 802-773-1800 x5 to ask before you go [3].
  • The UPS Store: 188 Shopping Plaza Rd advertises notary; fees vary by location. Vermont sets a $30 commission fee for notaries but we didn't find a statutory cap on customer notarial-act charges [6][13][14][15].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Vermont's notary chapter sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary public (26 V.S.A. § 5324). We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on what a Vermont notary may charge a customer for an ordinary notarial act in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Remote and electronic notarial acts need a special endorsement from the Office of Professional Regulation [14][15][16].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Rutland City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 1 Strongs Avenue / 52 Washington Street (PO Box 969). Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (vaults close at 3:45 p.m.; office does not close for lunch). Phone 802-773-1800 x5 [3].
  • Fees: certified copies of vital records are $10.00 each. The office does not accept personal checks; cash, money order, debit, or credit (card processing surcharge via Xpresspay) [4][3]. Statewide VDH certified copies are also $10 (plus $2 online) [7][20].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Town or city clerk (statewide): Vermont town and city clerks issue certified copies of vital records; since 2019 clerks can print eligible Vermont birth and death records filed anywhere in the state [7].
  • Vermont Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): 280 State Drive, Waterbury, VT 05671-8300. Certified copies with raised seal are $10 each; online orders add a $2 processing fee [7][20].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified (or notarized, as required) copy from a clerk or VDH, then submit it to the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration for an apostille [21][7].

Marriage licenses (Rutland City Clerk)

  • Fee: $80.00, cash payment [4].
  • Waiting period: none; the license is valid for 60 days from the date it is issued (signed by the town clerk and one of the applicants) [4][22].
  • Certified copy after the ceremony: the Clerk does not collect the $10 certified-copy fee when the license is issued — request a copy after the returned license is filed [4].
  • State rules (statewide): obtain a marriage license from any Vermont town or city clerk. The license costs $80, there is no waiting period, and it is valid for 60 days. Both parties must sign the application; at least one must sign in front of the clerk. You must be 18 or older [22].

Land records (Rutland City Clerk)

  • Office: same Clerk address. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [23][8].
  • Recording (effective July 1, 2019 per City page): $15 per page; Property Transfer Tax Return $15; maps $25; research $4 per hour; uncertified copies $1 per page [23][3].
  • Certified copies: $10.00 per copy for land records (same as vitals on the Clerk page) [3] — confirm against statewide statute wording of $10.00 per page [9].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): 32 V.S.A. § 1671 sets $15.00 per page to record most documents at the town or city clerk; certified copies of recorded documents are $10.00 per page; uncertified copies are $1.00 per page (minimum $2.00). A property transfer tax return filing is $15.00; survey plats are $25.00 per sheet size listed in the statute. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration (VSARA) on behalf of the Secretary of State. Physical/drop-box address: 1078 U.S. Route 2, Middlesex; mailing address 1078 U.S. Route 2 - Middlesex, Montpelier, VT 05633-7701 [21].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per apostille or authentication. Do not send cash by mail; credit and debit cards are not accepted. Checks or money orders payable to the VT Secretary of State, in U.S. dollars [21].
  • Mail (statewide): include the VSARA-35 request form, payment, and documents; preliminary review before submitting is encouraged [21].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I get a notary in Rutland?

The UPS Store at 188 Shopping Plaza Rd advertises notary [6]. We didn't find a City Clerk notary page (last checked Nov 5, 2026); call 802-773-1800 x5 to ask [3].

How much is a birth certificate in Rutland?

Certified copies are $10.00 at the City Clerk; no personal checks [4][3]. VDH statewide is also $10 certified plus $2 online [7][20].

How much can a Vermont notary charge?

26 V.S.A. § 5324 sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary. We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on customer charges for ordinary notarial acts in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [14][15].

Where do I record a deed for Rutland city property?

Rutland City Clerk land records, 1 Strongs Avenue. Recording is $15 per page. Vermont has no county registry of deeds [23][9].
