Springfield, Massachusetts document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Springfield, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. This is Springfield, Massachusetts (Hampden County)—not Springfield, Missouri.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Springfield, Springfield City Library, Hampden County Registry of Deeds, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Springfield (Springfield SDC post office) [1]. Springfield City Library prints for 15¢ a page and faxes for $1 per job [2]. Birth, death and marriage certificates are $20 at City Hall Room 123 [3]. Massachusetts does not cap ordinary notary fees (statewide) [4].
Counties in Massachusetts (statewide): Census geography still uses counties, but Berkshire, Essex, Franklin, Hampden, Hampshire, Middlesex and Worcester counties are no longer legal governmental units [5]. Land records are kept by county Registries of Deeds under the Secretary of the Commonwealth. Suffolk County and Boston city are governmentally consolidated [5].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Springfield City Library (Central, 220 State St., and branches): black-and-white printing is 15¢ a page (color not listed). Traditional fax is $1 per job; scanning is free. Libraries cannot receive faxes [2].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Springfield page lists 7 locations, including FedEx Office Print & Ship Centers at 1383 Main St. and inside MGM (1 MGM Way), plus FedEx at Walgreens and a Pak Mail authorized ship center [6].
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find a The UPS Store in Springfield, MA on The UPS Store find-a-store search (Springfield / 01103, last checked Nov 5, 2026); the Springfield city locator URL also 404s [7]. The nearest official city page is West Springfield (900 Riverdale St., (413) 733-8877)—a separate city [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Springfield

  • City Clerk notary: the City Clerk fee schedule lists notary service at $1.25 to $2.00 (schedule effective Jan. 1, 2015; confirm current fee) [9].
  • Library: Springfield City Library's services page does not list notary (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [10]. Springfield City Library is also not on the Massachusetts Board of Library Commissioners list of libraries that offer notary public service [11].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [12]. See West Springfield above if you use that store [8].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Massachusetts does not set a maximum fee for ordinary notarial acts such as acknowledgments and jurats. G.L. c. 262, § 41's $1.25 figure applies only to "noting" a dishonored negotiable instrument [13]. The Supreme Judicial Court held that cap does not limit fees for other notarial acts [4]. Ask the fee before you sit down.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Springfield City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: City Hall, 36 Court St., Room 123. Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.; Thursday 8:30 a.m.–6 p.m. Phone 311 or 413-736-3111 [3].
  • Fees: certified birth, death and marriage copies are $20 each in person or by mail. Cash, debit/credit, money order or certified bank check; no personal checks. Make money orders/certified checks payable to "City of Springfield" [3][9].
  • Online: the city partners with VitalChek for credit-card orders (VitalChek adds its own fee); the city itself does not accept online orders [3].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): you can usually get a certified copy from the city or town where the event happened (or where marriage intentions were filed). Fees vary by city or town [17].
  • State Registry of Vital Records and Statistics (statewide): 150 Mt. Vernon St., 1st Floor, Dorchester. Counter hours Monday–Friday 8:45 a.m.–4:45 p.m. State fees: $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54 for the first copy ordered online or by phone ($42 for each additional copy in that order) [18].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): bring a certified copy with an original signature of a Massachusetts notary, city/town clerk, court clerk or Registrar of Vital Records to the Secretary of the Commonwealth [19].

Marriage licenses (Springfield City Clerk)

  • How: both of you apply in person (walk-in) at City Hall Room 122. Marriage filing hours are shorter than general clerk hours (Mon–Wed 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m.; Thu 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m.; Fri 8:30 a.m.–2 p.m.) [20].
  • Fee: $40 due when filing intentions [20][9].
  • Waiting period: 3-day wait; license valid 60 days and only in Massachusetts [20].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at any Massachusetts city or town clerk. There is a 3-day waiting period; the license is valid for 60 days and only inside Massachusetts. You must both be 18 or older. License fees vary by town [17].

Land records (Hampden County Registry of Deeds)

  • Springfield office: 436 Dwight St., Room B042 (Springfield State Office Building). Hours 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recording 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Monday–Friday. Phone (413) 755-1722. There is also a Westfield satellite office [21].
  • Recording fees: deed $155, mortgage $205, mortgage discharge $105, declaration of homestead $35. Cash or check to Hampden County Registry of Deeds or Commonwealth of Massachusetts [22].
  • Copies: certified copies $1 per page [22].
  • Recording fees (county registries): each Registry of Deeds posts its own fee schedule (often a flat amount per document). See the city section for that county's posted fees.

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth. Walk-in at Commissions Section, One Ashburton Place, Room 1719, Boston, or regional offices in Springfield and Fall River [19][23].
  • Fee (statewide): $6.00 per document; check or money order payable to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts [19][23].
  • Mail (statewide): mailed requests take about two to three weeks; include the destination country and a prepaid return envelope [19].
  • Counter limit (statewide): up to three documents per customer at the Boston counter; four or more are ready the next business day after 3 p.m. [19].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is a birth certificate in Springfield, MA?

$20 in person or by mail at City Hall Room 123 [3]. Statewide RVRS copies are $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54/$42 online or by phone [18].

Is this the same Springfield as Missouri?

No. This guide is Springfield, Massachusetts (Hampden County). Errand Desk also has a Springfield, Missouri guide.

How much can a Massachusetts notary charge?

There is no statewide maximum for ordinary notarial acts. The $1.25 figure in G.L. c. 262, § 41 applies only to "noting" a dishonored instrument (statewide) [13][4]. Ask the fee first.

Can I get an apostille on a Springfield birth certificate?

Bring a certified copy with an original clerk or RVRS signature to the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The apostille is $6 per document (statewide) [19][23].
