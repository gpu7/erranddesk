Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This page describes what information is involved when you visit Errand Desk. The site is operated by Richard Casey. It is a static informational website and has no user accounts.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted as static files on Amazon S3 and delivered through Amazon CloudFront. Those services may keep standard request logs. A log entry can include the page or file requested (including a query string, such as a search term in /search/?q=), the date and time, your IP address, and your browser's user-agent string.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate use, such as page views. The site's own source describes this as cookie-free. A script from static.cloudflareinsights.com is included on pages for this purpose. The site does not use Google Analytics or similar advertising trackers.

Cookies and local storage

Errand Desk's own pages and scripts do not set cookies and do not use local storage or session storage. Cloudflare Web Analytics is included in the site build as a cookie-free tool.

Some links on this site are affiliate links, marked "(paid link)," currently for Notarize (Proof) and iFax. The footer also links to Ko-fi for optional tips. If you follow one of those links, that company may set its own cookies or collect information under its own policy. See the affiliate disclosure.

Forms and accounts

Errand Desk has no user accounts and no contact, comment, or signup forms. The home page and the search page include a search box. Search runs in your browser against a static index; there is no search backend. A search query may appear in the page URL and therefore in the hosting logs described above.

Questions

For privacy questions, email erranddesk@protonmail.com. There is also a contact page.

How we check prices: every figure on this site comes from the provider's own official page (or a clearly cited source) and carries a "last checked" date. If a provider doesn't publish a national price, we say so instead of guessing. More about our method.

Affiliate disclosure: some links on this site are affiliate links, marked "(paid link)". If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change our prices, facts or the order we list options in. Full disclosure.

Support Errand Desk: this site has no ads and no paid rankings. If a guide saved you a trip, you can leave a small tip. It's optional and doesn't change what we publish.
