Trenton, New Jersey document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Trenton, New Jersey, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Trenton, Mercer County, the Trenton Free Public Library, the New Jersey Department of Health, the New Jersey Department of the Treasury, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport offices in Trenton, the Mercer County Clerk and the Downtown Station post office [1]. The city's Office of Vital Statistics charges $20 for a certified copy [2], and the state's vital records walk-in center is also in Trenton [3]. The library sends faxes for $1 for the first page [4].
Who does what: Trenton is in Mercer County [5][6], and it's the county seat and the state capital. The City of Trenton Office of Vital Statistics holds records of births, marriages, civil unions, domestic partnerships and deaths in Trenton only [2]. The state Office of Vital Statistics and Registry handles events elsewhere in New Jersey [3]. Deeds are recorded by the Mercer County Clerk [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Trenton Free Public Library, fax and scan: outgoing faxes only, within the United States and up to 19 pages. Faxing is $1.00 for the first page and $0.50 for each additional page. Scanning is free [4].
  • Library printing and copies: computer prints are 20¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color, and photocopies are 10¢ and 50¢. Both are cash only. Scanning to email, Google Drive or a flash drive is free [8][9].
  • The UPS Store: its Trenton page lists one store marked Coming Soon at 334 S. Broad St. The open store it lists, at 1977 N. Olden Ave. Ext., is outside Trenton city limits per the Census Geocoder [10][11].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Trenton

  • Trenton Free Public Library: basic notary service by appointment only, Monday–Friday 9:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; call 609-392-7188 ext. 34. The limit is 3 documents per person per visit, and it's $2.50 per seal with exact change. Bring a valid photo ID. It won't notarize deeds, wills, I-9 forms, living wills or blank documents [12][9].
  • Mercer County Clerk: its office page lists Notary Public hours of 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [13].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Jersey's Division of Revenue says a notary may charge $2.50 each for an oath or affirmation, a jurat, a proof of a deed or an acknowledgment [14]. The state Notary Public Manual covers the real-estate closing fees [15].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Trenton Office of Vital Statistics (City Hall)

  • Where and when: 319 E. State St., Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Visit in person for same-day certified copies [2].
  • Fees: $20 for one certified copy, $5 for each extra copy bought at the same time. Personal checks aren't accepted [2].
  • Apostille copies: certified copies that need an apostille must come from the state office, not the city [2].
  • Marriage and civil union applications: $28, by appointment only [2]. Statewide rule: apply in the municipality where either of you lives, with a 72-hour wait [19].

State Office of Vital Statistics walk-in center (statewide)

  • 140 E. Front St., Trenton: same-day service for records from anywhere in New Jersey. It stopped taking cash after June 18, 2026 [3].

Birth, death and marriage records from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the New Jersey Office of Vital Statistics and Registry charges $25 for the search and one certified copy (or a certification, or a no-record statement), and $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. VitalChek adds a processing fee of $6 by mail or $12.95 by phone [20].
  • Certified copy or certification: certified copies have a raised seal and are legal documents. Certifications are on plain paper, can't be used for identity or legal purposes, and are mainly useful for genealogy [21].
  • Walk-in (statewide, in Trenton): the walk-in center at 140 E. Front Street, Trenton offers same-day service. It has not taken cash since June 18, 2026 [3]. Online orders go through VitalChek [3][22].
  • Local copies: municipal local registrars also issue copies, and their fees vary by municipality [20].

Deeds and land records (Mercer County)

  • Mercer County Clerk: copies are available from the Indexing Section, 609-989-6476. A certified copy is $10 for the first page plus $1.50 for each additional page. Plain copies are 10¢ a page on the office copiers or $2 a page by mail or made by staff [7][23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (Department of the Treasury), for documents signed by a New Jersey notary or selected public officials such as county clerks and the State Registrar. It gives an apostille for Hague Convention countries and a certification for other countries [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 per certificate, or $5 per certificate for documents for a child's adoption. The fee table also lists 2-hour and 1-hour expedite fees for walk-in requests, but we couldn't match those amounts to their rows (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [25].
  • Vital records: order a certified copy from the state Office of Vital Statistics, and say on the application that it needs an apostille [21].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past event: the Mercer County Improvement Authority held a free paper shredding event on Oct. 17, 2026, on S. Broad St. across from the Mercer County Administration Building, with a limit of 8 boxes or bags [27]. We didn't find a later 2026 or 2027 date on an official page (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Trenton?

At the Mercer County Clerk, 209 S. Broad St., by appointment, or at the Downtown Station Post Office, 20 S. Montgomery St. [1][16].

How much is a Trenton birth certificate?

$20 for the first certified copy from the city, and $5 for each extra copy bought at the same time [2].

Can I fax at the Trenton library?

Yes, outgoing only: $1.00 for the first page and $0.50 for each additional page [4].

Does the Trenton library have a notary?

Yes, by appointment: $2.50 per seal, exact change, up to 3 documents per visit [12].
