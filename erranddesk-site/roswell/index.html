Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Roswell Public Library

  • Copies: ask at the front desk. Copies are black and white only, can be double-sided ($0.25 per side) and can only be made on letter-size paper [1].
  • Print from your device: print through Princh to printer ID 108325. The printer is black and white only, $0.25 per page. Jobs are held for up to 2 hours, then deleted. Pay and release the job at the front desk [2].
  • Public computers: residents need a library card; non-residents can agree to the rules and register for a tech card. Printing is black and white at $0.25 per page. Students doing homework can sign up for a computer in the children's area and print free [3].
  • Scan: a public scanner near the front desk; a USB drive is required. The scanner has no network connection, so it can't print or email; use a public computer for that [4]. We didn't find a scanning fee on the page.
  • Fax: we didn't find a public fax service on the library's service pages [1][2][3][4].
  • Address and hours: 301 N Pennsylvania Avenue, Roswell, NM 88201, 575-622-7101. Mon–Thu 10 a.m.–7 p.m., Fri–Sat 10 a.m.–6 p.m., Sun 2–6 p.m. [1].

The UPS Store

The UPS Store #4804, 115 E College Blvd, Roswell, (575) 623-6245, lists color and black-and-white printing and copying, mobile printing, fax (send and receive), notary, passport and ID photos, Live Scan fingerprinting and shredding. Its page lists hours of Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–6 p.m., Sat 9 a.m.–5 p.m. and Sun 10 a.m.–3 p.m. [15]. We didn't find prices on the store's page.

Notary in Roswell

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Roswell Public Library's service pages [1][2][3][4].
  • County Clerk: we didn't find a notary service on the Chaves County Clerk's pages we checked. Its recording page says documents must be notarized with proper acknowledgment before filing [8].
  • The UPS Store: the 115 E College Blvd store lists notary service [15]. We didn't find its notary price.
  • Fees (statewide): the New Mexico Secretary of State's Notarial Officer Handbook (revised July 2023) says a notary "may charge up to" $5.00 per acknowledgment, $5.00 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature, and $5.00 per jurat. Copy certifications are $.50 a page with a $5.00 minimum. A travel fee is allowed only if you agree to it in advance and are told it's separate and not required by law. Electronic notarizations may add a technology fee of no more than $25 per act. An employer can't set higher fees [11]. Nebraska's acknowledgment cap is also $5; Utah's is $10 per signature, Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document, and Kansas has no dollar cap.
  • Spouses (statewide): a New Mexico notary may not notarize a record in which the notary or the notary's spouse or domestic partner is a party or has a direct beneficial interest [11]. We didn't find a broader rule on other relatives on the pages we checked.
  • ID and journal (statewide): a notary may not act without identifying you as required by 14-14A-6 NMSA 1978, and must keep a journal that records the fee charged [11]. New Mexico's Revised Uniform Law on Notarial Acts has applied since January 1, 2022 [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. We didn't check which Roswell branches offer it. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: NMDOH Vital Records

  • In Roswell: NMDOH's Chaves County public health office, 1601 S. Garden Ave., Roswell, NM 88203, 575-624-6050, lists Vital Records (birth certificates) and says to call to verify hours. The office is open Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. (closed noon–1 p.m.). Appointments are preferred, but walk-ins are accepted as staffing allows [10].
  • Death certificates (statewide): NMDOH says death certificates must be ordered through its Santa Fe office, 2554 Camino Entrada, Santa Fe, NM 87505, 505-827-0121 [10].
  • Fees (statewide): the non-refundable search fee is $10 per certified birth certificate and $5 per certified death certificate, if the record is on file [10].
  • Who can order (statewide): New Mexico birth and death certificates are restricted. Access is limited to the registrant, immediate family (mother, father, sibling, child, grandchild, current spouse or grandparent) or someone with proof of a legal interest [10].
  • By mail: send the form or letter with a certified check or money order payable to "New Mexico Vital Records" in the exact amount to Post Office Box 26110, Santa Fe, NM 87502. Don't send cash [10].
  • Online or by phone (VitalChek): NMDOH doesn't take credit cards or online orders itself, but you can order through VitalChek, online or at 877-284-0963. With 3–5 business day processing, regular mail delivery costs $26.00 for a birth certificate or $21.00 for a death certificate, and UPS Next Day Air costs $44.50 or $39.50 [10].

Marriage licenses: Chaves County Clerk

  • Fee and payment: $55.00, cash, American Express, MasterCard, Visa or Discover. Cards carry a $2.00 service charge, which the county doesn't keep [9].
  • ID: proof of age and identity is required, normally a government-issued photo ID. Without one, bring an original or certified copy of a birth certificate plus another form of photo ID [9].
  • In person: both applicants must appear in person; if one can't, a District Judge must authorize the license. You can start the application online, then both applicants visit the Clerk's Office to finish it [9].
  • Rules: buy the license before the ceremony. It's valid for a ceremony anywhere in New Mexico, and the signed license must be returned to the Clerk's Office within 90 days of the ceremony. Applicants who are 16 or 17 need extra documents, including parental consent [9].
  • Copies: a certified copy of a marriage license is $2.50 per copy, and a duplicate marriage certificate is $5.00. Request certified copies through the county's Tyler Web Public Document Search and include a phone number; staff will call to process the request [9].

Recorded documents: Chaves County Clerk

  • Where and when: 1 St. Mary's Place Suite #110, Roswell, NM 88203, 575-624-6614. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Saturday and Sunday [7].
  • Recording fees: $25.00 per document, plus $25.00 for each additional block of ten or fewer entries beyond ten index entries (fees effective July 1, 2019, under 14-8-15 NMSA). Plats and surveys are $25.00 regardless of entry count [8].
  • Requirements: the document must be an original, notarized with proper acknowledgment, and should contain a legal description or a book-and-page or document number reference. For mail-back, a self-addressed, stamped envelope is preferred [8].
  • Copy fees: copies you make are $0.50, copies made by staff are $1.00, hard copies of surveys and plats are $3.00, and certification of recorded documents is $1.00 [8].

State ID cards: New Mexico MVD (statewide)

You must go to an MVD Field Office for a New Mexico ID card. You get a temporary ID while the card is processed and mailed [14]. MVD's base fee list shows $10 for a four-year ID and $18 for an eight-year ID, and says municipal offices may charge an additional fee that varies by location [14].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does printing cost at the Roswell library?

Black and white only, $0.25 per page [2]. Copies are also $0.25 per side [1].

Can I scan documents at the Roswell library?

Yes, on the public scanner near the front desk. Bring a USB drive [4].

How much is a marriage license in Chaves County?

$55.00, plus a $2.00 service charge if you pay by card [9].

Where can I apply for a passport in Roswell?

The State Department's locator lists the Roswell Main Post Office at 415 N Pennsylvania Ave [5].

Can I get a birth certificate in Roswell?

NMDOH lists birth certificates at its public health office at 1601 S. Garden Ave.; call 575-624-6050 to verify hours [10].
