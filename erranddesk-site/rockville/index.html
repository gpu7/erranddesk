Rockville, Maryland document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Rockville, Maryland, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: The Montgomery County Circuit Court is in Rockville [1]; a marriage license there is $55 [2]. Montgomery County libraries print or copy up to 15 black-and-white pages a day free [3]. Three Rockville post offices accept passport applications [4].
Who does what: Rockville is in Montgomery County [5][6]. The Clerk of the Montgomery County Circuit Court, at 50 Maryland Avenue in Rockville, handles marriage licenses, land records and court records [2][1].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Montgomery County Public Libraries: up to 15 black-and-white or 2 color pages a day are free to print or copy; after that it's $0.15 per black-and-white side and $1.00 per color side [3]. You can carry at most $24 on your print balance [7]. We didn't find a library fax service or price (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Rockville page lists 2 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [8]. The Census Geocoder places both (451 N Hungerford Dr and 891-I Rockville Pike) in Rockville city [9]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Rockville

  • The UPS Store: its Rockville page lists notarizing [8].
  • Library notary: we didn't find a Montgomery County Public Libraries notary service on the pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maryland's notary fees are set by state regulation (COMAR 01.02.08.02, effective Jan 22, 2024): at most $8 per notarial act, or $30 per remote notarial act. For extra copies of the same record signed at the same time, $4 per signature on each extra copy. Photocopies are at most $2 each, certifying a journal copy is $6, and travel is IRS mileage plus up to $5 [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Maryland Department of Health's Division of Vital Records charges $10 for a birth certificate and $10 for a death certificate, $12 for each additional death certificate in the same transaction and $12 for a marriage certificate. Internet orders add $13, and expedited shipping (internet only) adds $20. Local health departments' fees vary [13].
  • How (statewide): the Division's lobby serves customers by appointment only; you can also order by mail to P.O. Box 68760, Baltimore, MD 21215-0036, or online through VitalChek [14][15].

Marriage licenses (Montgomery County Circuit Court)

  • Fee: $55, by cash, Visa or Mastercard; no personal checks [2].
  • Where: License Department, North Tower, First Floor, Room 1300, 240-777-9460. Open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; marriage applications 8:30 a.m.–3:45 p.m. [2]. The License Department page gives 3:15 p.m. as the last appointment for new marriage licenses [16]; the two pages differ, so arrive early.
  • Copies: certified copies are $5.50; non-certified copies 50 cents. For licenses before 1990, contact the Maryland State Archives at 410-260-6400 [2]. Courthouse ceremonies are by appointment [2].

Court records, divorce decrees and land records

  • Court copies: 50 cents a page; certified copies 50 cents a page plus $5; triple seals $10 plus 50 cents a page. Central Files, North Tower, First Floor, Room 1100, 240-777-9501. Cards are accepted only in person [1].
  • Land records (statewide): the Maryland Judiciary's circuit court fee schedule, effective Sept 2, 2026, sets copies at 50 cents a page, plus $5 for each certification or $10 to exemplify [17]. Plat copies are 50 cents a page for 11 x 17 inches or smaller and $1 a page for larger sheets [17]. Montgomery County has no county land-records fee page that we found, so this statewide schedule applies (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maryland Secretary of State, 16 Francis Street, Wineland Building, Annapolis, 410-974-5521. Certifications and apostilles are $5 each, by mail or in person [18].
  • Walk-ins, the page conflicts: the same page says "WALK-INS NOT AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME" (scheduled appointments may change because of building construction) and also that the desk is open for walk-ins until 1 p.m., 9 a.m.–1 p.m. weekdays, with a limit of 10 documents per customer a day [18]. Call before you go.
  • Notarized documents (statewide): take them first to the Clerk of the Circuit Court in the county where the notary was commissioned [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Rockville page lists shredding [8].
  • Free events: we didn't find a City of Rockville or Montgomery County shred event on official pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Shredded paper doesn't go in Rockville curbside recycling [20].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where do Rockville residents get a marriage license?

At the Montgomery County Circuit Court, 50 Maryland Avenue, for $55 [2][1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Rockville?

At the Rockville Main, Pike Station and Twinbrook post offices [4].

Is library printing free?

Up to 15 black-and-white or 2 color pages a day [3].
