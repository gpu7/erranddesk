Norfolk, Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Norfolk, Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: Norfolk Public Library prints for 15 cents a page in black and white but doesn't offer fax [1][2]. The Norfolk Circuit Court Clerk accepts passport applications without an appointment and takes photos on site [3]. Certified copies from the clerk are $2.50 plus 50 cents a page [4]. Virginia notaries can charge up to $10 per act (statewide) [5].
An independent city, no county: Norfolk isn't part of any county. The Census Bureau lists "Norfolk city" [6] as its own county-level area [7], so the city's own Circuit Court Clerk handles marriage licenses, recordings and copies [4][8]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Norfolk Public Library

  • Printing: 15 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color, prepaid [1]. Color printers are at the Jordan-Newby Anchor Branch at Broad Creek, Lafayette and Larchmont [2].
  • Printing from your own device (pages differ): the library's printing page describes Princh printing from a phone, tablet or laptop, at the library or from home [10]; its policy page says printing over the wireless network isn't available [1].
  • Scanning: every branch has public scanners [2]. We didn't find a scanning price.
  • Fax: the library doesn't currently offer fax [2].
  • Copies: we didn't find a copier price on the library pages we checked (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Norfolk page lists 4 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. The Census Geocoder places 3 in Norfolk city; it couldn't match the 1570 Mall Drive address [9]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Norfolk

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Norfolk Public Library pages we checked (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Norfolk page lists notarizing [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth commissions Virginia notaries. The application or renewal fee is $45, nonrefundable [13]. The new notary then takes the oath before the clerk of the circuit court the commission was sent to, within 60 days [14][13]; the clerk's fee for qualifying a notary is $10 [15].
  • Fees (statewide): a Virginia notary may charge up to $10 for an acknowledgment, oath, affidavit or a true-copy certification, and up to $25 for the same acts on an electronic document. Travel costs can be added only if you agree and the notary goes somewhere other than their usual place of business [5].
  • Copy certification (statewide): unlike some states, Virginia law lists "certifying that a copy of a document is a true copy" among the acts a notary may charge for [5]. Some documents, such as vital records, still have to come from the office that holds them.
  • Electronic notaries (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth also commissions electronic notaries and has a search for current notaries [13].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Norfolk: the city's courts page says the clerk keeps marriage and divorce records [4]. We didn't find a city office that issues birth or death certificates; see the state details below.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): since July 1, 2026, the Virginia Department of Health charges $15 for each certified copy, or for each search when no record is found [19].
  • Who can order (statewide): only immediate family (mother, father, spouse, child, brother, sister and grandparents) with valid ID; a legal guardian with proof of custody can get a birth record [19].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): walk in Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–4 p.m. at 8701 Park Central Drive, Suite 100, Richmond. Drop-off requests for same-day pickup must be in by 2 p.m. Mail goes to VDH, Office of Vital Records, P.O. Box 1000, Richmond, VA 23218-1000. The customer care line is (804) 662-6200 [19].

Marriage licenses (Norfolk Circuit Court Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the minimum age to marry is 18 [20]. A license is good for 60 days from the day it's issued [21]. The clerk's statutory fee for issuing it is $10 [15]; the clerk's own fee list (below) may show a different total.
  • Norfolk: the clerk's marriage license page didn't load for us, so we haven't confirmed the local fee or payment rules (needs browser read, last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Ceremony: a Marriage Commissioner is available during business hours; the fee is $75, cash only, (757) 664-4175 [4].

Recorded documents and court copies (Norfolk Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in Virginia, deeds are recorded by the clerk of the circuit court, and the same statewide clerk fees apply [15].
  • Clerk copy fees (statewide): circuit court clerks charge $0.50 a page for copies not priced elsewhere in the law, and $2 to attach the court's seal and clerk's certificate (another $0.50 for a judge's certificate) [15] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Norfolk: 150 St. Paul's Boulevard, 7th Floor, 757-389-8942 [8]. Certified copies are $2.50 plus 50 cents a page. Request them online with the document request form (preferred) or at the office [4][22]. The Census Geocoder places the office in Norfolk city [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth's Authentication Division. Depending on the country, it issues a Great Seal or an Apostille. It authenticates documents from a Virginia notary, Virginia clerk of court or Virginia deputy state registrar; it doesn't notarize documents [23].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document (not per page). Several documents signed by the same official on the same date for the same country are $10.00 for the first and $5.00 for each additional one [24].
  • How to send (statewide): USPS mail goes to Secretary of the Commonwealth, Authentication Division, 1111 East Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219; FedEx, UPS and DHL go to 400 East Cary Street, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone 804-692-0114 [23][24]. In-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Thursday; appointments for the next week open Fridays at 10 a.m., up to 10 documents per customer or family [24].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [25]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Norfolk Public Library?

15 cents a page for black and white and 25 cents for color [1].

Can I fax at Norfolk Public Library?

No, the library doesn't currently offer fax [2].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the Norfolk Circuit Court Clerk?

No, it's first come, first served [3].

How much is a certified copy from the Norfolk clerk?

$2.50 plus 50 cents a page [4].

Where do I get a Virginia birth certificate?

From the Virginia Department of Health, $15 per copy (statewide) [19].
