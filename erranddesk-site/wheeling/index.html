Wheeling, West Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Wheeling, West Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with Ohio County, the Ohio County Clerk or Circuit Clerk, Ohio County Public Library, the West Virginia Secretary of State, the West Virginia Health Statistics Center, the State of West Virginia, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: Ohio County Public Library notarizes free with no appointment, and copies are 10 cents black and white, 25 cents color [1]. The Ohio County Circuit Clerk's Office accepts passport applications weekdays 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. with no appointment required [2]. The Ohio County Clerk lists a $62 marriage license, $20 off with a completed marriage course [3].
Two counties, two clerks: Wheeling spans Ohio County and Marshall County [4][5]. This page covers Ohio County offices; if you live in the Marshall County part, check with Marshall County. In Ohio County the county clerk handles marriage licenses, vital records and recording [3][6]; the circuit clerk handles court records and is also a passport acceptance facility [2].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Ohio County Public Library (52 16th Street)

  • Copies: 10 cents per black-and-white page, 25 cents per color page, at the circulation desk [1].
  • Printing: computer prints are 10 cents per page; a WiFi printer at the reference desk connects to phones, tablets and laptops [1].
  • Fax and scanning: we didn't find fax or scanning prices on the library pages we checked (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Hours and phone: Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–9 p.m., Friday 10 a.m.–5 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; 304-232-0244 [7]. The Census Geocoder places the library in Wheeling city [8].
  • The UPS Store: its Wheeling page lists 1 location, 47 Washington Ave, (304) 242-9299, and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. The Census Geocoder places it in Wheeling city [8]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

Notary in Wheeling

  • Library (free): no appointment needed and no charge, except 10 cents a page for any copies or printouts. All signers and witnesses must be present with current photo ID; don't sign beforehand. You may want to call ahead, 304-232-0244 [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Wheeling page lists notarizing [9]. Notary rules vary by state [10].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State commissions notaries. The application or recommission fee is $52, waived for state and local government notaries; a commission is good for five years [11][12]. No fee is charged to add e-notarization authorization [11].
  • Fees (statewide): under W. Va. Code §39-4-30, a notary may charge up to $10.00 for each signature notarized, up to $10.00 per 8½ × 11 page for certifying a copy (the notary keeps a copy on file), and up to $10.00 for any other notarial act [13].
  • Copy certification (statewide): if no other office can certify a document, a West Virginia notary can certify a true copy after comparing it with the original and keeping a copy [14][13]. Vital records, court orders and recorded deeds still come from the office that holds them [14].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Ohio County Clerk: vitals line (304) 234-3729; office at 1500 Chapline Street, Rm 205, open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:45 p.m. [6]. We didn't confirm a county certificate fee on a page we fetched; state law sets $5 for a county clerk's certified copy [17].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $12 per certified copy from the state Vital Registration Office; the fee includes a nonrefundable search. If the record isn't found, staff search the year given plus the year before and after; more searches are $12 per three-year period [18].
  • In person (statewide): same-day walk-in service at 350 Capitol Street, Room 165, Charleston, WV 25301-3701. Cash, check and money order are accepted; cards at the walk-up counter add a 4% surcharge. Expect a wait of at least 15 minutes, longer from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. [18].
  • By mail or online (statewide): mail the form with a color copy of your ID and a check or money order (no cards by mail). Online orders go through third-party vendors such as VitalChek; the state gets $12 a copy whatever the vendor charges [18].
  • County clerk copies (statewide law): W. Va. Code lets the county clerk charge $5 for a certified copy of a birth certificate, death certificate or marriage license it holds [17]. Check with the county clerk which records it has.
  • Divorce records (statewide): a divorce decree comes from the circuit clerk of the court that issued it, not from Vital Registration [14].

Marriage licenses (Ohio County Clerk)

  • Ohio County fee: $62.00, cash or credit card, no checks; a completed marriage course gets a $20.00 discount [3]. State code sets $55, or $35 with premarital education [17]; we show both and haven't confirmed what accounts for the difference. Pay the county's posted fee.
  • How: both applicants appear together; the license must be used in West Virginia within 60 days [3]. The last couple is taken at 4:00 p.m. [6].
  • State law (statewide): every West Virginia marriage needs a license from a county clerk (the clerk of the county commission) [19]. The age of consent is 18; 16- and 17-year-olds need written consent under the statute's conditions [20]. The statutory fee is $55, or $35 if you show a premarital education course completion certificate [17].

Recorded documents (Ohio County Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in West Virginia, the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) records deeds; the circuit clerk handles court records [17][14]. Recording a deed or trust deed is $30; most other documents are $10; documents over five pages add $1 per extra page. Copies are $1.50 (plus $1 per page over two), and attaching the clerk's seal is $1 (§59-1-10) [17].
  • Photocopies: the clerk prices copies per document on a sliding scale by page count, for example 1–2 pages $1.50, 3 pages $2.50, 5 pages $4.50, 10 pages $9.50 and 22 pages $21.50; see the full table [21].
  • Ohio County: recording costs come from the Recording Calculator in the clerk's online records search [22]. Recording stops at 4:30 p.m. [6]. We didn't find a separate local per-document surcharge on the clerk's Recording Costs page (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Court records and divorce decrees (Ohio County Circuit Clerk)

  • Circuit clerk fees (statewide): W. Va. Code §59-1-11 sets $1 a page for a copy the circuit clerk makes to go out of the office, $135 to file for divorce, separate maintenance or annulment, and $200 for most other civil filings [23] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Ohio County: 1500 Chapline Street (4th Floor), front office 304-234-3611; the county lists office hours Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. [24]. The State Department locator lists passport hours of 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [2]; we show both. The clerk's site says payments must be cash or money order, with no Visa, Mastercard or debit cards (read Nov 3, 2026) [25]. We didn't find copy or certified-copy prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State, at the State Capitol Building in Charleston. It issues Apostille Certificates for countries in the Hague Apostille Convention and Authentication Certificates for other countries [14].
  • What it certifies (statewide): only documents attested by a proper West Virginia officer, such as a notary public, county clerk or the State Registrar. Recorded deeds and marriage certificates need the county clerk's certified copy; court orders and divorce decrees need the circuit clerk's [14].
  • Fee and hours (statewide): $10 per certification; if several documents are certified by the same official, the first is $10 and each additional one is $5. In-person service runs 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday–Friday [14].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Wheeling page lists shredding [9]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.
  • Free events: we found no upcoming free shred event in Wheeling as of Nov 3, 2026.

Questions

Is there a free notary in Wheeling?

Yes. Ohio County Public Library notarizes free, no appointment needed [1].

Can I get a passport at the Ohio County courthouse?

Yes. The locator lists the Ohio County Circuit Clerk's Office, 1500 Chapline St, 4th floor, weekdays 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., no appointment required [2].

How much is a marriage license in Ohio County?

The county lists $62, with $20 off for a completed marriage course [3].

How much are copies at the Wheeling library?

10 cents black and white, 25 cents color [1].
