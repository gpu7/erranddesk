Fort Collins document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Fort Collins, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Short version: Poudre Libraries give each cardholder 200 free print and copy credits a month. Scanning is free, and outgoing fax within the U.S. is free, no card needed [1]. The Larimer County Clerk and Recorder notarizes for $10 per document [10] and accepts passport applications by appointment [5]. Larimer County Vital Records issues birth certificates for any Colorado birth, at $25 for the first copy [13].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax: Poudre Libraries

ServiceWhat Poudre River Public Library District says
Print and copyFree. Every cardholder gets 200 copy/print credits per month. Credits reset on the first of the month and don't roll over. Black and white is 1 credit per page, color is 5 credits, and tabloid counts double. Letter, legal and tabloid paper are available. The library doesn't sell extra credits or paid prints [1][2].
Mobile printingSend files from a phone, tablet or laptop with your library card number, and release them at any library location. Jobs are deleted after 3 days if not released [1].
ScanScan to USB or email on the library Xerox machines. ScanEZ stations at Old Town and Harmony libraries add scan-to-cloud and photo restoration [1].
FaxFree nationwide outgoing fax on the library Xerox machines, "available without a library card." The public machines can't receive incoming faxes [1].

Locations listed by the district [3]: Old Town Library, 201 Peterson Street (80524); Harmony Library, 4616 S. Shields Street (80526); Council Tree Library, 2733 Council Tree Avenue #200 (80525). Library phone: 970-221-6740 [1]. Large-format and bulk jobs aren't offered, so the library suggests a local print and copy center for those [1]. Compare printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Fort Collins

  • Larimer County Clerk and Recorder: "Our office does provide notary services for $10 per document" at 200 West Oak Street, 1st floor, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. [10]. The Recording page says notary service is onsite with "No appointment necessary" [7].
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [15].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [16]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 2601 S Lemay Ste 7, Fort Collins, CO 80525 says a commissioned notary public is available and lists notary hours as "Available 7 Days." Phone (970) 226-4008. Its page doesn't post a price [17]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [15].
  • Poudre Libraries: we didn't find a notary service on the library district's pages as of Oct 28, 2026.

  • Larimer County Clerk and Recorder: the office is a designated Passport Acceptance Facility in the Larimer County Administrative Services building at 200 West Oak Street. It takes up to 3 applicants per appointment and offers photo services [5]. It "does not handle adult renewals," and the Department of State fee needs its own check or money order [7]. The county's fees for a first-time adult application are a $35 execution fee and a $15 photo fee if you use their photo. Pay by cash, check or card, and card payments carry a 3.5% processing fee [6].
  • CSU Passport Office: it's in Laurel Hall, 700 Oval Drive. Appointments run Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 8:30 a.m.–12 p.m. and 1–3 p.m. Drop-ins are every Wednesday during the same hours. It's closed on university holidays [8].
  • Post Office: the locator says to schedule at www.usps.com/scheduler [4]. USPS fees are national: a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [9].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Larimer County Vital Records

  • Where: 1525 Blue Spruce Drive, Fort Collins, CO 80524, (970) 498-6710. Open Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–12 p.m. and 12:30–4:15 p.m., closed county holidays. In-person requests are by appointment only. Call 970-498-6710 [12]. The county says the Fort Collins location "is the only vital records office in Larimer and Jackson Counties," and its Loveland and Estes Park offices don't offer vital records [13].
  • Which births and deaths: birth certificates for any birth in Colorado from 1900 to the present. You don't need to order from the county of birth. Death certificates cover deaths in Colorado from 1975 to the present [12][13].
  • Fees (effective Jan 1, 2026): $25 for the first certified birth or death certificate, and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [13][14]. VitalChek online orders add a $7.50 surcharge [13].
  • Timing: appointments typically last 15 to 20 minutes, and the office is currently processing applications within 2 to 5 business days, not counting shipping [12].

Recorded documents: Larimer County Clerk and Recorder

  • Recording (flat fees effective July 1, 2025): $43 per document. Military discharge (DD-214) and death certificates are $0 [10].
  • Copies: $0.25 per page, and certified copies add $1.00 per document. Map copies are $2.00 per page [10].
  • Certified copies of recorded documents, including marriage and civil union licenses, can be requested by mail or phone. Allow 72 hours for processing. The office says there is an additional fee to use a credit card in the office [11].
  • Recording checks or money orders should be payable to Larimer County Clerk. In-person recording is at the Fort Collins office only [7][10].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

Post offices, including those in Fort Collins, sell USPS money orders. Fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. The Larimer County Clerk and Recorder takes money orders for recording fees [10]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

We didn't find an upcoming free shred event published by the City of Fort Collins or Larimer County as of Oct 28, 2026. If you shred at home, the City of Fort Collins says to put shredded paper loose in the office paper bin at a recycling drop-off center, such as the Timberline Recycling Center. Don't put it in your curbside recycling bin [19]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Is printing free at the Fort Collins library?

Yes, for cardholders. Poudre Libraries give each cardholder 200 free copy/print credits a month. Black and white uses 1 credit per page and color uses 5 [1].

Can I send a fax at the library in Fort Collins?

Yes. Nationwide outgoing fax on the library Xerox machines is free and doesn't need a library card. Incoming faxes can't be received [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Fort Collins?

The State Department's locator lists the Larimer County Clerk and Recorder at 200 W Oak St., the Fort Collins Main Post Office at 301 E. Boardwalk Dr., and Colorado State University's passport office at 700 Oval Drive [4].

How much is a birth certificate in Larimer County?

$25 for the first copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time, effective Jan 1, 2026. VitalChek orders add $7.50 [13].
