Portland, Maine document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Portland, Maine, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. This page is for Portland, Maine — not Portland, Oregon.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Portland, Portland Public Library, Cumberland County Registry of Deeds, the State of Maine, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Portland (post office and Cumberland County Probate) [1]. City Clerk staff provide notary and dedimus services in Room 203 (call for dedimus appointments) [2]. Certified vital copies are $15 for the first copy and $6 for each additional copy of the same record [3][4][5]. Maine does not set a statutory dollar cap on ordinary notary customer fees [6].
Counties in Maine (statewide): Maine has 16 counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds (not by town clerks). Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the Maine CDC Division of Vital Records [7][8][9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Portland Public Library (Downtown Branch; Main Entrance on Congress St.): public computers, Wi‑Fi, printing and scanning are available at the Downtown Branch. We didn't find a published per-page black-and-white or color print price on the computers page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) — ask at the desk [10][11].
  • FedEx Office Print & Ship Center: FedEx's Portland page lists 7 locations, including FedEx Office at 127 Marginal Way (self-service copying, faxing, scanning, printing, passport photos and shredding) and another FedEx Office at 50 Monument Sq [12][13].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Portland stores — 110 Marginal Way ((207) 761-0173) and 91 Auburn St ((207) 878-3393). Store pages advertise printing, faxing, shredding and notarizing [14][15][16].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Portland, Maine

  • City Clerk (Room 203): 389 Congress Street. The Clerk lists notary and dedimus services; call 207-874-8610 to make an appointment for dedimus. Walk-ins are open for other clerk services in Room 203. We didn't find a published notary customer fee on the Clerk page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • The UPS Store: both Portland stores advertise notary; fees vary by location. Maine's Course of Study says the state does not set statutory fees for notarial acts — any fee must be disclosed and agreed to before the act [15][16][17][6].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maine charges $50 for a notary public commission that lasts seven years [18]. The Secretary of State's Course of Study says Maine does not set statutory fees for notarial acts; any fee must be disclosed in writing and agreed to before the act [6]. Remote and electronic notarization need separate SOS approval [18].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Portland City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: Room 203, 389 Congress Street. City Hall hours Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Clerk phone 207-874-8610. Marriage licenses and ceremonies are by appointment; other clerk services take walk-ins in Room 203 [2][22].
  • Fees: $15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional certified copy of the same record ordered at the same time (matches 30-A M.R.S. § 2652). Mail or email requests need government photo ID and a self-addressed stamped envelope; checks payable to "City of Portland" [3][4][5].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): 30-A M.R.S. § 2652 sets municipal clerk fees at $15 for the first certified copy of a birth, marriage, or death record and $6 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [5].
  • Maine CDC Division of Vital Records (statewide): 220 Capitol Street, Augusta. Certificate fee $15.00 per certificate, $6.00 for each additional copy ordered at the same time; VitalChek online orders add a service fee [7].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified vital record from a clerk or Maine CDC, then request a special certification (apostille or authentication) from the Secretary of State [23][7].

Marriage licenses (Portland City Clerk)

  • Where: Room 203 by appointment only — call 207-874-8610 Monday–Friday 9:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Late arrivals (more than 15 minutes) may need to reschedule [22].
  • Fee: $40 marriage license (Intentions of Marriage Form and accompanying documents). City Clerk office ceremony $150; State of Maine Room package during business hours $300 [22].
  • Waiting period: none; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in Maine (statewide) [5][24].
  • State rules (statewide): file marriage intentions with a municipal clerk (or the Department). The clerk fee is $40 for recording intentions and issuing a license ($20 each when two licenses are required). There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in Maine. Both parties must usually sign; you must be 18 or older [5][24].

Land records (Cumberland County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 27 Northport Dr., Portland, ME 04103. Copying and recording Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Phone 207-871-8389. Portland is in Cumberland County [25][8].
  • Recording: private submitters $40 flat ($35 + $5 surcharge); state/municipal $25; plans $50 ($45 + $5). Matches the statewide statute total for private instruments [26][9][27].
  • Recording fees (statewide): 33 M.R.S. § 751 sets a flat $35 per instrument for most private submitters ($25 when the State or a municipality records). 33 M.R.S. § 752 adds a $5 records-preservation surcharge for most non-government recordings, so the usual private total is $40 per instrument [9][27]. The Maine Registry of Deeds Association posts the same private $40 ($35 + $5) and $25 for state/municipal submitters; it lists plans at $50 per sheet [28]. § 751 lists plans at $45 — association and statute figures both cited; confirm with the registry [9][28].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maine Secretary of State, Bureau of Corporations, Elections & Commissions. Mail: 101 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0101. Courier: 6 E. Chestnut Street, Augusta. Phone 207-624-7752 [23].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per special certification (apostille or authentication), payable to the Secretary of State. Processing is typically 10–15 business days; call for an appointment when more than 5 documents need certification [23].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, documents, and payment; VitalChek orders can be forwarded to this office on request [23].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [29]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • FedEx Office: 127 Marginal Way lists shredding among in-store services; confirm price before you go [12].
  • The UPS Store: both Portland store pages advertise shredding [15][16].
  • We didn't find a City of Portland household paper-shredding event schedule on the Clerk pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026).

Questions

Where can I get a notary in Portland, Maine?

City Clerk, Room 203, 389 Congress Street — notary and dedimus listed; call 207-874-8610 for dedimus appointments [2]. The UPS Store at 110 Marginal Way and 91 Auburn St also advertise notary [15][16].

How much is a birth certificate in Portland, Maine?

$15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional copy of the same record at the City Clerk [3][4]. Maine CDC statewide uses the same $15 / $6 schedule [7][5].

How much can a Maine notary charge?

Maine does not set statutory fees for notarial acts; any fee must be disclosed in writing and agreed to before the act. Commission fee is $50 for seven years [6][18].

Can I get an apostille on a Portland birth certificate?

Get a certified copy from the City Clerk or Maine CDC, then request a special certification from the Secretary of State in Augusta. Apostille is $10 per document; typically 10–15 business days [23].

Where do I record a deed for Portland, Maine property?

Cumberland County Registry of Deeds, 27 Northport Dr., Portland. Private recording is $40 flat ($35 + $5 surcharge) [25][26][9].

Where can I apply for a passport in Portland?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 2 Maine facilities with Portland addresses [1]. The Census Geocoder places both inside Portland city [19].
