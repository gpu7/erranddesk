Milford, Delaware document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Milford, Delaware, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Milford, Kent County, Sussex County, the Delaware Courts, the Milford Public Library, the Delaware Solid Waste Authority, the Delaware Secretary of State, the Delaware Division of Public Health, USPS or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the Milford Public Library prints and copies for 25¢ a page in black and white, sends faxes for $1 a page and offers notary service for $5 a document by appointment [1][2]. The Milford Main Post Office on Causey Avenue accepts passport applications [3][4].
Two counties: Milford city lies partly in Kent County and partly in Sussex County [5][6]. The Census Geocoder places the library, the post office and the transfer station on the Sussex side [4]. Each county runs its own Clerk of the Peace (Marriage Bureau) and Recorder of Deeds, and the fees differ (shown side by side below). Neither office is in Milford: Kent's are in Dover and Sussex's in Georgetown [7][8][9]. Birth and death certificates come from the state Office of Vital Statistics [10].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Milford Public Library, 11 S E Front Street, (302) 422-8996: printing is $0.25 per black-and-white page and $0.50 per color page; copies are $0.25 (black and white) and $0.50 (color) a page; scanning to email is free; outgoing faxes are $1.00 a page, with international faxes costing more. The fax machine is send-only [1]. The Census Geocoder places the library inside Milford city [4].
  • Not one statewide price: Delaware's public libraries each post their own print and fax prices, so these prices don't carry over to other Delaware cities.
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find a The UPS Store location listed for Milford (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Milford

  • Milford Public Library: $5.00 per document, by appointment; call (302) 422-8996 Monday–Friday to make sure the notary is available. Bring valid photo ID and the unsigned document. A notarization can take up to 30 minutes per document [2].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Delaware law caps a notary's fee at $5 per notarial act on a paper record and $25 per act on an electronic record, and a notary may waive the fee [11][12]. The Secretary of State's notary FAQ says Delaware law does not address travel fees [12].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Office of Vital Statistics charges $25 per certified birth certificate and $25 per certified death certificate; if the record isn't found, the fee is kept for the search [15][16]. Online orders through GoCertificates or VitalChek add those companies' own fees [10].
  • In person (statewide): three offices, open weekdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m. except holidays: New Castle County at 258 Chapman Road, Newark, 302-283-7130; Kent County (the central office) in Dover; and Sussex County at 546 S. Bedford St., Georgetown [10].
  • The sources conflict on two details: the office web page lists the Kent County central office at 805 River Road, Dover (James Williams State Service Center), 302-744-4549, and Georgetown at 302-515-3190 [10]; the state's application forms list the Dover office at the Jesse S. Cooper Building, 417 Federal Street, and Georgetown at 302-856-5495 [15]. Mail requests go to the Dover address printed on the form [10]. Call before you go.

Marriage licenses: Kent vs Sussex

  • The law (statewide): the Clerk of the Peace in each county issues marriage licenses. You need the license at least 24 hours before the ceremony, and it's good for 30 days. The Delaware Code says clerks issue licenses for $10 each [17]. Both counties list higher totals today, shown below.
  • Kent County (in Dover): $70 total for Delaware residents and $120 for nonresidents; cash only; licenses issued 8 a.m.–4:15 p.m. Monday–Friday; 302-744-2346 [7].
  • Sussex County (in Georgetown): $50 for Delaware residents and $100 for nonresidents, plus a $20 recording fee on every license; certified copies $25; cash and money orders. By appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., 2nd Floor, Room 226, 2 The Circle, Georgetown; (302) 853-1717 [8][18].
  • Which county's office: Delaware law says the clerks of the peace of the counties issue marriage licenses (13 Del. C. §109) to people marrying in Delaware (§107) [17]. We didn't find an official page that limits Milford residents to the Kent or the Sussex office (last checked Nov 4, 2026); see the Kent and Sussex marriage bureau pages. Kent's own page says to apply at least 24 hours before the ceremony “regardless of residency” [7].

Court records and land records

  • Superior Court copies (statewide fee schedule): a certified copy is $15 for up to 3 pages and an exemplified copy $25; extra copies are at most $2 a page, and fax copies at most $8 a page [19].
  • Sussex County Prothonotary (in Georgetown, not Milford): Room 214, 2nd Floor, Sussex County Courthouse, 1 The Circle, Suite 2, Georgetown, open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; main number (302) 855-7055 [20].
  • Kent County deeds (in Dover): certified copies $7 a page, office copies $1 a page and mailed copies $2 a page [21]; online viewing is free, and downloads cost $2 a page or $75 a month [22].
  • Sussex County deeds (in Georgetown): certified copies $5 a page plus a $5 certify fee, office copies $0.40 a page, mailed copies $0.40 a page plus postage, online access $50 a month [23]. Office: 2 The Circle, Georgetown, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday; (302) 855-7785 [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Delaware Division of Corporations (Secretary of State), John G. Townsend Building, 401 Federal Street, Suite 4, Dover, for documents signed by a Delaware notary or public official. Mail requests are typically processed the same business day; in-person drop-off is by appointment, 302-739-3077 [24].
  • Fee (statewide): for personal use, $30 for any number of documents presented together, with no expedite fees; commercial documents are $30 each, plus $50 same-day or $40 24-hour [24][25].
  • Not accepted: documents notarized remotely or electronically. The Division doesn't notarize [24].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Delaware Solid Waste Authority (statewide program): residents can bring up to two boxes of paper for free shredding [27].
  • Milford event (past): DSWA's 2026 schedule listed a Saturday collection event with document shredding at the Milford Transfer Station, 1170 S. DuPont Boulevard, on July 18, 8 a.m.–2 p.m. It has passed. The Census Geocoder places the transfer station inside Milford city [28][4].
  • Next listed event: Saturday, Nov 14, 2026, 8 a.m.–2 p.m., at the Newark Recycling Center, 470 Corporate Blvd, outside Milford. DSWA says events can change [28].
