East Providence, Rhode Island document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in East Providence, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of East Providence, East Providence Public Library, the State of Rhode Island, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in East Providence, a post office [1]. The library has a notary on staff (call first), and City Hall has notaries too [2]. Library prints are 15¢ a page and faxes are $1.75 for the first page [3][4]. Birth certificates are $22 walk-in at City Hall [5].
No county clerk in Rhode Island (statewide): Rhode Island has no county government [6]. Its counties were abolished as governmental units and survive only as geographic areas [7]. Land records are recorded in the city or town where the property is, and city and town clerks issue birth, death and marriage copies.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • East Providence Public Library: printing is 15¢ a page black and white and 50¢ color, up to 50 pages a day; mobile printing is available [3]. Fax is $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page to U.S. numbers, at Weaver Library (41 Grove Ave.) and the Riverside Branch. Pay by debit or credit card or a prepaid card from the circulation desk [4].
  • FedEx: 2 East Providence locations: FedEx Authorized ShipCenter E P Mail & Freight at 105 Waterman Ave. and FedEx at Walgreens at 655 Warren Ave. [8]. We didn't find a FedEx Office print center in East Providence.
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find an East Providence store in The UPS Store's Rhode Island list [9].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in East Providence

  • East Providence Public Library: a notary is on staff; call 401-434-2453 to confirm availability. We didn't find a library notary fee (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • City Hall: the library says notaries are available in the City Clerk and Assessment offices. We didn't find a City Hall notary fee (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • Friday City Hall hours (sources differ): the library's notary page lists City Hall notary hours as Monday–Wednesday 8 a.m.–4 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m.–6 p.m. and Friday 8–11 a.m. [2]. The City Clerk's page lists operating hours as Monday–Wednesday 8 a.m.–4 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m.–6 p.m. and Friday 8 a.m.–1 p.m. [10]. Ask which applies before you go.
  • Notary fees (statewide): a Rhode Island notary may charge up to $25 per document or notarization, including remote online notarization, and must tell you the fee before notarizing [11][12]. That cap is higher than in most nearby states, so ask first.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

East Providence City Clerk (City Hall, 1st floor, 145 Taunton Ave.)

  • Birth, death and marriage copies: $22 walk-in or $25 by mail for the first copy, $18 for each extra copy. Cash, check or money order, payable to the City of East Providence. Bring government photo ID, or 2 documents with your name and current address. By mail, send the application, a copy of your ID, a check and a self-addressed stamped envelope [5].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Any city or town hall (statewide): births from 1960 on, and deaths and marriages from 2022 on, can be ordered at any Rhode Island city or town hall. Older records come from the city or town where the event happened or where you lived [16].
  • Fees (statewide): $22 for the first copy in person and $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy of the same record bought at the same time [17].
  • State office (statewide): RIDOH Center for Vital Records, Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd., Cranston, weekdays 7:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Mail orders take 4–6 weeks; rush orders cost $7 more [17].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): order the copy from the state office, not a city hall. RIDOH says vital records issued by a city or town can't be apostilled [16].

Marriage licenses (City Clerk)

  • Hours: Monday–Wednesday 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m., Thursday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m., Friday 8:30 a.m.–noon. Both of you must be present [18].
  • Fee and documents: $24 by cash, check or money order. Bring a certified birth certificate (hospital certificates aren't accepted), government photo ID, and proof of residence if it isn't on your ID [18].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at a city or town clerk. Apply where either of you lives if one of you lives in Rhode Island; if neither does, apply where the ceremony will be. RIDOH lists the fee as $24, and the license is good for 3 months [19][20].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): R.I. Gen. Laws § 15-2-9 sets the clerk's license fee at $14.00 [21], while RIDOH lists $24 [20]. We didn't find which law adds the other $10 (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Clerks charge the $24.

Land records (City Clerk)

  • Recording times: Monday–Wednesday 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m., Friday 8 a.m.–12:30 p.m. [22].
  • City fee list (includes the $4 RI Historical Records Trust fee): warranty or quitclaim deed $84 and mortgage $64, plus $1 per extra page [22].
  • Copy fees (sources differ): the city's page lists copies at $1 a page and certified copies at $3.50 for the first page and $1.50 for each additional page; the page was last updated April 3, 2024 [22]. State law sets copies at $1.50 a page and $3 to certify (statewide) [23]. Ask the clerk which applies.
  • Recording fees (statewide law): R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-13-7 sets a base fee by document type, such as $80 for a deed and $60 for a mortgage, plus $1 for each extra page [24]. A $4 Historical Records Trust charge is added to each recorded document [25]. That's why clerks post $84 and $64.
  • Copies (statewide law): § 34-13-9 sets copies of recorded documents at $1.50 a page and $3.00 to certify [23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Rhode Island Department of State, Business Services Division, 148 West River Street, Providence. Bring or mail the document with a request form; checks and money orders go to the RI Department of State [26].
  • Fee (statewide): $5 per document [26]. Ordering online adds a $0.75 enhanced access fee per document; you still mail in the original [27]. For related documents in one matter filed together, the total is capped at $150 [28].
  • Returns (statewide): orders come back by USPS first-class mail unless you include a prepaid DHL, FedEx or UPS label [26].
  • Vital records (statewide): only state-issued copies can be apostilled [16]. You can ask RIDOH to send the record directly to the Department of State [26].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [29]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past event: the city and the library listed a free paper shredding event for East Providence residents at Weaver Library, 41 Grove Ave. (side parking lot), 9 a.m.–noon, with a two-file-box maximum [30]. The library's calendar dates it Saturday, October 10, 2026 [31]. It has passed.
  • Upcoming events: we didn't find an upcoming official shredding event in East Providence (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at the East Providence Public Library?

15¢ a page black and white and 50¢ color, up to 50 pages a day [3].

Where can I get a notary in East Providence?

The library has one on staff (call 401-434-2453 first), and City Hall has notaries in the City Clerk and Assessment offices [2].

How much is a birth certificate in East Providence?

$22 walk-in or $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy [5].

How much can a Rhode Island notary charge?

Up to $25 per document or notarization, disclosed in advance (statewide) [11].

Where can I apply for a passport in East Providence?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 1 Rhode Island facility with an East Providence address [1]. The Census Geocoder places it inside East Providence city [13].
