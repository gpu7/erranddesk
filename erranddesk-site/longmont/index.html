Longmont, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport, get certified copies and shred documents in Longmont, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Longmont, the Longmont Public Library, Boulder County, USPS, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Longmont: the Longmont Public Library is run by the City of Longmont at 355 Emery St. [2]. Longmont is served by Boulder County offices: Boulder County Public Health has a birth and death certificate office at 515 Coffman St., Second Floor, Longmont [9], and the Boulder County Clerk & Recorder has a 24-hour recording drop box at 529 Coffman St., Longmont, though recording itself is processed in Boulder [11][13]. For passports, we used only the facility the State Department locator lists with a Longmont, Colorado address [7].
Short version: The library prints 10 free black-and-white pages per user per week, and its scan-to-fax or email service has no charge [1]. The Longmont Post Office at 201 Coffman St. accepts passport applications by appointment [7]. Longmont residents can shred up to three file boxes free at the city's monthly event; the next one listed is Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026 [15].

Notary in Longmont

  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [5].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [6]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 1716 Main Street Ste A, Longmont, CO 80501 says it has "a commissioned notary public available," that the store is "Open 7 Days," and that you can make an appointment online or stop by. Phone (303) 702-0805. Its page doesn't post a price [4]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [5].
  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Longmont Public Library pages we checked [1][2].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Boulder County Public Health, Longmont office

  • In person in Longmont: 515 Coffman St., Second Floor, Longmont, CO 80501, 303-678-6175. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., closed for lunch noon–1 p.m. In-person orders are processed "within about 15 – 20 minutes." The other office is at 3450 Broadway, Boulder [9].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 for one copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. If you pay by credit card, there's an extra fee of $.75 plus 2.25% of the total [9][10].
  • Payment: cash, check or money order, or Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express [9].
  • Which records: certified birth records for people born in Colorado from 1904 to the present [9]. Death certificates go to eligible relatives and representatives with proof of relationship [10].
  • Election Day: Boulder County government offices, except the Elections Division, are closed Nov. 3, 2026 [9].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Boulder County Clerk & Recorder

  • Longmont drop box: a stand-alone 24-hour box outside the Motor Vehicle entrance at 529 Coffman St., Longmont. Mark the folder or envelope "Recording" and include your contact information [11]. Documents dropped off in Longmont go by interoffice mail to Boulder, which "may add 1-2 business days to the recording date." Make checks payable to Boulder County Clerk & Recorder [13].
  • Recording Division: 1750 33rd St., Suite 201, Boulder, 303-413-7770. In-person service Monday–Thursday, 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Fridays. "You can only get marriage licenses online" [11].
  • Fees (effective July 1, 2025): $43 to record each document. Recording a death certificate is free. Marriage or civil union license: $30. Certified copy of a marriage license: $1.25. Certifying a copy of a recorded document: $1. Non-digital copies: $0.25 per page [11].
  • Copies: watermarked copies are free to view and download through the county's Public Search portal. A military record or DD 214 has no charge for 1–3 certified copies per request [12].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. Boulder County Public Health accepts money orders for certificates [9]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding: City of Longmont free events

  • Where and when: Green Girl Recycling, 1801 Boston Avenue, monthly from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. [14]. Remaining 2026 dates: Saturday, Nov. 7 and Saturday, Dec. 5. The page also lists 2027 dates, starting Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027 [14].
  • Limits: up to three standard-sized file boxes of personal documents [14][15].
  • Who can come: City of Longmont residents only. Bring a current City of Longmont utility bill and photo ID. "Businesses and non-residents are not eligible." Others can contact Green Girl Recycling to schedule shredding (fees apply) [15].

City of Longmont Help & Information Line: 303-776-6050 [14]. For paid per-pound shredding elsewhere, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Can I send a fax at the Longmont library?

Yes. The library's scan-to-fax or email service has no charge. You need a library card to use its computers and printing [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Longmont, Colorado?

The State Department locator lists the Longmont Post Office, 201 Coffman Street, by appointment through usps.com/scheduler [7].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Longmont?

Boulder County Public Health, 515 Coffman St., Second Floor. The first copy is $25 and in-person orders take about 15–20 minutes [9].

When is the next free shred day in Longmont?

Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, 9 a.m.–1 p.m., at Green Girl Recycling, for Longmont residents with a utility bill and photo ID [15].
