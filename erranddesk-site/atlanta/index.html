Atlanta, Georgia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Atlanta, Georgia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: the Fulton County Library System charges $0.10 a page for black-and-white copies and prints, and doesn't offer fax or scanning [1]. The Fulton County Board of Health sells birth and death certificates for $25, with $5 for extra copies [2]. A Fulton County marriage license is $68.50, or $28.50 with a premarital education certificate [3]. The Fulton County Clerk takes passport applications for a $35 fee, and no longer accepts cards for it [4].
Two counties: the Census places Atlanta city in both Fulton County and DeKalb County [5]. Most offices on this page are Fulton County offices in downtown Atlanta. Georgia lets residents get a marriage license in any county [6], and the state says any county vital records office offers same-day service [7], so DeKalb residents can use them too. We list the DeKalb offices where we found them.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Fulton County Library System

  • Prices: copies and prints are $0.10 a page for black and white and $0.50 a page for color [1][8]. At Central Library, you can pay for prints and photocopies by cash or credit card [9].
  • Fax and scanning: the library's FAQ says it doesn't currently offer faxing or document scanning [1].
  • Card: you print with a library card or a courtesy card from the Patron Services desk [8]. Central Library has 94 public computers; a library card gets one hour and a guest pass 30 minutes [9].
  • Where: Central Library, One Margaret Mitchell Square, Atlanta, GA 30303, (404) 612-8700. Hours are Monday–Tuesday 10 a.m.–8 p.m. and Wednesday–Saturday 10 a.m.–6 p.m.; closed Sunday. It's closed all day Nov. 11 for Veterans Day, Nov. 26–27 for Thanksgiving, Dec. 24–26 for Christmas and Dec. 31, 2026–Jan. 2, 2027 for New Year's [10] (last checked Nov 3, 2026). The Census Geocoder places Central Library in Atlanta city [11].
  • Fulton County Clerk: its fee table lists uncertified copies of court records at $0.50 a page without help or $1.00 a page with help, and faxed or electronic copies at $2.50 for the first page and $1.00 for each page after [12].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 31 locations with Atlanta addresses. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [13]. The Census Geocoder places these 15 in Atlanta city: 2870 Peachtree Rd; 1270 Caroline St, Ste D120; 931 Monroe Dr, Ste 102; 925B Peachtree St NE; 207 13th St NE A-1; 2221 Peachtree Rd NE, Ste D; 572 Hank Aaron Dr SE, Suite 4100; 830 Glenwood Ave SE, Ste 510; 2275 Marietta Blvd NW, Ste 270; 541 Tenth St NW; 2020 Howell Mill Rd NW; 1579 Monroe Dr, Ste F; 2625 Piedmont Rd, Ste 56; 1266 W Paces Ferry Rd; and 4279 Roswell Rd NE, Ste 208. The Caroline St store is in the DeKalb County part of the city [13][11]. It places 10 others in nearby cities (Brookhaven, Chamblee, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs and South Fulton), three in unincorporated DeKalb County and three in unincorporated Cobb County [13][11]. Prices aren't listed online: its 925B Peachtree St NE store page shows no prices and says products, services, pricing and hours of operation may vary by location [14], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Atlanta

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Fulton County Library System pages we read [1][8].
  • The UPS Store: its Atlanta page lists notarizing [13]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store [14]. Notary rules vary by state [15].
  • Becoming a notary (Fulton County): the Clerk handles notary commissions by appointment, with walk-ins welcome [16]. Its fee table lists a new or renewed commission at $55.00 in person or $60.00 for a renewal by mail, and a certificate of authority at $2.00 [12]. We didn't find a fee on the Clerk's pages for notarizing your documents.
  • Becoming a notary (DeKalb County, differs): the DeKalb Clerk's notary checklist (updated 6/13/2025) lists the cost as $54.00, paid by cash, debit or credit card (2% plus $1.00 surcharge), business check or money order; personal checks aren't accepted [17].
  • How notaries are appointed (statewide): Georgia notaries are commissioned for a four-year term by the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where they live; residents of neighboring states who work or own a business in Georgia can apply in that county. Applicants must be at least 18 and complete the notary training course. The appointment fee is $40.00–$55.00 depending on the county. A Georgia notary can act anywhere in the state [18].
  • Fees (statewide): the GSCCCA's summary of Georgia notary law says the authorized fee is $2.00 for each notarial act. On request, a notary may charge $2.00 more for a certificate from the Clerk of Superior Court showing the commission is in effect. A notary doesn't have to charge, but must tell you beforehand if there's a fee and the amount allowed by law [19].
  • Journal (statewide): since January 1, 2025, a notary must keep a written or electronic journal entry for each notarial act done for a self-filer [19].
  • The law itself (statewide): Georgia notary law is in Title 45, Article 17 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated [19]. The official online code needs JavaScript, so we didn't read the statute text; it needs a browser read [20].
  • Check a notary (statewide): the GSCCCA keeps the statewide notary database and has an online Notary Public Search Index. Its Notary Division phone is 404-327-6023 [18].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (Fulton County Board of Health)

  • Fee: a $25 non-refundable search fee, which includes one certified copy if the record is found. Extra copies of the same record ordered at the same time are $5 each [2]. That's the same as the state fees below.
  • Which records: the office is the local agent for Fulton County residents and can also print certificates for births and deaths in any Georgia county. At least 95% print on demand; some can take up to 72 hours [2].
  • Where: Fulton County Government Center, 141 Pryor Street, Suite 1029A [2][28], where the county's Vital Records Division issues birth and death certificates [29]. The Census Geocoder places it in Atlanta city [11]. The Board of Health also lists offices at 1920 John Wesley Avenue, College Park, and 4700 North Point Parkway, Alpharetta [2]; the Geocoder places the College Park office in College Park city [11].
  • Pages differ on hours: the Board of Health's vital records page says walk-in service runs Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [2]. Its College Park hours notice lists the Government Center at Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and says College Park is closed Mondays, open Tue–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [30].
  • Payment: walk-ins pay by cash, certified check, money order, or Visa or MasterCard; American Express isn't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek. Phone (770) 520-7500, press 1, then 4 [2].
  • DeKalb County: the state lists the DeKalb County Health Department at 445 Winn Way, Decatur [28]. The Census Geocoder places it in unincorporated DeKalb County, outside Atlanta [11].

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): birth and death certificates are $25.00 each, plus $5.00 for each additional copy. Marriage certificates are $10.00 plus $5.00 for additional copies, and amendments are $10.00 plus the cost of the certificate [31].
  • Payment (statewide): credit or debit cards at walk-in service or online, cash at walk-in service only, certified checks and money orders. Personal checks aren't accepted [31].
  • Who can get one (statewide): certified birth and death certificates go only to people with a direct and tangible interest, such as immediate family members or legal representatives of the family [32][33]. Georgia.gov says only the registrant, an immediate family member or a legal representative can request one [7].
  • Older births (statewide): the State Office of Vital Records has births from January 1919 on. For older births, contact the county where the birth happened or the Georgia Archives [32].
  • In person (statewide): for same-day service, visit the State Office of Vital Records, 1680 Phoenix Boulevard, Suite 100, Atlanta, GA 30349, 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Monday–Friday except state holidays, or any county vital records office [7]. The state works with 159 county offices [34]. Georgia Vital Records doesn't do same-day cruise rush requests [32].
  • By mail (statewide): mail the request form and payment to the State Office of Vital Records at the Phoenix Boulevard address. Georgia.gov gives a response time of 8–10 weeks [7], and the Department of Public Health says 8 to 10 weeks for mail-in death certificate requests [33].
  • Online (statewide; pages differ on timing): the state's ROVER service charges $25 per certified copy plus $8 for processing. FedEx shipping is $16 per order and speeds up delivery only; the Department of Public Health says standard processing takes up to 10 weeks [35]. Georgia.gov gives an online response time of 2–4 weeks, or up to 5 business days for expedited orders, and lists ROVER, GO Certificates and VitalChek [7]. Ask the office which timing applies before you order.
  • Divorce records (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where the divorce was granted holds the copies [7].

Marriage licenses (Fulton County Probate Court)

  • Who issues licenses (statewide): county probate courts. You must be at least 18, or 17 if certain conditions are met. If at least one of you lives in Georgia, any county's probate court can issue the license; if neither does, you must apply in the county where the ceremony will be. Both of you must be present [6].
  • After the ceremony (statewide): the signed license should go back to the probate court within 30 days of the ceremony, and the certificate typically arrives by mail within 30 days [6].
  • Older copies (statewide): the State Office of Vital Records holds marriage applications and certificates from June 1952 to August 1996, with a non-refundable $10.00 search fee and $5.00 for each additional copy. Records outside those years are at the probate court of the county that issued the license [36].
  • Fulton County fee: $68.50, including one mailed certified copy of the marriage certificate, or $28.50 if you bring a certificate of completion from a qualified premarital education program. American Express isn't accepted, and fees aren't refundable. The annex locations are cashless; cash is accepted only downtown, and credit and debit cards work at all locations [3].
  • Where: the Licensing Division moved to 136 Pryor Street, Second Floor, on Aug. 21, 2025; follow signs to J2 / Justice Center Tower [37]. Both of you must come with current ID. The court says the license has no expiration date. Phone 404-613-4583 [3].
  • After the ceremony: mail the completed license to Fulton County Probate Court, Attn: License Division, 136 SW Pryor Street, 2nd Floor C230, Atlanta, Georgia 30303. Allow up to 30 days for the court to record it [3].
  • Copies: by mail from the Probate Court's Records Division, same address, for $10 per copy. Include the request form, a self-addressed stamped envelope and a money order; personal checks aren't accepted [3].
  • DeKalb County (differs): the DeKalb County Probate Court charges $66.00, or $26.00 with a premarital education certificate for a course of at least six hours, plus $2.00 shipping and handling either way. It works by appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:00–11:30 a.m. and 12:30–4:00 p.m., at 556 North McDonough Street, Decatur [38]. The Census Geocoder places it in Decatur city [11].

Recorded documents (Fulton County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • State law (statewide): we didn't read the state's recording fee law, because the official online Georgia code needs JavaScript; it needs a browser read [20]. The county fee schedule below is what you pay.
  • Fulton County recording fees: the Real Estate section of the Clerk's fee table lists deeds and other real estate instruments at $25.00, and cancellations, satisfactions, releases or assignments at $25.00 per instrument. Other instruments are $5.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, and plats and maps are $10.00 a page [12].
  • Same table differs: the table's Miscellaneous section lists "Recording any instrument or writing Fee not otherwise specified - 1st page" at $0.50 [12]. Ask the Clerk which applies.
  • Payment: the Real Estate Division doesn't accept credit cards. Pay by cash, business check or money order [12].
  • DeKalb County Clerk of Superior Court (Decatur, outside the city): DeKalb County Courthouse, 556 North McDonough Street, Ground Floor, Decatur, GA 30030; 404-371-2836. The Judicial/Real Estate Division is open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. [23]. DeKalb County's Judicial System page links to dksuperiorclerk.com as the Clerk's site [39]. The Census Geocoder places the courthouse in Decatur city [11].
  • DeKalb recording fees (sheet dated 3/26/2025): deeds and other grantor-grantee recordings are $25.00. Liens, including general execution, federal tax, hospital and materialman liens, lis pendens and notices of commencement, are $25.00 each; the first cross reference is included and each additional one is $25.00. Ad valorem tax liens and Georgia Department of Revenue liens are $5.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each page after. UCC filings are $25.00, or $50.00 with an assignment; plats, maps and floor plans are $10.00 a page; and recording a military DD-214 is free [40].
  • DeKalb copy fees (sheets differ): the recording sheet lists certified copies at $2.50 for the first page and $0.50 for each page after, other copies at $1.00 and $0.50 (its columns are labeled "Clerk Assisted" and "W/Account Per Page"), and plat copies at $2.00 [40]. The civil and family fee sheet (effective 10/10/2025) lists certified copies at $2.50 for the first page and $0.50 for each page after, and regular copies at $1.00 per document [41].
  • DeKalb card payments: the Clerk's fees page lists a credit card surcharge of 2% plus $1.00 [42].

Court records (Fulton County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • State law (statewide): the official online Georgia code needs JavaScript, so we didn't read the state's clerk fee law; it needs a browser read [20]. The county fee schedule below is what you pay.
  • Fulton County copy fees: certified or exemplified copies are $2.50 for the first page and $0.50 for each page after. Uncertified copies are $0.50 a page without help or $1.00 with help [12].
  • Where and when: Fulton County Courthouse, 136 Pryor Street SW, Room C-155, open Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. except government holidays [43]. The Census Geocoder places it in Atlanta city [11].

Apostilles and other certifications (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA) is the only state agency that issues apostilles for Georgia documents, and Atlanta is its only location. For countries outside the Apostille Convention, the Georgia Secretary of State issues a Great Seal Certification instead (404-656-2871) [44].
  • Fees (statewide): $3.00 per document for an apostille. A certification of notary public, for documents used in the U.S., is $2.00 per document [44].
  • What it takes (statewide): birth, death and marriage certificates, divorce decrees and court documents must be official certified copies signed and sealed by the issuing Georgia official. Notarized documents don't need a county certification first. Out-of-state and federal documents go elsewhere [44].
  • Walk-in (statewide): 1875 Century Blvd., Ste. 100, Atlanta, GA 30345, 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Monday–Friday, no appointment needed; it typically takes less than 20 minutes. Pay by cash, a check with a pre-printed name and address payable to GSCCCA, a money order or credit card [44].
  • By mail (statewide): GSCCCA, Attn: Notary Division, at the same address. Include a cover letter naming the destination country and a prepaid, self-addressed envelope or airbill; processing normally takes one to two business days. Contact notary@gsccca.org, 404-327-6023 or 800-304-5175 [44].

The GSCCCA's apostille office at 1875 Century Blvd. is outside the city: the Census Geocoder places it in Chamblee city [11]. More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [45]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Atlanta?

At the Fulton County Board of Health's vital records office in the Government Center, 141 Pryor Street, Suite 1029A, for $25 [2], or at the State Office of Vital Records, which offers same-day walk-in service [7].

How much is a marriage license in Atlanta?

$68.50 at the Fulton County Probate Court, or $28.50 with a premarital education certificate [3]. DeKalb County charges $66.00, or $26.00, plus $2.00 shipping [38].

Can I pay the Fulton County Clerk's passport fee by card?

No. The Clerk no longer accepts credit or debit cards for the $35 fee. Downtown takes cash or money order; the North and South Service Centers take money orders only [4].

How much does a Georgia notary charge?

$2.00 per notarial act, according to the GSCCCA's summary of Georgia notary law [19].
