Where to scan documents: free and paid options (2026)

If you need a paper document as a PDF, you can use a self-service machine at an office or shipping store, a public library scanner, or your phone. This page lists only what each provider officially publishes, with a source for every figure. Most stores don't publish a national scanning price, and we say so rather than guess.

Short version: several big public library systems let you scan for free, usually to a USB drive or email (see examples). Staples, FedEx Office and Office Depot have self-service scanning, but none publishes a national per-page price [1][2][7]. The UPS Store says only some locations scan [5]. Your phone can also scan to a PDF with Apple Notes or the Google Drive app [15][16]. Prices vary by location, so call ahead.

Where can I scan documents for free?

Your best bet is a public library. In the examples below, Seattle, Chicago, Houston and San Francisco all say scanning is free, at least to a USB drive or email [8][9][10][11]. Bring your own USB drive, since Houston's printers scan "to a USB (Flash) drive only" [10].

Your phone is the other option. Apple's help page shows how to scan with the Notes app or the Files app on an iPhone or iPad [15]. Google's help page shows how to scan to a searchable PDF with the Google Drive app on Android or iOS [16]. See the steps below.

How much does it cost to scan at Staples?

Staples doesn't publish a scanning price. Its in-store printing page says you can "Copy, print, scan and fax all in one place. Just pay at the machine and go," and "Scan documents to email or upload to a USB drive" [1]. No national price published, so check the price on the machine or call your store.

How much does it cost to scan at FedEx Office?

FedEx Office's scanning page says self-service scanning "is available at most locations," and "Prices vary by store. Contact your local store for details" [2]. How it works, from that page [2]:

  • Paying: you pre-authorize payment "based on the number of pages you plan to scan," and any unused balance is returned to your payment method.
  • Size: scans up to 11" x 17" on self-service copiers. Oversized items such as blueprints go on dedicated large-format machines.
  • Files: saved as PDF or JPG and sent to email, a USB drive or cloud storage. File size limits are 60 MB to USB, 9 MB to email and 50 MB to cloud storage.

Two examples of store-level pricing:

  • FedEx Office inside Walmart: "Self-service scanning $0.39 per page." FedEx says these prices are "available only for in-store purchases at participating FedEx Office locations inside Walmart" and may vary by location [4].
  • Dallas, TX store (W Northwest Hwy): the store page says it has no dedicated photo scanner, but offers self-serve and full-service document scanning, both sheet-fed and glass. A flatbed scanner at its all-access station under Office Center mode is "priced at $0.75 per minute" [3].

These are examples, not a national price. Other stores may differ.

How much does it cost to scan at The UPS Store?

The UPS Store publishes no national scanning price, and not every store scans. Its copies page says "Some of our locations have the capability to capture your hardcopy documents and convert them to digital format. Contact your neighborhood location" [5]. For example, the Warson Woods, MO store (9909 Manchester Road) lists "Scanning from $2.00" among services it says are "full service and provided by the staff." The page doesn't say whether that's per page or per job [6]. Its scanning page adds that "Products, services, pricing and hours of operation may vary by location" [6]. Call your store for its price.

How much does it cost to scan at Office Depot or OfficeMax?

Office Depot's self-service page says "Bring your files or photos to any store and use our self-service printer to scan them," and you can "Scan to email, cloud, or USB." It doesn't state a scanning price [7]. Varies by location; call ahead.

Can I scan at CVS, Walgreens or Walmart?

  • CVS: the CVS Photo copy and print page says "you can purchase a CD that contains your saved scanned document," with pricing "based on the number of pages or CDs." It doesn't list a price, and the service is at select stores [14]. Call your store.
  • Walgreens: we found no official Walgreens page about document scanning. Not stated, so call your store.
  • Walmart: select Walmart stores have a FedEx Office inside, which lists self-service scanning at $0.39 per page [4].

How do I scan a document with my phone?

  • iPhone or iPad, Notes app: open a note, tap the Attachment button, then tap Scan Documents. Place the document in view of the camera. In Auto mode it scans automatically. Apple's page also shows how to sign a scanned document [15].
  • iPhone or iPad, Files app: tap the More button, then Scan Documents [15].
  • Android or iPhone, Google Drive app: Google says you can scan "receipts, letters, and billing statements" to save them "as searchable PDFs" in the Drive mobile app. "The web version of Google Drive does not offer this" [16].

We list these because their makers publish official instructions. This is not a ranking of scanning apps.

Tips before you go

  1. Call ahead. FedEx Office says prices vary by store, and The UPS Store says only some locations scan [2][5]. Ask the price per page and how many pages you have.
  2. Bring a USB drive. Houston's free scanning is to USB only [10], and FedEx Office allows larger files on USB (60 MB) than by email (9 MB) [2].
  3. Watch email size limits. A long, high-resolution scan may be too big to email. FedEx Office says resolution and page count affect file size [2].
  4. Need to send it by fax instead? Seattle's ScanEZ kiosks can send free faxes to the US and Canada [8]. For store prices, see how much it costs to fax.
  5. Need a paper copy too? See how much it costs to print at Staples, FedEx Office, The UPS Store, Office Depot and libraries.

FAQ

Where can I scan documents for free?

At many public libraries. Seattle, Chicago, Houston and San Francisco say scanning is free, at least to USB or email [8][9][10][11]. You can also scan with your phone using Apple Notes or the Google Drive app [15][16].

How much does it cost to scan at Staples?

Staples publishes no scanning price. Its self-service machines let you scan to email or USB, and you "pay at the machine" [1]. Check the price on the machine or call your store.

How much does it cost to scan at FedEx Office?

FedEx Office says "Prices vary by store" [2]. One example: FedEx Office locations inside Walmart list self-service scanning at $0.39 per page [4].

Does The UPS Store scan documents?

Some locations do. The UPS Store says "Some of our locations" can convert hard copies to digital and asks you to contact your store [5]. One example store lists "Scanning from $2.00" [6].

Can I scan a document to email?

Yes, at Staples, FedEx Office and Office Depot self-service machines [1][2][7], and at library scanners in Seattle, Chicago, San Francisco and Denver [8][9][11][13].

Can I scan with my phone instead?

Yes. Apple shows how to scan in the Notes and Files apps on iPhone and iPad [15]. Google shows how to scan to a PDF in the Google Drive app on Android and iOS, but not on the Drive website [16].
