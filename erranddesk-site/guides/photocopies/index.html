Where to make photocopies: prices and free options (2026)

Staples and CVS publish national copy prices, and FedEx Office store pages list "starting at" prices. The UPS Store sets prices by location, and many public libraries have copiers at low or no cost. This page uses only official pages. Prices for printing from a file are in our printing price guide.

Short version: Black-and-white copies are from $0.34 a page at Staples and 29¢ at select CVS stores. FedEx Office says prices vary by store, and two FedEx Office store pages list $0.23 and $0.24 [1][2][5][6][12]. Library copiers are often cheaper. Some, like Seattle and Denver, include free pages [14][15].

How much does it cost to make copies at Staples?

Staples lists copies from $0.34 per page in black and white and from $0.82 per page in color [1][2]. It offers online, full-service and self-service copies [2]. The self-service machines can copy, print, scan and fax, and you "pay at the machine and go" [3].

How much does it cost to make copies at FedEx Office?

FedEx Office's national page says self-service copy and print prices vary by store, so contact your local store [4]. Two store pages, as examples: Dallas, TX lists "Starting at price $0.23 for black and white and $0.71 for color," and Washington, DC lists $0.24 and $0.76 [5][6]. Both say you can use self-service copiers for letter, legal or anything up to tabloid size [5][6].

How much does it cost to make copies at The UPS Store?

There's no national price. The UPS Store says its locations are individually owned and operated, so services may vary. You can bring in a job or call for a quote [7]. Two store pages, as examples:

  • Mechanicsburg, PA (4900 Carlisle Pike): black-and-white letter copies are $0.14 each for 1-99, $0.12 for 100-499, $0.10 for 500-999 and $0.08 for 1,000 or more. Legal is $0.18 and 11" x 17" is $0.22 (1-99). Color letter copies are $0.53 (1-49) down to $0.43 (500+) [8].
  • Warson Woods, MO (9909 Manchester Road): black-and-white copies are $0.15 for letter or legal and $0.30 for 11" x 17". Color is $0.69 for letter. These are staff-made copies, "Double sided counts as two copies," stapling, folding and hole punching are extra, and tax is not included [9].

How much does it cost to make copies at Office Depot or OfficeMax?

Office Depot's national pages we checked don't state a separate per-page copy price. Its document printing page lists starting at $0.18 per page in black and white and $0.70 per page in color, and stores have self-service printers [10][11]. Ask your store what it charges to copy paper originals.

Can I make copies at CVS or Walgreens?

CVS: yes, at select stores. CVS says it offers copy and print services at a KODAK Picture Kiosk and accepts "physical documents or hard copies" as well as USB drives with PDF files. Black-and-white copies are 29¢ single-sided and 40¢ double-sided. Color copies are $1.19 and $1.30 [12].

Walgreens: its business printing price list shows document printing, for example $0.28 per single-sided black-and-white page and $0.99 per single-sided color page for 1-499 pages [13]. That list is for printing files. It doesn't say whether stores copy paper originals, so call your store.

Where can I make copies for free or cheap?

Many public libraries have copiers. Prices and free allowances are set by each library system. These are examples, not a complete list:

  • Seattle Public Library: "You can print or copy 10 free black-and-white pages each week, or 3 free color pages." After that, letter or legal pages are $0.15 single-sided black and white and $0.50 single-sided color. The free balance reloads on Sunday [14].
  • Denver Public Library: free printing, and "Printing and copying is limited to 100 pages." Only library paper can be used [15].
  • Metropolitan Library System (Oklahoma County, OK): "All Metro libraries have a photocopier." Copies are $0.10 per page in black and white and $0.50 in color. Pay with bills, coins or a card. A card payment puts a $10 hold on the card [17].
  • Orange County Library System (FL): photocopies are $.15 a page in person. If you ask staff to make copies to mail or fax, there's a $5.00 handling fee plus $.25 per page, with a 10-page maximum [18].
  • Houston Public Library: "Black and white prints are $0.15, and color prints are $0.50," paid by credit card or Apple Pay/Google Pay. There's a $1 minimum charge and an automatic $5 card hold [16].

Search your library's website for "copy" or "print" to find its prices. Some libraries are cashless and some take cash only.

Tips before you go

  1. Count double-sided pages. At least one UPS Store counts a double-sided page as two copies, and CVS charges more for double-sided [9][12].
  2. Bring a card or cash, depending on the place. Houston Public Library is cashless, and Metro Library takes bills, coins or cards [16][17].
  3. Ask for a quote on a big job. The UPS Store quotes each job, and Mechanicsburg's per-page price drops with quantity [7][8].
  4. Copying an ID or a document for a passport application or notary? See where to get something notarized and passport photo prices.
  5. Printing from a file, or want a digital copy? See how much it costs to print and where to scan documents.

FAQ

How much does it cost to make copies at Staples?

Staples lists copies from $0.34 per page in black and white and from $0.82 per page in color, with full-service and self-service options [1][2].

How much are copies at FedEx Office?

FedEx Office says prices vary by store. Two store pages list starting prices of $0.23 and $0.24 for black and white, and $0.71 and $0.76 for color [4][5][6].

How much are copies at The UPS Store?

There's no national price, because locations are individually owned. Two store pages, as examples, list black-and-white letter copies at $0.14 (1-99 copies) and $0.15 [7][8][9].

Does CVS make copies?

Yes, at select stores, at a KODAK Picture Kiosk. Black-and-white copies are 29¢ single-sided and color copies are $1.19 single-sided [12].

Where can I make copies for free?

Check your public library. For example, Seattle Public Library gives 10 free black-and-white pages a week, and Denver Public Library offers free printing and copying up to 100 pages [14][15].

Is a double-sided copy charged as one page or two?

It depends on the store. The UPS Store in Warson Woods, MO says double-sided counts as two copies. CVS lists a separate double-sided price of 40¢ for black and white [9][12].
