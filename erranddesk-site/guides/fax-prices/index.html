Where to fax and what it costs: UPS Store vs FedEx Office vs Staples vs your library (2026)

If you need to send a fax without a fax machine, you have four common options in the US. This page lists only what each provider officially publishes, with a source for every figure. If a provider publishes no national price, we say so rather than guess.

Short version: FedEx Office is the only chain here that publishes a starting price, and only on its individual store pages [2][3]. The UPS Store and Staples publish no national fax price [4][7]. Some public libraries let you send faxes free, and others charge from $0.50 a page [8][9][10]. Prices vary by location, so call before you go.

Per-page vs flat pricing

Every in-store option we checked charges per transmission or per page, not a flat fee:

  • First-page + additional-page pricing: Mid-Continent Public Library and Carlsbad City Library charge $1.75 for the first US page and $1.00 for each additional page [12][13].
  • Flat per-page pricing: Orange County Library System (FL) charges $0.50 per page, tax included, "for all types of faxes" [10].
  • Free: Denver Public Library and Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library [8][9].
  • FedEx Office publishes only "starting at" figures, and they're tied to individual stores [2][3]. Ask the store for the total for your page count before you send.

Receiving a fax at UPS, FedEx or Staples

What each option's official pages say about receiving faxes (the same facts as the comparison table):

  • FedEx Office: "Some FedEx Office locations" receive faxes for pickup, paid at the counter [1]. Store pages: "Pricing starts at $1.59 to receive a fax" [2][3].
  • The UPS Store: Yes. The national page lists "sending and receiving faxes" [4]. No price published.
  • Staples: Not stated on Staples' official page [7].
  • Public libraries: Usually not. Four of our five examples say they send only [8][10][12][13].

Tips before you go

  1. Call ahead. Ask whether that location faxes today, the total for your page count and destination, and whether international is supported. FedEx Office and The UPS Store both say services or pricing vary by location [1][6].
  2. Bring or prepare a cover sheet. Put the recipient's name, fax number, your contact details and the page count on it. FedEx Office provides one free [1]. Elsewhere, ask whether it's free and whether it counts as a billed page.
  3. Ask about confirmation. Not every option gives you proof of sending. Denver Public Library, for example, gives no receipt [8]. If you need proof, use a place that prints a confirmation page, and keep it.
  4. Double-check the fax number before sending, including any country code for international faxes.
  5. Take your originals with you, and don't leave confirmation pages behind.
  6. Does the document also need to be notarized? See where to get something notarized and what it costs.
  7. Need to receive a fax? Most library examples above can't receive. Ask a store for its fax number and its pickup fee first.

FAQ

How much does it cost to fax at The UPS Store?

The UPS Store publishes no national fax price. Its locations are independently owned franchises, and the company says "Services, prices, and hours of operation… may vary by location" [6]. Call your local store for its rate.

How much does FedEx Office charge to send a fax?

FedEx Office store pages say pricing "starts at $2.19 to send a local fax and $2.49 to send a domestic fax," and "starts at $1.59 to receive a fax" [2][3]. FedEx's national fax page notes that "Prices vary by store" [1].

Can I fax for free?

At some public libraries, yes. Denver Public Library and Cincinnati & Hamilton County Public Library both list free outgoing faxing [8][9]. Other libraries charge (see the library table).

Can I receive a fax at the library?

Often not. Denver, Orange County (FL), Mid-Continent (MO) and Carlsbad (CA) all say their machines send only [8][10][12][13]. Cincinnati's page doesn't mention receiving [9].

Does Staples send international faxes?

Staples' in-store printing page lists "local, long-distance and international faxing along with a printed confirmation." It does not publish a price [7].
