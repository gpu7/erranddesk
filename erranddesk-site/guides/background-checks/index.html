How to get an FBI background check and fingerprints (2026)

If you need a copy of your own criminal history record, you can ask the FBI for an Identity History Summary or ask your state for its record. Both start with fingerprints. Here is what each costs and how to send it in, using only government pages.

Short version: The FBI fee for your own Identity History Summary is $18 per request [1][2]. You can mail a fingerprint card with your request [1][3], or apply through the FBI's Electronic Departmental Order site [4][7]. Some Post Offices take digital fingerprints for this check [5]. State checks are separate and have their own fees, for example $25 in California plus the fingerprint site's fee [6].

Not legal advice. This page is general information from official pages. Which check you need is set by whoever asked for it. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the FBI, the U.S. Postal Service, any state agency or any fingerprinting company.

How do I request my FBI Identity History Summary?

Federal rules call this an FBI identification record, "often referred to as a 'rap sheet.'" It lists information from fingerprints the FBI has on file from arrests, and in some cases from federal employment, naturalization or military service [3]. You can ask for your own copy.

By mail. Federal rules say to send a written request to the FBI's Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) Division in Clarksburg, West Virginia, with your name, date and place of birth and a set of rolled fingerprints on a fingerprint card [3]. The FBI's checklist says to include [1]:

  • A completed, signed application form (if the request is for a couple or family, everyone signs)
  • A completed fingerprint card with your name, date of birth, descriptive data, all 10 rolled fingerprints and the plain impressions, including both thumbs
  • A credit card payment form, certified check or money order for $18.00 per request. "This amount must be exact"
  • An email address or phone number in case the FBI needs to contact you

Make a certified check or money order payable to the "Treasury of the United States." The checklist says "CASH OR PERSONAL/BUSINESS CHECKS ARE NOT AN ACCEPTED FORM OF PAYMENT" [1]. Our money order guide covers where to buy one. The FBI's own fingerprint card for this request is form FD-1164 [10]. Check the FBI's current instructions for the full mailing address before you send anything.

Online. The FBI's Electronic Departmental Order site, edo.cjis.gov, lists Identity History Summary help at identity@fbi.gov and 304-625-5590 [4]. Texas DPS sends people who need an FBI personal review to that site [7]. We couldn't load the FBI's step-by-step pages for the online request when we checked, so processing times and how results are delivered are Not stated here.

Channelers. The FBI also publishes a list of FBI-approved channelers, which are companies that can submit your request. Use only companies on that list. We couldn't load the list when we checked, so we don't name any here.

Where can I get fingerprinted for an FBI background check?

The U.S. Postal Service says it offers "digital fingerprinting services for the FBI IdHSC" at participating Post Offices. It adds: "This service is not yet available nationwide. Please verify that a Post Office offers this service before going in person for fingerprinting" [5]. USPS's location search lets you check which offices take part [5]. The price wasn't on that page, so it's Not stated here.

For a card you mail yourself, the FBI asks for all 10 rolled prints plus the plain impressions [1]. The FD-1164 card's tips are to wash and dry your fingers, roll each one from nail to nail, keep the prints in the right order and note any missing fingers [10]. In California, the state says fingerprinting is "available at most local police departments, sheriff's offices or any public applicant Live Scan site" [6].

How do I get my state criminal record? Examples

A state check is separate from the FBI check. These are examples from three state agencies:

  • California: submit fingerprints and pay a $25 DOJ processing fee. California residents use Live Scan with form BCIA 8016RR, marked "Record Review." The Live Scan site sets its own rolling fee. People outside California mail an FD-258 card with form BCIA 8705. A fee waiver may be available [6].
  • Texas: book a DPS FAST appointment operated by IdentoGO with service code 11FT12. "The cost of this service is $10.00 plus a $15.00 fee for the CHRI." The vendor takes cards, business checks and money orders, but not personal checks or cash [7].
  • Illinois: the Illinois State Police fee schedule lists "Illinois Access and Review/Record Challenge" as "No Charge" [8].

Who orders a background check for a job or license?

Usually the employer or licensing agency, not you. The FBI's fee notice covers checks "submitted by authorized users for noncriminal justice purposes including employment and licensing" [9]. California says access to its records is limited by law to law enforcement and "authorized applicant agencies" [6]. Texas law authorizes fingerprint checks for job and license applicants in many fields, such as child care providers, teachers and security guards [11].

From Oct 1, 2026, the FBI's fee to authorized users is $15.00 for a fingerprint check, up from $12.00 [9]. In Illinois, a state and FBI check costs $35 on a paper card or $30 by Live Scan [8]. Follow the requester's instructions, because the request goes through them. In Texas, people fingerprinted for a requesting agency "will not receive copies of their background check or fingerprints from DPS". Texas says to ask that agency for a copy, or request a personal review [11].

FAQ

How much does an FBI background check cost?

$18 per request for your own Identity History Summary [1][2]. Fingerprinting may cost extra.

Can I pay the FBI with a personal check or cash?

No. The FBI checklist accepts a credit card payment form, a certified check or a money order, and says cash and personal or business checks aren't accepted [1].

Can I get fingerprinted at the post office?

At some. USPS offers digital fingerprinting for the FBI Identity History Summary at participating Post Offices only, and says to check before you go [5].

Can I use my state record for immigration or a visa?

California says records you request for your own review can't be used for visa, immigration or foreign transactions [6]. Check what the requesting office needs before you order.

Can someone else request my record for me?

California says third-party requests "are not authorized and will not be processed" [6]. For the FBI check, the FBI lists approved channelers that can submit requests. Use only companies on its list.
