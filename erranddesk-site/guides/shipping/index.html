How to ship a package: USPS, UPS and FedEx basics (2026)

USPS publishes its full retail price list, so this page quotes it. UPS and FedEx prices depend on weight, size, distance and service, and both point you to their own rate tools. This page also covers what to bring, drop-off vs pickup, and tracking, using only each carrier's official pages.

Short version: At a Post Office, Ground Advantage starts at $8.40, Priority Mail at $11.50, a Priority Mail Flat Rate Envelope is $13.90 and Media Mail starts at $4.39 [1][4]. UPS and FedEx don't publish one simple price. Use their calculators [7][8][16]. USPS picks up eligible packages free during regular mail delivery [5]. FedEx says drop-off at a retail location is free, and charges for on-call pickup [12][13].

How much does USPS charge to ship a package?

USPS publishes all retail prices in Notice 123. The current list is "Effective October 04, 2026" [1]. Some examples at a Post Office:

  • Ground Advantage: priced by weight and zone. Items under 1 lb are rounded up to 4, 8, 12 or 15.999 oz. Heavier packages are charged by the pound up to 70 lbs [2]. Prices run from $8.40 (4 or 8 oz, Zone 1) to $204.30 (70 lbs, Zone 8) [1]. Long or large packages pay extra fees, listed in Notice 123 [1].
  • Priority Mail: from $11.50. Except for Flat Rate packaging, prices are based on weight and zone [3]. Large, light packages over 1 cubic foot are priced by dimensional weight (length × width × height ÷ 139) [3].
  • Priority Mail Flat Rate: Flat Rate Envelope $13.90, Legal Flat Rate Envelope $14.25, Padded Flat Rate Envelope $15.00, Small Flat Rate Box $14.65, Medium $25.80 and Large $36.10 [1].
  • Priority Mail Express: from $37.05 by weight, or $38.25 for a Flat Rate Envelope [1][4].
  • First-Class Mail letters: from $0.82. This is for envelopes, not packages [4].

What can I send by Media Mail?

Media Mail is for eligible books, media and educational materials, such as books of 8 or more pages, sound and video recordings, CDs and DVDs, printed music, manuscripts and computer-readable media. Video games and computer or digital drives don't qualify. Packages can weigh up to 70 lbs, and USPS may open and inspect them. If a package has items that aren't allowed, you or your recipient will pay higher postage [4]. It starts at $4.39 for 1 lb [1].

How much does UPS charge to ship a package?

UPS doesn't post one simple price. It says the cost "will depend on its origin, destination, service, package weight and other considerations" [8]. If you ship from a UPS retail location, such as The UPS Store or a UPS Customer Center, or don't have an account, retail rates apply. UPS's retail rates page has a calculator, the Retail Rate and Service Guide, and zone charts by ZIP code [7].

The UPS Store says you can bring in an item and staff will take it from there. Stores have boxes and packaging, and most locations can coordinate pickup [10]. Many UPS Store locations also sell USPS services such as Priority Mail and Media Mail [11]. Locations are individually owned, so ask your store what it charges.

How much does FedEx charge to ship a package?

FedEx doesn't post one simple price either. Its shipping page sends you to a tool to "Calculate shipping costs" [16]. You can drop off at FedEx Office, FedEx Ship Center, FedEx Authorized ShipCenter, FedEx Drop Box, Walgreens, Dollar General and grocery locations [14].

What do I need to bring to ship a package?

  1. Your item, packed. USPS Ground Advantage needs your own packaging [2]. Priority Mail boxes and envelopes are free from USPS [4]. The UPS Store and FedEx can help you pack [10][14]. FedEx says to tape all seams and black out old labels and barcodes [14].
  2. The full delivery address, and your return address.
  3. A label, or pay at the counter. You can buy USPS postage at a Post Office, a self-service kiosk or online [2]. No printer? USPS prints a Click-N-Ship label for you for $1.65 [1][3]. FedEx's mobile app can give you a barcode instead of a printed label, for drop-off at FedEx Office or a participating FedEx Ship Center [14]. To print a label yourself, see how much it costs to print.
  4. A way to pay. Need to mail a payment? See where to get a money order.
  5. FedEx, "Shipping": fedex.com/en-us/shipping.html (last checked Oct 27, 2026)

USPS packages that use only stamps for postage and weigh more than 10 ounces, or are more than half an inch thick, must be handed to a retail counter employee at a Post Office [6].

How do I track a package?

USPS: most services include USPS Tracking, except First-Class Mail [4]. Ground Advantage and Priority Mail include it [2][3]. Keep your receipt or label with the tracking number.

Mailing a letter and need proof it was delivered, or a signature? See Certified Mail, Registered Mail and Return Receipt.

UPS and FedEx: enter the tracking number on the carrier's tracking page. The UPS Store points you to its tracking tool for an estimated arrival date [10], and FedEx's tracking page takes one or more tracking numbers [15].

FAQ

What is the cheapest way to ship a package with USPS?

It depends on weight, distance and contents. At a Post Office, Ground Advantage starts at $8.40, and Media Mail starts at $4.39 but is only for eligible books and media [1][4].

How much is a USPS Flat Rate box?

At retail, the Small Flat Rate Box is $14.65, Medium is $25.80 and Large is $36.10. The Flat Rate Envelope is $13.90 [1].

Does USPS pick up packages for free?

Yes, for eligible packages during regular mail delivery. A time-specific Pickup on Demand costs $26.50 per pickup [1][5].

Does FedEx charge to drop off a package?

No. FedEx says there are no additional fees for dropping off a package. On-call pickup costs extra, from $9.00 per stop for a future-day pickup scheduled online [13][14].

How much does UPS charge to ship a package?

UPS doesn't publish one simple price. Cost depends on origin, destination, service and weight, and retail rates apply at The UPS Store. Use UPS's calculator or its Retail Rate and Service Guide [7][8].

Can I ship a package without a printer?

Yes. You can pay at a Post Office counter, have USPS print a Click-N-Ship label for $1.65, or use the FedEx mobile app barcode at FedEx Office or a participating FedEx Ship Center [1][2][14].
