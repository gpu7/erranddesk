Where to get a money order and what it costs (2026)

You can buy a money order at any Post Office, at Walmart, and at many stores that sell Western Union or MoneyGram money orders. Fees, limits and accepted payment methods differ. This page uses only official pages from USPS, the retailers, the money order companies, the FDIC and the U.S. Department of State, and says so when a fee isn't published.

Not affiliated with USPS or any government agency. Errand Desk is an independent site. This page is general information, not legal or financial advice. Confirm fees with the location before you go.
Short version: a USPS money order costs $2.65 for up to $500 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000, and you can pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card [1]. Walmart says its money order fee is never over $1 [3]. Western Union says fees vary by location [4]. Most money orders top out at $1,000 each [1][6].

How much is a money order at the Post Office?

USPS publishes its fees nationally [1]:

  • $0.01 to $500.00: $2.65
  • $500.01 to $1,000.00: $3.75
  • Postal Military Money Orders (issued by military facilities): $0.85

You can send up to $1,000 in a single domestic money order. Go to any Post Office, bring cash or a debit card, fill out the money order at the counter with a retail associate, and pay the amount plus the fee. Keep your receipt to track it [1].

USPS's payment FAQ lists money orders among things that can't be paid with a credit card, and says checks can't be accepted for the purchase of money orders [2].

Where can I get a money order?

  • Any Post Office: USPS says you can buy or cash postal money orders at any Post Office location [1].
  • Walmart: money orders are sold at Walmart Money Centers and Customer Service Desks, "Powered by Western Union" [3].
  • Western Union agent locations: use Western Union's location finder to check that a location sells money orders before you go [4].
  • 7-Eleven: Western Union says you can buy a Western Union money order "at select 7‑Eleven locations" [5].
  • MoneyGram agent locations: MoneyGram money orders are sold at retail locations across the US [6].
  • Banks and credit unions: the FDIC says you can buy a money order from "a bank, store, or the U.S. Postal Service," and you don't need an account at a financial institution to buy one [9]. Whether a particular bank sells them to non-customers isn't stated, so call.

What do I need to bring to buy a money order?

  • Payment: cash or a debit card at USPS and Walmart [1][3]. At Western Union locations it depends on the location [4].
  • Who you're paying: Walmart says to "bring payee & purchaser details" [3].
  • ID for larger amounts: MoneyGram suggests bringing identification if needed and checking whether ID may be required for larger amounts [6].

Money order vs cashier's check: what's the difference?

According to the FDIC [9]:

  • Money order: you pay the amount plus a fee to a bank, store or the Postal Service. You don't need a bank account. It can't "bounce," because it's paid for up front, and money orders are usually easy to replace if lost or stolen.
  • Cashier's check: a check guaranteed by a financial institution. The bank takes the money from your checking or savings account, then writes the check to the person or business you're paying. It's also guaranteed not to bounce and can be replaced if lost or stolen.

Cashier's check fees and limits are set by each bank. We didn't find a national price, so check your bank's fee schedule.

How do I track a money order?

  • USPS: check the status in USPS's Money Orders application. You need the serial number, Post Office number and dollar amount [1].
  • MoneyGram: check online with "Money Order Status," or call the automated line at 1-800-542-3590, with the money order number and exact dollar amount. If it hasn't been cashed, you can request a refund online [7].
  • Western Union (including Walmart and 7-Eleven): keep your receipt. Western Union says it has the details you need to check status or request a refund [4][5].

What if a money order is lost or stolen?

  • USPS: you can't stop payment, but a lost or stolen postal money order can be replaced. Confirming the loss may take up to 30 days, and the investigation may take up to 60 days. The processing fee is $23.00. Start a Money Order Inquiry at a Post Office with your receipt [1].
  • Western Union: submit the online Money Order Refund Request Form. "Additional fees may apply" [4].
  • MoneyGram: check the status first. If it hasn't been cashed, request a refund online. A photocopy of a cashed money order has a non-refundable $18 processing fee and takes 3 to 8 business days [7].

How do I cash a money order?

  • USPS money orders: cash them free at a Post Office. Don't sign it until you're at the counter with a retail associate, and bring a primary photo ID. You can also cash them at most banks and some stores [1].
  • Western Union money orders: you may be able to cash them at banks and some retailers, which may charge a fee, or deposit them. Not every Western Union location cashes them [4], and 7-Eleven doesn't cash Western Union money orders [5].
  • Do they expire? USPS domestic money orders never expire [1]. Western Union money orders don't expire, but depending on the state, a non-refundable service charge may be deducted if one isn't used or cashed within one to three years [4].

Tips before you go

  1. Bring cash or a debit card. USPS doesn't take credit cards for money orders [1].
  2. Need more than $1,000? USPS's limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [1]. MoneyGram says you may be able to buy more than one in the same visit, though limits may apply [6].
  3. Keep the receipt. USPS, Western Union and MoneyGram all use it, or its details, for tracking and refunds [1][4][7].
  4. Got a money order? Check it's real. USPS lets you verify a postal money order online, by scanning its QR code, or at 1-866-459-7822 [1].
  5. Paying a passport fee? The U.S. Department of State accepts a money order payable to "U.S. Department of State" for the passport application fee [10]. See the US passport application checklist for what else to bring.
  6. Sending paperwork with it? If the form also needs a notary stamp, see where to get something notarized or the free notary options. If the payee takes documents by fax, see how much it costs to fax.

FAQ

How much does a money order cost at the Post Office?

USPS charges $2.65 for money orders from $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1].

How much does a money order cost at Walmart?

Walmart says exact charges vary by location but won't be over $1 [3].

Can I buy a money order with a credit card?

Not at the Post Office. USPS says to bring cash or a debit card, and you cannot pay with a credit card [1][2]. Walmart lists cash or debit card [3].

What is the maximum amount for a money order?

USPS domestic money orders are limited to $1,000 each. MoneyGram's US limit is also $1,000, and it may vary slightly by state and location [1][6].

How do I check the status of a money order?

For USPS, use the Money Orders application with the serial number, Post Office number and amount. For MoneyGram, use Money Order Status online or call 1-800-542-3590 [1][7].

Is a cashier's check the same as a money order?

No. The FDIC says a cashier's check is guaranteed by a financial institution and drawn from your account, while you can buy a money order without a bank account by paying the amount plus a fee [9].
