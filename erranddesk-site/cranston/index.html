Cranston, Rhode Island document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Cranston, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Cranston, Cranston Public Library, the State of Rhode Island, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Cranston, a post office [1]. Cranston Public Library's notary has no fee (a $5.00 donation is requested), and faxing at the Central Library is free [2][3]. Birth certificates are $22 picked up from the City Clerk [4]. The state's Center for Vital Records is also in Cranston, at Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd. (statewide office) [5].
No county clerk in Rhode Island (statewide): Rhode Island has no county government [6]. Its counties were abolished as governmental units and survive only as geographic areas [7]. Land records are recorded in the city or town where the property is, and city and town clerks issue birth, death and marriage copies.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Cranston Public Library: printing is 25¢ a page black and white and 50¢ color at every branch. Photocopies are 25¢ a page, black and white only. The Central Library (140 Sockanosset Cross Rd.) has a self-service scan station with free scanning and free faxing; fax is at Central only. Central also prints from mobile devices [3].
  • FedEx: we didn't find a FedEx locations page for Cranston (the page returned "not found" on Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: 1 Cranston store, at 148 Atwood Ave. ((401) 654-6381) [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Cranston

  • Cranston Public Library (no fee, $5.00 donation requested): a notary is generally available Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; call 401-943-9080 x100 to confirm. The library's notaries don't notarize mortgages or documents that need extra witnesses [2].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [9].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a Rhode Island notary may charge up to $25 per document or notarization, including remote online notarization, and must tell you the fee before notarizing [10][11]. That cap is higher than in most nearby states, so ask first.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Cranston City Clerk

  • Hours and payment: Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., closed 12–1 p.m. for lunch. In person, the office takes cash, check or money order only; cards work only through the online portals [15]. City Hall is at 869 Park Ave. [15].
  • Birth, death and marriage copies: $22.00 for the first copy picked up, $25.00 mailed, $18.00 for each extra copy. Online orders add a nonrefundable $5 service and card fee, and standard USPS shipping is $1.00 [4].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Any city or town hall (statewide): births from 1960 on, and deaths and marriages from 2022 on, can be ordered at any Rhode Island city or town hall. Older records come from the city or town where the event happened or where you lived [16].
  • Fees (statewide): $22 for the first copy in person and $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy of the same record bought at the same time [5].
  • State office (statewide): RIDOH Center for Vital Records, Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd., Cranston, weekdays 7:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Mail orders take 4–6 weeks; rush orders cost $7 more [5].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): order the copy from the state office, not a city hall. RIDOH says vital records issued by a city or town can't be apostilled [16].

Marriage licenses (City Clerk)

  • By appointment only: 3 slots a business day (9:30 a.m., 11:30 a.m. and 1:30 p.m.). Call Matthew Goyette at (401) 780-3195 [15][17].
  • Fee: $24.00 by cash, check or money order; no cards. Both of you must be 18 and attend in person. Bring a birth certificate and photo ID for each of you, plus proof of Cranston residency if your ID shows another address. Return the signed license within 72 business hours of the ceremony [17].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at a city or town clerk. Apply where either of you lives if one of you lives in Rhode Island; if neither does, apply where the ceremony will be. RIDOH lists the fee as $24, and the license is good for 3 months [18][19].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): R.I. Gen. Laws § 15-2-9 sets the clerk's license fee at $14.00 [20], while RIDOH lists $24 [19]. We didn't find which law adds the other $10 (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Clerks charge the $24.

Land records (City Clerk)

  • Recording: in person, by mail or by e-recording through Simplifile and CSC. The office records daily until 4 p.m. (not 12–1 p.m.), and documents received before 4 p.m. are recorded the same day. Recordings with the wrong fee are rejected [15].
  • Fees: we didn't find a Cranston land record fee list on the city's pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026). The statewide fees below apply.
  • Recording fees (statewide law): R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-13-7 sets a base fee by document type, such as $80 for a deed and $60 for a mortgage, plus $1 for each extra page [21]. A $4 Historical Records Trust charge is added to each recorded document [22]. That's why clerks post $84 and $64.
  • Copies (statewide law): § 34-13-9 sets copies of recorded documents at $1.50 a page and $3.00 to certify [23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Rhode Island Department of State, Business Services Division, 148 West River Street, Providence. Bring or mail the document with a request form; checks and money orders go to the RI Department of State [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $5 per document [24]. Ordering online adds a $0.75 enhanced access fee per document; you still mail in the original [25]. For related documents in one matter filed together, the total is capped at $150 [26].
  • Returns (statewide): orders come back by USPS first-class mail unless you include a prepaid DHL, FedEx or UPS label [24].
  • Vital records (statewide): only state-issued copies can be apostilled [16]. You can ask RIDOH to send the record directly to the Department of State [24].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Is the Cranston Public Library notary free?

There's no fee, but the library requests a $5.00 donation [2].

Where can I fax for free in Cranston?

At the Central Library's self-service scan station, 140 Sockanosset Cross Rd. [3].

How much is a birth certificate in Cranston?

$22.00 picked up or $25.00 mailed, plus $18.00 for each extra copy [4].

Is the state vital records office in Cranston?

Yes. RIDOH's Center for Vital Records is at Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd., open weekdays 7:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. (statewide office) [5]. Order there if you need an apostille [16].

How much can a Rhode Island notary charge?

Up to $25 per document or notarization, disclosed in advance (statewide) [10].
