Toledo, Ohio document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Toledo, Ohio, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Toledo, the Toledo-Lucas County Health Department, Lucas County, the Lucas County Probate Court, the Lucas County Clerk of Courts, the Lucas County Recorder, the Lucas County Solid Waste Management District, the Toledo Lucas County Public Library, the Ohio Department of Health, the Ohio Secretary of State, the State of Ohio, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: the Toledo Lucas County Public Library sends outgoing faxes for free and gives cardholders $1.50 of printing or copying a day, but it doesn't offer notary service [1]. Passports there are by appointment [2]. The Toledo-Lucas County Health Department sells birth and death certificates for $25 each, usually issued in about 15 minutes in person [3]. A Lucas County marriage license is $60.00 in person [4]. The Clerk of Courts' Auto Title offices also take passport applications [5].
One county: the Census places all of Toledo city in Lucas County [6], so the county offices on this page are Lucas County offices.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Toledo Lucas County Public Library

  • Prices: library card holders get $1.50 of printing or copying a day. After that, black and white is $0.15 a page and color is $0.50 a page. All locations can help [1].
  • Fax: outgoing faxes are free [1]. We didn't find whether the library receives incoming faxes.
  • From your device: upload files up to 100 MB, or email them to bw@toledolibrary.org for black and white or color@toledolibrary.org for color. You get a release code to show staff. Pick up your printing the same day you send it. The library can't print on your own paper [1].
  • Scanning: we didn't find scanning on the library's printing, copying and faxing page [1].
  • Main Library: 325 Michigan Street, 419-259-5200 [1], which the Census Geocoder places in Toledo city [7].
  • The UPS Store: its Toledo page lists 2 stores and says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [8]. The Census Geocoder places 3550 Executive Pkwy, Ste 7 in Toledo city. It places 6725 W Central Ave, Ste M outside any city [8][7]. Prices aren't listed online: the 3550 Executive Pkwy store page shows no prices and says products, services, pricing and hours of operation may vary by location [9], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 1, 2026).

Notary in Toledo

  • Toledo Lucas County Public Library: the library says it doesn't provide notary services [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Toledo page lists notarizing [8]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store [9].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide law): the Ohio Secretary of State issues all notary commissions. Applicants must be at least 18 and Ohio residents (or attorneys admitted in Ohio who practice here), and most must submit a criminal records check from the past six months and complete an education program and test [10]. A commission lasts five years unless revoked; attorneys keep theirs as long as they qualify [11]. Ohio notaries are commissioned "for the state" [10] and have statewide jurisdiction [12].
  • Fees (statewide law): since April 3, 2025, an Ohio notary may charge up to $5 for a notarial act that isn't online, or up to $30 for an online notarization. Fees can't be charged per signature. A notary may also charge a reasonable travel fee agreed before the notarial act, and a technology fee of up to $10 for an online notarization [13].
  • Ohio Secretary of State pages: we tried to read the Secretary of State's notary pages on Nov 1, 2026, but they blocked our check, so the notary rules above come from the Ohio Revised Code.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (Toledo-Lucas County Health Department)

  • Fee: $25 a certified copy, for birth or death certificates. You can pay by cash, check, credit card or money order, with a processing fee for credit cards. Make checks payable to Toledo-Lucas County Health Department [3][18]. That's more than the state's $21.50 in the statewide section below.
  • What it issues: certified birth certificates for anyone born in Ohio after 1908, and death certificates for people who died in Ohio. Non-certified copies are no longer issued [3][18].
  • In person (about 15 minutes): 635 N. Erie St., downtown only. Hours are Monday–Wednesday and Friday 8:00 a.m.–3:45 p.m., and Thursday 9:00 a.m.–3:45 p.m. [3]. The Census Geocoder places 635 N Erie St in Toledo city [7].
  • By phone: 419-213-4100 with a credit card, for an added fee. Certificates are usually mailed the same or the next business day; allow 7–10 days [3].
  • By mail: send the request form, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope to Vital Statistics at 635 N. Erie Street, Toledo, OH 43604. The office mails certificates within 2 business days; allow 7–10 business days for return mail [3].
  • Online: through VitalChek, which charges $7.00 plus shipping [3][18].

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fee (statewide): the Ohio Department of Health (ODH) charges $21.50 for a certified birth or death record. Since Jan. 1, 2025, that $21.50 is a search fee, charged whether or not a record is found [19]. State law says the state fee can't be less than $12, and that local offices and the state add a $5 fee to each certified copy [20]. Local offices can charge more than the state: compare the city fee above.
  • Any Ohio birth, at local offices (statewide): ODH says more than 100 local offices offer same-day certified copies of Ohio vital records in person or in other ways [19]. State law lets a local registrar issue a certified copy to any applicant, unless the record is no longer public [21].
  • How to order from the state (statewide): online by credit card (processed within five business days; about three weeks for delivery by mail; no expedited service), or by mail to ODH Bureau of Vital Statistics, PO Box 15098, Columbus, Ohio 43215-0098, with a check or money order (four to six weeks) [19]. Online birth certificates are abstract copies that ODH says are valid for all U.S. legal uses, including passports [19].
  • State office walk-in (statewide): 4200 Surface Road, Columbus, Monday through Friday 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., only for central Ohio customers who need an expedited copy of their birth record and pay by credit card [19].
  • Deaths before 1971 (statewide): the state's records are with the Ohio History Connection. ODH says to get certified copies from the local health department where the death happened [19].

Marriage licenses (Lucas County Probate Court)

  • Where to apply (statewide law): both of you appear in person at the probate court of the county where either of you lives. If neither of you lives in Ohio, apply in the county where you plan to marry [22].
  • Waiting period and validity (statewide law): a probate judge may grant the license "at any time after the application is made" [22], and the license expires 60 days after it's issued [23]. A passport is one of the accepted proofs of age [22]. Ohio law doesn't set one statewide license fee; check the county fee below.
  • Lucas County fee: $60.00 in person, paid by cash or credit card (credit cards carry a convenience fee) [4]. The fee includes one certified copy [24].
  • Online fee (the court's pages differ): the marriage license page says the online license costs $63.54, by debit or credit card only [4]. The court costs page lists $61.40 online [24]. There are no refunds once you submit [4].
  • How: apply in person together, with no appointment, or apply online. If you apply online, you both still go to the Marriage License office to take the oath, with no appointment, within 30 days of the application being processed [4].
  • What to bring: each applicant brings a valid government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or state ID. If you use a U.S. passport, also bring proof of residency. Applicants under 21 need a certified birth certificate listing their parents' names. If you were married before, bring proof of how your most recent marriage ended [4].
  • Who can apply here: Ohio residents apply in the county where at least one of you lives. If you both live out of state, you can marry only in Lucas County [4].
  • Waiting period and validity: there's no waiting period in Lucas County, and the license is valid for 60 days from the date it's issued [4].
  • Where and when: 700 Adams St., Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. in person. Phone lines are open 8:30–11:30 a.m. and 1:30–4:00 p.m. [4][25]. The Census Geocoder places 700 Adams St in Toledo city [7].
  • Phone (the court's pages differ): the marriage license page and the county records center list (419) 213-4361 [4][26]. The court's hours page lists 419-213-4750 for marriage [25].
  • Upcoming closures: the court's 2026 list shows it closed on Nov. 11 (Veterans Day), Nov. 26–27 (Thanksgiving), and Dec. 24, 25 and 31 [25].
  • Weddings at the courthouse: a minister is usually available Tuesday–Friday, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m., for $50.00, cash only. For November 2026, the court lists hours of 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. Tuesday–Friday, and 10:00 a.m.–noon on Nov. 25 [4].
  • Copies: more certified copies are $2.00 each [24]. You can order certified copies online. They're mailed to you, can't be emailed or faxed, and take about three weeks [4].

Recorded documents (Lucas County Recorder)

  • Recording fees: $39.00 for the first two pages and $8.00 for each additional page, with a $39.00 minimum [27]. The recorder says the fee rose by $5.00 on April 1, 2026, because of Senate Bill 94 and Ohio Revised Code Section 317.32 [28]. The same page still carries an older note about the 2019 increase to $34.00 [28].
  • Copy prices (the recorder's pages differ): the fee schedule lists $2.00 a page for staff copies, $0.25 a page for self-service copies and $1.00 per document for certification [27]. The recorder's main page says all copies processed by its office are $2.00 a page [28]. Ask before you copy.
  • Payment: cash or checks payable to Lucas County Recorder [27]. The main page also lists escrow accounts [28].
  • Postage: if you don't include a self-addressed stamped envelope, include postage. The recorder says the postage for a standard 1 oz. first-class letter went from $0.80 to $1.00 on Aug. 17, 2026 [27][28].
  • Where and when: One Government Center, Suite 700; (419) 213-4400. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m., and the e-recording cutoff is 4:00 p.m. The office is closed Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026 [28]. The Census Geocoder didn't match the One Government Center address [7].

Court records (Lucas County Clerk of Courts)

  • Copy fees: the clerk's 2026 public records policy lists paper copies at $0.05 a page, with no charge for email delivery [29]. We didn't find a certified-copy price in the policy [29].
  • Where: 700 Adams Street; (419) 213-4484. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [15].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [31]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Shred events: we didn't find a shred event on the Lucas County Solid Waste Management District page or the City of Toledo trash and recycling page as of Nov. 1, 2026 [32][33]. The district's number is (419) 213-2230 [32].
  • Shredded paper at the curb: a May 2019 Republic Services flyer on Lucas County's site lists shredded office paper as recyclable in Toledo [34]. The city's current service guide says to place recyclables loose, not bagged [35]. Recycling is picked up every other week; call Republic Services at 419-936-2511 with questions [33].
  • Paid shredding: The UPS Store's Toledo page lists shredding; prices aren't listed online [8][9].

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Toledo?

At the Toledo-Lucas County Health Department, 635 N. Erie St., for $25 a copy. In person, it's usually issued in about 15 minutes [3].

How much is a marriage license in Toledo?

$60.00 in person at the Lucas County Probate Court. The online price is listed as $63.54 on the marriage page and $61.40 on the court costs page [4][24].

Can I get something notarized at the Toledo library?

No. The library says it doesn't provide notary services [1]. The UPS Store's Toledo page lists notarizing [8].

Can I send a fax at the Toledo library?

Yes. Outgoing faxes are free [1].
