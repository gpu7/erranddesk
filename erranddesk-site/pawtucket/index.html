Pawtucket, Rhode Island document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Pawtucket, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Pawtucket, Pawtucket Public Library, the State of Rhode Island, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 Pawtucket passport facilities, but Pawtucket Public Library says it isn't currently accepting applications, which leaves the post office [1]. The library sends notary seekers to the City Clerk's Office, which it lists as free [2]. Library prints start at 10¢ a page [3]. Birth certificates are $22 in person at City Hall [4].
No county clerk in Rhode Island (statewide): Rhode Island has no county government [5]. Its counties were abolished as governmental units and survive only as geographic areas [6]. Land records are recorded in the city or town where the property is, and city and town clerks issue birth, death and marriage copies.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Pawtucket Public Library: prints and copies are 10¢ single-sided and 20¢ double-sided in black and white, 50¢ single-sided and $1.00 double-sided in color. Scanning is free. The self-service fax sends only (it can't receive): $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page to the U.S., Canada and Caribbean; $3.95 and $3.45 for international [3].
  • FedEx: 2 Pawtucket locations, both FedEx at Walgreens: 100 Broad St. and 385 Cottage St. [7]. We didn't find a FedEx Office print center in Pawtucket.
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find a Pawtucket store in The UPS Store's Rhode Island list [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Pawtucket

  • Pawtucket Public Library: no notaries on site. The library lists the City Clerk's Office at City Hall as free, and Providence Public Library as free too [2]. We didn't find the City Clerk's own notary page.
  • Notary fees (statewide): a Rhode Island notary may charge up to $25 per document or notarization, including remote online notarization, and must tell you the fee before notarizing [9][10]. That cap is higher than in most nearby states, so ask first.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Pawtucket City Clerk (City Hall, 3rd floor, 137 Roosevelt Ave.)

  • Contact: (401) 728-0500; vital records ext. 349, real estate records ext. 261 [14].
  • Birth certificates: $22 in person or $25 by mail or online for the first copy; $18 for each extra copy of the same record ordered together. Online card orders add $5. Bring photo ID in person [4].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Any city or town hall (statewide): births from 1960 on, and deaths and marriages from 2022 on, can be ordered at any Rhode Island city or town hall. Older records come from the city or town where the event happened or where you lived [15].
  • Fees (statewide): $22 for the first copy in person and $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy of the same record bought at the same time [16].
  • State office (statewide): RIDOH Center for Vital Records, Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd., Cranston, weekdays 7:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Mail orders take 4–6 weeks; rush orders cost $7 more [16].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): order the copy from the state office, not a city hall. RIDOH says vital records issued by a city or town can't be apostilled [15].

Marriage licenses (City Clerk)

  • No appointment needed: it takes about 20–30 minutes. Both of you apply in person 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Monday–Wednesday, 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Thursday and 8 a.m.–noon Friday [17].
  • Fee: $24.00 by cash, check or credit card. The license is valid for 90 days [17].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at a city or town clerk. Apply where either of you lives if one of you lives in Rhode Island; if neither does, apply where the ceremony will be. RIDOH lists the fee as $24, and the license is good for 3 months [18][19].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): R.I. Gen. Laws § 15-2-9 sets the clerk's license fee at $14.00 [20], while RIDOH lists $24 [19]. We didn't find which law adds the other $10 (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Clerks charge the $24.

Land records (City Clerk, Recorder of Deeds)

  • Recording: in person, by e-recording, by mail or through the City Hall drop box. Hours: 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Monday–Wednesday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Thursday, 8–11:30 a.m. Friday [21].
  • City fee list (includes the $4 RI Historical Records Trust fee): warranty or quitclaim deed $84.00 and mortgage $64.00, plus $1.00 per extra page [21].
  • Copies: $1.50 a page plus $3.00 per document certification. Records from 1969 on are online [21].
  • Recording fees (statewide law): R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-13-7 sets a base fee by document type, such as $80 for a deed and $60 for a mortgage, plus $1 for each extra page [22]. A $4 Historical Records Trust charge is added to each recorded document [23]. That's why clerks post $84 and $64.
  • Copies (statewide law): § 34-13-9 sets copies of recorded documents at $1.50 a page and $3.00 to certify [24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Rhode Island Department of State, Business Services Division, 148 West River Street, Providence. Bring or mail the document with a request form; checks and money orders go to the RI Department of State [25].
  • Fee (statewide): $5 per document [25]. Ordering online adds a $0.75 enhanced access fee per document; you still mail in the original [26]. For related documents in one matter filed together, the total is capped at $150 [27].
  • Returns (statewide): orders come back by USPS first-class mail unless you include a prepaid DHL, FedEx or UPS label [25].
  • Vital records (statewide): only state-issued copies can be apostilled [15]. You can ask RIDOH to send the record directly to the Department of State [25].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [28]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past event: the Rhode Island Resource Recovery Corporation listed an RI Credit Union shred day at 594 Central Ave. on Saturday, May 2, 2026, a third-party event [29]. It has passed.
  • Upcoming events: we didn't find an upcoming official shredding event in Pawtucket (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Questions

Does Pawtucket Public Library take passport applications?

Not right now. The locator says it is currently not accepting passport applications [1].

Where can I get something notarized free in Pawtucket?

The library lists the City Clerk's Office at City Hall as free [2].

Can the Pawtucket library fax machine receive faxes?

No. It sends only [3].

How much is a birth certificate in Pawtucket?

$22 in person or $25 by mail or online, plus $18 for each extra copy [4].

How much can a Rhode Island notary charge?

Up to $25 per document or notarization, disclosed in advance (statewide) [9].
