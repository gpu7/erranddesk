Bangor, Maine document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Bangor, Maine, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Bangor, Bangor Public Library, Penobscot County Registry of Deeds, the State of Maine, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Bangor (Bangor Post Office) [1]. Clerk & Treasury fee schedule: first vital copy $15, marriage license $40, dedimus/attestation $5 [2]. Bangor Public Library has printers, copiers and scanners (we didn't find a published per-page price) [3].
Counties in Maine (statewide): Maine has 16 counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds (not by town clerks). Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the Maine CDC Division of Vital Records [4][5][6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Bangor Public Library: 145 Harlow St., Bangor, ME 04401 / (207) 947-8336. Services include public computing, printers, copiers and scanners. We didn't find a published per-page print price (last checked Nov 5, 2026) — ask at the desk [3].
  • FedEx: Bangor listing shows 6 locations, including a FedEx Ship Center at 124 Banair Dr [7].
  • The UPS Store: 499 Broadway advertises printing, faxing, scanning, shredding and notary [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Bangor, Maine

  • City Clerk / dedimus: Clerk & Treasury fee schedule (Revised 06/30/2026, CO 16-123, adopted March 28, 2016) lists Dedimus Justice $5 and Attestation $5; Photocopies $0.10; Fax $1. The schedule has no separate “notary” line. Office hours Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:15 p.m. at 73 Harlow St. [2][9].
  • The UPS Store: 499 Broadway advertises notary; fees vary. Maine does not set statutory notary customer fees [8][10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maine charges $50 for a notary public commission that lasts seven years [11]. The Secretary of State's Course of Study says Maine does not set statutory fees for notarial acts; any fee must be disclosed in writing and agreed to before the act [10]. Remote and electronic notarization need separate SOS approval [11].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Bangor City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 73 Harlow Street, Bangor, ME 04401. Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4:15 p.m. [9][15].
  • Fees: Clerk & Treasury schedule: 1st Copy 15$; marriage license 40$. Marriage page: $15 first certified copy / $6 each additional; marriage license application $40; City Hall ceremony $200 by appointment [2][16][17].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): 30-A M.R.S. § 2652 sets municipal clerk fees at $15 for the first certified copy of a birth, marriage, or death record and $6 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [17].
  • Maine CDC Division of Vital Records (statewide): 220 Capitol Street, Augusta. Certificate fee $15.00 per certificate, $6.00 for each additional copy ordered at the same time; VitalChek online orders add a service fee [4].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified vital record from a clerk or Maine CDC, then request a special certification (apostille or authentication) from the Secretary of State [18][4].

Marriage licenses (Bangor City Clerk)

  • Fee: $40 application [16][2].
  • Ceremony: $200 at City Hall by appointment; preferred windows Monday–Friday 8–10 a.m. or 2:30–3:30 p.m. [16].
  • Waiting period: none; license valid 90 days anywhere in Maine (statewide) [17][19].
  • State rules (statewide): file marriage intentions with a municipal clerk (or the Department). The clerk fee is $40 for recording intentions and issuing a license ($20 each when two licenses are required). There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in Maine. Both parties must usually sign; you must be 18 or older [17][19].

Land records (Penobscot County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: Maine Registries directory lists Penobscot County at 97 Hammond St, PO Box 2070, Bangor, ME 04401; phone 207-942-8797; penobscotdeeds.com. Bangor is in Penobscot County [20][5].
  • Recording: statewide private instruments $40 total ($35 + $5 surcharge). Confirm current local add-ons with the registry [6][21].
  • Recording fees (statewide): 33 M.R.S. § 751 sets a flat $35 per instrument for most private submitters ($25 when the State or a municipality records). 33 M.R.S. § 752 adds a $5 records-preservation surcharge for most non-government recordings, so the usual private total is $40 per instrument [6][21]. The Maine Registry of Deeds Association posts the same private $40 ($35 + $5) and $25 for state/municipal submitters; it lists plans at $50 per sheet [22]. § 751 lists plans at $45 — association and statute figures both cited; confirm with the registry [6][22].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maine Secretary of State, Bureau of Corporations, Elections & Commissions. Mail: 101 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0101. Courier: 6 E. Chestnut Street, Augusta. Phone 207-624-7752 [18].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per special certification (apostille or authentication), payable to the Secretary of State. Processing is typically 10–15 business days; call for an appointment when more than 5 documents need certification [18].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, documents, and payment; VitalChek orders can be forwarded to this office on request [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I get a notary in Bangor, Maine?

City Clerk fee schedule (Revised 06/30/2026) lists Dedimus Justice $5 / Attestation $5 at 73 Harlow St and has no separate notary line [2]. The UPS Store at 499 Broadway also advertises notary [8].

How much is a birth certificate in Bangor, Maine?

$15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional copy [16][2].

Where is the Bangor passport office?

Bangor Post Office is the one locator facility with a Bangor address — book at usps.com/scheduler [1].

Where do I record a deed for Bangor property?

Penobscot County Registry of Deeds, 97 Hammond St, Bangor. Private recording is $40 under the statewide schedule [20][6].
