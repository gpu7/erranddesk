Edmond, Oklahoma document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Edmond, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Edmond: Edmond Library is part of the Metropolitan Library System [1]. The Oklahoma County Court Clerk has an Edmond satellite office at 7 N. Broadway that issues marriage licenses and accepts passport applications [2][3][4]; the Oklahoma County Clerk records deeds in Oklahoma City [5]. Birth and death certificates come from the Oklahoma State Department of Health, which has a Will Call pickup in Oklahoma City [6], and apostilles from the Oklahoma Secretary of State, also in Oklahoma City [7]. This guide covers Oklahoma County offices.
Short version: Metropolitan Library System prints and copies are $0.10 a page in black and white and $0.50 in color, and scanning and outgoing faxing to U.S. numbers are free [8]. The passport locator lists 2 Edmond facilities: Coffee Creek Station post office and the Oklahoma County Court Clerk's Edmond office [9]. Marriage licenses are $50, or $5 with an original premarital counseling certificate [3]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [10], and apostilles are $25.00 at the Secretary of State [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Metropolitan Library System

  • Prints: every Metro library has printers for its public computers: $0.10 a page in black and white and $0.50 in color, paid with cash or a debit or credit card. You can also send a print job from your own computer or phone for the same prices [8].
  • Copies, scans and fax: every Metro library has photocopiers that scan to email or a USB drive and send faxes to U.S. numbers. Scanning and faxing are free; copies are $0.10 a page in black and white and $0.50 in color. Incoming faxes and international faxes aren't supported [8].
  • Card hold: paying for prints or copies by debit or credit card puts a $10 hold on the card; unused funds are refunded in 24–48 hours [8].
  • Edmond Library: 10 S. Boulevard, (405) 341-9282. Hours: Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–9 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–6 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., Sunday 1–6 p.m. Its page lists a color copy machine with free outbound faxing (U.S. numbers only) and scanning, and mobile and offsite printing [1].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 3 locations in Edmond: 2011 W Danforth Rd, (405) 562-2148; 3126 S Boulevard St, (405) 348-0334; and 1050 E 2nd St, (405) 359-2226. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [12].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Edmond

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Metropolitan Library System's site: its notary page loads but shows no notary information, and a search of the site found none [13]. Call Edmond Library at (405) 341-9282.
  • The UPS Store: its Edmond page lists notarizing [11]. We didn't find store notary prices; Oklahoma's legal maximum is below.
  • Fee cap (statewide): under 49 O.S. § 5, the most an Oklahoma notary may charge is $5.00 for each notarial act, and notarizing an official absentee ballot affidavit is free [10]. The Secretary of State's Notary Public Guide (dated 12/2025) gives the same $5.00 maximum and the same absentee ballot rule [14].
  • Remote online notarization (statewide): 49 O.S. § 209 lets a notary charge up to $25.00 for a remote online notarial act [15].

A $35 acceptance fee is paid separately to the acceptance facility, and expedited service is $60 more [17].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Oklahoma State Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees: OSDH Vital Records charges $15.00 per certified birth or death certificate. Phone and online orders add a $5.00 convenience fee for Oklahoma residents or $12.95 for non-residents [18]; the FAQ puts phone and online orders at $20.00 for the first copy and $15 for each additional copy [18].
  • No walk-ins: the Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins. Online orders (through VitalChek) and phone orders at (405) 426-8880 can be picked up at "Will Call" in Oklahoma City, Tulsa or McAlester, usually in about 2 business days; bring a photo ID and the order confirmation [6][19]. Will Call hours are 12:00–4:45 p.m., Monday–Friday [18].
  • By mail: send the application, a photo ID and a check or money order payable to OSDH to Vital Records Service, Oklahoma State Department of Health, PO Box 248964, Oklahoma City, OK 73124-8964 [18][20]. OSDH lists about 4–6 weeks for mail orders [6], and its death certificate page says mail orders may take 6 weeks [19].
  • Marriage and divorce records: OSDH points these to the county court clerk where the license or divorce was filed [6]; the county block below has the local office.

Marriage licenses: Oklahoma County Court Clerk

  • Fee: $50, or $5 if you bring an original certificate showing you finished a premarital counseling program under 43 O.S. § 5.1. Copies, faxes and emailed certificates aren't accepted [3].
  • How: Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. except holidays; call 405-713-1705 to book an appointment. Bring proof of age. There's no residency requirement and no waiting period unless an applicant is under 18 (then 72 hours) [3].
  • Where: the Edmond satellite office at 7 N. Broadway, Edmond, OK 73034, 405-359-4515, or 320 Robert S. Kerr Avenue, Room 500, Oklahoma City, 405-713-1705. Oklahoma County judges don't perform daily marriage ceremonies [3].
  • Court Clerk hours: the Court Clerk's page lists the Satellite Office (Edmond) at 7 North Broadway, 405-359-4515, and hours of Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., open during the lunch hour. It doesn't say whether those hours differ at the Edmond office [2]; the passport locator lists Edmond passport hours of 8 a.m.–4 p.m., closed noon–1 [9].

Court records and marriage license copies: Oklahoma County Court Clerk

  • Fees: copies are $1.00 for the first page and $0.50 for each additional page, certification is $0.50 per document, a certified marriage license is $2.00 and an authentication certificate is $5.00 [21].
  • Online request: fill in the court's online records request form. The office emails you within 10 business days to confirm the fee, you pay by check or money order, and it takes 10 business days after payment [21].

Recorded documents: Oklahoma County Clerk

  • Recording fees: $18 for the first page of a deed, mortgage or other instrument and $2 for each additional page, including the $10.00 preservation fee [5].
  • Copies: $1 a page for copies of recorded instruments, $3 for a paper plat copy and $5 for Mylar, and $0.25 per electronic image. Redacted "unofficial" land record images are free [5].
  • Where: Oklahoma County Annex Building, 320 Robert S. Kerr Avenue, #203, Oklahoma City, 405-713-1540, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., closed on holidays [5]. The County Clerk's page lists only this office; it doesn't list an Edmond office [5].
  • County clerk fees (statewide): 28 O.S. § 32 sets county clerk recording fees of $8.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, plus a $10.00 preservation fee per instrument, and $1.00 a page for copies and $1.00 a page to certify them [22].

Court clerk copy fees (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): under 28 O.S. § 31, a court clerk charges $1.00 for the first page of a copy of a record and $0.50 for each additional page, $0.50 to certify an instrument and $5.00 to authenticate court records. Recording a marriage license is $5.00 with a premarital counseling certificate (43 O.S. § 5.1) or $50.00 without one [23]. A judge or justice may also collect a $10.00 marriage ceremony fee on top of that (28 O.S. § 56) [24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Oklahoma apostilles and authentications come from the Secretary of State, not the county. They can be attached to an original notarized document or a certified copy of a recordable document, such as an Oklahoma birth certificate, marriage license or divorce decree [7].
  • Fee: $25.00 per apostille (per notarial act or certified document), or $10.00 per apostille for international adoption proceedings; an authentication is $20.00. Credit card payments add a 4% convenience fee [7].
  • In person: 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, (405) 521-4211, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. In person you can pay by cash, cashier's check, money order, credit card or business check [7].
  • By mail: include a cover letter naming the country, a phone number or email address and the return mailing address, and pay by certified cashier's check, money order or business check payable to Oklahoma Secretary of State [7]. See the apostille guide.

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [25]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I fax at the library in Edmond?

Yes. Edmond Library lists free outbound faxing to U.S. numbers [1]; Metro library copiers can't receive faxes [8].

How much does printing cost at the library?

$0.10 a page in black and white and $0.50 in color, for prints and copies; scanning is free [8].

Where can I get something notarized in Edmond?

The UPS Store's Edmond page lists notarizing [11]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [10].

Where can I apply for a passport in Edmond?

The locator lists Coffee Creek Station and the Oklahoma County Court Clerk's office at 7 North Broadway [9]. The Edmond office doesn't accept cash for the execution fee [4].

Can I get a marriage license in Edmond?

Yes, at the Oklahoma County Court Clerk's Edmond satellite office, 7 N. Broadway: $50, or $5 with an original premarital counseling certificate [3].

Can I walk in for a birth certificate?

No. OSDH Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins; order online or by phone and pick up at Will Call in Oklahoma City, or order by mail (statewide) [6].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Oklahoma Secretary of State, 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, for $25.00 per apostille (statewide) [7].
