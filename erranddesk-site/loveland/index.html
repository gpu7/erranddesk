Loveland, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Loveland, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Loveland, Loveland Public Library, Larimer County, USPS, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Loveland: Larimer County has a Loveland office at 200 Peridot Ave, First Floor, Loveland, CO 80537, open Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. (except holidays), phone (970) 619-4521 [1]. It issues marriage and civil union licenses by appointment only [2]. Recording a document over the counter is at the Fort Collins office only [2], and the Loveland office doesn't offer birth or death certificates [6]. Passport search results for "Loveland" also include Loveland, Ohio. That's a different city, so check that a result says Loveland, CO [7].
Short version: The Loveland Main Post Office at 446 East 29th Street takes passport applications by appointment [7]. Marriage and civil union licenses are issued at the Larimer County Loveland office by appointment only [2]. Birth certificates come from Larimer County Vital Records in Fort Collins, at $25 for the first copy [6].

Notary in Loveland

  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example stores): the stores at 1151 Eagle Dr and 1437 Denver Ave each say they have "a commissioned notary public on staff." Their notary pages don't post a price [10][11]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [12].
  • Larimer County Clerk and Recorder: the county notarizes for $10 per document, but at 200 West Oak Street in Fort Collins, with no appointment necessary [2][5]. We didn't find notary service listed for the Loveland office.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Larimer County Vital Records (Fort Collins)

  • Not in Loveland: the county says its Fort Collins location "is the only vital records office in Larimer and Jackson Counties," and its offices in Loveland and Estes Park "do not offer vital record services" [6].
  • Where: 1525 Blue Spruce Drive, Fort Collins, CO 80524, (970) 498-6710. Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–12 p.m. and 12:30–4:15 p.m., closed county holidays. In-person requests are by appointment only. You can also order by mail or online through VitalChek, which adds a $7.50 surcharge [6].
  • Fee (effective Jan 1, 2026): $25 for the first certified birth certificate, and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. The office issues birth certificates for any Colorado birth from 1900 to the present [6].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Larimer County Clerk and Recorder

  • Marriage or civil union license in Loveland: the Loveland office issues licenses by appointment only. Fill out the application online first. Licenses must be used within 35 days of issuance [2].
  • Recording: over-the-counter recording is at the Fort Collins location only [2]. You can also mail documents to Larimer County Clerk and Recorder, Attn: Recording Department, PO Box 1280, Fort Collins, CO 80522. Fees (flat, effective July 1, 2025): $43 per document; military discharge (DD-214) and death certificates are $0. Checks or money orders go to Larimer County Clerk [5].
  • Copies: $0.25 per page, and certified copies add $1.00 per document. Map copies are $2.00 per page [5]. Certified copies, including marriage and civil union licenses, can be requested by mail or phone; allow 72 hours [15].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [14]. The Larimer County Clerk and Recorder takes money orders for recording fees [5]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Loveland, Colorado?

The State Department's locator lists the Loveland Main Post Office at 446 East 29th Street, by appointment [7]. The Larimer County Clerk and Recorder also accepts applications by appointment at 200 West Oak Street in Fort Collins [3].

Can I get a birth certificate at the Larimer County office in Loveland?

No. The county says its Loveland and Estes Park offices don't offer vital record services. Go to 1525 Blue Spruce Drive in Fort Collins by appointment, or order by mail or online [6].

Can I get a marriage license in Loveland?

Yes, at the Larimer County Loveland office, by appointment only. Fill out the application online first [2].

How much does it cost to record a document in Larimer County?

$43 per document under the flat fees effective July 1, 2025. DD-214s and death certificates are $0 [5].
