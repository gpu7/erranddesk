Lake Charles, Louisiana document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Lake Charles, Louisiana, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Lake Charles, Calcasieu Parish, the Calcasieu Parish Public Library, the Calcasieu Parish Clerk of Court, the Louisiana Department of Health, the Louisiana Secretary of State, the State of Louisiana, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: the Calcasieu Parish Public Library (CPPL) charges $0.20 a page for black-and-white printing and copying and $0.50 for color, and its fax is outgoing only [1]. The Calcasieu Parish Clerk of Court accepts passport applications by appointment only [2]. Birth and death certificates aren't sold at the Lake Charles courthouse; the clerk issues them only at its office in Sulphur [3]. Marriage licenses are $27.50, by appointment [4]. Lake Charles is in Calcasieu Parish [5].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Calcasieu Parish Public Library (CPPL)

  • Printing and copying: black and white is $0.20 a page and color is $0.50 a page [1]. The FAQ lists the same prices [6].
  • Mobile printing: send a job through the ePRINTit website, the ePRINTit SaaS app or email (bw-cppl@eprintitsaas.com for grayscale, color-cppl@eprintitsaas.com for color), then pick it up at any branch [1].
  • Fax: available at all branches, outgoing only; incoming faxes can't be received [1].
  • Scanning: at the Central, Sulphur Regional, Moss Bluff and SWLA Genealogical & Historical libraries, on coin-operated machines that scan both sides of a page [1].
  • Cost of fax and scanning: the same page says fax, scanning, charging stations, device checkout and PCs "are available at no additional cost", but also calls the scanners coin-operated. We didn't find a fax or scan price. Confirm with the library [1].
  • Public PCs: sessions last up to two hours, with two sessions a day. USB flash drives work on the PCs, and the Central Library sells flash drives for $5. Wi-Fi is available daily from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. at all locations [1].
  • Branches: the Central Branch is at 301 W. Claude Street, (337) 721-7116, and the Census Geocoder places it inside Lake Charles city. The Moss Bluff Branch, 261 Parish Road, (337) 721-7128, has a Lake Charles mailing address, but the geocoder didn't match it, so we couldn't confirm whether it's inside the city [6][7]. We didn't find branch hours on the pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 2 locations with Lake Charles addresses: 630 W Prien Lake Rd, Ste. B, (337) 478-0691; and 5400 Nelson Road, Suite 400, (337) 990-5591. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [8]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [9].
  • Inside the city: the Census Geocoder places 630 W Prien Lake Rd inside Lake Charles city, and places 5400 Nelson Road outside any incorporated place [8][7].

Notary in Lake Charles

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the CPPL pages we checked [1][6].
  • The UPS Store: its Lake Charles page lists notarizing [8].
  • Fees (statewide): the Louisiana Secretary of State says Louisiana "does not have a statutory fee schedule which would determine or limit what a notary can charge for his services." Ask for the price before the notary starts [10].
  • How Louisiana notaries differ (statewide): the Secretary of State says a Louisiana notary's signature is his seal, notaries are commissioned for life, and each is commissioned in the parish where they live; reciprocal parish groups set by law let a notary work in neighboring parishes. Attorney-notaries have statewide notarial powers. Its Notary Database lists contact information for all notaries [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death records: Louisiana Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): from the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH) Vital Records, a long-form birth certificate is $15.00, a birth certificate plus short-form birth card is $24.00 a pair, and a death certificate is $7.00 a copy. Bought at a participating Clerk of Court instead, a long-form birth certificate is $34.00, a death certificate $26.00 and the birth card plus long-form pair $48.00. Mail-in and VitalChek orders add a $0.50 state charge, and VitalChek internet, phone and kiosk orders add a security fee. If no record is found, state law has LDH keep the fee for the search [16]. LDH's FAQ lists the short-form birth card alone at $9.00 and says some agencies may not accept it [17].
  • Closed records (statewide): Louisiana birth and death certificates aren't public records. Requests need proper ID, the fee and an application, and only the person named, close family or their attorney can order a birth certificate [18].
  • Walk-in (statewide office): the Vital Records Central Office, 1450 Poydras St., Suite 400, New Orleans, LA 70112, is open for walk-in service 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., excluding state holidays. Bring photo ID; walk-in payment is cash (exact amount), check or money order [18]. Customer service: 504-593-5100 [19].
  • Clerks of Court: participating Clerks of Court sell certified birth copies (long and short form), and death certificates for deaths on or after July 7, 2012, for an added fee [18][20].
  • Mail and online: mail the application, a copy of photo ID and the fee to Vital Records Registry, PO Box 60630, New Orleans, LA 70160, and allow about 8–10 weeks. Internet, phone and fax orders go through VitalChek (1-877-605-8562), which charges extra [18].
  • Marriage and divorce records (statewide): LDH keeps only Orleans Parish marriage records; for a divorce decree, contact the Clerk of Court in the parish where the divorce was granted [21].
  • Not in Lake Charles: the Calcasieu Parish Clerk of Court issues birth and death certificates only at its West Calcasieu Annex in Sulphur, not at the Lake Charles courthouse [3]. The Census Geocoder places the annex in the city of Sulphur [7].
  • Annex address: the clerk lists a new location at 1300 E. Napoleon St., Sulphur (shared with the DMV office), 337-990-8279 [3][22]. LDH's list of Clerk of Court issuance locations still shows 500A N. Huntington Street, Sulphur, (337) 313-1140. Confirm with the office [23].
  • Hours and fees there: Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., including lunch, and no certificate applications after 3:30 p.m. Birth certificate $34, birth certificate plus card $48 and death certificate $26. Cash and credit cards are accepted (minimum card processing fee $2.95, no bills over $20); checks and money orders aren't [3].
  • Wait: about 30 minutes on average; births before 1983 can take up to an hour. Death certificates usually reach the clerk 30 days after the date of death [3].
  • Death certificate dates: the clerk says it can provide Louisiana death certificates registered from July 9, 2012 forward; LDH says Clerks of Court sell them for deaths on or after July 7, 2012. Confirm with the office [3][20].
  • ID: a current photo ID, such as a state driver's license or ID card, U.S. passport or military ID, with clear issue and expiration dates [3].

Marriage licenses: Calcasieu Parish Clerk of Court

  • Appointments only: all couples must book an appointment between 9 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.; no walk-ins. Call 337-437-3558 ext. 492 and ask for Recording Dept / Marriage License appointments [4].
  • Apply online first: you get a confirmation number to bring. If you have extenuating circumstances, you can arrive 30 minutes before the appointment and fill out the application on a tablet [24]. Allow 30–40 minutes for processing [4].
  • Fees: license $27.50, cash or credit card only, no bills larger than $20. A certified copy is $10, a souvenir copy $5 and waivers $4 a page; the card processing fee is $2 [4][13]. Correcting a mistake after you get the license costs $10 [4].
  • Who signs: both parties before a deputy clerk, or one before a notary and one before a deputy clerk, among other options the clerk lists [4].
  • Birth certificate: if born in Louisiana, a certified copy (short form acceptable). If born in another U.S. state or territory, a certified copy (short form not allowed). If born outside the U.S., one of the IDs the clerk lists [4].
  • ID: a valid, unexpired driver's license, government ID, passport or visa with I-94. LA Wallet is accepted only with a physical copy of the license for the clerk to keep [4].
  • Previous marriages: know the date the last one ended; no papers are needed unless the divorce was within 30 days, which requires a certified copy of the judgment [4].
  • Timing and age: buy it at least 24 hours before the ceremony; it expires 30 days after issuance, with no refunds. 24-hour waivers are only issued in extenuating circumstances, at the judges' discretion. No one 15 or under can marry; 16- and 17-year-olds need judicial authorization and both parents present, and can't marry an adult three or more years older [4].
  • Old licenses: licenses from 1987 on are in the Recording Department; 1910 to 1986 are in the archives, Criminal Records Department [25].

Recorded documents and court copies: Clerk of Court

  • Recording fees (as of July 1, 2025): per recording book, $105 for 1–5 pages, $205 for 6–25, $305 for 26–50, and $305 plus $5 a page over 50. That includes up to 10 indexed names, a certified copy and the LCRAA Portal Fee; each name after the 10th is $5. Paper larger than 8½ x 14 is $20 a page, and a single cancellation or partial release is $55 [13].
  • Civil records copies: $1.00 a page; true copies $5; certification of mortgages and conveyances $10.00; a 10-year civil name search $15.00 per name [13].
  • Court copies: civil filings list copies at $1.00 a page and certified copies at $5.00 per seal (plus $1.00 a page if the clerk makes the copy). Criminal records lists copies at $1 a page (excluding court minutes), certifications $5, extracts of court minutes $10 and criminal background checks $20 [13].
  • Emailed or faxed copies: $5 for the 1st page plus $1 for each added page; card processing fee $2 [13].
  • Courthouse: 1000 Ryan Street, Lake Charles; main phone 337-437-3550. Open 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday; passports and birth and death certificates aren't processed after 3:30 p.m. [12]. Scheduled closures follow the Louisiana Clerks' Association holiday list [26]. The Recording Department's direct line is 337-437-3558 [25].
  • Divorce decrees: LDH says to contact the Clerk of Court in the parish where the divorce was granted [21].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Fee: the Louisiana Secretary of State charges $20 per document, or $10 per document if it's related to an adoption [27]. Its request form adds a $5.00 state fee for credit card users and says to allow two to three business days for processing [28].
  • Where: Commissions Division, 8585 Archives Ave., Baton Rouge, LA 70809. Walk-in service is Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., except state holidays and closures; questions 225.922.0330. By USPS mail: P.O. Box 94125, Baton Rouge, LA 70804-9125. Include a written request naming the country, a daytime phone number and a prepaid return envelope or label [27].
  • Which copy: send the original document. Documents issued by Clerks of Court and Louisiana Vital Records don't need notarizing, and photocopies of them aren't accepted. Other documents must be notarized by a Louisiana notary, with the notary's printed name and ID number beneath the signature [27].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [29]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is printing at the Lake Charles library?

$0.20 a page for black and white and $0.50 for color [1].

Can I receive a fax at the library?

No. CPPL fax is outgoing only [1].

Can I walk in for a passport at the Clerk of Court?

No. Passports are by appointment only: 337-437-3558, ext. 147 [2].

Can I get a birth certificate at the Lake Charles courthouse?

No. The clerk issues birth and death certificates only at its Sulphur office: $34 for a birth certificate [3]. From LDH it's $15.00 (statewide fee) [16].

How much is a marriage license in Calcasieu Parish?

$27.50, cash or credit card only, by appointment; a certified copy is $10 [4].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Louisiana Secretary of State in Baton Rouge, in person or by mail: $20 per document (statewide) [27].
