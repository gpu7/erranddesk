Reading, Pennsylvania document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Reading, Pennsylvania, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with Berks County, its Register of Wills, Recorder of Deeds or Prothonotary, the City of Reading, Reading Public Library, the Pennsylvania Department of State, the Pennsylvania Department of Health, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: Reading Public Library prints for 25 cents a black-and-white page and sends or receives faxes for $1 a fax [1]. Berks County's marriage license is $65, with a virtual appointment [2]. Deed copies are 50 cents a page [3]. The Main library accepts passports [4].
Who does what: Reading is in Berks County [5][6]. The Register of Wills issues marriage licenses [2], the Recorder of Deeds keeps deeds [3] and the Prothonotary keeps civil court records, including divorce decrees [7]. All three are at 633 Court Street.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Reading Public Library

  • Printing: 25 cents per black-and-white page, 50 cents color, from any branch computer or from a phone by email [1].
  • Copies: 25 cents black-and-white letter size, 50 cents legal size; color copies, 50 cents, only at the Northeast, Northwest and Southeast branches. Double-sided counts as 2 pages [1].
  • Fax: at all branches, sent by staff: $1 per fax to U.S. numbers including Puerto Rico, no international faxing; receiving is also $1 per fax [1].
  • Scanning: free to a USB drive [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Reading page lists 2 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [8]. The Census Geocoder places neither inside Reading city [9]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Reading

  • Library and county: we didn't find a public notary service on the Reading Public Library or Berks County pages we checked. The Recorder of Deeds' schedule is about notaries' own commissions, not notarizing documents for the public [3].
  • The UPS Store: its Reading page lists notarizing [8].
  • Notary fees (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State sets notary fees by regulation, and a notary can't charge more. The maximums are $5 per acknowledgment (plus $2 for each additional name on the same certificate), $5 per oath or affirmation, $5 per verification, $5 per signature witnessed, $5 per certified copy and $3 a page to note a protest. Electronic and remote notaries may add up to $20 per act [10].
  • Extra charges (statewide): a notary may waive the fee. Clerical or administrative fees, such as copying, postage or travel, aren't set by the Department; they must be reasonable, disclosed before the notarization and itemized on the receipt [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $20 per birth certificate and $20 per death certificate from the Pennsylvania Department of Health; online orders through VitalChek, the state's only authorized online vendor, add a $10 fee [13][14].
  • Who can order (statewide): birth certificate applicants must be at least 16 for in-person and mail requests; death certificate applicants must be at least 18 [13][14].
  • In person (statewide): the Department of Health has six Vital Records public offices: Erie, Harrisburg, New Castle, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Scranton. A request received before 2:30 p.m. may be filled the same day [15].

Marriage licenses (Register of Wills)

  • Fee: $65 by Visa or Mastercard; license reprints are $15 [2].
  • How: apply online, then attend a virtual appointment. If you don't finish within 60 days, the license expires and you pay the $65 again. Pick up at the Register of Wills, 2nd floor, Berks County Services Center, 633 Court Street, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. [2].
  • Certified copies: mail the request form with the fee by check or money order. Berks can only certify licenses it issued [16]. We didn't find the copy fee on the page.

Deeds (Recorder of Deeds)

  • Copies: 50 cents per letter-size page (schedule effective Nov 14, 2025). Berks County Services Center, 3rd floor, 610-478-3380, 8 a.m.–4 p.m. weekdays [3].
  • Certification, sources differ: the dated fee schedule (effective Nov 14, 2025) lists certifications at $2.00 and e-certifications at $10.00 [3]. The office's undated Consumer Notice says certification is $10.00 per document [17]. Call 610-478-3380 to confirm (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Court records and divorce decrees (Prothonotary)

  • Office: Berks County Courthouse, 2nd floor, 633 Court Street, 610-478-6970, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. It posts a copy request form and a divorce decree request form [7].
  • Fees (fee bill effective Sept 28, 2026): copies $0.25 a page; certifying a copy or divorce decree $10.00; exemplification $45.00 [18] (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State, North Office Building, Room 201, 401 North Street, Harrisburg. The fee is $15 per document (not per page). Walk-in service no longer takes cash; checks, money orders and credit cards are accepted. The building's front doors lock after 3 p.m. Mail requests typically take two to three weeks, not counting mailing time [19].
  • County documents (statewide): marriage certificates must be signed by the register of wills or clerk of the orphans' court, not only the officiant [19].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Reading page lists shredding [8].
  • Berks County paper shredding (outside Reading): Saturday, April 17, 2027, 8 a.m.–noon, at the County of Berks South Campus, 400 E. Wyomissing Ave., Mohnton, outside Reading city. Free to Berks County residents; pre-registration required [21] (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Reading Public Library?

25 cents black-and-white, 50 cents color [1].

Can I receive a fax at the Reading library?

Yes, for $1 per fax; sending to U.S. numbers is also $1 per fax [1].

How much is a marriage license in Berks County?

$65 [2].

Where do I apply for a passport in Reading?

Reading Public Library's Main branch at 100 S 5th St is in the city; the locator also lists the Gus Yatron Post Office [4].
