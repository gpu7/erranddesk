Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: Carnegie Library of Pittsburgh prints for 25 cents a black-and-white page and faxes for 25 cents a page; scanning is free [1][2]. Allegheny County's marriage license is $90 [3]. Deed copies are $1 a page in person [4]. The county Department of Court Records in the City-County Building accepts passports [5].
Who does what: Pittsburgh is in Allegheny County [6][7]. The Department of Court Records handles marriage licenses, and its Civil/Family Division does the work of the former Prothonotary [3][8]. Deeds are with the Division of Real Estate (the Recorder of Deeds) [4].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Carnegie Library of Pittsburgh

  • Printing: $0.25 per black-and-white page, $0.50 per color page, from library computers or sent from your own device and picked up the same day [1].
  • Fax and scan: scanning to email or a USB drive is free; faxing is $0.25 a page. The machines send faxes but can't receive them. Stations are at ACLL, Allegheny, Beechview, Brookline, Downtown, Hazelwood, Homewood, LAMP, Lawrenceville, Main, Mt. Washington, South Side, Squirrel Hill and Woods Run [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Pittsburgh page lists 9 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. The Census Geocoder places only 2 of them, 1735 E Carson St and 3945 Forbes Ave, in Pittsburgh city; the others have Pittsburgh mailing addresses in nearby municipalities or didn't match [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Pittsburgh

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Carnegie Library pages we checked (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Pittsburgh page lists notarizing [9]. Notary rules vary by state [11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State sets notary fees by regulation, and a notary can't charge more. The maximums are $5 per acknowledgment (plus $2 for each additional name on the same certificate), $5 per oath or affirmation, $5 per verification, $5 per signature witnessed, $5 per certified copy and $3 a page to note a protest. Electronic and remote notaries may add up to $20 per act [12].
  • Extra charges (statewide): a notary may waive the fee. Clerical or administrative fees, such as copying, postage or travel, aren't set by the Department; they must be reasonable, disclosed before the notarization and itemized on the receipt [12].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $20 per birth certificate and $20 per death certificate from the Pennsylvania Department of Health; online orders through VitalChek, the state's only authorized online vendor, add a $10 fee [15][16].
  • Who can order (statewide): birth certificate applicants must be at least 16 for in-person and mail requests; death certificate applicants must be at least 18 [15][16].
  • In person (statewide): the Department of Health has six Vital Records public offices: Erie, Harrisburg, New Castle, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Scranton. A request received before 2:30 p.m. may be filled the same day [17].
  • Pittsburgh state office: 411 Seventh Avenue, Room 360, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. [17]. The Census Geocoder places it in Pittsburgh city [10].

Marriage licenses (Department of Court Records)

  • Fee: $90 total, including state taxes and surcharges, plus $10 for each prior divorce decree [3]. The county's pages differ on payment: the application page says cash or credit card [3], and the Department's FAQ adds money order [18].
  • How: City-County Building, 414 Grant Street, First Floor, 412-350-4230. The license is issued after a mandatory three-day wait and is valid for 60 days [3].

Deeds and recorded documents (Division of Real Estate)

  • In person: $1 a page, plus $1 per document to certify (fees effective Jan 1, 2026) [4]. Room 101 of the County Office Building; cash, certified or business check, or money order; no personal checks [19].
  • By mail: $10 per document request, to 542 Forbes Ave, Suite 101 [4].

Court records and divorce decrees (Department of Court Records, Civil/Family Division)

  • Fees: $20 for a certified copy and $25 for a search; no personal checks [20].
  • Office: 414 Grant Street, First Floor, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., 412-350-5729 [8].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Pennsylvania Department of State, North Office Building, Room 201, 401 North Street, Harrisburg. The fee is $15 per document (not per page). Walk-in service no longer takes cash; checks, money orders and credit cards are accepted. The building's front doors lock after 3 p.m. Mail requests typically take two to three weeks, not counting mailing time [21].
  • County documents (statewide): marriage certificates must be signed by the register of wills or clerk of the orphans' court, not only the officiant [21].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [22]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Pittsburgh page lists shredding [9]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.
  • Free event (upcoming): State Senator Jay Costa's office lists a free shredding event on November 14, 2026, at the Linton Middle School parking lot, 250 Aster Street, for individuals only, not businesses [23]. The page lists the time as 9 a.m.–noon but its text says 9:30 a.m. to noon. The Census Geocoder places the school in Penn Hills, outside Pittsburgh city [10].

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Carnegie Library of Pittsburgh?

$0.25 per black-and-white page, $0.50 color [1].

Can I fax at the Pittsburgh library?

Yes, at many branches, for $0.25 a page; the machines can't receive faxes [2].

How much is a marriage license in Allegheny County?

$90, plus $10 per prior divorce decree [3].

Where do I apply for a passport in Pittsburgh?

Inside the city, the locator lists the Department of Court Records at 414 Grant St and the Liberty Center Post Office at 1001 Liberty Ave [5][10].
