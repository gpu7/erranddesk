Essex Junction, Vermont document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Essex Junction, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Essex Junction, Brownell Library, the State of Vermont, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: we didn't find a passport acceptance facility with an Essex Junction address; the nearest locator listings are in Burlington city [1]. City Clerk notary is available during office hours (we didn't find a fee listed) [2]. Brownell Library: first 10 black-and-white pages free then 10¢; first 5 color free then 50¢; scanners available; we didn't find fax on the computers page [3]. Certified vital copies are $10 [4][5][6].
Counties in Vermont (statewide): Vermont has 14 counties. Land records are kept by each town or city clerk — there is no county registry of deeds. Vital records are issued by town and city clerks and by the Vermont Department of Health Vital Records Office [5][7][8].
Place name: the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 Gazetteer lists this place as Essex Junction city [9]. Census Places by County still labels it Essex Junction village [7]. The municipal site styles itself City of Essex Junction. We use the Gazetteer NAME (city) for map coordinates and this guide's place label.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Brownell Library (6 Lincoln St): first ten black-and-white pages are free, then 10 cents per page; first five color pages are free, then 50 cents per page. Flatbed and document scanners are available. Free Wi‑Fi. We didn't find fax listed on the library computers page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [3].
  • The UPS Store (Essex): 4 Carmichael St Ste 111 ((802) 879-6959). Provider city pages list this store under Essex, VT (same 05452 ZIP as Essex Junction). Store pages advertise printing, shipping, shredding, notarizing, faxing, passport photos and mailboxes [10][11].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Essex Junction page lists 1 location — FedEx at Walgreens, 9 Susie Wilson Rd — drop-off and hold for pickup of pre-packaged, pre-labeled shipments [12][13]. The same city page's marketing copy mentions "FedEx Office in Essex Junction" and passport photos at FedEx Office; the locations list does not name a staffed FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in Essex Junction (both cited; last checked Nov 5, 2026). FedEx's official locate search for Essex Junction, VT likewise lists no staffed FedEx Office in Essex Junction—Walgreens only [14]. Nearest Office-branded locations on locate: Ship Center at 921 Marshall Ave, Williston ((802) 923-4174, ~3.25 mi) and Print & Ship at 199 Main St, Burlington [14][15]. Burlington 199 Main lists self-service copying, printing, passport photos and shredding; we did not open the Williston detail page for service claims [15].

Notary in Essex Junction

  • City Clerk: 2 Lincoln Street. Hours Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (except holidays). The Clerk page lists notary services during normal business hours; we didn't find a notary fee on that page (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Phone 802-878-6944 option 7 [2].
  • The UPS Store (Essex): 4 Carmichael St Ste 111 advertises notary; fees vary by location. Vermont sets a $30 commission fee for notaries but we didn't find a statutory cap on customer notarial-act charges [11][16][17][18].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Vermont's notary chapter sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary public (26 V.S.A. § 5324). We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on what a Vermont notary may charge a customer for an ordinary notarial act in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Remote and electronic notarial acts need a special endorsement from the Office of Professional Regulation [17][18][19].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Essex Junction City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 2 Lincoln Street, Essex Junction, VT 05452. Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Phone 802-878-6944 option 7. Certified copies of birth and death records for any Vermont resident; complete the application with payment and ID [2][4].
  • Fees: the City's vital-records application form prices certified copies at $10.00 each [4]. Statewide VDH certified copies are also $10 (plus $2 online) [5][6].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Town or city clerk (statewide): Vermont town and city clerks issue certified copies of vital records; since 2019 clerks can print eligible Vermont birth and death records filed anywhere in the state [5].
  • Vermont Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): 280 State Drive, Waterbury, VT 05671-8300. Certified copies with raised seal are $10 each; online orders add a $2 processing fee [5][6].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified (or notarized, as required) copy from a clerk or VDH, then submit it to the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration for an apostille [23][5].

Marriage licenses (Essex Junction City Clerk)

  • Where: 2 Lincoln Street. Arrive with the completed application before 4:00 p.m. when possible [24][2].
  • Fee: $80; certified copy an additional $10. Cash, checks, and credit or debit cards (convenience fee on cards) [24].
  • Waiting period: none; the license is valid for 60 days from issuance and must be returned within ten days of the ceremony [24][25].
  • State rules (statewide): obtain a marriage license from any Vermont town or city clerk. The license costs $80, there is no waiting period, and it is valid for 60 days. Both parties must sign the application; at least one must sign in front of the clerk. You must be 18 or older [25].

Land records (Essex Junction City Clerk)

  • Office: 2 Lincoln Street. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system. Online Land Record Index is linked from the Clerk page [2][7].
  • Copies: copies from the web are $3.50 per page; in-office copies are $1 per page. No appointment needed for title searches during office hours [2].
  • Recording: we didn't find a local recording-fee schedule on the Clerk page beyond copy prices (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Statewide statute sets recording at $15.00 per page and certified copies of recorded documents at $10.00 per page — confirm at the counter [8].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): 32 V.S.A. § 1671 sets $15.00 per page to record most documents at the town or city clerk; certified copies of recorded documents are $10.00 per page; uncertified copies are $1.00 per page (minimum $2.00). A property transfer tax return filing is $15.00; survey plats are $25.00 per sheet size listed in the statute. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [8].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration (VSARA) on behalf of the Secretary of State. Physical/drop-box address: 1078 U.S. Route 2, Middlesex; mailing address 1078 U.S. Route 2 - Middlesex, Montpelier, VT 05633-7701 [23].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per apostille or authentication. Do not send cash by mail; credit and debit cards are not accepted. Checks or money orders payable to the VT Secretary of State, in U.S. dollars [23].
  • Mail (statewide): include the VSARA-35 request form, payment, and documents; preliminary review before submitting is encouraged [23].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

  • FedEx Office (Burlington): 199 Main St lists shredding among in-store services; confirm price before you go [15].

Questions

Where can I get a notary in Essex Junction?

City Clerk, 2 Lincoln Street, during normal business hours (we didn't find a fee listed) [2]. The UPS Store at 4 Carmichael St Ste 111 (listed under Essex, VT) also advertises notary [11].

How much is a birth certificate in Essex Junction?

Certified copies are $10.00 each on the City vital-records application [4]. VDH statewide is also $10 certified plus $2 online [5][6].

Is there a passport office in Essex Junction?

We didn't find one with an Essex Junction address in the federal locator. The nearest listed facilities are the Burlington Post Office (11 Elmwood Ave) and Chittenden Superior Court (175 Main Street) [1].

How much can a Vermont notary charge?

26 V.S.A. § 5324 sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary. We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on customer charges for ordinary notarial acts in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [17][18].

Where do I record a deed for Essex Junction property?

Essex Junction City Clerk land records, 2 Lincoln Street. Vermont has no county registry of deeds. Confirm current recording fees at the counter; statewide statute is $15 per page [2][8].
