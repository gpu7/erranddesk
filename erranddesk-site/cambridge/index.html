Cambridge, Massachusetts document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Cambridge, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Cambridge, Cambridge Public Library, Middlesex South Registry of Deeds, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Cambridge (Central Square and Harvard Square post offices) [1]. Cambridge Public Library gives 25 free B&W pages per day, then 15¢ [2]. Birth, death and marriage certificates are $15 (effective April 1, 2025) [3]. City Clerk notary is $5 per page [4]. Massachusetts does not cap ordinary notary fees (statewide) [5].
Counties in Massachusetts (statewide): Census geography still uses counties, but Berkshire, Essex, Franklin, Hampden, Hampshire, Middlesex and Worcester counties are no longer legal governmental units [6]. Land records are kept by county Registries of Deeds under the Secretary of the Commonwealth. Suffolk County and Boston city are governmentally consolidated [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Cambridge Public Library: black-and-white printing only at all locations. Up to 25 pages free each day; additional pages are 15¢ each (cash only). Wireless printing via camb.ma/printcpl. Scanning to printer, email or USB is available. We didn't find a listed fax fee on the library technology page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Cambridge page lists 3 locations, including FedEx Office Print & Ship Centers at 1 Eliot St. and 600 Technology Sq. Suite 110, plus FedEx at Walgreens (330 River St.) [7].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Cambridge stores at 955 Massachusetts Ave. ((617) 868-5055) and 1770 Mass Ave. ((617) 661-7171) [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Cambridge

  • City Clerk (795 Massachusetts Ave., Room 103): $5.00 per page effective April 1, 2025. Cash recommended (credit card adds $2.95 processing). Does not notarize deeds, mortgages, wills, living wills, living trusts, codicils, depositions, incomplete documents, or real-estate closing documents [4].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [9].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Massachusetts does not set a maximum fee for ordinary notarial acts such as acknowledgments and jurats. G.L. c. 262, § 41's $1.25 figure applies only to "noting" a dishonored negotiable instrument [10]. The Supreme Judicial Court held that cap does not limit fees for other notarial acts [5]. Ask the fee before you sit down.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Cambridge City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: City Hall, 795 Massachusetts Ave., Room 103. Monday 8:30 a.m.–8 p.m.; Tuesday–Thursday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m.; Friday 8:30 a.m.–12 p.m. Phone 617-349-4260 [3].
  • Fees: certified birth, death and marriage copies are $15 each (effective April 1, 2025). Online orders accept card (2.95% / $2.95 minimum) or e-check ($0.40); e-check takes 10 business days [3][14][15].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): you can usually get a certified copy from the city or town where the event happened (or where marriage intentions were filed). Fees vary by city or town [16].
  • State Registry of Vital Records and Statistics (statewide): 150 Mt. Vernon St., 1st Floor, Dorchester. Counter hours Monday–Friday 8:45 a.m.–4:45 p.m. State fees: $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54 for the first copy ordered online or by phone ($42 for each additional copy in that order) [17].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): bring a certified copy with an original signature of a Massachusetts notary, city/town clerk, court clerk or Registrar of Vital Records to the Secretary of the Commonwealth [18].

Marriage licenses (Cambridge City Clerk)

  • How: both of you apply in person (cannot be done by mail). Marriage application hours: Monday 8:30 a.m.–7:30 p.m.; Tue–Thu 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Friday 8:30 a.m.–11:30 a.m. [19].
  • Fee: $50 to apply for a marriage license [19].
  • Waiting period: 3-day wait; license valid 60 days and only in Massachusetts [19].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at any Massachusetts city or town clerk. There is a 3-day waiting period; the license is valid for 60 days and only inside Massachusetts. You must both be 18 or older. License fees vary by town [16].

Land records (Middlesex South Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 208 Cambridge St., Cambridge, MA 02141. Office 8 a.m.–4 p.m.; recording 8 a.m.–3:45 p.m. Monday–Friday. Phone 617-679-6300 [20].
  • Recording fees: deed $155, mortgage $205, mortgage discharge $105, declaration of homestead $35. Cash or check to Middlesex South Registry of Deeds or Commonwealth of Massachusetts [21].
  • Copies: certified copies $1 per page [21].
  • Recording fees (county registries): each Registry of Deeds posts its own fee schedule (often a flat amount per document). See the city section for that county's posted fees.

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth. Walk-in at Commissions Section, One Ashburton Place, Room 1719, Boston, or regional offices in Springfield and Fall River [18][22].
  • Fee (statewide): $6.00 per document; check or money order payable to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts [18][22].
  • Mail (statewide): mailed requests take about two to three weeks; include the destination country and a prepaid return envelope [18].
  • Counter limit (statewide): up to three documents per customer at the Boston counter; four or more are ready the next business day after 3 p.m. [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • City of Cambridge document shredding (Consumers' Council): free for Cambridge residents only; first come, first served (or until the truck is full); limit 5 paper-size boxes per household. 2026 dates at the Cambridge Senior Center, 806 Massachusetts Ave.: Saturday May 16, 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m.; Monday August 3, 3:00–5:30 p.m.; Saturday September 19, 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. [24].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I get a notary in Cambridge?

City Clerk notary is $5 per page at Room 103 (cash recommended). The UPS Store also offers notary; fees vary [4][9].

How much is a birth certificate in Cambridge?

$15 per certified copy (effective April 1, 2025) at City Hall Room 103 [3]. Statewide RVRS copies are $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54/$42 online or by phone [17].

How much can a Massachusetts notary charge?

There is no statewide maximum for ordinary notarial acts. The $1.25 figure in G.L. c. 262, § 41 applies only to "noting" a dishonored instrument (statewide) [10][5]. Ask the fee first.

Can I get an apostille on a Cambridge birth certificate?

Bring a certified copy with an original clerk or RVRS signature to the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The apostille is $6 per document (statewide) [18][22].
