Yonkers, New York document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Yonkers, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide, countywide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Yonkers, Westchester County, the Yonkers Public Library, New York State, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: 2 passport acceptance facilities are inside the city: the Yonkers City Clerk and the main post office [1][2]. A marriage license is $40 at City Hall, with no appointment needed [3]. New York notaries may charge $2.00 per act (statewide) [4]. Yonkers Public Library sends faxes free at three branches [5].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Yonkers Public Library printing: computer-room printing is 15¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color, cash only [6].
  • Library copiers: every branch has one. Copies are 15¢ in black and white; 35¢ color copies are at the Riverfront and Will libraries. The machines take coins and bills up to $1 [7].
  • Library fax: free sending at the Riverfront, Grinton I. Will and Crestwood libraries. You can't receive faxes [5].
  • The UPS Store: its Yonkers page lists 3 locations offering printing, shredding, notarizing and faxing [8]. Prices aren't listed online.
  • FedEx Office: FedEx's Yonkers page lists no FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in Yonkers (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [9].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Yonkers

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Yonkers Public Library pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Yonkers page lists notarizing [8]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a New York notary may charge $2.00 for an oath or affirmation, or for taking an acknowledgment or proof of execution [11]. Executive Law §136 sets $2 for an acknowledgment by one person and $2 for each additional person, plus $2 for each witness sworn [12]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [11]. The Westchester County Clerk's notary FAQ gives the same $2.00 fee [4].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Birth certificates (Yonkers City Clerk, local registrar)

  • Who: the City Clerk files birth certificates for people born in Yonkers. In person, you must be 18 or older with photo ID, and only the person named or a parent listed on the certificate can get one. Mail applications must be notarized [15].
  • Fee: $10 per copy. Cash or credit card payments are accepted in person only; checks and money orders go to the City of Yonkers [16]. Card transactions add 2.25% ($1.49 minimum). Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds a fee, and mail applications must be notarized [15].
  • State alternative: the New York State Department of Health issues certificates for births outside New York City for $30 by mail (statewide) [17].

Marriage licenses (Yonkers City Clerk)

  • Where and when: City Hall, 40 South Broadway. Couples come in together; no appointment is needed. Hours are 9 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, and 9 a.m.–6:30 p.m. Tuesday [3].
  • Fee and wait: $40, non-refundable. Cards carry a 2.25% fee ($1.49 minimum). There's a 24-hour wait, and the license must be used within 60 days [3].
  • Compared with New York City: the statewide fee outside NYC is $40 [18]; the NYC City Clerk charges $35 [19].

Deeds and land records (Westchester County Clerk, countywide)

  • Certified copies by mail: $5.00 for up to 4 pages plus $1.25 for each additional page, by check to "Westchester County Clerk". Cash isn't accepted [20].
  • Recording a deed: the statutory recording fee is $45 plus $5 per page; a 3-page residential deed in one municipality is $190 in total [21].
  • Plain copies: we didn't find an uncertified copy fee on the pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New York State Department of State, for $10 per document. You can apply by mail or walk in at its offices in New York City, Albany, Binghamton, Buffalo and Utica. Drop-off is offered only in Albany and New York City, and same-day walk-in service is limited to 10 documents [22].
  • By mail (statewide): NY Department of State, Division of Licensing Services, Apostille and Authentication Unit, PO Box 22001, Albany, NY 12201-2001. Express carriers use the physical address instead. Phone 518-474-4429 [22].
  • State vital records (statewide): the state Department of Health can forward a state-issued certificate to the Department of State if you order by mail with expedited processing ($45.00 per copy) and enclose a separate $10.00 check or money order per certificate, payable to the Department of State [23].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past event: Westchester County's Yonkers Mobile Shredding Event was Oct. 29, 2026, 10 a.m.–1 p.m. [25].
  • County rules (countywide): Westchester residents can bring up to four file-sized boxes of personal papers per household to any county mobile shredding event. Business papers, junk mail and newspapers are rejected [25]. The county's Yonkers event was on Oct 29, 2026 and has passed [26]. The county calendars for November and December 2026 list no Yonkers shredding event (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [27][28].
  • The UPS Store: its Yonkers page lists shredding [8].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Yonkers?

At the Yonkers City Clerk or the Yonkers main post office [1][2].

How much is a marriage license in Yonkers?

$40, with a 24-hour wait before the ceremony [3].

Can I send a fax at the Yonkers library?

Yes, free, at the Riverfront, Grinton I. Will and Crestwood libraries; you can't receive faxes [5].

How much does a New York notary charge?

$2.00 per oath, affirmation or acknowledgment (statewide) [11][12]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [11].
