Fairbanks, Alaska document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Fairbanks, Alaska, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Fairbanks, Fairbanks North Star Borough, Alaska Department of Health, Alaska DNR Recorder's Office, the Lieutenant Governor, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Boroughs and recording in Alaska (statewide): Alaska uses boroughs and census areas rather than counties. Vital records are issued by the state Health Analytics and Vital Records Section (HAVRS), not by city clerks. Land records are kept by the Department of Natural Resources Recorder's Office under a statewide fee schedule [1][2][3].
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport facilities in Fairbanks [4]. HAVRS vital copies $30/$25; marriage license $60 (apply at HAVRS or an Alaska Court — Fairbanks courts can issue) [1][5]. Fairbanks Recorder's Office on Airport Way [6]. FNSB libraries: 10¢ B&W / 50¢ color print [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • FNSB Libraries (Noel Wien / North Pole): black-and-white print/copy .10 per page; color .50 per page. Outbound fax to U.S./Canada/Caribbean $1.75 first page + $1.00 each additional [7].
  • FedEx: Fairbanks listings include a FedEx Office. We didn't find published per-page print prices on the city listing (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [8].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Fairbanks locations; pages advertise printing, faxing, shredding and notary [9].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Fairbanks, Alaska

  • City Clerk: 800 Cushman Street. Phone 907-459-6702. Hours Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. except Municipal holidays. The Clerk fee materials we checked (including a fee PDF Effective 06-14-2026) do not list a public notary line (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [10].
  • The UPS Store: Fairbanks locations advertise notary; fees vary [9][11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Alaska charges $40 for a regular notary public commission that lasts four years and requires a $2,500 bond [12]. The Lieutenant Governor's FAQ says there is no statutory maximum fee for notarial acts; a notary who charges must provide a published fee schedule before the act (AS 44.50.062(4)). Limited Governmental notaries may not charge [13].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Health Analytics and Vital Records (statewide): certified birth, death, and marriage certificates are $30 for the first copy and $25 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. Order in person at Anchorage or Juneau offices, by mail/fax, or through VitalChek (vendor fees may apply) [1].
    • Anchorage HAVRS office: 3901 Old Seward Highway, Suite 101, Anchorage, AK 99503. Phone 907-269-0991 [1].
    • Juneau HAVRS office: PO Box 110675, Juneau, AK 99811. Phone 907-465-3391 [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $5 per certificate [17].

    Marriage licenses

    • Where: HAVRS offices or any Alaska Court that issues licenses (Fairbanks courts can issue; Anchorage and Juneau courts do not) [5][1].
    • Fee: $60 [1].
    • State rules (statewide): marriage licenses are $60 (re-issue $15) through Alaska Vital Records [1]. You may apply at HAVRS offices in Anchorage or Juneau, or at any Alaska Court — except the Anchorage and Juneau courts do not issue marriage licenses (use HAVRS there) [5][1].

    Land records (DNR Fairbanks Recorder's Office)

    • Office: 3700 Airport Way, Fairbanks, AK 99709-4699. Phone 907-452-3521. Hours Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; closed 9:00–10:30 a.m. on the second Wednesday of each month [6].
    • Recording fees (statewide): DNR Recorder's Office charges $20 for the first page and $5 for each additional page of the same instrument, under AS 40.17.030 and 11 AAC 05.200. Extra names/claims over six are $2 each; non-standard documents $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [17].
    • Fee (statewide): $5 per certificate (apostille or authentication) [17].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Questions

    Where can I get a notary in Fairbanks, Alaska?

    The UPS Store lists two Fairbanks locations that advertise notary [9]. City Clerk fee materials we checked had no notary line [10].

    How much is library printing in Fairbanks?

    FNSB libraries: .10 B&W and .50 color per page; fax $1.75 + $1.00 [7].

    Where are Fairbanks passport offices?

    Downtown Station (315 Barnette St.) and UAF Campus Mail Center appear in the locator [4].

    Where do I record a deed for Fairbanks property?

    Fairbanks Recorder's Office, 3700 Airport Way. $20 first page + $5 additional [6][2].
