Stamford, Connecticut document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Stamford, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Stamford, the Ferguson Library, the State of Connecticut, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Stamford, the Camp Ave. post office [1]. The Ferguson Library stopped taking passport applications but still takes passport photos [2]. Its notary service is free, by appointment [3][4]. A marriage license is $50, with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [5].
No county clerk in Connecticut (statewide): Connecticut abolished county government effective Oct. 1, 1960, and counties remain only as geographic areas [6][7]. Town clerks keep land records, and each town's registrar of vital statistics issues birth, death and marriage records.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Ferguson Library printing and copies: letter-size 10¢ black and white, 50¢ color; double-sided 20¢ and $1.00. Legal-size copies are 20¢ and $1.00 (copies only, no print jobs). You can print wirelessly, by email or from the ePRINTit app [8].
  • Library fax: public fax machines are at the Main Library and the Harry Bennett and South End branches [8]. We didn't find a fax price on the page (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • FedEx: FedEx's Stamford page lists 6 locations, including 1 FedEx Office Print & Ship Center at 980 High Ridge Rd. and a FedEx Ship Center at 24 Ardmore St. [9].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Stamford stores, at 1127 High Ridge Rd. and 65 High Ridge Rd. [10]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Stamford

  • Ferguson Library (free): appointments Monday–Friday, 11 a.m.–3 p.m., at several locations. Bring two IDs, one with a photo [3]. Up to 3 documents per visit, in English only. The library won't notarize real estate documents, wills, trusts, powers of attorney or I-9 forms [4].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): since July 1, 2026, a Connecticut notary may charge up to $10.00 per notarial act, plus travel at no more than the IRS business mileage rate. Public Act 26-81 raised the fee from $5.00 [12][13].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): the General Assembly's compiled statute page for §3-95, revised to January 1, 2026, still shows the old cap of $5.00 plus 35¢ a mile [14]. The Secretary of the State's pages give the $10.00 fee in effect since July 1, 2026 [12][13]. We show the newer figure.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Stamford Town Clerk (vital records)

  • Where: 888 Washington Boulevard, ground floor. In-person requests 8 a.m.–3:45 p.m. [18][19].
  • Paying: cash, check or card in person (cards carry a service fee). By mail, send a check or money order payable to The City of Stamford [18].
  • Fee: the Town Clerk lists marriage certificate copies at $20.00 per copy [29]. Its death certificate application says the fee is $20.00 per certified copy; mail requests take 7–10 business days [30]. We didn't find a local per-copy price for birth certificates on the Stamford pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026). The state's town copy fee is $20 (statewide) [20].
  • Online: VitalChek, for Stamford events from 1920 on, for an extra fee [18][19].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Where (statewide): from the vital records office of the town where the event happened, or from the State Vital Records Office. Town requests are generally faster [20].
  • Fees (statewide): town-issued birth, death, marriage and civil union certificates are $20 per copy. State-issued birth certificates are $30 per copy [20][21].
  • Paying a town (statewide): by mail, towns take a money order payable to the town; personal checks aren't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds fees [20].

Marriage licenses (Stamford Town Clerk)

  • Fee and wait: $50 at your appointment, by cash, check or card. Since Oct. 1, 2026, 48 hours must pass before the license is issued. Stamford's schedule: apply Monday, pick up Thursday; apply Wednesday–Friday, pick up the next Monday [5].
  • Plan ahead: the clerk suggests booking at least 7 days before the ceremony. The license is valid for 65 days [5].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at the vital records office of the town where the wedding will be. The fee is $50.00. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the town can't issue the license until 48 hours after the second person signs the application. The license is good for 65 days [22][23].

Land records (Stamford Town Clerk)

  • Stamford's posted fees (as of July 1, 2025): recording $70 for the first page and $5 for each extra page; copies $1 a page; certification $2 per document; maps $20 each. The vault is open 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Monday–Friday, and records back to 1998 are online [24].
  • Copy fees (statewide law): CGS §7-34a sets town clerk copies at $1 per page and $2 to certify a copy. Recording is $10 for the first page and $5 for each extra page, plus $10 and $50 per-document state surcharges [25].
  • How they compare: Stamford's $70 first-page recording fee matches the statute's $10 base plus the $10 and $50 per-document surcharges [24][25].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Connecticut Secretary of the State. All orders must be placed online at sots.service.ct.gov/apostille; paper order forms are no longer accepted [26].
  • Fee (statewide): $40 per document, or $15 for documents for a child adoption. Expedited service is $50 more per document [27][14]. These figures come from the SOTS order form and the statute; the online-ordering page we read doesn't list fees.
  • Delivery (statewide): paper documents go by courier to 165 Capitol Avenue, Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06106, or by mail to P.O. Box 150470, Hartford, CT 06115-0470. E-apostilles began July 6, 2026, but vital records still need a paper apostille. E-apostille and expedited orders are processed within 24 hours [26].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [28]. Other sellers: money order guide.

  • The UPS Store: 2 stores in Stamford [10]; we didn't find shredding prices online.

Questions

Can I still apply for a passport at the Ferguson Library?

No. The library ended passport applications but still takes passport photos for $18 printed or $22 digital [2].

Where can I apply for a passport in Stamford?

The locator lists the Camp Ave. post office [1].

How much is a marriage license in Stamford?

$50, with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [5].

How much does a Connecticut notary charge?

Up to $10.00 per act since July 1, 2026, plus mileage (statewide) [12][13].
