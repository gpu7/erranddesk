Baltimore, Maryland document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Baltimore, Maryland, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Baltimore, the Circuit Court for Baltimore City or Baltimore County, the Maryland Judiciary, the Enoch Pratt Free Library, the Maryland Secretary of State, the Maryland Department of Health, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: a Baltimore City marriage license is $85, cash only [1]. Land record copies are $0.50 a page, plus $5 to certify [1]. The Pratt Library gives 20 free black-and-white prints a day [2]. 11 passport offices in the State Department's locator are confirmed inside the city [3][4].
City or county? Baltimore City is an independent city, not part of Baltimore County: the Census lists Baltimore city as its own county-level area [5][6]. The Circuit Court for Baltimore City clerk handles marriage licenses, land records and court records for city residents [1][7]. If you live in Baltimore County, including many addresses with a "Baltimore, MD" mailing address, use the Circuit Court for Baltimore County clerk instead: 401 Bosley Avenue, 2nd Floor, Towson, 410-887-2601 [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Enoch Pratt Free Library: 20 free black-and-white prints a day, then $0.10 a page; color is $0.20 a page. Copies are $0.10 black-and-white and $0.20 color, and copy service is at all locations. Domestic faxes (US, Canada and the Caribbean) are $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page; international faxes are $3.95 for the first page and $3.45 for each additional page [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Baltimore page lists 6 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [9]. The Census Geocoder places 5 of them in Baltimore city; 6400 Baltimore National Pike is in Baltimore County [4]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Baltimore

  • The UPS Store: its Baltimore page lists notarizing [9].
  • Circuit Court for Baltimore City: the Land Records & Licenses Division lists notary oath fees for notaries themselves ($11 for a new commission) and $1.00 per document to authenticate a notary's signature [1].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maryland's notary fees are set by state regulation (COMAR 01.02.08.02, effective Jan 22, 2024): at most $8 per notarial act, or $30 per remote notarial act. For extra copies of the same record signed at the same time, $4 per signature on each extra copy. Photocopies are at most $2 each, certifying a journal copy is $6, and travel is IRS mileage plus up to $5 [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Maryland Department of Health's Division of Vital Records charges $10 for a birth certificate and $10 for a death certificate, $12 for each additional death certificate in the same transaction and $12 for a marriage certificate. Internet orders add $13, and expedited shipping (internet only) adds $20. Local health departments' fees vary [13].
  • How (statewide): the Division's lobby serves customers by appointment only; you can also order by mail to P.O. Box 68760, Baltimore, MD 21215-0036, or online through VitalChek [14][15].

Marriage licenses (Circuit Court for Baltimore City)

  • Fee: $85.00 for the application, cash only. A courthouse ceremony is $25.00, cash only, and you must bring a witness 18 or older. Certified copies are $5.50 each [1]. The $85 includes an extra $75 that city law requires the clerk to collect for domestic violence shelters [16].
  • Where: Room 627, Clarence M. Mitchell, Jr. Courthouse, 100 North Calvert Street, (410) 333-3790. Licenses: Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; ceremonies: Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–2:30 p.m. [1].

Deeds and land records

  • Copies: $0.50 a page, $5.00 per instrument to certify, and $2.00 more for mail requests sent without return postage [1].
  • Where: Room 610, Mitchell Courthouse, 100 North Calvert Street, (410) 333-3760, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. [1].

Court records and divorce decrees

  • Copy fees (statewide): the Maryland Judiciary's circuit court fee schedule, effective Sept 2, 2026, sets copies at $0.50 a page, plus $5 for each certification or $10 to exemplify [17].
  • Where: the Clerk's Office lists the Cummings Courthouse, 111 N. Calvert Street, and the Mitchell Courthouse, 100 North Calvert Street, both (410) 396-5188, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [7].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maryland Secretary of State, 16 Francis Street, Wineland Building, Annapolis, 410-974-5521. Certifications and apostilles are $5 each, by mail or in person [18].
  • Walk-ins, the page conflicts: the same page says "WALK-INS NOT AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME" (scheduled appointments may change because of building construction) and also that the desk is open for walk-ins until 1 p.m., 9 a.m.–1 p.m. weekdays, with a limit of 10 documents per customer a day [18]. Call before you go.
  • Notarized documents (statewide): take them first to the Clerk of the Circuit Court in the county where the notary was commissioned [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Baltimore page lists shredding [9].
  • Free events (past): the Department of Public Works' last shred event of 2026 was Sunday, Nov 1, 8 a.m.–noon, at the Public Safety Training Center, 3500 W Northern Pkwy [20]. That date has passed; we didn't find a 2027 date (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Questions

How much is a marriage license in Baltimore City?

$85, cash only, at the Circuit Court for Baltimore City [1].

I live in Baltimore County. Where do I go?

The Circuit Court for Baltimore County clerk, 401 Bosley Avenue, Towson [8].

How much does printing cost at the Pratt Library?

The first 20 black-and-white prints a day are free, then $0.10 a page; color is $0.20 [2].
