Charlotte, North Carolina document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Charlotte, North Carolina, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: the Charlotte Mecklenburg Library charges 25 cents a page for photocopies and black-and-white prints and 50 cents for color [1]. Mecklenburg County birth and death certificates are $10 each from the county Office of Vital Records or the Register of Deeds [2][3]. A marriage license is $60 at the Register of Deeds [3]. North Carolina notaries can charge at most $10 per signature (statewide) [4]. The State Department's locator lists 5 passport offices with a Charlotte address [5].
City and county: this page covers the city the Census Bureau calls "Charlotte city" [6]. The city lies entirely in Mecklenburg County, which has 10 Census places, including Matthews and Huntersville [7]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [8]. A Charlotte mailing address doesn't always mean the city itself, so we note where the Geocoder disagrees.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Charlotte Mecklenburg Library

  • Prices: photocopies $0.25 a page; black-and-white prints $0.25 a page; color prints $0.50 a page [1].
  • Fax: free. Charlotte Mecklenburg Library branch pages list "Free Faxing", including South County Regional, 5801 Rea Road [9]. The branches that list it are Allegra Westbrooks Regional, Cornelius, Davidson, Hickory Grove, Independence Regional, Matthews, Mint Hill, Mountain Island, Myers Park, North County Regional, Pineville, Plaza-Midwood, South Boulevard, South County Regional, SouthPark Regional, Steele Creek, Sugar Creek, University City Regional and West Boulevard [10]. Founders Hall, ImaginOn, Robinson-Spangler Carolina Room and the Main Library, which is closed for construction, don't list it [10]. We didn't find a page that posts fax page limits or rules (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Register of Deeds copies: non-certified copies of recorded documents are 25 cents a page in person or $1 a page by mail; map copies are $1 a page in person or $2 by mail [3].
  • The UPS Store: its Charlotte page lists 36 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. The Census Geocoder places 31 of them in Charlotte city. It places 9611 Brookdale Dr in Harrisburg and 8334 Pineville Matthews Rd in Pineville, puts 5009 Beatties Ford Rd in Mecklenburg County outside any Census place, and didn't match 4009 Corning Place Dr or 3335 Potomac River Parkway [8]. Prices aren't listed; the page says products, services, pricing and hours may vary by location [11], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Charlotte

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service at the Charlotte Mecklenburg Library: its site search for "notary" returned no results, and its fee list and branch pages don't mention one [12][1][10] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Register of Deeds: the Mecklenburg County Register of Deeds handles notary commissions [13], which means it swears in new notaries; it doesn't notarize documents for the public. Its fee for the notary oath is $10 [3].
  • The UPS Store: its Charlotte page lists notarizing [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [14].
  • Campus passport office: the State Department's locator says notary service is available at Union Station, UNC Charlotte [5]. We didn't find a fee.
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions North Carolina notaries for a five-year term. A new applicant takes an approved classroom course of at least six hours and a written exam, pays a $50 nonrefundable application fee, and then takes the oath before the county Register of Deeds within 45 days [4]. The Register of Deeds fee for that oath is $10 [15].
  • Fees (statewide): at most $10 per notarized signature for an acknowledgment, jurat, verification or proof, and $10 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature. Remote notarization is at most $25 per signature. Travel can be charged only as actual mileage at the federal business rate, and only if you agree in writing before the trip. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, and doesn't have to charge at all [4].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide; two official pages differ): the statute lists $15 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment or jurat [4]. The Secretary of State's FAQ says $10.00 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment [16]. Ask the notary which fee applies before you start.
  • No certified copies (statewide): the Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy [16]. For a certified copy of a recorded or vital record, go to the office that holds it (below). The Secretary of State's site has a "Find a Notary Near You" search [17].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (Mecklenburg County)

  • County Office of Vital Records: 618 N. College St., phone 704-336-2819, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Births and deaths that happened in Mecklenburg County from 1913 on are $10 per certificate, by request online, in person or by mail. For births and deaths anywhere in North Carolina from 1971 on, the office searches the state registry in person only, for $24 including one copy, and $15 for each extra copy from the same search [2].
  • Register of Deeds: certified birth and death certificates are also $10 per copy [3]. Online orders add a non-refundable $4 service fee per order plus a card processing fee, and delivery orders add a $1 "Vital Verify" fee. Records go back to 1913 [21].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • County first (statewide): NC Vital Records says birth, death and marriage certificates can also be ordered from the Register of Deeds in the county where the event happened, usually the same day [22]. State law sets the Register of Deeds fee at $10 for a certified copy of a birth or death certificate or marriage license [15].
  • State fees (statewide): $24 for a search of one three-year period, including one copy if the record is found. The search fee isn't refunded if no record is found. Extra copies of the same certificate are $15 each, expedited processing is $15, and VitalChek adds a $13.95 processing fee [23].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): in-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m. (last appointment 2:30 p.m.), at the Cooper Memorial Health Building, 225 N. McDowell St., Raleigh. Book at 919-733-3000, option 3; the $15 expedited fee applies to in-person service. Mail goes to North Carolina Vital Records, 1903 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-1900 [24].
  • Divorce (statewide): the state has divorce certificates from 1958 on [25]; the divorce decree itself is kept by the Clerk of Court in the county where the divorce was filed [22].

Marriage licenses (Mecklenburg County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): unmarried people 18 or older may marry [26]. The Register of Deeds fee for a license is $60, and $10 for each certified copy [15]. The license form in the statute authorizes the marriage "at any time within 60 days" [26].
  • Mecklenburg County fee and application: the license is $60 and each certified copy is $10 [3]. Both applicants go to the Register of Deeds in person between 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.; the office suggests filling in the online application within 30 days before you go. Both bring a valid photo ID and proof of a Social Security number. Applicants 18 to 20 also bring a certified copy of their birth certificate, and applicants under 18 should call 980-314-4951 first [27].
  • Using the license: it is valid for 60 days and can be used anywhere in North Carolina, but must be returned to the office that issued it [27]. Wait 10 days after the wedding before ordering a certified copy [21].
  • Ceremonies: the Register of Deeds doesn't perform weddings; for a civil ceremony, the page points to the Magistrates Office in the Mecklenburg County Courthouse [27]. The court says weddings there are by appointment only, Monday–Friday 2–4:30 p.m. [28].
  • Office: County Courts & Office Building, 720 E. 4th Street, Room 105, Charlotte, NC 28202 [27]. The Census Geocoder places it in Charlotte city [8].

Recorded documents (Mecklenburg County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): registers of deeds charge $5 for the first page of a certified copy of a recorded instrument and $2 for each additional page [15]. Uncertified copy prices are set by each office [15].
  • Mecklenburg County fees: certified copies of real estate documents are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page. Non-certified copies are 25 cents a page in person or $1 a page by mail. Cash is accepted in person [3].
  • Where and when: 720 East Fourth Street, phone 704-336-2443 [21]. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [13].

Court record copies (Mecklenburg County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • Court copy fees (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court charges $2.00 for the first page of each document copied and $0.25 for each additional page, $3.00 for a certificate under seal and $10.00 for exemplification of records. These are state fees under G.S. 7A-308, shown in the NC Judicial Branch costs chart in effect Jan. 1, 2026 [29][30][31] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Where and when: Mecklenburg County Courthouse, 832 East Fourth St., phone (704) 686-0400. The Clerk of Superior Court's office is open Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., and public records can be viewed there during those hours [28]. The Census Geocoder places it in Charlotte city [8].
  • Older files: for criminal, civil or estate documents filed before Oct. 9, 2023 that aren't in the state's online Portal, the court says to email the matching Clerk division [28].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the North Carolina Secretary of State's Authentication Office, for North Carolina documents used in foreign countries, such as birth and marriage certificates, statements of marital status, powers of attorney, diplomas and transcripts [32].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document; for adoptions, a duplicate original ordered at the same time is $5.00. Pay by check or money order to "North Carolina Secretary of State" [33][34].
  • How to send (statewide): mail a cover letter, payment, the original documents with all certifications attached and a prepaid return envelope or courier label [33] to North Carolina Secretary of State, Authentication Office, P.O. Box 29622, Raleigh, NC 27626-0622. The office is at 2 South Salisbury Street, Raleigh [32]. It no longer takes appointments; you can bring documents in without one [35].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [36]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • County event: Mecklenburg County Solid Waste and Recycling lists a free secure paper shredding event on Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026, 9 a.m.–noon, at Compost Central, 140 Valleydale Road. It is for residents only, with a limit of three banker-box-size boxes, six paper bags or one 18-gallon tote. Register in advance; time slots open on the first day of the event's month [37]. It is the only shredding event the county lists [38] (last checked Nov 3, 2026). The Census Geocoder places the site in Charlotte city [8].
  • Past event: the county held the same event on Oct. 17, 2026 [39].
  • The UPS Store: its Charlotte page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Charlotte?

For a Mecklenburg County birth, at the county Office of Vital Records, 618 N. College St., for $10 [2], or from the Register of Deeds, also $10 [3].

How much does it cost to print at the Charlotte library?

$0.25 a page for black-and-white and $0.50 for color; photocopies are $0.25 a page [1].

How much is a marriage license in Mecklenburg County?

$60, applied for in person at the Register of Deeds; it is valid for 60 days [3][27].

Can a North Carolina notary certify a copy?

No. The Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy (statewide) [16].

Where can I apply for a passport in Charlotte?

The State Department's locator lists the City of Charlotte Passport Acceptance Facility, the Northeast Charlotte and South Providence post offices, Charlotte Westside SDC and Union Station at UNC Charlotte [5].
