Raleigh, North Carolina document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Raleigh, North Carolina, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Raleigh, Wake County, the Wake County Register of Deeds, Wake County Public Libraries, the North Carolina Judicial Branch, the North Carolina Secretary of State, NC Vital Records, the State of North Carolina, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Wake County Public Libraries charge 20 cents a page for black-and-white prints and copies and 80 cents for color, and don't offer scanning or faxing [1]. Wake County birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each at the Register of Deeds [2]. A marriage license is $60 [3]. North Carolina notaries can charge at most $10 per signature (statewide) [4]. The State Department's locator lists 2 passport offices with a Raleigh address [5].
City and county: this page covers the city the Census Bureau calls "Raleigh city" [6]. Most of the city is in Wake County, and a small part is in Durham County [7]. The county offices on this page are Wake County's. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [8]. A Raleigh mailing address doesn't always mean the city itself, so we note where the Geocoder disagrees.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Wake County Public Libraries

  • Prices: black-and-white prints and copies 20 cents a page; color prints 80 cents a page. Pay by cash ($1 bills or coins) or credit card at the kiosks [1]. Printing is single-sided only, and American Express isn't accepted (library FAQ updated Dec 1, 2025) [9].
  • Fax and scanning: not available at the library [1][10].
  • Register of Deeds copies: certified copies of recorded documents are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page [3]. We didn't find a fee for non-certified copies.
  • The UPS Store: its Raleigh page lists 21 locations, one of them marked "Coming Soon" (3901 Capital Blvd), and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. Of the 20 open stores, the Census Geocoder places 17 in Raleigh city. It places 8105 Fayetteville Rd in Fuquay-Varina and didn't match 7283 Veterans Parkway or 9660 Falls of Neuse Rd [8]. Prices aren't listed; the page says products, services, pricing and hours may vary by location [11], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Raleigh

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service at Wake County Public Libraries. A wake.gov search for "notary" returned no results, and the library's technology, printing and appointments pages don't list one [1][9][12] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Register of Deeds: the Wake County Register of Deeds gives the oath to new Wake County notaries and authenticates notary signatures [13]; it doesn't notarize documents for the public. The oath is $10 and an authentication is $5 per notary per page [3].
  • The UPS Store: its Raleigh page lists notarizing [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [14].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions North Carolina notaries for a five-year term. A new applicant takes an approved classroom course of at least six hours and a written exam, pays a $50 nonrefundable application fee, and then takes the oath before the county Register of Deeds within 45 days [4]. The Register of Deeds fee for that oath is $10 [15].
  • Fees (statewide): at most $10 per notarized signature for an acknowledgment, jurat, verification or proof, and $10 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature. Remote notarization is at most $25 per signature. Travel can be charged only as actual mileage at the federal business rate, and only if you agree in writing before the trip. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, and doesn't have to charge at all [4].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide; two official pages differ): the statute lists $15 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment or jurat [4]. The Secretary of State's FAQ says $10.00 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment [16]. Ask the notary which fee applies before you start.
  • No certified copies (statewide): the Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy [16]. For a certified copy of a recorded or vital record, go to the office that holds it (below). The Secretary of State's site has a "Find a Notary Near You" search [17].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death and marriage certificates (Wake County Register of Deeds)

  • Wake County records: certified copies of Wake County birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each, online or in person at any of the county's five Register of Deeds locations [2]. Birth and death records go back to 1913 [21].
  • Other North Carolina records: only the downtown office searches records from other counties [21]. It provides birth certificates for births anywhere in North Carolina from 1971 on and death certificates for deaths from 2021 on, for $24 with one copy and $15 for each extra copy [2].
  • Downtown office: 300 S. Salisbury St., 1st floor, Wake County Justice Center, phone 919-856-5460, 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. It takes cash, check, money order or credit/debit card [2]. The online ordering page gives in-person hours of Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [22]. The Census Geocoder places the building in Raleigh city [8].
  • Departure Regional Center: 5809 Departure Drive, Raleigh, phone 919-431-4000, 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., cash, check or money order only [2]. The Census Geocoder places it in Raleigh city [8].
  • Online fees (sources differ): the county's page says online orders add a non-refundable $4 convenience and card processing fee [21]. The online ordering page says $3, plus a non-refundable $0.35 "Vital Verify" fee on delivery orders [22]. The fee schedule says only that a vendor fee "varies" [3].
  • Marriage records (sources differ): the county's page says marriage records go back to 1931 [21]; the online ordering page says 1932 [22].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • County first (statewide): NC Vital Records says birth, death and marriage certificates can also be ordered from the Register of Deeds in the county where the event happened, usually the same day [23]. State law sets the Register of Deeds fee at $10 for a certified copy of a birth or death certificate or marriage license [15].
  • State fees (statewide): $24 for a search of one three-year period, including one copy if the record is found. The search fee isn't refunded if no record is found. Extra copies of the same certificate are $15 each, expedited processing is $15, and VitalChek adds a $13.95 processing fee [24].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): in-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m. (last appointment 2:30 p.m.), at the Cooper Memorial Health Building, 225 N. McDowell St., Raleigh. Book at 919-733-3000, option 3; the $15 expedited fee applies to in-person service. Mail goes to North Carolina Vital Records, 1903 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-1900 [25].
  • Divorce (statewide): the state has divorce certificates from 1958 on [26]; the divorce decree itself is kept by the Clerk of Court in the county where the divorce was filed [23].

Marriage licenses (Wake County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): unmarried people 18 or older may marry [27]. The Register of Deeds fee for a license is $60, and $10 for each certified copy [15]. The license form in the statute authorizes the marriage "at any time within 60 days" [27].
  • Wake County fee and application: the license is $60 [3]. Both applicants come in person with a valid government-issued ID and proof of a Social Security number. No appointment is needed: walk in Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. at any of five locations, including the downtown office in the Wake County Justice Center (300 S. Salisbury St., Suite 1700) and the Departure Regional Center in Raleigh (cash only). Applicants 16 to 20 bring a certified birth record [28].
  • Paying (sources differ): the marriage license page says cash or credit cards are accepted [28]. The certificates page says credit cards are not accepted for marriage licenses [2]. Bring cash to be safe.
  • Using the license: the ceremony must take place in North Carolina within 60 days [28].
  • Civil ceremonies: magistrates perform walk-in weddings at the Wake County Justice Center, 9th floor, Room 901, Monday–Friday 9:30–11:30 a.m. and 1:30–3:30 p.m., for $50 cash in exact change. Bring two witnesses 18 or older [28].

Recorded documents (Wake County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): registers of deeds charge $5 for the first page of a certified copy of a recorded instrument and $2 for each additional page [15]. Uncertified copy prices are set by each office [15].
  • Wake County fees: certified copies are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page, and the county doesn't charge for its trailer page. Certified copies of recorded maps are $5 a page [3].

Court record copies (Wake County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • Court copy fees (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court charges $2.00 for the first page of each document copied and $0.25 for each additional page, $3.00 for a certificate under seal and $10.00 for exemplification of records. These are state fees under G.S. 7A-308, shown in the NC Judicial Branch costs chart in effect Jan. 1, 2026 [29][30][31] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Where and when: the court says civil matters, except marriages, are handled at the Wake County Courthouse, and criminal matters and marriages at the Wake County Justice Center, 300 S. Salisbury St., phone (919) 792-4000, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [32].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the North Carolina Secretary of State's Authentication Office, for North Carolina documents used in foreign countries, such as birth and marriage certificates, statements of marital status, powers of attorney, diplomas and transcripts [33].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document; for adoptions, a duplicate original ordered at the same time is $5.00. Pay by check or money order to "North Carolina Secretary of State" [34][35].
  • How to send (statewide): mail a cover letter, payment, the original documents with all certifications attached and a prepaid return envelope or courier label [34] to North Carolina Secretary of State, Authentication Office, P.O. Box 29622, Raleigh, NC 27626-0622. The office is at 2 South Salisbury Street, Raleigh [33]. It no longer takes appointments; you can bring documents in without one [36].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [37]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past city event: City of Raleigh Solid Waste Services held its 2026 Shred + Dead event on Saturday, Oct. 24, 2026, 9 a.m.–noon, at the Wilders Grove Solid Waste Services Facility, 630 Beacon Lake Dr. It limited paper to four banker-box-size boxes or four 13-gallon bags per household or business [38]. That event is past. We didn't find a later shredding date on raleighnc.gov or wake.gov (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Raleigh page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Raleigh?

For a Wake County birth, at any Wake County Register of Deeds location, for $10 [2]. The downtown office can also search North Carolina births from 1971 on, for $24 [2].

How much does it cost to print at the Raleigh library?

20 cents a page for black-and-white and 80 cents for color [1].

How much is a marriage license in Wake County?

$60, applied for in person; the wedding must be within 60 days [3][28].

Can I renew my passport at the Wake County Register of Deeds?

No. The office accepts new applications on Form DS-11 and doesn't accept DS-82 renewals, except renewals for minors [18].

Can a North Carolina notary certify a copy?

No. The Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy (statewide) [16].
