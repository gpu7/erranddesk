Richmond, Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Richmond, Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Richmond Public Library prints and copies for 15 cents a page in black and white, and fax, scanning and notary service are free [1][2][3]. A marriage license from the Circuit Court Clerk is $30, by appointment [4]. The only passport facility the State Department lists inside the city is the Main Post Office on Brook Road [5][6]. Virginia notaries can charge up to $10 per act (statewide) [7].
An independent city, no county: Richmond isn't part of any county. The Census Bureau lists "Richmond city" [8] as its own county-level area [9], so the city's own Circuit Court Clerk handles marriage licenses, recordings and oaths [4][10]. Many "Richmond" mailing addresses are in Henrico or Chesterfield County; we use the Census Geocoder to check which are inside the city [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Richmond Public Library

  • Printing: 15 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents in color at every branch's print station; you can also print from a phone, tablet or laptop with Princh [1].
  • Copies: 15 cents a page at every branch. The pay box takes change and dollar bills [2].
  • Scanning: free at all locations, to email, phone, USB drive, Google Drive or OneDrive; USB drives are $5 at the desk [2].
  • Fax: outgoing fax is free at all locations, local, 800 and long distance. The library doesn't receive faxes [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Richmond page lists 5 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. The Census Geocoder places 2 in Richmond city (3126 W Cary St and 5806 Grove Ave) and 3 in Henrico County (Staples Mill Rd, Pump Rd and Gayton Rd) [6]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Richmond

  • Library (free): there's no charge, but call ahead to make sure a notary is in that day [3].
  • Notary oath: an appointment is required for oath ceremonies at the Circuit Court [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Richmond page lists notarizing [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth commissions Virginia notaries. The application or renewal fee is $45, nonrefundable [13]. The new notary then takes the oath before the clerk of the circuit court the commission was sent to, within 60 days [14][13]; the clerk's fee for qualifying a notary is $10 [15].
  • Fees (statewide): a Virginia notary may charge up to $10 for an acknowledgment, oath, affidavit or a true-copy certification, and up to $25 for the same acts on an electronic document. Travel costs can be added only if you agree and the notary goes somewhere other than their usual place of business [7].
  • Copy certification (statewide): unlike some states, Virginia law lists "certifying that a copy of a document is a true copy" among the acts a notary may charge for [7]. Some documents, such as vital records, still have to come from the office that holds them.
  • Electronic notaries (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth also commissions electronic notaries and has a search for current notaries [13].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • State office in Richmond: the Virginia Department of Health's Office of Vital Records is in Richmond; details are below (statewide). We didn't find a separate city office that issues them.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): since July 1, 2026, the Virginia Department of Health charges $15 for each certified copy, or for each search when no record is found [18].
  • Who can order (statewide): only immediate family (mother, father, spouse, child, brother, sister and grandparents) with valid ID; a legal guardian with proof of custody can get a birth record [18].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): walk in Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–4 p.m. at 8701 Park Central Drive, Suite 100, Richmond. Drop-off requests for same-day pickup must be in by 2 p.m. Mail goes to VDH, Office of Vital Records, P.O. Box 1000, Richmond, VA 23218-1000. The customer care line is (804) 662-6200 [18].

Marriage licenses (Richmond Circuit Court Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the minimum age to marry is 18 [19]. A license is good for 60 days from the day it's issued [20]. The clerk's statutory fee for issuing it is $10 [15]; the clerk's own fee list (below) may show a different total.
  • Richmond: $30, cash or credit/debit card; both of you must come with photo ID. Licenses are issued 8:30 a.m.–4:15 p.m. Monday–Friday at the John Marshall Courts Building, 400 North 9th Street [4]. The statute's clerk fee is $10 [15], so expect to pay the clerk's $30.
  • Appointment phone (pages differ): the marriage page says to call (804) 646-6767 [4]; the clerk's announcements say to call 804-646-0832 for marriage license and oath appointments [10].
  • Ceremonies and copies: the clerk doesn't perform weddings but has a list of civil celebrants. Certified copies are $2.50 [4].

Recorded documents and court copies (Richmond Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in Virginia, deeds are recorded by the clerk of the circuit court, and the same statewide clerk fees apply. Recording costs $18 for 10 or fewer pages, $32 for 11 to 30 pages and $52 for 31 or more (§ 17.1-275 A.2) [15].
  • Clerk copy fees (statewide): under Va. Code § 17.1-275, circuit court clerks charge $0.50 a page for copies not priced elsewhere in the law (A.8), and $2 to attach the court's seal and clerk's certificate, plus $0.50 for a judge's certificate (A.9) [15] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Richmond: office hours are 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and recording hours 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m., Monday–Friday; general information (804) 646-6505, Record Room (804) 646-6530 [4]. The Census Geocoder places the courthouse in Richmond city [6]. We didn't find a local copy-price list (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth's Authentication Division. Depending on the country, it issues a Great Seal or an Apostille. It authenticates documents from a Virginia notary, Virginia clerk of court or Virginia deputy state registrar; it doesn't notarize documents [21].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document (not per page). Several documents signed by the same official on the same date for the same country are $10.00 for the first and $5.00 for each additional one [22].
  • How to send (statewide): USPS mail goes to Secretary of the Commonwealth, Authentication Division, 1111 East Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219; FedEx, UPS and DHL go to 400 East Cary Street, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone 804-692-0114 [21][22]. In-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Thursday; appointments for the next week open Fridays at 10 a.m., up to 10 documents per customer or family [22].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Recycling and Disposal Event (upcoming): "Bring One for the Chipper," Saturday, Jan. 9, 2027, 10 a.m.–2 p.m., at 1710 Robin Hood Road, with on-site paper shredding plus Christmas tree and electronics collection (some electronics fees apply) [24]. We didn't find eligibility rules on that page (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Richmond page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at Richmond Public Library?

15 cents a page for black and white and 50 cents for color [1].

Can I fax at Richmond Public Library?

Yes, outgoing fax is free, including long distance [2].

How much is a marriage license in Richmond?

$30 at the Circuit Court Clerk, by appointment [4].

Where do I apply for a passport in Richmond?

Inside the city, the locator lists the Main Post Office at 1801 Brook Rd [5][6].

Where do I get a Virginia birth certificate?

From the Virginia Department of Health, $15 per copy (statewide) [18].
