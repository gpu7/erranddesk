Parkersburg, West Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Parkersburg, West Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: every Parkersburg & Wood County library branch has a free West Virginia notary on staff during open hours [1]. Faxing is $1 a page to send and 50 cents a page to receive [2], and mobile printing is 10 cents a black-and-white letter page [3]. The Wood County Clerk lists a $57 marriage license, or $37 with a premarital counseling certificate [4]. The locator lists 1 passport facility, the Simmons Station Post Office [5].
Two clerks: Parkersburg is in Wood County [6][7]. The county clerk, at the Courthouse at 1 Court Square, records documents and issues birth, death and marriage copies [4][8]; the circuit clerk handles court records and divorces, (304) 424-1700 [4][9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Parkersburg & Wood County Public Library

  • Copies: self-service black-and-white and color copiers at all branches, on library-supplied paper [2].
  • Mobile printing (ePRINTit): black-and-white 10 cents per letter or legal page, 20 cents ledger; color 50 cents letter or legal, $1.00 ledger. You don't need a library card [3].
  • Fax: $1 per page to send, 50 cents per page to receive [2].
  • Scanning: the Emerson branch has free self-service scan to flash drive and scan to email [2].
  • Branches: Emerson, 3100 Emerson Avenue, 304-420-4587; South Parkersburg, 1807 Blizzard Drive, 304-428-7041; Williamstown, 201 West 5th Street, Williamstown, WV 26187, 304-375-6052. The library also runs a Bookmobile, 304-420-4587 [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Parkersburg page lists 1 location, at 2107 Pike Street Suite 16, marked "Coming Soon" [11]. We didn't find an opening date or prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Parkersburg

  • Library (free): each branch has a West Virginia notary on staff during operating hours, free. Arrive at least 30 minutes before closing, don't sign beforehand, bring valid ID and bring your own witnesses if needed [1].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State commissions notaries. The application or recommission fee is $52, waived for state and local government notaries; a commission is good for five years [12][13]. No fee is charged to add e-notarization authorization [12].
  • Fees (statewide): under W. Va. Code §39-4-30, a notary may charge up to $10.00 for each signature notarized, up to $10.00 per 8½ × 11 page for certifying a copy (the notary keeps a copy on file), and up to $10.00 for any other notarial act [14].
  • Copy certification (statewide): if no other office can certify a document, a West Virginia notary can certify a true copy after comparing it with the original and keeping a copy [15][14]. Vital records, court orders and recorded deeds still come from the office that holds them [15].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Wood County Clerk: certified copies of birth, death and marriage certificates are $5.00 each; ID is required. Birth and death records are in Room 201 on the 2nd floor, (304) 424-1845. Requests can be mailed to Wood County Clerk, PO Box 1474, Parkersburg, WV 26102 [8][9].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $12 per certified copy from the state Vital Registration Office; the fee includes a nonrefundable search. If the record isn't found, staff search the year given plus the year before and after; more searches are $12 per three-year period [19].
  • In person (statewide): same-day walk-in service at 350 Capitol Street, Room 165, Charleston, WV 25301-3701. Cash, check and money order are accepted; cards at the walk-up counter add a 4% surcharge. Expect a wait of at least 15 minutes, longer from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. [19].
  • By mail or online (statewide): mail the form with a color copy of your ID and a check or money order (no cards by mail). Online orders go through third-party vendors such as VitalChek; the state gets $12 a copy whatever the vendor charges [19].
  • County clerk copies (statewide law): W. Va. Code lets the county clerk charge $5 for a certified copy of a birth certificate, death certificate or marriage license it holds [20]. Check with the county clerk which records it has.
  • Divorce records (statewide): a divorce decree comes from the circuit clerk of the court that issued it, not from Vital Registration [15].

Marriage licenses (Wood County Clerk)

  • Wood County fees: $57.00 without a premarital counseling certificate, $37.00 with one, by cash or card through Certified Payments (2.4% service fee, $2.00 minimum) [4]. These are $2 more than the $55 and $35 in state code [20]; we show both and haven't confirmed what accounts for the difference. Pay the county's posted fee.
  • Where and rules: fourth floor of the Courthouse, (304) 424-1860, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. You must marry in West Virginia within 60 days. A certified copy of a recorded license is $5.00; personal checks aren't accepted for mail-in requests [4].
  • State law (statewide): every West Virginia marriage needs a license from a county clerk (the clerk of the county commission) [21]. The age of consent is 18; 16- and 17-year-olds need written consent under the statute's conditions [22]. The statutory fee is $55, or $35 if you show a premarital education course completion certificate [20].

Recorded documents (Wood County Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in West Virginia, the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) records deeds; the circuit clerk handles court records [20][15]. Recording a deed or trust deed is $30; most other documents are $10; documents over five pages add $1 per extra page. Copies are $1.50 (plus $1 per page over two), and attaching the clerk's seal is $1 (§59-1-10) [20].
  • Wood County: mail or bring documents in; the site has a Recording Fee Calculator. Record Room copies are $1.50 for the first two pages and $1.00 for each additional page, Room 103 [4]; Record Room phone 304-424-1870 [9].

Court records and divorce decrees (Wood County Circuit Clerk)

  • Circuit clerk fees (statewide): W. Va. Code §59-1-11 sets $1 a page for a copy the circuit clerk makes to go out of the office, $135 to file for divorce, separate maintenance or annulment, and $200 for most other civil filings [23] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Wood County: Circuit Clerk, 304-424-1700 [9]; the county site lists 1 Court Square, 304-424-1984, and office hours Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. It accepts credit cards, cash, money orders or business checks [24].
  • Circuit court fees: copies $1.00 a page; abstracts $5.00; exemplified copies $3.00 plus the cost of copies [25]. We didn't find a separate certified-copy price (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State, at the State Capitol Building in Charleston. It issues Apostille Certificates for countries in the Hague Apostille Convention and Authentication Certificates for other countries [15].
  • What it certifies (statewide): only documents attested by a proper West Virginia officer, such as a notary public, county clerk or the State Registrar. Recorded deeds and marriage certificates need the county clerk's certified copy; court orders and divorce decrees need the circuit clerk's [15].
  • Fee and hours (statewide): $10 per certification; if several documents are certified by the same official, the first is $10 and each additional one is $5. In-person service runs 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday–Friday [15].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.
