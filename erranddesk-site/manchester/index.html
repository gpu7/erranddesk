Manchester, New Hampshire document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Manchester, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Manchester, Manchester City Library, Hillsborough County Registry of Deeds, the State of New Hampshire, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Manchester, both post offices [1]. City Clerk Justices of the Peace notarize for $5 per signature/seal [2]. Certified vital copies are $15 for the first copy and $10 for each additional copy of the same record [3][4][5]. Statewide notary fee cap is $10 per ordinary act [6].
Counties in New Hampshire (statewide): New Hampshire has ten counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds. Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the state Division of Vital Records Administration (Department of State) [4][7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Manchester City Library (405 Pine St.; West Branch 76 North Main St.): black-and-white copies and prints are $0.20/page; color $0.35/page; cash only. Scanning is free if you bring your own USB drive. We didn't find a library fax price on the printing page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [8].
  • City Clerk photocopying: $1 for the first page and $0.50 for each additional page [2].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Manchester page lists 3 locations: FedEx at Walgreens at 227 S Main St. and 606 Valley St., and Copy Express at 923 Elm St. We didn't find a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center listed on that page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [9].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Manchester stores — 373 S Willow St. ((603) 666-0000) and 497 Hooksett Rd. ((603) 666-9800). Store pages advertise printing, faxing, shredding and notarizing [10].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Manchester

  • Manchester City Clerk ($5): One City Hall Plaza. Justices of the Peace on staff; $5 per signature/seal. Each signer must appear in person. The office does not sign documents that specifically require a "Notary" signature (as distinct from a JP) [2][11].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state; NH's statewide cap is $10 per ordinary act [12][6].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Hampshire caps ordinary notarial acts at $10 each (oath, witness, service, or certification). Remote notarial acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act. Depositions follow RSA 517:19 (SOS manual: $5 to $50, plus $0.20/mile to swear witnesses). No fee for administering oaths of office for town officers [6][13].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Manchester City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: One City Hall Plaza. Open Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Phone (603) 624-6455. The clerk keeps official birth and death records for events in the city and marriage licenses issued here [11][17].
  • Fees: certified marriage certificates are $15 for the first copy and $10 for each additional copy of the same record at the same time [3]. Statewide search fee is the same $15 / $10 schedule for birth, death, marriage, divorce and civil union certificates at clerks and at DVRA [4][5]. Cash, check or credit card (card surcharge $1.95 or 2.75%, whichever is greater) [3].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): any of New Hampshire's 234 city and town clerks can issue certified copies when you have a direct and tangible interest; many clerks use the NHVRIN system for records since 1935 [4].
  • State Division of Vital Records Administration (statewide): 9 Ratification Way, Concord, NH 03301-2455. Search fee $15 for the first copy (kept whether or not a record is found); $10 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [4][5].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): request a notarized certified copy from a city/town clerk or DVRA, then take it to the Secretary of State (vital-record photocopies are not accepted) [18][4].

Marriage licenses (Manchester City Clerk)

  • Where: One City Hall Plaza. Both applicants must appear together between 8 a.m. and 3 p.m. (no later) [19][11].
  • Fee: $50 application fee; cash, check or credit card to the City of Manchester (card surcharge applies) [19].
  • Waiting period: none in New Hampshire; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in the state [19].
  • State rules (statewide): apply for a marriage license at any New Hampshire city or town clerk, regardless of where you live. There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in New Hampshire. Both parties must usually appear together. You must be 18 or older [4].

Land records (Hillsborough County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 19 Temple Street, Nashua (P.O. Box 370, Nashua, NH 03061-0370). Open Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–3:45 p.m. Phone (603) 882-6933. Manchester is in Hillsborough County [20][7].
  • Copies: $1 per page at the Registry counter; $2 per page via internet account; certified copies add $2 per document plus the copy charge. AVA public search prints are $2.05 per page [20].
  • Fax at the Registry: $2 per page incoming or outgoing [20].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): RSA 478:17-g sets $10 for the first recorded page plus $4 for each additional page for most documents; mortgage discharges and many lien releases are $15 first page plus $4 per additional page. An additional $25 LCHIP surcharge applies to each deed, mortgage, mortgage discharge, or plan [21].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the New Hampshire Secretary of State's Office, Room 204, State House, 107 North Main Street, Concord. Counter Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:15 p.m. [18].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per document; cash or check payable to the State of New Hampshire. Same-day service on 10 or more documents adds $25 per 10 documents [18].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, payment, and a self-addressed prepaid return envelope [18].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [22]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: Manchester store listings advertise shredding among other services; confirm price and drop-off rules with the store before you go [10].

Questions

Where can I get a $5 notary in Manchester?

Manchester City Clerk, One City Hall Plaza. Justices of the Peace on staff charge $5 per signature/seal; each signer must appear [2]. Note: the office will not sign documents that specifically require a "Notary" (as opposed to JP) signature [2].

How much is a birth certificate in Manchester?

Statewide search fee is $15 for the first certified copy and $10 for each additional copy of the same record [4][5]. Get it at the City Clerk (One City Hall Plaza) or at DVRA in Concord [17][4].

How much can a New Hampshire notary charge?

Up to $10 per ordinary notarial act (oath, witness, service, or certification). Remote acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act (statewide) [6][13].

Can I get an apostille on a Manchester birth certificate?

Request a notarized certified copy from the City Clerk or DVRA, then bring it to the Secretary of State in Concord. The apostille is $10 per document (statewide) [18].

Where do I record a deed for Manchester property?

Hillsborough County Registry of Deeds, 19 Temple Street, Nashua. Recording fees follow RSA 478:17-g ($10 first page + $4 each additional, plus $25 LCHIP on deeds) [20][21].

Where can I apply for a passport in Manchester?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 2 New Hampshire facilities with Manchester addresses [1]. The Census Geocoder places both inside Manchester city [14].
