Newark, New Jersey document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Newark, New Jersey, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Short version: the Essex County Clerk (by appointment) and the Newark Post Office at 2 Federal Square accept passport applications [1][2]. New Jersey notaries may charge $2.50 per act [3]. Newark Public Library printouts are 15¢ a page in black and white [4].
Who does what: Newark is in Essex County [5][6]. In New Jersey, you apply for a marriage license with the local registrar of the municipality where either of you lives [7]. The state Office of Vital Statistics and Registry in Trenton issues birth, death and marriage records [8]. Deeds are recorded by the Essex County Register of Deeds and Mortgages [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Newark Public Library (Main Library, 5 Washington Street): the Main Branch has computers, printers and scanners. Printouts are 15¢ a page in black and white and 45¢ in color, paid from your library card or a guest pass. The Victoria Technology Center is on the third floor, 973-733-3603 [4].
  • No library fax: the library doesn't have fax machines and suggests The UPS Store [4].
  • The UPS Store: its Newark page lists 3 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [10]. The Census Geocoder places all 3 (8 Lombardy St, 1244-1254 McCarter Hwy and 386 South St) inside Newark [2]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Newark

  • The UPS Store: its Newark page lists notarizing [10]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [11].
  • Library notary: we didn't find a notary service at the Newark Public Library (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Jersey's Division of Revenue says a notary may charge $2.50 each for an oath or affirmation, a jurat, a proof of a deed or an acknowledgment [3]. The state Notary Public Manual covers the real-estate closing fees [12].

  • Post office fees (national): a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [15].
  • Faster return delivery (national): two official pages list different prices. USPS lists "$22.05—1- to 3- day delivery service for faster return shipping to you from the State Department" [15]. The State Department's fee page lists 1–3 business day return delivery at $26.87, for passport books only [16]. We couldn't re-read the State Department page on Nov 3, 2026, because it blocked our check. Confirm the fee with the acceptance facility before you pay.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Birth, death and marriage records from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the New Jersey Office of Vital Statistics and Registry charges $25 for the search and one certified copy (or a certification, or a no-record statement), and $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. VitalChek adds a processing fee of $6 by mail or $12.95 by phone [17].
  • Certified copy or certification: certified copies have a raised seal and are legal documents. Certifications are on plain paper, can't be used for identity or legal purposes, and are mainly useful for genealogy [18].
  • Walk-in (statewide, in Trenton): the walk-in center at 140 E. Front Street, Trenton offers same-day service. It has not taken cash since June 18, 2026 [8]. Online orders go through VitalChek [8][19].
  • Local copies: municipal local registrars also issue copies, and their fees vary by municipality [17].

Marriage licenses and local copies (Newark local registrar)

  • The rules (statewide): apply in the New Jersey municipality where either of you lives. If neither of you lives in New Jersey, apply where the ceremony will be held. You need a witness 18 or older, and the application fee is $28. There's a 72-hour waiting period before the license is issued [7]. The state fee list also shows the $28 application [17].
  • Newark's office: the City of Newark's Vital Statistics page refused our request, so we couldn't confirm Newark's address, hours or local copy fees. It needs a browser read (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [20]. Local copy fees vary by municipality [17].

Deeds and land records (Essex County)

  • Essex County Register of Deeds and Mortgages: Hall of Records, Room 130, 465 Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., Newark; 973-621-4960; Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., last transaction 4:15 p.m. Its free Record Alert system tells you if a document is recorded under your name [9].
  • Copy fees: we didn't find a current copy fee on the Register's site (last checked Nov 4, 2026). The Essex County Clerk says it is not the records custodian for open-records requests and points deed recording to the Register [13].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New Jersey Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (Department of the Treasury), for documents signed by a New Jersey notary or selected public officials such as county clerks and the State Registrar. It gives an apostille for Hague Convention countries and a certification for other countries [21].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 per certificate, or $5 per certificate for documents for a child's adoption. The fee table also lists 2-hour and 1-hour expedite fees for walk-in requests, but we couldn't match those amounts to their rows (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [22].
  • Vital records: order a certified copy from the state Office of Vital Statistics, and say on the application that it needs an apostille [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Essex County Paper Shredding Day (past): the 2026 event was Saturday, June 6, 2026, from 8:30 a.m. to noon at the Essex County Department of Public Works, 99 West Bradford Avenue, Cedar Grove, outside Newark [24][25][2]. We didn't find a later 2026 county event (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Newark page lists shredding [10].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Newark?

At the Essex County Clerk's Office, 495 Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd. (by appointment), or the Newark Post Office, 2 Federal Square [1][13].

How much does a New Jersey notary charge?

$2.50 per oath, jurat, proof or acknowledgment [3].

How much is a New Jersey birth certificate from the state?

$25 for the search and one certified copy, plus $2 for each extra copy ordered at the same time [17].
