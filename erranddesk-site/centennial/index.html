Centennial, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Centennial, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Centennial, Arapahoe County, Arapahoe Libraries, USPS, the U.S. Department of State or any business named here. This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Centennial: Arapahoe County has a Centennial branch of the Clerk and Recorder at Lima Plaza, 6954 S. Lima St. [11], and Arapahoe County Public Health offers vital records appointments at Lima Plaza, 6964 S. Lima Street [14]. Library service comes from Arapahoe Libraries, which has four libraries with Centennial addresses: Castlewood, Koelbel, Smoky Hill and Southglenn [2][3][4][5]. Passport search results can include other places with "Centennial" in a street name, such as Centennial Parkway in Sandy, Utah, so check that a result says Centennial, Colorado [7].
Short version: Arapahoe Libraries offers free printing, free scanning and free outgoing fax at every library [1]. The City of Centennial accepts passport applications by appointment at the Centennial Civic Center, 13133 E. Arapahoe Rd. [8]. Birth certificates are $25 for the first copy (statewide fee), and Arapahoe County Public Health doesn't add a card fee [13].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax: Arapahoe Libraries

ServiceWhat Arapahoe Libraries says
Printing and copying"Free printing from computers at all libraries," or wirelessly from your own device. Color or black and white, with a daily limit of up to 200 single-sided pages. Only library paper can be used. Letter, legal and tabloid (11"x17") sizes [1].
ScanningEvery library has a scan station that can send to an email address, save to a USB drive or save online. "There is no charge for this service" [1].
Fax"Fax machines are available at every library, free of charge, to send outgoing documents" [1]. The page doesn't mention receiving faxes.
Computers and Wi-FiLibrary computers are available for 30 minutes at a time, extended if no one is waiting. Laptops for in-library use are offered at Castlewood, Koelbel, Smoky Hill and Southglenn, among others. Free Wi-Fi at all locations [1].

Notary in Centennial

  • Arapahoe Libraries: its notary guide says "Arapahoe Libraries does not currently offer notary services" [6].
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [17].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [18]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 16748 E Smoky Hill Rd Ste 9C, Centennial, CO 80015 says it has a commissioned notary public and lists notary hours as "Available 7 Days." Phone (303) 680-2300. Its page doesn't post a price [19]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [17].
  • City of Centennial: the City Clerk's list of services doesn't include notary service [9].

Passports in Centennial

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator lists one acceptance facility with a Centennial, Colorado address [7]:

FacilityAddress and phonePassport hours listedPhotos
City of Centennial13133 E. Arapahoe Rd., Centennial, CO 80112. 303-325-8000Tue–Fri 8:15 a.m.–4 p.m., closed 12–1 p.m., by appointment (the city's own page says Tue–Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m.)Listed as on site
  • City of Centennial Passport Office: takes first-time adult applications and new and renewal minor applications. It does not accept adult renewals. Appointments are by appointment only through the city's online portal, at the Centennial Civic Center, and "Walk-ins are not accepted." Each 20-minute slot is for one applicant, so book one slot per person. Arriving more than 5 minutes late means rescheduling [8].
  • City fees: acceptance fee $35 and optional photo fee $15, paid by cash, check or money order to the City of Centennial. The city doesn't take debit or credit cards, and you must bring a checkbook or money order or you'll be turned away. The city's page lists the State Department fees as $130 for adults (16 and older) and $100 for ages 15 and younger, by check or money order to the U.S. Department of State. Phone 303-754-3400, email Passport@centennialco.gov [8].
  • Arapahoe County Clerk and Recorder: the locator lists the county's passport offices in Littleton (5334 South Prince St.) and Aurora (490 S Chambers Rd), not at the Centennial branch [7]. Book through the county's online scheduler, because "Appointments cannot be made by phone." Appointments are released 21 days ahead. The county's processing fee is "exactly $35 per application," by check or money order to "Arapahoe County Clerk." The Littleton office has a self-service photo booth: two photos for $14.99 plus tax [10].
  • Post Office: USPS fees are national: a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [20]. The locator lists no post office with a Centennial address [7]. Search by ZIP code to find nearby ones.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Arapahoe County Public Health

  • Fee (statewide): $25 for the first copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [13].
  • Any Colorado county: the office provides birth and death certificates "that occurred in any Colorado county." It "does not charge additional service or credit card fees," and it doesn't take Apple, Samsung or Google Pay [13].
  • In person in Centennial: appointments at Centennial—Lima Plaza, 6964 S. Lima Street, or Aurora—Altura Plaza. Call 303-734-4300 to schedule [14]. Hours are Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., closed daily 12–1 p.m. In-person payment can be cash, check, money order or credit card, and certificates are "typically issued within 20 minutes" [13].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Arapahoe County Clerk and Recorder

  • Centennial branch: Arapahoe County Lima Plaza, 6954 S. Lima St., Centennial, CO 80112, (303) 795-4500. Since Jan. 1, 2026, all Clerk and Recorder branches open at 8 a.m. and close at 4 p.m. Some in-person services are by appointment only. Red 24-hour document drop boxes are outside every branch and take check or money order only [11].
  • Recording: a flat $43 per document under Colorado HB 24-1269 (a statewide change). DD214 recording and 2 certified copies are free. Death certificates and verification of death are free [12].
  • Copies: $0.25 per page non-certified. Add $1 per document for a certified copy. Online purchases add a $2 card fee per transaction. Marriage or civil union license: $30 [12].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Money orders work for the City of Centennial's passport fees and the county's passport processing fee and drop-box payments [8][10][11]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Is printing free at Arapahoe Libraries in Centennial?

Yes. Arapahoe Libraries says printing from library computers is free at all libraries, up to 200 single-sided pages a day. Scanning and outgoing fax are free too [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Centennial, Colorado?

The City of Centennial accepts first-time adult and minor applications by appointment at the Centennial Civic Center, 13133 E. Arapahoe Rd. It doesn't take adult renewals or walk-ins [8].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Centennial?

Arapahoe County Public Health has appointments at Lima Plaza, 6964 S. Lima Street. Call 303-734-4300. It issues certificates for births in any Colorado county [13][14].

Can I get something notarized at the library in Centennial?

No. Arapahoe Libraries says it "does not currently offer notary services" [6]. Colorado caps notary fees at $15 per document in person [17].
