Bowie, Maryland document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Bowie, Maryland, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Bowie, Prince George's County, the Prince George's County Circuit Court, the Maryland Judiciary, the Prince George's County Memorial Library System, the Maryland Secretary of State, the Maryland Department of Health, USPS, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: a Prince George's County marriage license is $70, from the Clerk's Office in Upper Marlboro (outside Bowie) [1]. County libraries print the first 10 black-and-white pages free, then $0.15 a page, and send faxes for $1.75 for the first page [2]. Two Bowie post offices accept passport applications [3].
Who does what: Bowie is in Prince George's County [4][5]. The Clerk of the Prince George's County Circuit Court, at 14735 Main Street in Upper Marlboro (outside Bowie), handles marriage licenses [1].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Prince George's County Memorial Library System: every branch has black-and-white printers at $0.15 a page, with the first 10 pages free. Color printing ($0.60 a page, first 2 free) is only at the Hyattsville, Laurel, Oxon Hill and Spauldings branches [2]. Faxes to U.S. numbers cost $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page; the machines take cards, not cash, and only send [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Bowie page lists 2 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [6]. The Census Geocoder places 15480 Annapolis Rd in Bowie city; it couldn't match 3540 Crain Hwy [7]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Bowie

  • The UPS Store: its Bowie page lists notarizing [6].
  • Library notary: we didn't find a Prince George's County Memorial Library System notary service on the pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maryland's notary fees are set by state regulation (COMAR 01.02.08.02, effective Jan 22, 2024): at most $8 per notarial act, or $30 per remote notarial act. For extra copies of the same record signed at the same time, $4 per signature on each extra copy. Photocopies are at most $2 each, certifying a journal copy is $6, and travel is IRS mileage plus up to $5 [8].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Maryland Department of Health's Division of Vital Records charges $10 for a birth certificate and $10 for a death certificate, $12 for each additional death certificate in the same transaction and $12 for a marriage certificate. Internet orders add $13, and expedited shipping (internet only) adds $20. Local health departments' fees vary [11].
  • How (statewide): the Division's lobby serves customers by appointment only; you can also order by mail to P.O. Box 68760, Baltimore, MD 21215-0036, or online through VitalChek [12][13].

Marriage licenses (Prince George's County Circuit Court, Upper Marlboro)

  • Fee: $70, by cash, credit card or money order; no personal checks [1].
  • Where and when: Clerk's Office, 14735 Main Street, Upper Marlboro (outside Bowie), 301-952-3331, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [1].
  • Copies and extras: certified copies $5.50 each; replacement licenses $10 within the six-month validity; civil ceremonies $25 [1].

Court records, divorce decrees and land records

  • Copies (statewide): the Maryland Judiciary's circuit court fee schedule, effective Sept 2, 2026, sets copies at 50 cents a page, plus $5 for each certification or $10 to exemplify [14]. Plat copies are 50 cents a page for 11 x 17 inches or smaller and $1 a page for larger sheets [14]. Prince George's County has no county land-records fee page that we found, so this statewide schedule applies (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maryland Secretary of State, 16 Francis Street, Wineland Building, Annapolis, 410-974-5521. Certifications and apostilles are $5 each, by mail or in person [15].
  • Walk-ins, the page conflicts: the same page says "WALK-INS NOT AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME" (scheduled appointments may change because of building construction) and also that the desk is open for walk-ins until 1 p.m., 9 a.m.–1 p.m. weekdays, with a limit of 10 documents per customer a day [15]. Call before you go.
  • Notarized documents (statewide): take them first to the Clerk of the Circuit Court in the county where the notary was commissioned [15].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Bowie page lists shredding [6].
  • Free events (past): the City of Bowie's 2026 paper shredding events were Saturday, March 21 and Saturday, June 20, 2026, at 16500 Annapolis Road. Residents could bring up to five tall kitchen bags or five archive boxes [17]. Both dates have passed, and we didn't find a later paper shredding event (last checked Nov 4, 2026). One city calendar event page needs a sign-in, so we couldn't read it.
  • Not paper shredding: the city's Public Works Electronic Recycling Event on Nov 7, 2026, 8 a.m.–2 p.m., takes electronics for free, but hard drives won't be shredded on site [18].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where do Bowie residents get a marriage license?

At the Prince George's County Clerk's Office in Upper Marlboro, for $70 [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Bowie?

At the Bowie Main and Mitchellville post offices [3].

Does the library send faxes?

Yes: $1.75 for the first U.S. page, $1.00 after, card only [2].
