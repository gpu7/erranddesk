Topeka, Kansas document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Topeka, Kansas, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Which offices serve Topeka: the Shawnee County Register of Deeds is at 200 SE 7th St., Room 108 [10]. Marriage licenses come from the Shawnee County District Court Probate Department in the Clerk's Office, not a county clerk [6]. Birth, death and marriage certificates come from the state KDHE Office of Vital Statistics, which is in Topeka [6][17].
Short version: the Topeka & Shawnee County Public Library gives 2 free prints a day, then charges 10¢ a page in black and white, and self-service faxing is free [1]. It also offers free notary service by appointment [3]. A marriage license is $85.50 [6]. Kansas certified birth, death and marriage copies are $20 each (statewide) [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Topeka & Shawnee County Public Library

  • Where and when: 1515 SW 10th Ave., Topeka, KS 66604. Open Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–9 p.m., Sat 9 a.m.–6 p.m. and Sun noon–9 p.m. [2].
  • Printing: 2 free pages a day, then 10 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents in color. All printing is single-sided on standard white paper; you can't add your own paper. A large-format printer in the Level 2 Tech Center costs $2.00 per linear foot [1].
  • From your phone or laptop: the SmartALEC service prints uploads, email attachments and web pages. Uploads last 7 days or until printed [1].
  • Copies: 10 cents black and white and 50 cents color. Copiers take letter, legal or 11x17 paper and can copy double-sided [1].
  • Paying: prints and copies are paid from your library card account. Add money online with a credit card, or at a checkout kiosk with cash or a credit card [1].
  • Fax: self-service faxing is free for local, toll-free and long-distance numbers, at machines near the Customer Service Desk. The library doesn't accept incoming faxes [1].
  • Scanning: scan to a USB drive at the copiers, or use the flatbed scanners in the Level 2 Tech Center [1]. We didn't find a scanning charge.

The UPS Store

The UPS Store #3349, 4011 SW 29th St, Topeka, (785) 271-6245, lists fax, notary and shredding services. Its page lists hours of Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–7 p.m. and Sat 10 a.m.–4 p.m., with Sunday closed [16]. It's the only Topeka store on the company's list. We didn't find prices on its page.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Topeka

  • Library (free): free notary service by appointment, Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Sat 3–4:30 p.m. Book online or call (785) 580-4400. Bring government-issued original ID (current or expired within the last 3 years) and unsigned documents. Bring your own witnesses if needed. The appointment is cancelled if you're 15 minutes late. Library notaries can't notarize I-9 forms and only notarize English-language documents [3].
  • The UPS Store: the 4011 SW 29th St store lists notary service [16]. We didn't find its notary hours or price.
  • Fees (statewide): the Kansas Secretary of State says "State law does not prohibit a notary from charging a fee for a notarial act." We didn't find a dollar limit in its notary page or handbook. Under K.A.R. 7-43-16 a fee must be disclosed to you and agreed to before the notarization, you must be told that the fee is permitted but not required by state law, and it must be collected at the time and recorded in the notary's journal [12][13]. Other states we cover set dollar caps instead: Nebraska's $5 acknowledgment cap, Utah's $10 per signature, Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document. Ask the price before you start.
  • Spouses (statewide): a Kansas notary may not notarize a record in which the notary or the notary's spouse is a party or has a direct financial or beneficial interest [12][13]. We didn't find a broader Kansas rule on other relatives on the pages we checked.
  • ID and journal (statewide): a notary may accept a driver's license, passport or other government ID that expired not more than three years before the notarization, and since January 1, 2022 Kansas notaries must keep a journal [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [14]. We didn't check which Topeka branches offer it. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death and marriage certificates: KDHE Office of Vital Statistics

  • Where: the Shawnee County court sends you to Vital Statistics for certified marriage copies [6]. The Wyandotte County District Court lists the office at 1000 SW Jackson Ste. 120, Topeka, (785) 296-1400 [17].
  • Fees (statewide): under K.A.R. 28-17-6, as amended July 1, 2022, certified copies of birth, death, stillbirth, marriage and divorce certificates are $20.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [8].
  • Walk-in hours and ordering options: KDHE's own pages blocked our automated check, so we didn't confirm walk-in hours or online ordering fees. Check kdhe.ks.gov or call 785-296-1400.

Marriage licenses: Shawnee County District Court

  • Applying: the court says you can apply online at the Kansas Judicial Branch's website. Licenses are handled by the Probate Department in the Clerk's Office [6].
  • Fee: $85.50, paid when you apply through the Centralized Court Payment Center [6]. The statewide Kansas Self-Help page adds a $2.14 credit or debit card fee or a $1.25 electronic check fee [7].
  • Timing (statewide): processing can take two weeks from your application, and the license is valid for six months. Return it to the court within 10 days of the marriage. Wait at least two weeks after the ceremony before requesting a certified copy [7].
  • Contact: Shawnee County Courthouse, 200 SE 7th Street, Room 209 (second floor), Topeka, KS 66603. Probate/Marriage (785) 251-6362; Clerk's Office (785) 251-6700. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m., excluding legal holidays [6].

Court records: Shawnee County District Court

  • How: view digital records at casesearch.kscourts.gov or at public access terminals in the courthouse. For copies, submit a KORA (Kansas Open Records Act) request form in person or by mail to the Clerk of the District Court, 200 SE 7th Room 209, Topeka, KS 66603 [9].
  • Fees: $0.50 a page for microfilm records from before imaging, and $0.50 per document for imaged records. Mail requests usually require prepaying a $20.00 research and copy fee. Large requests may cost more [9].

Recorded documents: Shawnee County Register of Deeds

  • Where and when: 200 SE 7th St., Room 108, 785.251.4020. Office hours Mon–Thu 7:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Fri 7:30–11:30 a.m. (closed Friday afternoon). No documents are recorded after 4:15 p.m. [10].
  • Fees: recording a deed, mortgage or other instrument is $21.00 for the first page and $17.00 for each additional page. Certifying any instrument of record is $13.00 [11]. We didn't find a per-page copy price.
  • Paying: cash, credit or debit (a 2.5% fee is added), or check payable to Register of Deeds [11].
  • Returns: a self-addressed stamped envelope is required for return of recorded documents [10].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [15]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: the 4011 SW 29th St store offers shredding through Iron Mountain, charged per pound [16]. We didn't find its price.
  • Free events: we didn't find a City of Topeka or Shawnee County document-shredding event on an official page.

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Can I fax for free in Topeka?

Yes. Self-service faxing at the Topeka & Shawnee County Public Library is free for local, toll-free and long-distance numbers [1].

Is there a free notary in Topeka?

The library offers free notary service by appointment; call (785) 580-4400 [3].

Where can I apply for a passport in Topeka?

The State Department's locator lists Topeka SANDDC, 1410 NW Gage Blvd [4].

Where do I get a marriage license in Topeka?

From the Shawnee County District Court; apply online. The license is $85.50 [6].
