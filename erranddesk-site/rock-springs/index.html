Rock Springs, Wyoming document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Rock Springs, Wyoming, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Wyoming rules differ from Colorado's in several places, and we point those out.

Which offices serve Rock Springs: Rock Springs is in Sweetwater County, Wyoming. This page covers the Sweetwater County Library System, which has two libraries in Rock Springs [1]; the Sweetwater County Clerk, whose office is in Green River, for recording and marriage licenses [4]; the Rock Springs Post Office for passports [8]; and Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, the state office in Cheyenne that issues birth and death certificates [9][10].
Short version: Both Rock Springs libraries take print jobs you send from home [3], but we didn't find printing or fax prices on the library's site. Passports go through the Rock Springs Post Office, since the Sweetwater County Clerk of District Court is "no longer a passport application acceptance facility" [7][8]. WyHy Federal Credit Union says its notary services are free [18]. Birth certificates come from the state office, at $25 each [11].

Notary in Rock Springs

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on the Sweetwater County Library System's site [1].
  • Credit union (example): WyHy Federal Credit Union says "Notary services are free" [18]. Its Rock Springs branch, 2611 Foothill Blvd, Rock Springs, WY 82901, 800.442.2392, lists notary services. Branch hours: Mon, Tue, Thu and Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m., Wed 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; Saturday 9 a.m.–noon is drive-thru only [19].
  • Wyoming fee limit (statewide): under W.S. 32-3-126, a notarial officer may charge "not more than ten dollars ($10.00) per notarial act," may charge less or waive the fee, and may add a technology fee for electronic notarization or a travel fee (up to the IRS standard mileage rate) only if you agree to it in advance [13]. The Wyoming Secretary of State's notary page links to this law [12]. This differs from Colorado, where the limit is $15 per document.
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [14]. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Wyoming Vital Statistics Services (state office)

  • Who issues them (statewide): in Wyoming, certified birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates come from the state's Vital Statistics Services, part of the Wyoming Department of Health. Its office is in Cheyenne [11][12]. We didn't find a vital records counter in Rock Springs or Sweetwater County. This differs from Colorado, where county public health offices issue certificates.
  • In person (Cheyenne): 2300 Capitol Avenue, 1st Floor of the Hathaway Building, Cheyenne. Public hours Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; Friday by appointment only, (307) 777-7591 [12].
  • Fees (statewide): birth, marriage, divorce or stillbirth certificate $25.00 each. Death certificate $25.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy per request [13].
  • Closed records (statewide): "Wyoming is a closed record state." Birth records are closed to the public for 100 years and death, marriage and divorce records for 50 years, so only entitled people (such as the person named, a parent, or immediate family for a death record) can get copies. You need a government-issued photo ID, or a notarized signature on the form [13].
  • By mail: send the form with ID, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Checks or money orders go to "Vital Statistics Services"; cash is accepted but not recommended [13]. The state's How to order page also links an online ordering portal [11]. We couldn't read the portal's fees because it needs JavaScript.

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Sweetwater County Clerk

  • Office: Sweetwater County Clerk, 80 W. Flaming Gorge Way, Suite 150, Green River, WY 82935. Phone (307) 872-3732, or (307) 922-5208 from Rock Springs. Hours Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; recording hours 8 a.m.–4 p.m. [4].
  • Recording: $12 for the first page and $3 for each additional page. Military discharge and separation papers are recorded at no charge. Copies are $0.25 a page and certification is $5 [5]. This differs from Colorado's flat $43 per document.
  • Marriage licenses: $30.00, paid by cash, credit or money order. Both people must appear in person with photo ID. Licenses are issued 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., can be used anywhere in Wyoming and expire one year from issue. Return the license within 10 days of the ceremony. The marriage page lists a copy of a lost license at $5.00 and says certified marriage copies are available through Wyoming Vital Statistics Services [6]. The fee page lists a certified copy of a marriage license on file at $5 [5]. Call the clerk to confirm which copy you need.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. The Sweetwater County Clerk accepts money orders for marriage licenses [6]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Rock Springs?

At the Rock Springs Post Office, 2829 Commercial Way. Schedule at usps.com/scheduler [8]. The Clerk of District Court no longer takes applications [7].

Where can I get something notarized for free in Rock Springs?

WyHy Federal Credit Union says its notary services are free; its Rock Springs branch is at 2611 Foothill Blvd [18][19]. Bank of America offers free notary to its customers [14].

Where is the Sweetwater County Clerk?

In Green River, at 80 W. Flaming Gorge Way, Suite 150. Call (307) 922-5208 from Rock Springs [4].

How much is a marriage license in Sweetwater County?

$30.00, paid by cash, credit or money order [6].
