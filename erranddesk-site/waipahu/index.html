Waipahu, Hawaii document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Waipahu CDP, Hawaii, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Counties and recording in Hawaii (statewide): Vital records and marriage licenses are issued by the state Department of Health (and licensed marriage agents), not by city clerks. Land records are recorded at the state Bureau of Conveyances in Honolulu under a statewide fee schedule — Hawaii does not use county recorders for deeds [1][2][3].
Short version: UPS lists 2 Waipahu stores (94-1221 Ka Uka Blvd #108; 94-830 Moloalo St #102) [4]. FedEx: OfficeMax/Depot only (94-861 Lumiaina St) — no FedEx Office [5]. Passport locator dump: 0 Waipahu city-name matches [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Hawaii State Public Library System: a print donation of 15¢ per page is appreciated [7]. Photocopies: an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page; color and special paper may cost more — fees are displayed at each library [8].
  • FedEx: Waipahu has no FedEx Office — listings show FedEx at OfficeMax/Office Depot (94-861 Lumiaina St) [5]. Office Depot national document-printing starting prices: $0.18 B&W / $0.70 color per page — confirm at the Waipahu OfficeMax counter (store 6203) [9][10].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Waipahu locations (94-1221 Ka Uka Blvd #108; 94-830 Moloalo St #102); pages advertise printing, faxing, shredding and notary [4].

See printing prices.

Notary in Waipahu, Hawaii

  • City Clerk: City and County of Honolulu, 530 S King St Rm 100, (808) 768-3810. We didn't find a published public notary customer fee; /clerk/notary/ returned 404 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [11].
  • The UPS Store: Waipahu locations advertise notary; fees vary within statewide caps [4][12].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Hawaii commissions notaries through the Attorney General. Application $20, examination $10, commission $100; renewal $120; four-year term; $1,000 surety bond [13][14]. Under HRS §456-17, notaries may charge at most $5 for an acknowledgment (per party signing) or for administration of an oath; remote online notarial acts are capped at $25 [15][16]. A notary may charge less [16].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): certified birth and marriage certificates are $10 for the first copy and $4 for each additional copy of the same record, plus a $2.50 portal/administration fee per increment of up to five copies [20]. Death certificates use the same $10 / $4 copy fees plus the $2.50 fee [21].
    • Oahu Vital Records office: 201 Merchant St., Suite 100, City Financial Tower, Honolulu. Phone (808) 586-4539 (certificates). Hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (issuance/marriage); closed state holidays [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $3.00 per document [22].

    Marriage licenses

    • State rules (statewide): a marriage or civil union license costs $65.00 ($60 application fee + $5 portal fee), non-refundable. Apply online at emrs.ehawaii.gov, then both applicants meet a licensed agent together in person no more than 30 days before the ceremony [23][24].
    • Where for Waipahu residents: no separate Waipahu agent heading on the Oct 2026 list — use Honolulu / Oahu licensed agents (apply online first) [24][23].

    Land records (Bureau of Conveyances)

    • Bureau of Conveyances office: Kalanimoku Building, 1151 Punchbowl Street, Honolulu. Office hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recordation windows about 8:01 a.m.–3:29 p.m. (confirm before you go) [2][25].
    • Recording fees (statewide): Bureau of Conveyances Regular System: $41 per document up to 50 pages, or $106 for 51 pages or more. Land Court: $36 (≤50 pages) or $101 (51+); Certificate of Title issuance $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [22].
    • Fee (statewide): $3.00 per document (apostille or certification) [22].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Shredding

    • The UPS Store: Waipahu locations advertise shredding [4].
    • City and County of Honolulu ENV: we didn't find a city-run household paper-shredding event (last checked Nov 5, 2026). ENV lists commercial collectors Access, Shred It, and Shredex — not city-run events [27].

    For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

    Where can I get a notary in Waipahu?

    The UPS Store lists two Waipahu locations that advertise notary [4]. Statewide acknowledgment cap is $5 per party [15].
