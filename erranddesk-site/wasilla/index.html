Wasilla, Alaska document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Wasilla, Alaska, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Wasilla, Alaska Department of Health, Alaska DNR Recorder's Office, the Lieutenant Governor, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Boroughs and recording in Alaska (statewide): Alaska uses boroughs and census areas rather than counties. Vital records are issued by the state Health Analytics and Vital Records Section (HAVRS), not by city clerks. Land records are kept by the Department of Natural Resources Recorder's Office under a statewide fee schedule [1][2][3].
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport facility in Wasilla [4]. HAVRS vital copies $30/$25; marriage license $60 via HAVRS or any Alaska Court that issues licenses [1][5]. Palmer recording district is served by the Anchorage Recorder's Office [6]. UPS lists 2 Wasilla stores [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Wasilla Public Library: 500 N Crusey St, 907-376-5913. Print $0.15/page B&W only; wireless printing is not available [8].
  • FedEx: Wasilla has no FedEx Office — listings show FedEx at Walgreens, a FedEx Ship Center, and The Mail Depot (Authorized ShipCenter). No FedEx Office print price applies [9].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Wasilla locations; pages advertise printing, faxing, shredding and notary [7].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Wasilla, Alaska

  • City Clerk: 290 E Herning Avenue. Phone 907-373-9090. Hours Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. except city holidays. Municipal code says the clerk maintains notary qualifications; we didn't find a published public notary customer fee on the Clerk page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [10].
  • The UPS Store: Wasilla locations advertise notary; fees vary [7][11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Alaska charges $40 for a regular notary public commission that lasts four years and requires a $2,500 bond [12]. The Lieutenant Governor's FAQ says there is no statutory maximum fee for notarial acts; a notary who charges must provide a published fee schedule before the act (AS 44.50.062(4)). Limited Governmental notaries may not charge [13].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Health Analytics and Vital Records (statewide): certified birth, death, and marriage certificates are $30 for the first copy and $25 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. Order in person at Anchorage or Juneau offices, by mail/fax, or through VitalChek (vendor fees may apply) [1].
    • Anchorage HAVRS office: 3901 Old Seward Highway, Suite 101, Anchorage, AK 99503. Phone 907-269-0991 [1].
    • Juneau HAVRS office: PO Box 110675, Juneau, AK 99811. Phone 907-465-3391 [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $5 per certificate [17].

    Marriage licenses

    • Where: apply at a HAVRS office or any Alaska Court that issues licenses. There is no Wasilla court in the court directory we checked; Palmer court (435 S Denali St, 907-746-8181) is listed for the Mat-Su area — we did not find a page that says Wasilla residents must use Palmer. Confirm with HAVRS or the court before you go [5][1].
    • Fee: $60 [1].
    • State rules (statewide): marriage licenses are $60 (re-issue $15) through Alaska Vital Records [1]. You may apply at HAVRS offices in Anchorage or Juneau, or at any Alaska Court — except the Anchorage and Juneau courts do not issue marriage licenses (use HAVRS there) [5][1].

    Land records (DNR — Anchorage office serves Palmer district)

    • Office: Anchorage Recorder's Office, 550 W 7th Ave., Suite 108. Phone 907-269-8876. The Anchorage office lists the Palmer recording district among districts served (Wasilla is in the Matanuska-Susitna Borough) [6][18][3].
    • Recording fees (statewide): DNR Recorder's Office charges $20 for the first page and $5 for each additional page of the same instrument, under AS 40.17.030 and 11 AAC 05.200. Extra names/claims over six are $2 each; non-standard documents $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [17].
    • Fee (statewide): $5 per certificate (apostille or authentication) [17].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Questions

    Where can I get a notary in Wasilla, Alaska?

    The UPS Store lists two Wasilla locations that advertise notary [7]. We didn't find a published City Clerk notary customer fee [10].

    How much is a birth certificate in Alaska?

    $30 first / $25 additional from HAVRS [1].

    Where is the Wasilla passport office?

    Wasilla Post Office at 401 N. Main St. appears in the locator [4].

    Where do I record a deed for Wasilla property?

    Anchorage Recorder's Office (Palmer district), 550 W 7th Ave., Suite 108. $20 first page + $5 additional [6][2].

    How much does printing cost at the Wasilla library?

    Wasilla Public Library printing is $0.15 per page, black and white only. Wireless printing is not available [8].
