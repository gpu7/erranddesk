Dover, New Hampshire document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Dover, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Dover, Dover Public Library, Strafford County Registry of Deeds, the State of New Hampshire, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Dover (Dover Post Office) [1]. Dover Public Library prints are 15¢ B&W / 50¢ color; fax $0.50/page; notary $5/document (call ahead) [2][3]. Certified vital copies are $15 / $10 [4].
Counties in New Hampshire (statewide): New Hampshire has ten counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds. Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the state Division of Vital Records Administration (Department of State) [5][6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Dover Public Library (73 Locust Street): black-and-white prints 15¢; color 50¢ (coins, $1 and $5 bills). Outgoing fax $0.50/page (library cannot receive faxes) [2].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Dover page lists 1 location — FedEx at Walgreens, 1 Glenwood Ave. We didn't find a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center listed on that page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [7].
  • The UPS Store: 859 Central Ave ((603) 343-2903). Store page advertises printing, faxing, shredding and notarizing [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Dover

  • Dover Public Library ($5): limited notary by staffing; call (603) 516-6050 first. $5 per notarized document (cash, check or credit with 3.99% fee). Max 5 documents per person per visit. Fill out documents before you arrive; bring government photo ID. Will not notarize deeds, wills, living wills/trusts, codicils, depositions, mortgage or real-estate closing documents (policy adopted Jan 8, 2026) [3].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state; NH's statewide cap is $10 per ordinary act [9][10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Hampshire caps ordinary notarial acts at $10 each (oath, witness, service, or certification). Remote notarial acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act. Depositions follow RSA 517:19 (SOS manual: $5 to $50, plus $0.20/mile to swear witnesses). No fee for administering oaths of office for town officers [10][11].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Dover City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 288 Central Ave., Dover, NH 03820. City Hall hours Monday–Thursday 8:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m.; Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. City main phone (603) 516-6000; marriage desk (603) 516-6018 [4][15].
  • Fees: search fee $15.00 (payable whether or not a record is found); additional copies of the same record at the same time $10.00 [4][16].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): any of New Hampshire's 234 city and town clerks can issue certified copies when you have a direct and tangible interest; many clerks use the NHVRIN system for records since 1935 [5].
  • State Division of Vital Records Administration (statewide): 9 Ratification Way, Concord, NH 03301-2455. Search fee $15 for the first copy (kept whether or not a record is found); $10 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [5][16].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): request a notarized certified copy from a city/town clerk or DVRA, then take it to the Secretary of State (vital-record photocopies are not accepted) [17][5].

Marriage licenses (Dover City Clerk)

  • When: both parties must be present; arrive by 4:30 p.m. Monday–Thursday or 3:00 p.m. Friday. Process usually about 30 minutes while you wait [15].
  • Fee: $50.00 filing fee [15].
  • Waiting period: none; license valid for 90 days [15].
  • State rules (statewide): apply for a marriage license at any New Hampshire city or town clerk, regardless of where you live. There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in New Hampshire. Both parties must usually appear together. You must be 18 or older [5].

Land records (Strafford County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 259 County Farm Road, Suite 202, Dover, NH 03820. Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recording stops at 4:00 p.m. Phone (603) 742-1741. Dover is the county seat for Strafford County [18][6].
  • Copies: $1 per page of recorded document; certification adds $1 plus the copy charge. Fax copies $3 first page / $1 each additional outgoing; $1/page incoming [18].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): RSA 478:17-g sets $10 for the first recorded page plus $4 for each additional page for most documents; mortgage discharges and many lien releases are $15 first page plus $4 per additional page. An additional $25 LCHIP surcharge applies to each deed, mortgage, mortgage discharge, or plan [19].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the New Hampshire Secretary of State's Office, Room 204, State House, 107 North Main Street, Concord. Counter Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:15 p.m. [17].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per document; cash or check payable to the State of New Hampshire. Same-day service on 10 or more documents adds $25 per 10 documents [17].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, payment, and a self-addressed prepaid return envelope [17].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: Dover store listing advertises shredding among other services; confirm price with the store before you go [8].
  • We didn't find a City of Dover household paper-shredding event schedule on the City Clerk pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026).

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

How much is library notary in Dover?

$5 per notarized document at Dover Public Library; call (603) 516-6050 first. Limited to 5 documents per visit; some document types are excluded [3].

How much is a birth certificate in Dover?

$15 search fee for the first certified copy and $10 for each additional copy of the same record at the City Clerk [4].

How much can a New Hampshire notary charge?

Up to $10 per ordinary notarial act. Remote acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act (statewide) [10][11].

Can I get an apostille on a Dover birth certificate?

Request a notarized certified copy from the City Clerk or DVRA, then bring it to the Secretary of State in Concord. The apostille is $10 per document (statewide) [17].

Where do I record a deed for Dover property?

Strafford County Registry of Deeds, 259 County Farm Road, Suite 202, Dover. Recording fees follow RSA 478:17-g [18][19].

Where can I apply for a passport in Dover?

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator data lists 1 New Hampshire facility with a Dover address [1]. The Census Geocoder places it inside Dover city [12].
