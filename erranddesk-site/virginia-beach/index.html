Virginia Beach, Virginia document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Virginia Beach, Virginia, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. This is general information, not legal advice. Prices and hours change; check the linked source before you go.
Short version: Virginia Beach Public Library prints black-and-white for 20 cents a page, sends faxes for free and offers free notary service by appointment [1][2]. A marriage license from the Circuit Court Clerk is $30 [3]. The City Clerk's Office takes passport applications by appointment, with a $35 acceptance fee [4]. Virginia notaries can charge up to $10 per act (statewide) [5].
An independent city, no county: Virginia Beach isn't part of any county. The Census Bureau lists "Virginia Beach city" [6] as its own county-level area [7], so the city's own Circuit Court Clerk handles marriage licenses, deeds and notary oaths [8]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Virginia Beach Public Library

  • Printing: letter or legal size is 20 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents in color; tabloid is 40 cents or $1. Print terminals take credit and debit cards only; cash is still accepted at Central Library. The TCC/City Joint-Use Library is different: 5 cents a page, black and white only, paid with a cash-loaded card [1].
  • Cashless since July 1, 2026: the library went cashless on July 1, 2026; cash, checks and money orders are still taken at Central Library only [1].
  • Copies and scans: black-and-white letter copies are 20 cents a page. The multifunction printers can scan to a USB stick [1].
  • Fax: send-only fax is free at every location except the TCC/City Joint-Use Library [1].
  • The UPS Store: its Virginia Beach page lists 10 locations and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [10]. The Census Geocoder places all 10 in Virginia Beach city [9]. Prices aren't listed, so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Virginia Beach

  • Library (free): free notary service by appointment at select locations; call 757-385-0150. All signers need current photo ID, and you bring your own witnesses [2].
  • Copies (sources differ): the library's notary page says its notaries can't notarize copies of any kind, or birth, marriage or death certificates [2]. State law lets a Virginia notary charge for certifying that a copy is a true copy [5], and the Circuit Court Clerk's notary page lists that act for documents not held by a court [11]. So the library won't do it, but another notary may.
  • Becoming a notary: after the state approves you, you take the oath at the Circuit Court Clerk's Office and pay the clerk $10 [11].
  • The UPS Store: its Virginia Beach page lists notarizing [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth commissions Virginia notaries. The application or renewal fee is $45, nonrefundable [13]. The new notary then takes the oath before the clerk of the circuit court the commission was sent to, within 60 days [14][13]; the clerk's fee for qualifying a notary is $10 [15].
  • Fees (statewide): a Virginia notary may charge up to $10 for an acknowledgment, oath, affidavit or a true-copy certification, and up to $25 for the same acts on an electronic document. Travel costs can be added only if you agree and the notary goes somewhere other than their usual place of business [5].
  • Copy certification (statewide): unlike some states, Virginia law lists "certifying that a copy of a document is a true copy" among the acts a notary may charge for [5]. Some documents, such as vital records, still have to come from the office that holds them.
  • Electronic notaries (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth also commissions electronic notaries and has a search for current notaries [13].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Local office: we didn't find a Virginia Beach city office that issues birth or death certificates on the pages we checked (last checked Nov 3, 2026). The state office is below.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): since July 1, 2026, the Virginia Department of Health charges $15 for each certified copy, or for each search when no record is found [19].
  • Who can order (statewide): only immediate family (mother, father, spouse, child, brother, sister and grandparents) with valid ID; a legal guardian with proof of custody can get a birth record [19].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): walk in Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–4 p.m. at 8701 Park Central Drive, Suite 100, Richmond. Drop-off requests for same-day pickup must be in by 2 p.m. Mail goes to VDH, Office of Vital Records, P.O. Box 1000, Richmond, VA 23218-1000. The customer care line is (804) 662-6200 [19].

Marriage licenses (Virginia Beach Circuit Court Clerk)

  • State law (statewide): the minimum age to marry is 18 [20]. A license is good for 60 days from the day it's issued [21]. The clerk's statutory fee for issuing it is $10 [15]; the clerk's own fee list (below) may show a different total.
  • Virginia Beach fee and hours (sources differ on the fee): the clerk's page lists the license at $30, plus $2 if you pay by card [3], while the statute's clerk fee is $10 [15]; expect to pay the clerk's $30. Both of you must come in with ID, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.; arrive by 3:30 p.m. The office is in Building 10B, third floor, entered through Building 10 [3].
  • Same-day ceremony: a person authorized to perform marriages is on duty at the office 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. weekdays and charges $75, cash only [3].
  • Certified copies: $2.50 per copy, in person or by mail, from the clerk that issued the license [3].

Recorded documents (Virginia Beach Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in Virginia, deeds are recorded by the clerk of the circuit court, and the same statewide clerk fees apply. Recording costs $18 for 10 or fewer pages, $32 for 11 to 30 pages and $52 for 31 or more (§ 17.1-275 A.2) [15].
  • Virginia Beach: the Circuit Court Clerk records and stores all real estate records for the city; the Land Records Department is at 757-385-8821. A free property notification service emails you if a document with your name is filed [22]. The state court system's page for the Virginia Beach Circuit Court says the clerk's copy fees are $0.50 per sheet and $2.00 per certification of order, and a self-addressed stamped envelope is required to have requested documents returned [23] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Court record copies (Virginia Beach Circuit Court Clerk)

  • Clerk copy fees (statewide): under Va. Code § 17.1-275, circuit court clerks charge $0.50 a page for copies not priced elsewhere in the law (A.8), and $2 to attach the court's seal and clerk's certificate, plus $0.50 for a judge's certificate (A.9) [15] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Where and when: 2425 Nimmo Parkway, Building 10, Virginia Beach, VA 23456; 757-385-4181; open 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., closed state holidays. The office doesn't accept requests for information by email or fax [8]. The Census Geocoder places it in Virginia Beach city [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Secretary of the Commonwealth's Authentication Division. Depending on the country, it issues a Great Seal or an Apostille. It authenticates documents from a Virginia notary, Virginia clerk of court or Virginia deputy state registrar; it doesn't notarize documents [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document (not per page). Several documents signed by the same official on the same date for the same country are $10.00 for the first and $5.00 for each additional one [25].
  • How to send (statewide): USPS mail goes to Secretary of the Commonwealth, Authentication Division, 1111 East Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219; FedEx, UPS and DHL go to 400 East Cary Street, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone 804-692-0114 [24][25]. In-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Thursday; appointments for the next week open Fridays at 10 a.m., up to 10 documents per customer or family [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does it cost to print at the Virginia Beach library?

20 cents a page for black and white and 50 cents for color on letter paper; 5 cents at the TCC/City Joint-Use Library [1].

Can I fax at the Virginia Beach library?

Yes. Send-only fax is free at every location except the TCC/City Joint-Use Library [1].

How much is a marriage license in Virginia Beach?

$30 at the Circuit Court Clerk, plus $2 by card [3].

Where do I apply for a passport in Virginia Beach?

At the City Clerk's Office by appointment (757-385-4303) or the Acredale Post Office [4][16].

Where do I get a Virginia birth certificate?

From the Virginia Department of Health, $15 per copy (statewide) [19].
