Hartford, Connecticut document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Hartford, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Hartford, Hartford Public Library, the State of Connecticut, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 3 passport acceptance facilities in Hartford, all post offices [1]. We didn't find a passport services page on Hartford Public Library's site (last checked Nov 4, 2026). The library's notary service is $5 by appointment [2]. Birth certificates are $20 at Vital Records [3]. A marriage license is $50, with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [4].
No county clerk in Connecticut (statewide): Connecticut abolished county government effective Oct. 1, 1960, and counties remain only as geographic areas [5][6]. Town clerks keep land records, and each town's registrar of vital statistics issues birth, death and marriage records.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Hartford Public Library: printing and copies are 15¢ a page black and white and 25¢ color. Library card holders get $1.00 of free printing a day. Scanning is free. Fax is $1.50 for the first page and $1.00 for each extra page [7][8].
  • Printing from your phone: email your file to the library's print address, use the Print Center or use the ePRINTit SaaS app [8].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Hartford page lists 2 locations: a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center at 544 Farmington Ave. and a Walgreens counter at 1291 Albany Ave. [9].
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find one inside Hartford. The UPS Store's Connecticut list includes West Hartford, a separate town, but not Hartford [10].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Hartford

  • Hartford Public Library ($5): by appointment only, after a phone consultation. Call 860-695-6300 or email reference@hplct.org. English- and Spanish-speaking notaries are available [2].
  • Town and City Clerk: the clerk's page says its notary services rose to $10 on July 1, 2026 [11]. Its notary page lists notary seals at $5.00, "INCREASE TO $10 AFTER JULY1, 2026." Service is in person only, with two forms of ID. The clerk won't notarize powers of attorney (except for a motor vehicle total loss), legal matter documents or documents involving minors. Phone (860) 757-9690 [27].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [12].
  • Notary fees (statewide): since July 1, 2026, a Connecticut notary may charge up to $10.00 per notarial act, plus travel at no more than the IRS business mileage rate. Public Act 26-81 raised the fee from $5.00 [13][14].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): the General Assembly's compiled statute page for §3-95, revised to January 1, 2026, still shows the old cap of $5.00 plus 35¢ a mile [15]. The Secretary of the State's pages give the $10.00 fee in effect since July 1, 2026 [13][14]. We show the newer figure.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Hartford Bureau of Vital Records

  • Where: City Hall, 550 Main St., 1st floor, Room 103. Phone (860) 757-9690. Staff are there Monday–Thursday 8:15 a.m.–4 p.m. and Friday 8:15 a.m.–2:30 p.m. [4].
  • Birth certificates: $20 full size, $15 wallet size. In person, you get them right away. Pay by card or money order payable to the City of Hartford; no cash or personal checks. Online orders through VitalChek cost extra [3].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Where (statewide): from the vital records office of the town where the event happened, or from the State Vital Records Office. Town requests are generally faster [19].
  • Fees (statewide): town-issued birth, death, marriage and civil union certificates are $20 per copy. State-issued birth certificates are $30 per copy [19][20].
  • Paying a town (statewide): by mail, towns take a money order payable to the town; personal checks aren't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds fees [19].

Marriage licenses (Bureau of Vital Records)

  • Fee and wait: $50.00, valid 65 days, by card or money order; no personal checks. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the registrar holds the application at least 48 hours before issuing the license. Bring a photo ID and a Social Security card (nonresidents: a passport) [4].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at the vital records office of the town where the wedding will be. The fee is $50.00. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the town can't issue the license until 48 hours after the second person signs the application. The license is good for 65 days [21][22].

Land records (Town and City Clerk)

  • Office: City Hall, 550 Main St., 1st floor, Suite 104. Monday–Thursday 8:15 a.m.–4:30 p.m., Friday 8:15 a.m.–3 p.m.; last recording at 3 p.m. Search records on SearchIQS. For a document with no image, call 860-757-9749 to request it by mail [11].
  • Fees: we didn't find a land record fee list on the city's pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026). The statewide fees below apply.
  • Copy fees (statewide law): CGS §7-34a sets town clerk copies at $1 per page and $2 to certify a copy. Recording is $10 for the first page and $5 for each extra page, plus $10 and $50 per-document state surcharges [23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Connecticut Secretary of the State. All orders must be placed online at sots.service.ct.gov/apostille; paper order forms are no longer accepted [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $40 per document, or $15 for documents for a child adoption. Expedited service is $50 more per document [25][15]. These figures come from the SOTS order form and the statute; the online-ordering page we read doesn't list fees.
  • Delivery (statewide): paper documents go by courier to 165 Capitol Avenue, Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06106, or by mail to P.O. Box 150470, Hartford, CT 06115-0470. E-apostilles began July 6, 2026, but vital records still need a paper apostille. E-apostille and expedited orders are processed within 24 hours [24].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I apply for a passport at Hartford Public Library?

We didn't find a passport services page on the library's site, and the locator doesn't list the library [1]. The locator lists 3 Hartford post offices [1].

How much is a notary at Hartford Public Library?

$5 per appointment, by appointment only [2].

How much is a birth certificate in Hartford?

$20 full size or $15 wallet size [3].

How much does a Connecticut notary charge?

Up to $10.00 per act since July 1, 2026, plus mileage (statewide) [13][14].

How much does printing cost at the Hartford library?

Hartford Public Library printing and copies are 15¢ a page in black and white and 25¢ in color. Card holders get $1.00 of free printing a day. Scanning is free, and fax is $1.50 for the first page and $1.00 for each extra page [7][8].
