Bellevue, Nebraska document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Bellevue, Nebraska, in Sarpy County just south of Omaha, using only official and provider pages. This is not Bellevue, Washington. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Nebraska rules differ from Colorado's, Wyoming's and Utah's in several places, and we point those out.

Which offices serve Bellevue: this page is about Bellevue, Nebraska, which is in Sarpy County. Its library is Bellevue Public Library, 2206 Longo Drive, a City of Bellevue department [1]. The county offices are in Papillion, not Bellevue. The Sarpy County Clerk/Register of Deeds handles passports and marriage licenses at the Sarpy County Courthouse Campus, 1210 Golden Gate Drive, and real estate recording at 1102 E. 1st Street [6]. The Clerk of the District Court (divorce and other court records) is at 1210 Golden Gate Drive, Suite 3141 [13]. The state's Office of Vital Records, which issues Nebraska birth certificates, is in Lincoln [15].
Short version: Bellevue Public Library offers notary service "free to all patrons," with no appointment needed, though calling first is recommended [3]. Staff-assisted faxing is $1.75 for the first page and $1 for each page after that, and computer printouts are 10 cents a page in black and white and 50 cents in color [1][2]. The Sarpy County Clerk takes passport applications by appointment only, and a new application must be filled out before the appointment starts [6][7]. A Sarpy County marriage license costs $66 with one certified copy, paid by credit or debit card [8][9]. Nebraska law caps notary fees at $5 for an acknowledgment (statewide) [17].

Notary in Bellevue

  • Bellevue Public Library (free): "Bellevue Public Library offers Notary services free to all patrons." Notaries are available most days during library hours, but not less than 45 minutes before closing, depending on which certified staff are working. No appointment is needed, but the library recommends calling first, and it doesn't guarantee availability [3].
  • Library notary rules: bring a current valid photo ID, such as a driver's license, U.S. or foreign passport or U.S. military ID. The library doesn't provide witnesses. Don't sign in advance. Documents must be in English, and electronic and remote notarizations aren't available. Library notaries can't certify that a copy of a record such as a birth certificate is a true copy [3].
  • The UPS Store: the 505 Cornhusker Rd store lists notary hours as "Available 7 Days" [25]. We didn't find its price.
  • Nebraska fee limits (statewide): under Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-133, a notary may charge $5 for taking an acknowledgment, $5 for each certificate and seal, $2 for taking an affidavit and seal, and $2 for administering an oath or affirmation, plus mileage for serving notice. A state or local government employee may not charge these fees if the employer paid the notary's commission and bond fees [17]. Nebraska's caps are much lower than Utah's $10 per signature, Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document.
  • Relatives (statewide): the Secretary of State says a Nebraska notary may not notarize a document signed by their parents, grandparents, children, grandchildren, spouse, brothers or sisters, including in-laws, step and half relatives [19].
  • Commissions (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions Nebraska notaries. Its online filing process for notary forms took effect November 20, 2023 [18].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [22]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • Difference from Omaha and Lincoln: Omaha Public Library doesn't have a notary on staff, and we didn't find one at Lincoln City Libraries. Bellevue's library offers one for free [3].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Nebraska Office of Vital Records

  • No local counter found: the Sarpy/Cass Health Department's "How Do I Request" page doesn't list birth or death certificates [14]. We didn't find a Sarpy County counter for certified copies. Douglas County's counter in Omaha serves births and deaths that happened in Douglas County (see our Omaha guide).
  • Where: the state office is in the Nebraska State Office Building, 301 Centennial Mall South, First Floor, Lincoln, NE [15][16]. We didn't find walk-in hours on the pages we checked.
  • Fees (statewide): $17.00 for each birth certificate and $16.00 for each death, marriage or divorce certificate, for walk-in and mail requests [16].
  • How long: walk-in requests take a minimum of 30 minutes, regular mail a minimum of 15 days in-office processing, and online applications a minimum of 7 days [16].
  • By mail: send a check or money order made out to "Vital Records" (no credit cards) to P.O. Box 95065, Lincoln, NE 68509-5065 [15][16].
  • Voter ID (statewide): if you don't have a current Nebraska driver's license or state ID card, you can ask for a no-fee certified birth certificate for voting purposes. Say so on the application [15].

Marriage licenses and copies: Sarpy County Clerk

  • Applying: apply in person together, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Bring the completed Marriage Worksheet, current government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID or passport) and a credit or debit card [8]. Cash isn't accepted [9].
  • Fees: effective July 18, 2026, the license is $50 and additional certified copies are $16 each, for a total of $66 [8]. These match the statewide fees in Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-110 as amended by LB596 [20].
  • By phone or mail: the county will mail you an application if you call 402-593-2105. For a certified copy of an existing license by mail, use the county's Application for Marriage Record [8].
  • Ceremonies: the county lists four Sarpy County judges who perform ceremonies; Judge Martinez (402-431-2622) performs them in English or Spanish [8].

Court records: Sarpy County Clerk of the District Court

  • Where: 1210 Golden Gate Drive, Suite 3141, Papillion, NE 68046-3087. 402-593-2267. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:45 p.m. [13]. The Clerk keeps criminal, civil and juvenile case records [12].
  • Filing fees: effective July 1, 2026, the court lists $200 to file a dissolution of marriage and $144 for a name change [13].
  • Copies: we didn't find the District Court's price for a certified copy of a decree. The county's public-records page says the County Clerk policy charges $0.25 per page for copies and $15 an hour for clerical time [11].

Recorded documents: Sarpy County Register of Deeds

  • Where: the Sarpy County 1102 Building, 1102 E. 1st Street, Papillion, NE 68046. 402-593-2183 [6].
  • Fees: recording a deed, mortgage, lien or other instrument is $10 for the first page and $6 for each additional page. The office takes checks, cashier's checks, money orders and credit cards (portal fee) [10]. These match Neb. Rev. Stat. 33-109 (statewide), which also sets certified copies at $1.50 per page [21].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. We didn't find a Bellevue or Sarpy County government office that sells money orders. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • City and county: we didn't find an official City of Bellevue or Sarpy County shredding event on the pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: the 505 Cornhusker Rd store offers shredding through Iron Mountain, charged per pound [26]. We didn't find its price.

Questions

Is the notary at Bellevue Public Library free?

Yes, free to all patrons. No appointment is needed, but call 402-293-3157 first, because availability depends on staff [3].

Can I fax at the Bellevue library?

Yes, staff-assisted faxing is $1.75 for the first page and $1 for each page after that [1].

Where do Bellevue residents apply for a passport?

At the Bellevue post office, 2609 Victoria Avenue [5], or by appointment at the Sarpy County Clerk in Papillion [6][7].

Where can I get a birth certificate near Bellevue?

We didn't find a Sarpy County counter. The state Office of Vital Records in Lincoln issues them for $17.00, in person or by mail [15][16].
