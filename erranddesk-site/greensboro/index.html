Greensboro, North Carolina document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Greensboro, North Carolina, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: Greensboro Public Library charges 15 cents a page for black-and-white prints and copies, 50 cents for color and 10 cents a scan [1]. Guilford County birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each at the Register of Deeds [2]. A marriage license is $60 [2]. North Carolina notaries can charge at most $10 per signature (statewide) [3]. The State Department's locator lists 2 passport offices with a Greensboro address [4].
City and county: this page covers the city the Census Bureau calls "Greensboro city" [5], which is in Guilford County [6]. We use the Census Geocoder to check which addresses are inside the city [7]. A Greensboro mailing address doesn't always mean the city itself, so we note where the Geocoder disagrees.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Greensboro Public Library

  • Prices: black-and-white prints and copies 15 cents a page (letter or legal); color 50 cents a page; scans to a computer 10 cents each; microfilm prints 15 cents [1].
  • Wireless printing: the library offers printing from your own laptop or device [8].
  • Fax: we didn't find a fax service on the library's price list or FAQ [1][8].
  • Register of Deeds copies: uncertified copies are 5 cents a page in person and $1 a page by mail; certified copies of recorded documents are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Greensboro page lists 10 locations, one of them marked "Coming Soon" (6316 Old Oak Ridge Rd), and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. Of the 9 open stores, the Census Geocoder places 8 in Greensboro city; it places 5820 N Church St outside any city or town [7]. Prices aren't listed; the page says products, services, pricing and hours may vary by location [9], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Greensboro

  • Library: we didn't find a notary service on Greensboro Public Library's price list or FAQ [1][8].
  • Register of Deeds: the Guilford County Register of Deeds gives the oath to new Guilford County notaries and authenticates Guilford County notary signatures [10]; it doesn't notarize documents for the public. The oath is $10 and an authentication is $1 per signature [2][10].
  • The UPS Store: its Greensboro page lists notarizing [9]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [11].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the Secretary of State commissions North Carolina notaries for a five-year term. A new applicant takes an approved classroom course of at least six hours and a written exam, pays a $50 nonrefundable application fee, and then takes the oath before the county Register of Deeds within 45 days [3]. The Register of Deeds fee for that oath is $10 [12].
  • Fees (statewide): at most $10 per notarized signature for an acknowledgment, jurat, verification or proof, and $10 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature. Remote notarization is at most $25 per signature. Travel can be charged only as actual mileage at the federal business rate, and only if you agree in writing before the trip. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, and doesn't have to charge at all [3].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide; two official pages differ): the statute lists $15 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment or jurat [3]. The Secretary of State's FAQ says $10.00 per signature for an electronic acknowledgment [13]. Ask the notary which fee applies before you start.
  • No certified copies (statewide): the Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy [13]. For a certified copy of a recorded or vital record, go to the office that holds it (below). The Secretary of State's site has a "Find a Notary Near You" search [14].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death and marriage certificates (Guilford County Register of Deeds)

  • Guilford County records: certified copies of birth, death and marriage certificates are $10 each; uncertified copies are 5 cents a page in person and $1 a page by mail [2]. The first certified copy of your own birth certificate is free if you're 62 or older [18].
  • Where: vital records are on the first floor of 201 W. Market St., Greensboro, phone 336-641-7730, office hours 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; the county also has a High Point office [18]. The Census Geocoder places 201 W. Market St. in Greensboro city [7]. In person, pay cash, check or money order [19]. Mail requests take check or money order and are processed within three working days [18].
  • Other North Carolina births: the office issues birth certificates for births elsewhere in North Carolina from 1971 on (not adoptees), 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., for $24 cash only, with photo ID [19]. The fee schedule says to contact the office for that fee [2].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • County first (statewide): NC Vital Records says birth, death and marriage certificates can also be ordered from the Register of Deeds in the county where the event happened, usually the same day [20]. State law sets the Register of Deeds fee at $10 for a certified copy of a birth or death certificate or marriage license [12].
  • State fees (statewide): $24 for a search of one three-year period, including one copy if the record is found. The search fee isn't refunded if no record is found. Extra copies of the same certificate are $15 each, expedited processing is $15, and VitalChek adds a $13.95 processing fee [21].
  • In person or by mail (statewide): in-person service is by appointment only, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–3 p.m. (last appointment 2:30 p.m.), at the Cooper Memorial Health Building, 225 N. McDowell St., Raleigh. Book at 919-733-3000, option 3; the $15 expedited fee applies to in-person service. Mail goes to North Carolina Vital Records, 1903 Mail Service Center, Raleigh, NC 27699-1900 [22].
  • Divorce (statewide): the state has divorce certificates from 1958 on [23]; the divorce decree itself is kept by the Clerk of Court in the county where the divorce was filed [20].

Marriage licenses (Guilford County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): unmarried people 18 or older may marry [24]. The Register of Deeds fee for a license is $60, and $10 for each certified copy [12]. The license form in the statute authorizes the marriage "at any time within 60 days" [24].
  • Guilford County fee and hours: the license is $60, by cash, check, debit or credit card; an optional keepsake certificate is $1 [2][25]. Licenses are issued Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; arrive before 4:30 p.m. The license is good right away and valid for 60 days [25].
  • Ceremonies: two witnesses are required for all ceremonies, and magistrates perform marriage ceremonies; the county says to contact the Guilford County Magistrates Office [25]. We didn't find a magistrate ceremony fee on the county's page.

Recorded documents (Guilford County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide): registers of deeds charge $5 for the first page of a certified copy of a recorded instrument and $2 for each additional page [12]. Uncertified copy prices are set by each office [12].
  • Guilford County fees: certified copies are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page; uncertified copies 5 cents a page in person and $1 by mail [2]. Real estate and recording are on the second floor of 201 W. Market St. [18].

Court record copies (Guilford County Clerk of Superior Court)

  • Court copy fees (statewide): the Clerk of Superior Court charges $2.00 for the first page of each document copied and $0.25 for each additional page, $3.00 for a certificate under seal and $10.00 for exemplification of records. These are state fees under G.S. 7A-308, shown in the NC Judicial Branch costs chart in effect Jan. 1, 2026 [26][27][28] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Where and when: Guilford County Courthouse, 201 South Eugene St., Greensboro, phone (336) 412-7300. The clerk's office is open 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. and closed 12:45–1:45 p.m. [29]. The Census Geocoder places it in Greensboro city [7].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the North Carolina Secretary of State's Authentication Office, for North Carolina documents used in foreign countries, such as birth and marriage certificates, statements of marital status, powers of attorney, diplomas and transcripts [30].
  • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per document; for adoptions, a duplicate original ordered at the same time is $5.00. Pay by check or money order to "North Carolina Secretary of State" [31][32].
  • How to send (statewide): mail a cover letter, payment, the original documents with all certifications attached and a prepaid return envelope or courier label [31] to North Carolina Secretary of State, Authentication Office, P.O. Box 29622, Raleigh, NC 27626-0622. The office is at 2 South Salisbury Street, Raleigh [30]. It no longer takes appointments; you can bring documents in without one [33].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [34]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • City Recycle Rodeo: the City of Greensboro holds two Recycle Rodeos a year, in spring and fall, open to all Greensboro and Guilford County residents. Shredding is for sensitive documents only (tax returns and bank statements yes, magazines and catalogs no), with a five-box limit per vehicle, first-come, first-served while space allows [35]. The next one is Saturday, April 10, 2027, 8 a.m.–noon, at Guilford College United Methodist Church, 1205 Fleming Rd. [35] (browser read, last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Past events: the fall Recycle Rodeo on Saturday, Sept. 19, 2026, 8 a.m.–noon, at Evangel Fellowship Church of God, 2207 E. Cone Blvd., is past [36]. North Carolina A&T State University's AGGIE Shred and Electronics Recycle Day on Oct. 24, 2026 is also past [37].
  • The UPS Store: its Greensboro page lists shredding [9]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Greensboro?

For a Guilford County birth, at the Register of Deeds, 201 W. Market St., for $10 [2][18]. Births elsewhere in North Carolina from 1971 on are $24, cash only [19].

How much does it cost to print at the Greensboro library?

15 cents a page for black-and-white and 50 cents for color [1].

How much is a marriage license in Guilford County?

$60, and it's valid for 60 days [2][25].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the Guilford County Register of Deeds?

No. It's walk-in, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–2 p.m. [15].

Can a North Carolina notary certify a copy?

No. The Secretary of State says a North Carolina notary can't certify a document as a true copy (statewide) [13].
