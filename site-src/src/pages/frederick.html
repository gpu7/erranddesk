Frederick, Maryland document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Frederick, Maryland, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: a Frederick County marriage license is $75 [1]. Library prints and copies are 20 cents a page and outgoing faxes $1 a page [2]. The C. Burr Artz Library downtown takes passport applications with no appointment [3].
Who does what: Frederick is in Frederick County [4][5]. The Clerk of the Circuit Court for Frederick County, 100 W. Patrick Street, handles marriage licenses, land records and court records [6][1].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Frederick County Public Libraries: black-and-white printing and copying are 20 cents a page, full color 50 cents a page. Outgoing faxes are $1 a page, and scanning to email is free. Incoming faxes aren't accepted [2].
  • The UPS Store: its Frederick page lists 3 locations offering printing, faxing, notarizing and shredding [7]. The Census Geocoder places 1 of them, 7820B Wormans Mill Rd, in Frederick city; the two 21704 stores are outside the city [8]. Prices aren't listed online.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Frederick

  • Frederick County Public Libraries (outside city limits): notaries are at the Brunswick, Thurmont and Walkersville branches, not in Frederick itself. It's free with an FCPL card, or $2 per signature without one. Call ahead to make sure a notary is available [9].
  • The UPS Store: its Frederick page lists notarizing [7].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maryland's notary fees are set by state regulation (COMAR 01.02.08.02, effective Jan 22, 2024): at most $8 per notarial act, or $30 per remote notarial act. For extra copies of the same record signed at the same time, $4 per signature on each extra copy. Photocopies are at most $2 each, certifying a journal copy is $6, and travel is IRS mileage plus up to $5 [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and land record copies

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): the Maryland Department of Health's Division of Vital Records charges $10 for a birth certificate and $10 for a death certificate, $12 for each additional death certificate in the same transaction and $12 for a marriage certificate. Internet orders add $13, and expedited shipping (internet only) adds $20. Local health departments' fees vary [14].
  • How (statewide): the Division's lobby serves customers by appointment only; you can also order by mail to P.O. Box 68760, Baltimore, MD 21215-0036, or online through VitalChek [15][16].

Marriage licenses (Circuit Court for Frederick County)

  • Fee: $75.00 in Frederick County (fees vary by county), by cash, Visa, MasterCard or Discover; card payments may carry a third-party service fee. A civil ceremony by the clerk is $25.00 [1].
  • Copies: certified marriage record copies are $5.50 each; plain copies are $0.50. Mail requests go to the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Frederick County, Attn: Records Department, 100 West Patrick Street [1].
  • Contact: 301-600-1965 for marriage licenses; the clerk's office is open 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., 301-600-1976 [1].

Deeds, land records, court records and divorce decrees

  • Copy fees (statewide): the Maryland Judiciary's circuit court fee schedule, effective Sept 2, 2026, sets copies at $0.50 a page, plus $5 for each certification or $10 to exemplify [17]. Plat copies are 50 cents a page for 11 x 17 inches or smaller and $1 a page for larger sheets [17]. Last checked Nov 4, 2026.
  • Where: Clerk of the Circuit Court, 100 W. Patrick Street, 301-600-1976, open 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [6].
  • Recording fees (Frederick County clerk): the clerk's recording-fees page lists $20 for most instruments of nine pages or fewer and $5 a page for plats, plus a $40 surcharge on every instrument recorded in the land records, with some exceptions [18]. That page is about recording, not copies, and isn't dated. Last checked Nov 4, 2026.

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Maryland Secretary of State, 16 Francis Street, Wineland Building, Annapolis, 410-974-5521. Certifications and apostilles are $5 each, by mail or in person [19].
  • Walk-ins, the page conflicts: the same page says "WALK-INS NOT AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME" (scheduled appointments may change because of building construction) and also that the desk is open for walk-ins until 1 p.m., 9 a.m.–1 p.m. weekdays, with a limit of 10 documents per customer a day [19]. Call before you go.
  • Notarized documents (statewide): take them first to the Clerk of the Circuit Court in the county where the notary was commissioned [19].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Frederick page lists shredding [7].
  • Free events (past): the City of Frederick's Fall Freecycle Roundup on Saturday, Oct 17, 2026, 8 a.m.–2 p.m., at the Public Works Complex, 111 Airport Drive East, included free document shredding for City of Frederick residents only, with proof of residency [21]. That date has passed. Frederick County's recycling events page has a document shredding section [22], but we didn't find an upcoming dated event (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Questions

How much is a marriage license in Frederick County?

$75.00, by cash or card [1].

Do I need an appointment for a passport at the library?

No. The C. Burr Artz and Urbana libraries are first come, first served [3].

Can the library notarize for free?

Yes with an FCPL card, at the Brunswick, Thurmont and Walkersville branches; otherwise $2 per signature [9].

How much does printing cost at the Frederick library?

Frederick County Public Libraries charge 20 cents a page for black-and-white printing and copying and 50 cents a page for full color. Outgoing faxes are $1 a page, scanning to email is free, and incoming faxes aren't accepted [2].
