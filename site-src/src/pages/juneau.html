Juneau, Alaska document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Juneau, Alaska, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City and Borough of Juneau, Alaska Department of Health, Alaska DNR Recorder's Office, the Lieutenant Governor, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Boroughs and recording in Alaska (statewide): Alaska uses boroughs and census areas rather than counties. Vital records are issued by the state Health Analytics and Vital Records Section (HAVRS), not by city clerks. Land records are kept by the Department of Natural Resources Recorder's Office under a statewide fee schedule [1][2][3].
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Juneau [4]. HAVRS Juneau issues vital copies ($30/$25) and marriage licenses ($60) [1]. Recording for Juneau is at the Anchorage Recorder's Office [5]. Library print: 25¢ B&W / 50¢ color [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Juneau Public Libraries: self-serve printing and copying are 25 cents per page black-and-white and 50 cents per page color [6].
  • FedEx: Juneau listings include OfficeMax and a FedEx Ship Center. We didn't find published per-page FedEx print prices on the city listing (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [7].
  • The UPS Store: 2490 Industrial Blvd advertises printing, faxing, shredding and notary [8].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Juneau, Alaska

  • Municipal Clerk: the CBJ Clerk how-do-I pages point notary and vital records to the State of Alaska; we didn't find a published CBJ public notary customer fee (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [9].
  • The UPS Store: 2490 Industrial Blvd advertises notary; fees vary [8][10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Alaska charges $40 for a regular notary public commission that lasts four years and requires a $2,500 bond [11]. The Lieutenant Governor's FAQ says there is no statutory maximum fee for notarial acts; a notary who charges must provide a published fee schedule before the act (AS 44.50.062(4)). Limited Governmental notaries may not charge [12].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Health Analytics and Vital Records (statewide): certified birth, death, and marriage certificates are $30 for the first copy and $25 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. Order in person at Anchorage or Juneau offices, by mail/fax, or through VitalChek (vendor fees may apply) [1].
    • Anchorage HAVRS office: 3901 Old Seward Highway, Suite 101, Anchorage, AK 99503. Phone 907-269-0991 [1].
    • Juneau HAVRS office: PO Box 110675, Juneau, AK 99811. Phone 907-465-3391 [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $5 per certificate [16].

    Marriage licenses

    • Where in Juneau: apply at the HAVRS Juneau office — Juneau courts do not issue marriage licenses [9][1].
    • Fee: $60 [1].
    • State rules (statewide): marriage licenses are $60 (re-issue $15) through Alaska Vital Records [1]. You may apply at HAVRS offices in Anchorage or Juneau, or at any Alaska Court — except the Anchorage and Juneau courts do not issue marriage licenses (use HAVRS there) [9][1].

    Land records (DNR — Anchorage office serves Juneau)

    • Office: Anchorage Recorder's Office, 550 W 7th Ave., Suite 108, Anchorage. Phone 907-269-8876. Juneau recording district is served here [5][17].
    • Recording fees (statewide): DNR Recorder's Office charges $20 for the first page and $5 for each additional page of the same instrument, under AS 40.17.030 and 11 AAC 05.200. Extra names/claims over six are $2 each; non-standard documents $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [16].
    • Fee (statewide): $5 per certificate (apostille or authentication) [16].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Questions

    Where can I get a notary in Juneau, Alaska?

    The UPS Store at 2490 Industrial Blvd advertises notary [8]. CBJ Clerk pages point the public to state notary resources [9].

    How much is a birth certificate in Alaska?

    $30 first / $25 additional from HAVRS [1].

    Where are Juneau passport offices?

    Federal Building Station (709 W. 9th St.) and Mendenhall Station (9491 Vintage Blvd.) appear in the locator [4].

    Where do I record a deed for Juneau property?

    Anchorage Recorder's Office (serves the Juneau district), 550 W 7th Ave., Suite 108. $20 first page + $5 additional [5][2].
