Waterbury, Connecticut document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Waterbury, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Waterbury, Silas Bronson Library, the State of Connecticut, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Waterbury, both post offices [1]. Silas Bronson Library prints at 15¢ a page [2]. Birth certificates are $20 at Vital Statistics, and cash is accepted [3]. A marriage license is $50, with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [4].
No county clerk in Connecticut (statewide): Connecticut abolished county government effective Oct. 1, 1960, and counties remain only as geographic areas [5][6]. Town clerks keep land records, and each town's registrar of vital statistics issues birth, death and marriage records.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Silas Bronson Library: printing and copying cost 15¢ a page black and white and 50¢ color, from a designated computer. Add money to your library card at the circulation desk first [2].
  • Library fax: we didn't find a public fax service on the library's pages. The fax number it lists, (203) 574-8055, is for its administration office [7].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Waterbury page lists 1 location, a FedEx counter at Walgreens, 649 W. Main St. [8]. We didn't find a FedEx Office store in the city.
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find one inside Waterbury. The UPS Store's Connecticut list doesn't include Waterbury; the nearest town on it is Prospect [9].

Notary in Waterbury

  • Silas Bronson Library: we didn't find a notary service on the library's pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Town Clerk: the clerk's notary page says the office has offered notary services since March 1, 2016, 8:30 a.m.–4:15 p.m. Monday–Friday, and lists "a fee of $5.00" for each signature authenticated [27]. That page dates from 2016, and the statewide cap rose to $10 on July 1, 2026 (below), so call (203) 574-6806 to confirm the current fee [10].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): since July 1, 2026, a Connecticut notary may charge up to $10.00 per notarial act, plus travel at no more than the IRS business mileage rate. Public Act 26-81 raised the fee from $5.00 [12][13].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): the General Assembly's compiled statute page for §3-95, revised to January 1, 2026, still shows the old cap of $5.00 plus 35¢ a mile [14]. The Secretary of the State's pages give the $10.00 fee in effect since July 1, 2026 [12][13]. We show the newer figure.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Waterbury Vital Statistics (Town Clerk)

  • Where: City Hall, 235 Grand St., 1st floor. Phone (203) 574-6800. Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [3].
  • Birth certificates: $20 long form, $15 short form. Pay with cash, a money order or a cashier's check; no personal checks. A copy of an adopted person's original birth certificate is $65 [3].
  • Marriage and death certificates: $20 a copy. Online orders through VitalChek cost extra for processing and shipping [4].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Where (statewide): from the vital records office of the town where the event happened, or from the State Vital Records Office. Town requests are generally faster [18].
  • Fees (statewide): town-issued birth, death, marriage and civil union certificates are $20 per copy. State-issued birth certificates are $30 per copy [18][19].
  • Paying a town (statewide): by mail, towns take a money order payable to the town; personal checks aren't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds fees [18].

Marriage licenses (Vital Statistics)

  • Fee and wait: $50, paid by cash, money order or card when you apply. The ceremony must be in Waterbury. Since Oct. 1, 2026, there's a 48-hour wait after signing, with no exceptions. The license is valid 65 days from pickup [4].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at the vital records office of the town where the wedding will be. The fee is $50.00. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the town can't issue the license until 48 hours after the second person signs the application. The license is good for 65 days [20][21].

Land records (Town Clerk)

  • City fee list: recording is $70 for the first page and $5 for each extra page. Copies are $1 a page, maps $3 and certification $2. A handheld scanner is $20 a day [22]. These match the statewide fees below.
  • Copy fees (statewide law): CGS §7-34a sets town clerk copies at $1 per page and $2 to certify a copy. Recording is $10 for the first page and $5 for each extra page, plus $10 and $50 per-document state surcharges [23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Connecticut Secretary of the State. All orders must be placed online at sots.service.ct.gov/apostille; paper order forms are no longer accepted [24].
  • Fee (statewide): $40 per document, or $15 for documents for a child adoption. Expedited service is $50 more per document [25][14]. These figures come from the SOTS order form and the statute; the online-ordering page we read doesn't list fees.
  • Delivery (statewide): paper documents go by courier to 165 Capitol Avenue, Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06106, or by mail to P.O. Box 150470, Hartford, CT 06115-0470. E-apostilles began July 6, 2026, but vital records still need a paper apostille. E-apostille and expedited orders are processed within 24 hours [24].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.
