Lakewood, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Lakewood, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Lakewood is in Jefferson County ("Jeffco"), so several of these offices are county offices. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Which Jeffco office does what: Jefferson County Public Library handles printing, scanning and fax. The Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder handles passports, recorded documents and motor vehicles. Jefferson County Public Health (Vital Records) issues birth and death certificates. Lakewood, Colorado isn't Lakewood, California, Ohio, New Jersey or Washington, so check the state before you use a search result.
Short version: Printing, copying, scanning and outgoing fax are free at every full-service Jefferson County Public Library location, including Lakewood and Belmar libraries [1][2][3]. The Lakewood post office accepts passport applications by appointment [4]. Jefferson County Public Health charges $25 for a first birth certificate [10]. The Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder says it can't notarize documents [9].

Notary in Lakewood

  • Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder: its FAQ says, "No, we are unable to notarize documents at this time" [9].
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 12081 W Alameda Pkwy, Lakewood, CO 80228 (Green Mountain Shopping Center) says it has a commissioned notary public on staff. Phone (303) 986-2535. Its page doesn't post a price [14]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [12].
  • Jefferson County Public Library: we didn't find a notary service on the library's Office Center page as of Oct 28, 2026 [1].

  • Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder: passports are by appointment only. Slots "typically book very quickly" and usually open up to 30 days ahead. You can't make an appointment to renew a passport that expired less than five years ago; the Clerk points you to the State Department's renew-by-mail option instead [5].
  • Clerk fees: $35 per application, payable by check, money order or credit/debit card. The State Department fee must be paid by check or money order [6]. Each application needs its own check or money order to the State Department. Photos are $15 during your appointment [7]. The Clerk suggests applying in its slower season, October through December [6].
  • Post Office: the locator says to schedule at www.usps.com/scheduler [4]. USPS fees are national: a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [15].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Jefferson County Public Health, Vital Records

  • Where: 800 Jefferson County Parkway, Suite 1300, Golden, CO 80401. Phone 303-271-6450, fax 303-271-6451. Open Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–2 p.m., excluding holidays. You can get certificates by mail or wait for them at the office. A valid driver's license or other approved ID is required [10].
  • Which births: birth certificates for any birth in Colorado from 1903 to the present [10].
  • Fees (effective Jan 1, 2026): $25 for the first birth or death certificate, and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time. The office says these fees are set at the state level. From March 1, 2026, card payments carry a non-refundable 2.25% service fee. Cash, check and money order have no fee [10].
  • Death certificates from 1985 to 1995 take extra time to process [10].

Recorded documents: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder

  • Recording: $43 per letter or legal-size document. Death record documents are free. Mailed documents come back in 2 to 4 weeks [8].
  • Copies: digital copies are free. Paper requests under 20 pages are free; 20 pages or more cost $5.00 plus $0.25 per additional page. Certification is $1.00 per document, and certified copies come on paper only. Certified marriage license copies are $1.25 [8].
  • Recording office: 100 Jefferson County Parkway, Golden, CO 80419, 303-271-8168. Open Monday to Thursday, 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. [8].

Motor vehicle: Clerk and Recorder's Lakewood office

  • 2099 Wadsworth Boulevard, Suite K, Lakewood, CO 80214. Call center 303-271-8100. Open Monday–Thursday 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Friday. Appointments are required [11].
  • Most motor vehicle work can be done there. The exceptions are driver's licenses, which the state's offices issue, and manufactured-home title transfers, which are done in Golden [11].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

Post offices, including the one in Lakewood, sell USPS money orders. Fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. The Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder takes money orders for passport fees [6]. Questions

Is printing free at the library in Lakewood?

Yes. Jefferson County Public Library says printing and copying are free at all full-service locations. Lakewood and Belmar libraries are both full-service locations [1][2][3].

Can I send a fax at the library in Lakewood?

Yes. Faxing is free to local or long-distance numbers, with no page limit. Faxes can't be received [1].

Where can I apply for a passport in Lakewood?

The State Department's locator lists the Lakewood Post Office at 10799 W Alameda Ave. The Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder also takes applications at 100 Jefferson County Pkwy. in Golden. Both are by appointment [4][5].

How much is a birth certificate in Jefferson County?

$25 for the first copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time, from Jefferson County Public Health Vital Records. Card payments add 2.25% [10].
