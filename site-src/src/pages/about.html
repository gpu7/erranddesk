About this site and how we check prices

Last updated November 26, 2026

Errand Desk is an independent US site. Plain, sourced answers for everyday US paperwork errands: where to go, what it costs, and what to ask before you drive over. We publish topic guides and city guides. We are not a government office and are not affiliated with the agencies or businesses we list. For questions or corrections, email erranddesk@protonmail.com or use the contact page.

How we check prices

  • We take every price and service detail from the provider's own official page. Where that isn't possible, we use a clearly cited, reputable source and label the figure as reported, not official.
  • Every figure links to its source and carries the date we last checked it.
  • If a provider doesn't publish a national price, we say exactly that. We don't estimate or average prices.
  • Many locations are franchises or set their own prices, so we always recommend calling the location before you go.
  • We don't publish reviews, star ratings or testimonials we haven't verified.

Corrections

Prices change. If you find something out of date, email erranddesk@protonmail.com or see the contact page. Please include the city or guide URL and a link to the official source.
