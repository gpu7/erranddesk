About this site and how we check prices

How we check prices

  • We take every price and service detail from the provider's own official page. Where that isn't possible, we use a clearly cited, reputable source and label the figure as reported, not official.
  • Every figure links to its source and carries the date we last checked it.
  • If a provider doesn't publish a national price, we say exactly that. We don't estimate or average prices.
  • Many locations are franchises or set their own prices, so we always recommend calling the location before you go.
  • We don't publish reviews, star ratings or testimonials we haven't verified.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. Each one is marked "(paid link)" where it appears. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change the facts, prices or order of options we report. Read the full disclosure.

Corrections

Prices change. If you find something out of date, a corrections contact will be added here before the site goes public.
