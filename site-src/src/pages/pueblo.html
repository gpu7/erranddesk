Which offices serve Pueblo: Pueblo is in Pueblo County. The Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder and the Pueblo Department of Public Health and Environment's Vital Records Office both have offices in Pueblo [6][9]. Library service comes from the Pueblo City-County Library District (PCCLD), whose main library is Rawlings Library at 100 E Abriendo Ave. [2]. Pueblo West is a separate community with its own addresses (Pueblo West, CO 81007); we mark Pueblo West locations where they appear. Search results can also mix in other places with Pueblo in the name, so check that a result says Pueblo, Colorado.
Short version: PCCLD charges 10¢ a page for black and white printing and 10¢ a page for faxing [1][3]. The State Department's locator lists two Pueblo post offices for passport applications, both by appointment [5]. Pueblo Vital Records issues birth and death certificates for events anywhere in Colorado, with same-day service until 4:15 p.m. [9].

Printing, copying and fax: Pueblo City-County Library District

ServiceWhat Pueblo City-County Library District says
PrintingThe wireless printing page says printouts are 10¢ for black and white and 60¢ for color [1]. The district's posted fee schedule lists computer printing at $0.10 per page black and white and $1.00 per page for color, if available [3]. The two color prices differ, so ask at the desk.
How to payMost locations only accept cash. Greenhorn Valley, Rawlings, Barkman and Pueblo West also take credit cards [1].
Wireless printingSend a job from a laptop, tablet or phone through the Smart Alec service, logging in with your library card and PIN, or email an attachment from the email on your library account. Pick it up at the Print Release Station at any PCCLD library [1].
Photocopies$0.10 per page black and white; $1.00 per page color, if available. Copies from microfilm readers are $0.10 per page [3].
Fax$0.10 per page [3]. The fee schedule doesn't say which locations fax or whether incoming faxes are accepted.
FreeNo fee for a library card, a replacement card, or use of the internet computers and software [3].

Notary in Pueblo

  • Pueblo City-County Library District: the district lists a "Notary Service" in its Library Services menu, linking to an appointment page [1][4]. That page needs JavaScript, and we couldn't read its locations, hours or any price as of Oct 28, 2026 [4]. The district's fee schedule doesn't list a notary fee [3].
  • Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder: the recording fee schedule lists "$2.00 per acknowledgement - Taking Acknowledgments" and $1.00 per document for certifying and sealing documents [7]. The page doesn't say more about who can use the acknowledgment service.
  • City of Pueblo: the City Clerk's page lists liquor, marijuana and tobacco licensing, municipal elections, records management and Clerk of City Council. It doesn't list a notary service as of Oct 28, 2026 [11].
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [12].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 140 W 29th St (Pueblo Shopping Center, next to King Sooper) says it has a commissioned notary public available. It lists store hours as "Open 7 Days." Phone (719) 543-5800. Its page doesn't post a price [14]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [12].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Pueblo Vital Records Office

  • Where: Pueblo Department of Public Health and Environment, Vital Records Office, 101 W. 9th Street, 1st Floor, Pueblo, CO 81003. Phone (719) 583-4555 [9].
  • Hours: Monday through Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., except holidays, closed noon–1 p.m. Same-day service is available until 4:15 p.m., and walk-ins are welcome [9].
  • Which records: birth and death certificates for events that occur anywhere in Colorado. The office has birth, death, fetal death and ledger records with genealogy information from 1897 to the present [9].
  • Fees (statewide): the page says the statewide fee is $25 for the first copy and $20 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [9].
  • Payment: Visa, Mastercard, cash, checks and money orders [9].
  • What to bring: appropriate identification and proof of relationship [9].
  • Other ways to order: bring, email or mail a completed form to avoid extra fees. Online through VitalChek, for an added fee. The page says that as of June 2026, VitalChek is the only Colorado Vital Records approved third-party company for Pueblo County [9].
  • By bus: Pueblo Transit Route #6 (Orange Route) stops near 7th Street and Main Street, within walking distance [9].

Recorded documents: Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder

  • Recording: a flat $43.00 per document since July 1, 2025, with no per-page fees. The page says the change comes from Colorado HB 24-1269 (a statewide law). Recording death certificates and documents related to a death in Pueblo County is no charge [7].
  • Copies: $0.25 per page for letter or legal size records in the Clerk's custody; $5.00 per page for plats, tabloid or bigger; $3.00 flat per electronic document. The office no longer copies documents not in its custody [7].
  • Certification: $1.00 per document certified and sealed [7].
  • Marriage records: a marriage license is $30.00, cash or card only, no checks. A certified copy of a marriage license is $1.25 [7].
  • Military discharges: recording and copies are no charge [7].
  • Office: Recording and Clerk to the Board, 215 W. 10th St., 2nd Floor, Pueblo, CO 81003, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Clerk and Recorder phone 719-583-6507 [6].

Motor vehicle: Pueblo County Clerk and Recorder

  • Historic Courthouse: 215 W. 10th St., Pueblo, CO 81003, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [6].
  • Drive Thru: 1228 Routt Ave., Pueblo, CO 81004, Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–5:30 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–noon [6].
  • Pueblo West (not Pueblo): 267 S. Joe Martinez Blvd., Pueblo West, CO 81007, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [6].
  • You can join the line as early as 7:45 a.m. on the day of service; joining the line is for the Historic Courthouse and Pueblo West locations only. Driver license services aren't available at county Motor Vehicle offices; the county points to COmyDMV or 303-205-5600 [8].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. Pueblo Vital Records accepts money orders [9]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does printing cost at the library in Pueblo?

Black and white is 10¢ a page. The wireless printing page lists color at 60¢, while the district's fee schedule lists $1.00 a page for color [1][3].

Can I fax from the library in Pueblo?

The district's fee schedule lists faxing at $0.10 per page [3]. It doesn't say which locations fax, so call (719) 562-5600 first [2].

Where can I apply for a passport in Pueblo?

The State Department's locator lists Pueblo Sunset Station at 3000 Wedgewood Drive and Belmont Station at 1025 E US Hwy 50, both by appointment [5].

Can I get a birth certificate the same day in Pueblo?

Yes. The Pueblo Vital Records Office at 101 W. 9th Street offers same-day service until 4:15 p.m. on weekdays, and walk-ins are welcome [9].
