Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Clarksville-Montgomery County Public Library

  • Printing: 15¢ a page black and white, 25¢ a page color, from the public computers; mobile printing is also available. The library says prices may vary [1].
  • Copies: copiers on every floor, 15¢ a page [1]. We didn't find a scanning price.
  • Fax: self-service fax at the Main Library and North Branch: $1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each page after, credit or debit cards only. The library can't receive faxes [1].
  • Computers: free with a library card in good standing; without a card, ask for a Guest Pass with a valid photo ID [1].
  • Locations: Main Library, 350 Pageant Ln., Suite 501, and North Branch, 435 Jordan Rd.; 931-648-8826. Library hours: Sunday 1 to 5 p.m., Monday–Thursday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday–Saturday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. [1]. The Census Geocoder places both in Clarksville [7].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 4 locations with Clarksville addresses. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [8]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [9]. The Census Geocoder places 3 of the 4 inside Clarksville; it couldn't match 1556 Hankook Rd. [8][7].

Notary in Clarksville

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the library's pages.
  • The UPS Store: its Clarksville page lists notarizing [8].
  • Becoming a notary in Montgomery County: the County Commission elects notaries to four-year terms. Your notarized application and $10,000 bond must reach the County Clerk together by the last business day of the month. The fee is $44.70 for a new notary, or a renewing notary with a raised seal or black ink stamp; it covers the commission certificate and a new ink stamp. Renewing notaries who already have a non-black ink stamp pay $19.00. After the 10-day waiting period from the Commission's approval, you sign the record book in person and get your seal [10]. The Clerk publishes the bond dates for each month; for example, applications submitted in November 2026 are for a commission of December 28, 2026 to December 28, 2030 [11].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): in Tennessee you apply through your County Clerk, are elected by the county commission, buy a surety bond, and then pick up the commission the Secretary of State sends back to the County Clerk [12]. A term is four years [13].
  • Fees (statewide law): the Secretary of State quotes Tenn. Code Ann. § 8-21-1201: notaries "are entitled to demand and receive reasonable fees and compensation" [14]. We didn't find a fixed maximum on that page, so ask for the price before the notary starts.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death and marriage certificates (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): the Tennessee Department of Health (TDH) Office of Vital Records charges $15.00 for a birth, death, marriage or divorce certificate, and $15.00 for each additional copy. If no record is found, you get a no-record letter for the same fee [3].
  • Any county health department (statewide): TDH says all county health departments can issue any birth or death certificate registered statewide, so you don't have to go to the county where the birth or death happened. Bring ID [19][20].
  • State office in person (statewide): Tennessee Vital Records, 1st Floor, Andrew Johnson Tower, 710 James Robertson Parkway, Nashville; the entrance is on the Rosa Parks Blvd. side. Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., no appointment needed. It takes cash, check, credit or debit card, or money order [19]. TDH lists free parking on Rev. Kelly M Smith Way off Rosa Parks Blvd. [20].
  • By mail (statewide): send the signed application, a photocopy of a government-issued ID with your signature (or a notarized application), and a check or money order payable to Tennessee Vital Records, to the same address [19].
  • Online (statewide): TDH doesn't take online orders itself; VitalChek is its only authorized online vendor, and charges extra fees [19].
  • Montgomery County Health Department: 330 Pageant Ln., 931-648-5747 [4]. The Census Geocoder places it in Clarksville [7].
  • Birth and death certificates: certified copies of Tennessee birth certificates and death certificates, $15.00 each. It also handles paternity acknowledgments. Hours are 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., closed for lunch 12:30 to 1:30 p.m.; call (931) 648-7257 [4]. We didn't find the forms of payment on its page.

Marriage licenses

  • License taxes and fees (statewide): the Tennessee Department of Revenue lists a $15 and a $5 state privilege tax, a local option tax of up to $5, and a $62.50 additional state fee. $60 of that fee is waived if you complete an approved premarital course, or if you are out-of-state residents [21]. County clerks' totals also include their own fees, so check the county's price below.
  • Marriage certificate copies (statewide): $15.00 from TDH Vital Records [3].
  • Montgomery County Clerk: $107.50, or $47.50 if you complete a premarital preparation course of at least four hours with a qualified instructor. The instructor must complete and sign the Certificate of Completion before a notary, and you turn it in when you apply [5]. The $60 difference matches the state's course waiver (statewide) [21].
  • Out-of-state couples (two sources differ): the Department of Revenue says the $60 is also waived for out-of-state residents (statewide) [21]. The Montgomery County Clerk's page mentions only the course [5]. If you live outside Tennessee, ask the Clerk's office before you go.
  • What to bring and how it works: both of you must be 18 or older and appear in person to sign the record book. Bring a driver's license or government photo ID with your date of birth. There's no waiting period; the license is issued the same day and must be used within 30 days. You can apply online first. The Clerk takes cash, MasterCard, Discover, Visa and American Express; card payments carry a 2.25% fee plus 25¢ [5].
  • Where: County Clerk, 350 Pageant Ln., Suite 502, 931-648-5711, Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [16]. The office is closed for holidays, including November 11 and November 26–27, 2026 [22]. The Census Geocoder places it in Clarksville [7].
  • Copies of a Montgomery County marriage certificate: the Clerk's page links to an online request form [5]. We didn't find the copy price.

Recorded documents (deeds and other land records)

  • Montgomery County Register of Deeds: 350 Pageant Ln., Suite 101-A, 931-648-5713, Monday–Friday 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. It takes cash, checks, money orders, and debit or credit cards [23]. The Census Geocoder places it in Clarksville [7].
  • Recording fees: a deed is $12.00 for the first two pages and $5.00 for each additional page, plus any state tax [23].
  • Copies: 15¢ a page; certified copies $1.00 a page; plat copies $1.00 a page; mailed copies 50¢ a page [23]. The office can email copies, or mail them if you send a self-addressed stamped envelope and a check or money order. Most records are online only through a paid subscription [24]. The office has public computers but can't do title searches [25].
  • Who can get copies (two pages differ): the FAQ says documents are available to Tennessee residents with proof of Tennessee residency and a Record Request Form [25]. The Requesting Documents page says anyone may walk in during business hours and get copies [24]. If you live outside Tennessee, call 931-648-5713 first.

Court records

  • Circuit Court Clerk: also the clerk of General Sessions and Juvenile Courts, with civil, criminal and traffic divisions [26]. 2 Millennium Plaza, Suite 115, 931-648-5700, Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [26].
  • Circuit Civil copies: 50¢ a page; certification and seal $5.00. Fax filing is $5.00 for the first page and $1.00 for each additional page, up to 50 pages, only for filings that don't carry a filing fee (fee sheet revised October 1, 2026) [27].
  • General Sessions Civil copies: 50¢ a page; certification and seal $5.00 (effective October 1, 2026) [28].
  • Criminal and traffic: we didn't find copy prices. The Clerk offers free online case lookups for criminal and traffic cases from November 1, 1999 and civil cases from May 1, 2006 [29].
  • Chancery Court (Clerk and Master): physical copies 50¢ a page; certification and seal $5.00; computer searches $7.00; downloaded e-filing copies $1.25 (fees effective October 1, 2026) [30]. 2 Millennium Plaza, Suite 101, 931-648-5703, Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [31].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $2.00 for each apostille or authentication from the Tennessee Secretary of State [32].
  • Steps (statewide): get the document notarized by a Tennessee notary, then have the County Clerk of the county where that notary was commissioned certify the notary's signature, then send form SS-4504 [33]. Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates signed by the Tennessee State Registrar don't need to be notarized first [32].
  • Where (statewide): by mail to the Office of Authentications, Tennessee Secretary of State, Business Services Division, ATTN: ATS Section, 312 Rosa L. Parks Ave., 6th Floor, Nashville, TN 37243. For FedEx or UPS return, enclose a prepaid label with your name as sender and recipient, or documents come back by regular mail. In person at the 3rd floor customer service desk, Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4:15 p.m. Central; arrive by 2 p.m. for same-day service [33]. Phone (615) 741-0536 [32].
  • Montgomery County notary certification: we didn't find a Montgomery County Clerk page about certifying a notary's signature for an apostille, or its fee. Call 931-648-5711 [16].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [34]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I get a passport in Clarksville?

At the Montgomery County Clerk, weekdays 8 to 10 a.m. with no appointment [2], or at the Clarksville post office by appointment [15].

How much is a marriage license in Clarksville?

$107.50 at the Montgomery County Clerk, or $47.50 after a premarital preparation course [5].

How much is a birth certificate in Clarksville?

$15, from the Montgomery County Health Department or Tennessee Vital Records [4][3].

How much does the library charge to print?

15¢ a page black and white and 25¢ color; prices may vary [1].

How much can a Tennessee notary charge?

Tennessee law allows "reasonable fees" (statewide); we didn't find a fixed maximum [14].
