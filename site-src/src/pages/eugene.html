Eugene, Oregon document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Which offices serve Eugene: Lane County Deeds & Records issues marriage licenses and records documents [1][2]. Lane County Public Health issues birth and death certificates for events within the last 5 months and 28 days [3]; older records come from the Oregon Health Authority [4]. Apostilles come from the Oregon Secretary of State in Salem [5].
Short version: Eugene Public Library prints and copies are 10 cents per side in black and white and 50 cents in color [6]. The UO Ticket Office takes passport applications walk-up only, no appointments [7]. Oregon notaries may charge at most $10 per notarial act (statewide) [8]. A Lane County marriage license is $60 [1].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Eugene Public Library

  • Printing and copying: 10 cents per side for black and white and 50 cents per side for color. You can print from library computers or from your own device by email or upload [6].
  • Computers and wifi: internet computers are limited to one hour per person per day; wifi is free. Phone 541-682-5450 [6].
  • Fax and scanning: we didn't find these on the library page we checked.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 5 locations in Eugene: 1574 Coburg Rd, (541) 686-0233; 4325 Commerce St., Ste. 111, (541) 342-1773; 65 Division Ave W-1, (541) 461-0710; 2852 Willamette St, (541) 687-2836; and 1056 Green Acres Rd, (458) 210-3070. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. We didn't find prices on the store pages.

Notary in Eugene

  • Library events: the library's calendar listed a free notary session by appointment with King Mobile Notary Service on Oct. 6, 2026, 10:30 a.m.–noon at the Downtown Library, 100 W. 10th Ave. Registration was in advance only (no walk-ins), with a limit of two documents per person and no wills; bring government-issued ID and any witnesses you need [10]. That date has passed, so check eugene.libcal.com/calendar/notary for new sessions.
  • The UPS Store: its Eugene page lists notarizing [9]. We didn't find store notary prices; Oregon's legal maximum is below.
  • Fees (statewide): Oregon law says a notary's fee "may not exceed $10 per notarial act," or $25 for a notarial act performed remotely under ORS 194.277 [8].
  • Extras (statewide): a notary may add a travel fee only if they explain beforehand that it's on top of the notarial fee and not set by law, and you agree to it in advance. A notary who charges fees must display a list of them in English [8]. Oregon's $10 cap is lower than Washington's $15 and twice Idaho's $5.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Oregon Health Authority (statewide)

  • Who: the Oregon Health Authority's Center for Health Statistics (OHA Vital Records) handles Oregon birth, death, marriage and divorce records [4][14]. County offices issue birth and death certificates only for recent events in their own county; the local office for this city is below.
  • Online (VitalChek): $47.50 for the first certificate ($25 search fee and certificate, $7 expedite fee and $15.50 VitalChek fee) and $25 for each additional copy. UPS Next Day Air is $20 more [14]. Online and phone orders are typically processed in 3 workdays [4].
  • Phone (VitalChek, 1-888-896-4988): $49.20 for the first certificate, including a $17.20 VitalChek fee [14].
  • Mail or drop box: $25 for the first certificate and $25 for each additional copy, by check or money order to OHA/Vital Records, PO Box 14050, Portland, OR 97293; don't send cash [14][4]. Mail orders take about 3–5 weeks and drop box orders about 8–10 weeks [4].
  • In person (by appointment): $28 for the first certificate ($25 plus a $3 identity authentication fee), paid by card, cash or money order [14]. Appointments are 30 minutes, 9 a.m.–4 p.m. (last at 3:30), at the Portland State Office Building, 800 NE Oregon St., Portland, OR 97232, where orders are entered at a kiosk [4].
  • Search fee: when the date of a death, marriage or divorce is unknown, $25 covers a five-year search, plus $1 for each extra year [14].

Birth and death certificates in person: Lane County Public Health

  • Which records: Lane County events within the last 5 months and 28 days only; older records come from OHA (statewide, above) [3].
  • Where: 151 W. 7th Ave., Suite 100-1A, Eugene. Walk in Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Wednesday 10 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Phone (541) 682-4045 [3].
  • Price: $25 per certificate. You can also order by mail or through the drop box outside the office, which accepts cash [3].

Marriage licenses: Lane County Deeds & Records

  • Where: 125 East 8th Ave., Eugene, OR 97401. Marriage license hours are Monday–Friday 9–11:30 a.m. and 1–3:30 p.m. Phone 541-682-3653 [1].
  • Fee: $60, by cash, check, money order or card (3% fee; no American Express). An optional decorative certificate is $1, a replacement license $10 and a courthouse civil ceremony $117 [1].
  • Waiver: the County Clerk may waive the 3-day waiting period for a good and sufficient reason. The waiver is $10, and the license fee and waiver must be paid in cash [1].
  • Copies: the first certified copy is $7.75 and each additional copy in the same transaction is $4 [1].

Recorded documents: Lane County Deeds & Records

  • County fees: under House Bill 3175, the public land corner preservation fee rose from $10 to $25 per document, raising a one-page, one-title document from $87 to $102, plus $5 for each additional page [2].
  • Paying: checks and money orders to Lane County Deeds and Records, cards in person or by phone (3% fee) and cash in person only [2].

Court records: Oregon circuit court (statewide fees)

  • Fees (statewide): Oregon's circuit courts are part of the Oregon Judicial Department, and one fee schedule covers every county. Paper copies are $3 per request in a case plus 25 cents a page; electronic copies are $3 per request ($1 more on a CD); a copy of a recording of a proceeding is $10 per request. A certified copy is $5, plus 25 cents a page if on paper, and an exemplification is $10, plus 25 cents a page if on paper. Locating and inspecting court records is free [15].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Oregon apostilles come from the Secretary of State's Corporation Division in Salem, not the county [5]. Only originals are accepted, not photocopies [5].
  • Vital records first: birth and death certificates must be certified copies from OHA Vital Records or the county where the event happened; marriage and divorce certificates must come from OHA Vital Records. County documents must show the printed name of the clerk who certified them [5].
  • Fee: $10 per document. Pay by check or money order to "Corporation Division," or by MasterCard, Visa, Discover or American Express [16].
  • In person: Public Service Building, 255 Capitol St. NE, Suite 151, Salem, OR 97310. The front desk takes walk-ins Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; phone 503-986-2200 [5].
  • By mail: send the request form and documents; you can pick them up, have them mailed back in your self-addressed stamped envelope or send a prepaid FedEx or UPS label. Without a label they come back by regular mail. USPS Express and Priority Mail aren't delivered directly to the office [16]. The state's 2022 pamphlet says to allow 10 business days by regular mail and 2–3 business days with FedEx or UPS [17]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is printing at Eugene Public Library?

10 cents per side for black and white and 50 cents per side for color, for prints and photocopies [6].

Do I need an appointment for a passport in Eugene?

Not at the UO Ticket Office, which is walk-up only, Monday–Friday 11 a.m.–5:30 p.m. [7].

What does a notary cost in Oregon?

At most $10 per notarial act, or $25 for a remote notarial act, plus any travel fee you agree to in advance (statewide) [8].

Where do I get a marriage license in Eugene?

From Lane County Deeds & Records, 125 East 8th Ave., Monday–Friday 9–11:30 a.m. and 1–3:30 p.m. The license is $60; a waiver of the 3-day wait is $10, and both must be paid in cash [1].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Eugene?

For a Lane County birth within the last 5 months and 28 days, at Lane County Public Health, 151 W. 7th Ave., Suite 100-1A [3]. For older records, from the Oregon Health Authority (statewide) [4].
