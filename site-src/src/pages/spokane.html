Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Spokane Public Library

  • Printing: use the library's print-now option to upload a document from your computer or phone and choose a location, then release it at the library's self-service kiosk with your library card number or a guest pass [5].
  • Visitors: people who aren't Spokane residents can get a guest pass at the self-service kiosk at any location to use or print from the library's computers [5].
  • Prices, scan and fax: we didn't find print prices, scanning or fax details on the Spokane Public Library pages we could read; its printing page didn't load when we checked.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 4 locations in Spokane: 1314 S Grand Blvd, Ste. 2, (509) 747-2922; 1818 West Francis Ave, (509) 327-9772; 12402 N Division St, (509) 467-4451; and 2910 E 57th Ave, Ste. 5, (509) 448-6368. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [8]. We didn't find prices on the store pages.

Notary in Spokane

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Spokane Public Library pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: its Spokane page lists notarizing [8]. We didn't find store notary prices; Washington's legal maximum is below.
  • Fees (statewide): Washington caps a notary's fee at $15 for witnessing or attesting a signature, taking an acknowledgment or a verification on oath or affirmation, certifying or attesting a copy, administering an oath or affirmation, or certifying that an event occurred. A remote notarial act can cost up to $25 [7][9]. A notary need not charge at all [10].
  • Extras (statewide): a notary may charge the actual cost of copies, and may charge a travel fee only if you agree to it in advance and are told it isn't required by law [7]. Washington's $15 cap matches Nevada's and is three times Idaho's $5.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Washington Department of Health (statewide)

  • State price: state law sets $25 for each certified or informational copy, whether it comes from the Department of Health (DOH) or a local registrar, and allows an extra fee for electronic payment [13].
  • Online or phone (VitalChek): from $40.50 a copy ($25 certificate, $8.50 VitalChek fee and $7 DOH processing fee), plus $3 if you choose online identity verification. DOH says these orders ship 3–7 business days after it receives them [14].
  • Mail: from $25 a copy, by check or money order to DOH, PO Box 9709, Olympia, WA 98507 [14][15]. The DOH page gives two timelines: shipped within 6–8 weeks in one place, received within 8–10 weeks in another [14].
  • In person: DOH partners with local health departments, which may charge an extra fee; most of those orders are filled the same day [14]. The local office for this city is below.
  • Extras: the state order form lists heirloom birth certificates at $50 and an apostille at $15 [15].

Birth and death certificates in person: Spokane Regional Health District

  • Where: 1101 W. College Ave., Spokane. Monday–Thursday 7:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Friday 7:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m.; no walk-ins after 4:15 p.m. Monday–Thursday or after 12:15 p.m. Friday [3].
  • Price: $40 a copy walk-in, $35 by mail and $43 by phone or online through VitalChek (866-687-1464). Phone and online orders are mailed by USPS within 7 business days [3].

Marriage licenses: Spokane County Auditor

  • Where: the Recording and Marriage License Office, 1116 W. Broadway, Spokane, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.; arrive at least 30 minutes before closing. Phone 509-477-2271 [1][2].
  • Fee: $162: the $159 application fee plus $3 for a certified copy. Pay by cash, check or card in person (cards carry a processing fee). By mail, send notarized applications and a check, cashier's check or money order to Spokane County Auditor – Marriage, PO Box 2353, Spokane, WA 99210 [1].
  • Timing: you get the license when you apply; the ceremony must be in Washington 3–63 days after the application. Extra certified copies are $3 [1].

Recorded documents: Spokane County Auditor

  • Where: 1116 W. Broadway, Spokane, WA 99260, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Phone 509-477-2270 [2].
  • County fees (effective July 27, 2025): $303.50 for the first page of a standard document and $1 for each additional page; $304.50 for the first page of a deed of trust. Copies: $3 for the first certified page, $1 for the first regular page and $1 for each additional page. Record searches are $8 an hour [2].
  • Statewide law: RCW 36.18.010 sets the county auditor's base fees: $5 for the first page of a recorded document and $1 for each additional page, $3 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $1 a page for noncertified copies and $8 an hour for record searches. Surcharges from other state laws are added on top [16], which is why the county fee above is much higher.

Court records (statewide fees)

  • Local page: we didn't find the Spokane County Clerk's own copy-fee page, so only the statewide fees are listed here.
  • Fees (statewide law): the superior court clerk charges $5 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $2 for each additional seal when a copy is authenticated or exemplified, and 50 cents a page for a copy without a seal. A certificate is $2, and mailed copies cost postage plus a $2 handling fee [17].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Washington apostilles come from the Secretary of State, not the county [4]. Vital records must be apostilled by the state that issued them [4].
  • Fee: $15 per document; a document can be more than one page. Pay by check or money order to "Secretary of State" [4][18].
  • Timing: standard processing typically takes 7–10 business days. Expedited processing is $100 extra for each 10 documents and takes 2–3 business days [4].
  • Same day in person: at the Tumwater office or the newer Cheney (Eastern Washington) location, for $150 extra for the first 10 documents and $100 for each additional 10. Cheney offers same-day service only, and visits there are recorded [4]. We didn't find street addresses on the apostille page.
  • By mail: send the request form to PO Box 40228, Olympia, WA 98504-0234 [18]. Documents come back by first-class mail unless you include a prepaid, traceable label [4]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I fax in Spokane?

The UPS Store's Spokane page lists faxing at its 4 Spokane stores [8]; we didn't find prices. We didn't find fax details on the Spokane Public Library pages we could read.

Where can I apply for a passport in Spokane?

At Liberty Park Station, the Shadle Garland Post Office or Riverside Station; schedule at usps.com/scheduler [6].

What does a notary cost in Washington?

At most $15 per notarial act, or $25 for a remote notarial act, plus copy costs and any travel fee you agree to in advance (statewide) [7].

Where do I get a marriage license?

From the Spokane County Auditor's Recording and Marriage License Office, 1116 W. Broadway. The license is $162 and you get it when you apply, but the ceremony must be 3–63 days later [1].

Where can I get an apostille near Spokane?

The Washington Secretary of State offers same-day apostilles in person at its Cheney location, for $150 extra for the first 10 documents on top of the $15 fee (statewide) [4].
