Counties in New Hampshire (statewide): New Hampshire has ten counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds. Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the state Division of Vital Records Administration (Department of State) [5][6].

Notary in Rochester

  • Rochester City Clerk (basic notary free): City Clerk FAQ says basic Notary Public services have no fee. Extended notary (more than one notary, or travel within city limits) is noted separately; we didn't find a published dollar amount for extended service on that FAQ (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Office at 150 Wakefield Street (Foley Center); Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; (603) 332-2130 [2][9].
  • Rochester Public Library: we didn't find a notary service listing on the library pages we checked (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [4].
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state; NH's statewide cap is $10 per ordinary act [10][11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): New Hampshire caps ordinary notarial acts at $10 each (oath, witness, service, or certification). Remote notarial acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act. Depositions follow RSA 517:19 (SOS manual: $5 to $50, plus $0.20/mile to swear witnesses). No fee for administering oaths of office for town officers [11][12].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Rochester City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: James W. Foley Memorial Community Center, 150 Wakefield Street, Rochester, NH 03867. Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Phone (603) 332-2130. A Jan 14, 2025 city notice (past) announced the move to this address; the City Clerk page still lists it as the current office [9][3].
  • Fees: first certified copy $15.00; each additional copy $10.00. Mail orders: check payable to "City of Rochester" to City Clerk's Office, 150 Wakefield Street, Rochester, NH 03867 [3][16].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): any of New Hampshire's 234 city and town clerks can issue certified copies when you have a direct and tangible interest; many clerks use the NHVRIN system for records since 1935 [5].
  • State Division of Vital Records Administration (statewide): 9 Ratification Way, Concord, NH 03301-2455. Search fee $15 for the first copy (kept whether or not a record is found); $10 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [5][16].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): request a notarized certified copy from a city/town clerk or DVRA, then take it to the Secretary of State (vital-record photocopies are not accepted) [17][5].

Marriage licenses (Rochester City Clerk)

  • When: both parties must come in person with photo IDs and birth certificates [18].
  • Fee: $50 state-mandated licensing fee [18].
  • Waiting period: none; license good for 90 days from the application date and usable anywhere in New Hampshire [18].
  • State rules (statewide): apply for a marriage license at any New Hampshire city or town clerk, regardless of where you live. There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in New Hampshire. Both parties must usually appear together. You must be 18 or older [5].

Land records (Strafford County Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 259 County Farm Road, Suite 202, Dover, NH 03820 (Strafford County seat). Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recording stops at 4:00 p.m. Phone (603) 742-1741 [19][6].
  • Copies: $1 per page of recorded document; certification adds $1 plus the copy charge. Fax copies $3 first page / $1 each additional outgoing; $1/page incoming [19].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): RSA 478:17-g sets $10 for the first recorded page plus $4 for each additional page for most documents; mortgage discharges and many lien releases are $15 first page plus $4 per additional page. An additional $25 LCHIP surcharge applies to each deed, mortgage, mortgage discharge, or plan [20].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the New Hampshire Secretary of State's Office, Room 204, State House, 107 North Main Street, Concord. Counter Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:15 p.m. [17].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per document; cash or check payable to the State of New Hampshire. Same-day service on 10 or more documents adds $25 per 10 documents [17].
  • Mail (statewide): include the request form, payment, and a self-addressed prepaid return envelope [17].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is City Clerk notary in Rochester?

Basic Notary Public services at the City Clerk have no fee. Extended notary (multi-notary or travel) is noted separately; confirm any extended fee with the office [2].

How much is a birth certificate in Rochester?

$15 for the first certified copy and $10 for each additional copy at the City Clerk [3].

How much can a New Hampshire notary charge?

Up to $10 per ordinary notarial act. Remote acts for a remotely located individual are $25 per act (statewide) [11][12].

Can I get an apostille on a Rochester birth certificate?

Request a notarized certified copy from the City Clerk or DVRA, then bring it to the Secretary of State in Concord. The apostille is $10 per document (statewide) [17].

Where do I record a deed for Rochester property?

Strafford County Registry of Deeds, 259 County Farm Road, Suite 202, Dover. Recording fees follow RSA 478:17-g [19][20].
