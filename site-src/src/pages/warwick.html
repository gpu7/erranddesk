Warwick, Rhode Island document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Warwick, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Warwick, a post office [1]. Warwick Public Library prints at 20¢ a page and faxes for $1.75 for the first page [2]. Birth certificates are $22 walk-in at City Hall, 3275 Post Rd. [3][4].
No county clerk in Rhode Island (statewide): Rhode Island has no county government [5]. Its counties were abolished as governmental units and survive only as geographic areas [6]. Land records are recorded in the city or town where the property is, and city and town clerks issue birth, death and marriage copies.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Warwick Public Library (Central, 600 Sandy Lane): prints are 20¢ a page black and white and 50¢ color, from library computers or your own device. Copies are also 20¢ and 50¢; pay for copies with cash or check, and bills over $20 aren't accepted. Printing takes cash, cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Fax is $1.75 for the first page and $1 for each additional page. Scanning is free [2].
  • FedEx: 7 Warwick locations, including the FedEx Office Print & Ship Center at 1000 Bald Hill Rd. ((401) 826-0808) [7].
  • The UPS Store: 2 Warwick stores, at 988 Centerville Rd. Suite B ((401) 320-7174) and 1643 Warwick Ave. ((401) 738-4612) [8].

Notary in Warwick

  • Warwick Public Library: notaries are available, but call first at (401) 739-5440 x3; the library can't guarantee one without a call [9]. We didn't find a library notary fee on its page.
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [10].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a Rhode Island notary may charge up to $25 per document or notarization, including remote online notarization, and must tell you the fee before notarizing [11][12]. That cap is higher than in most nearby states, so ask first.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Warwick City Clerk (City Hall, 1st floor, 3275 Post Rd.)

  • Birth, death and marriage copies: $22 walk-in or $25 by mail for the first copy, $18 for each extra copy [3]. Copies are issued Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. [4], and the birth record system shuts down at 4 p.m. [3].
  • Payment (the city's pages differ): the Vital Statistics page says certified copies take only a money order or certified bank check, with no cards or out-of-state checks [3]. The City Clerk's page says the office takes cash, check or money order, but no out-of-state checks [4]. Bring a money order to be safe.
  • By mail: send the signed application, a copy of your driver's license, a money order or cashier's check to the City of Warwick, and a self-addressed stamped envelope [4].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Any city or town hall (statewide): births from 1960 on, and deaths and marriages from 2022 on, can be ordered at any Rhode Island city or town hall. Older records come from the city or town where the event happened or where you lived [16].
  • Fees (statewide): $22 for the first copy in person and $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy of the same record bought at the same time [17].
  • State office (statewide): RIDOH Center for Vital Records, Simpson Hall, 6 Harrington Rd., Cranston, weekdays 7:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Mail orders take 4–6 weeks; rush orders cost $7 more [17].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): order the copy from the state office, not a city hall. RIDOH says vital records issued by a city or town can't be apostilled [16].

Marriage licenses (City Clerk)

  • By appointment only: call 401-738-2006, ext. 1 [4].
  • Hours (the city's pages differ): the City Clerk's page says 8:30 a.m.–noon and 1–3 p.m. [4]; the marriage guidelines say 8:30–12 and 1–3:30, Monday–Friday [18].
  • Fee and documents: $24. Both of you apply in person with a certified long-form birth certificate, a driver's license, and 2 pieces of first-class mail from 2 different senders showing your current address [18].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at a city or town clerk. Apply where either of you lives if one of you lives in Rhode Island; if neither does, apply where the ceremony will be. RIDOH lists the fee as $24, and the license is good for 3 months [19][20].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): R.I. Gen. Laws § 15-2-9 sets the clerk's license fee at $14.00 [21], while RIDOH lists $24 [20]. We didn't find which law adds the other $10 (last checked Nov 4, 2026). Clerks charge the $24.

Land records (City Clerk)

  • Recording: in person Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., or by mail to the City Clerk's Office with a self-addressed stamped envelope. Electronic recordings aren't accepted after 3 p.m. [4].
  • Fees (city PDF, last checked Nov 5, 2026): deed, foreclosure deed, redemption deed or lis pendens $84 plus $1 per extra page; mortgage, mortgage agreement or lease $64 plus $1 per extra page; assignment or discharge of mortgage and many other instruments $49 plus $1 per extra page; tax certificate/MLC $8; UCC $16 for 1–2 pages or $32 for 3+; Rhode Island Housing documents no fee [22].
  • Copy fees (sources differ): Warwick's Recording Fee Schedule lists copies at $1.50 a page and certified copies at $4.50 plus $1.50 for each additional page [22]. State law sets copies at $1.50 a page and $3.00 to certify (statewide) [23]. Ask the clerk which applies.
  • Recording fees (statewide law): R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-13-7 sets a base fee by document type, such as $80 for a deed and $60 for a mortgage, plus $1 for each extra page [24]. A $4 Historical Records Trust charge is added to each recorded document [25]. That's why clerks post $84 and $64.
  • Copies (statewide law): § 34-13-9 sets copies of recorded documents at $1.50 a page and $3.00 to certify [23].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Rhode Island Department of State, Business Services Division, 148 West River Street, Providence. Bring or mail the document with a request form; checks and money orders go to the RI Department of State [26].
  • Fee (statewide): $5 per document [26]. Ordering online adds a $0.75 enhanced access fee per document; you still mail in the original [27]. For related documents in one matter filed together, the total is capped at $150 [28].
  • Returns (statewide): orders come back by USPS first-class mail unless you include a prepaid DHL, FedEx or UPS label [26].
  • Vital records (statewide): only state-issued copies can be apostilled [16]. You can ask RIDOH to send the record directly to the Department of State [26].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [29]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is faxing at the Warwick Public Library?

$1.75 for the first page and $1 for each additional page [2].

How much is a birth certificate in Warwick?

$22 walk-in or $25 by mail, plus $18 for each extra copy [3].

Can I pay for a Warwick birth certificate with a card?

No. The Vital Statistics page says no debit or credit cards [3].

How much can a Rhode Island notary charge?

Up to $25 per document or notarization, disclosed in advance (statewide) [11].
