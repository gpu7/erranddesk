Gillette, Wyoming document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Gillette, Wyoming, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Wyoming rules differ from Colorado's in several places, and we point those out.

Which offices serve Gillette: Gillette is in Campbell County, and the Campbell County Courthouse is at 500 S. Gillette Ave. [20]. This page covers the Campbell County Public Library, 2101 S. 4J Road, Gillette, WY 82718, 307.682.3223 [1]; the Campbell County Clerk, Suite 1600 of the courthouse, for recording and marriage licenses [6]; the Clerk of District Court, Suite 2600, for passports [5]; and Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, the state office in Cheyenne that issues birth and death certificates [11][12].
Short version: Copies and black and white printouts at the Campbell County Public Library are 10¢ a page, and faxing is $1 a page, sending or receiving [1][2]. The library has notaries on duty most open hours, at $3 per stamp [3]. The Clerk of District Court takes passport applications without an appointment [5]. The County Clerk doesn't issue birth or death certificates; those come from the state office [9][10].

Notary in Gillette

  • Library notary: "There are commissioned public notaries at both Campbell County Public Library and Wright Branch Library." With few exceptions, a notary is on duty all hours the library is open. The charge is $3 for each notary stamp. Photo ID is required for all signers, all signers must be present, and don't sign the document before it's notarized [3].
  • Wyoming fee limit (statewide): under W.S. 32-3-126, a notarial officer may charge "not more than ten dollars ($10.00) per notarial act," may charge less or waive the fee, and may add a technology fee for electronic notarization or a travel fee (up to the IRS standard mileage rate) only if you agree to it in advance [15]. The Wyoming Secretary of State's notary page links to this law [14]. This differs from Colorado, where the limit is $15 per document.
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [16]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example): its Gillette page lists notarizing among its services, without prices [19]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $10 per notarial act [15].

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Wyoming Vital Statistics Services (state office)

  • Not at the County Clerk: "The County Clerk does not provide copies of birth certificates," and the same goes for death certificates. The clerk's pages point you to Wyoming Vital Records [9][10].
  • Who issues them (statewide): in Wyoming, certified birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates come from the state's Vital Statistics Services, part of the Wyoming Department of Health. Its office is in Cheyenne [11][12]. This differs from Colorado, where county public health offices issue certificates.
  • In person (Cheyenne): 2300 Capitol Avenue, 1st Floor of the Hathaway Building, Cheyenne. Public hours Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; Friday by appointment only, (307) 777-7591 [12].
  • Fees (statewide, from the mail form): birth, marriage, divorce or stillbirth certificate $25.00 each. Death certificate $25.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy per request. A photocopy of an original certificate is $30.00. The 2026 form's footer reads "Form 1a - 2024" [13].
  • Closed records (statewide): "Wyoming is a closed record state." Birth records are closed to the public for 100 years and death, marriage and divorce records for 50 years, so only entitled people (such as the person named, a parent, or immediate family for a death record) can get copies. You need a government-issued photo ID, or a notarized signature on the form [13].
  • By mail: send the form with ID, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Checks or money orders go to "Vital Statistics Services"; cash is accepted but not recommended [13].
  • Online (statewide): the How to order page's Order Online link goes to the state's own VSS Portal at wyomingvitalrecords.wyo.gov [11]. You need an account, and you must attach an image of a valid government-issued ID. Online orders are paid by credit card only, with a 2.4% convenience fee ($1.00 minimum) on every order. For delivery other than USPS First Class Mail, you can attach a prepaid label you buy from UPS, FedEx or USPS [23]. Per-certificate online fees, processing times and shipping options are behind the portal sign-in; we didn't find them.
  • Payment (statewide): the state FAQ lists money orders, a personal check from the person applying, credit card (online or in-person orders only) and cash (in-person orders only). The office can't take payment over the phone [24].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Campbell County Clerk

  • Office: Campbell County Clerk, 500 S. Gillette Ave., Suite 1600, Gillette, WY 82716, (307) 682-7285. Hours Mon–Fri: titling 8 a.m.–5 p.m., recording 8 a.m.–4 p.m. [6].
  • Recording: the clerk's fee schedule, effective July 1, 2023, lists $12.00 for the first page of any instrument and $3.00 for each additional page. A copy is $0.50 a page at the time of recording or $1.00 a page afterward, paid in advance. Certification is $5.00 per instrument. Military discharge papers are recorded or certified at no charge [7]. This differs from Colorado's flat $43 per document.
  • Formatting: the clerk asks for a 1.5" top margin and a font no smaller than Calibri 12 point [22].
  • Marriage licenses: $30.00, cash or check/money order, by appointment at (307) 682-7285. No appointments from noon to 2 p.m., and the last one of the day is at 4 p.m. Both applicants must be present. One certified copy is available from the clerk for $5.50 within 60 days of the ceremony; after that, or for more copies, go to Wyoming Vital Records [8].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. The Clerk of District Court takes a money order for the federal passport application fee, and the County Clerk accepts one for marriage licenses [5][8]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

We didn't find a City of Gillette or Campbell County shred event on the official pages we could read. The UPS Store lists shredding at its Gillette location, without prices [19]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I get something notarized in Gillette?

At the Campbell County Public Library, where a notary is on duty most open hours for $3 per stamp [3].

Do I need an appointment for a passport in Gillette?

Not at the Clerk of District Court, which accepts applications from 8:00 to 12:00 and 2:00 to 4:00 [5]. The post office uses appointments at usps.com/scheduler [4].

How much does faxing cost at the Gillette library?

$1 per page, sending or receiving [1].

Can I get a birth certificate at the Campbell County Clerk?

No. The clerk doesn't provide copies; order from Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, at $25 each [9][13].
