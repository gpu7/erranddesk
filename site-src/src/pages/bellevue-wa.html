Short version: every King County Library System library has free faxing at its Document Stations, and cardholders get $5.00 of printing and copying a week [6][7]. A King County marriage license is $169 [1].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

King County Library System (KCLS)

  • Free credit: KCLS cardholders get $5.00 in printing and copying credits, refreshed weekly. Without a card, you can pay at the self-service kiosk or the information desk [7].
  • Prices: after the allowance, letter-size prints and copies are $0.15 black and white and $0.50 color [6].
  • Print from your own device: printers are at every KCLS library. Send jobs from a phone, tablet or laptop through print.princh.com or the Princh app, or by email to your library's printer address; jobs wait up to 12 hours [7].
  • Fax and scan: you can send a fax for free at Library Document Stations, which also scan to email, cloud storage or a USB drive. All libraries have faxing when they are open [6]. We didn't find whether you can receive faxes.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 5 locations in Bellevue: 1100 Bellevue Way NE, Ste. 8A, (425) 462-7162; 4957 Lakemont Blvd SE, Ste. C-4, (425) 747-3313; 12819 SE 38th St, (425) 562-0311; 14150 NE 20th St., Ste. F1, (425) 643-3630; and 15600 NE 8th Street, Ste. B1, (425) 746-6880. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [10]. We didn't find prices on the store pages.

Notary in Bellevue

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the KCLS pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: its Bellevue page lists notarizing [10]. We didn't find store notary prices; Washington's legal maximum is below.
  • Fees (statewide): Washington caps a notary's fee at $15 for witnessing or attesting a signature, taking an acknowledgment or a verification on oath or affirmation, certifying or attesting a copy, administering an oath or affirmation, or certifying that an event occurred. A remote notarial act can cost up to $25 [9][11]. A notary need not charge at all [12].
  • Extras (statewide): a notary may charge the actual cost of copies, and may charge a travel fee only if you agree to it in advance and are told it isn't required by law [9]. Washington's $15 cap matches Nevada's and is three times Idaho's $5.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

The King County offices below are in downtown Seattle, not Bellevue.

Birth and death certificates: Washington Department of Health (statewide)

  • State price: state law sets $25 for each certified or informational copy, whether it comes from the Department of Health (DOH) or a local registrar, and allows an extra fee for electronic payment [15].
  • Online or phone (VitalChek): from $40.50 a copy ($25 certificate, $8.50 VitalChek fee and $7 DOH processing fee), plus $3 if you choose online identity verification. DOH says these orders ship 3–7 business days after it receives them [16].
  • Mail: from $25 a copy, by check or money order to DOH, PO Box 9709, Olympia, WA 98507 [16][17]. The DOH page gives two timelines: shipped within 6–8 weeks in one place, received within 8–10 weeks in another [16].
  • In person: DOH partners with local health departments, which may charge an extra fee; most of those orders are filled the same day [16]. The local office for this city is below.
  • Extras: the state order form lists heirloom birth certificates at $50 and an apostille at $15 [17].

Birth and death certificates in person: Public Health – Seattle & King County

  • Where: 201 S. Jackson St., Suite 220, Seattle, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (opens at 9 a.m. on the first Wednesday of the month). Phone 206-837-0719 [3].
  • In person: orders are placed at a VitalChek kiosk in the 2nd floor East Lobby; the page says the kiosk fee is $28.50, and certificates are usually issued while you wait, in about 10–15 minutes [3].
  • Online or phone (VitalChek): $25 plus a $6 agency fee and $11.50 handling, processed in 4–5 business days; UPS rush shipping is $18.50 [3].
  • Mail: $25 plus $4; birth certificates arrive in about 3–4 weeks [3] and death certificates in about 2–3 weeks [18].

Marriage licenses: King County Recorder's Office

  • Where: the King County Customer Service Center, 201 S. Jackson St., Suite 204 (2nd floor), Seattle, WA 98104, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Apply online first, then finish in person; you can also apply by mail [1].
  • Fee: $169, paid by cash, check, money order or card (in person) or by check or money order to King County Recorder (mail) [1]. Card payments carry a 2.35% fee, $2 minimum [19].
  • Timing: there's a 3-day waiting period, and the license is valid for 60 days. Certified copies of a recorded marriage certificate are $3 each [1].

Recorded documents: King County Recorder's Office

  • Where: 201 S. Jackson St., 2nd floor, Seattle. Documents can also be recorded by mail or e-recording [2][20].
  • County fees: $303.50 for the first page of a standard document and $1 for each additional page, and $304.50 for the first page of a deed of trust [20].
  • Copies: certified recorded documents (legal size or smaller) are $3 for the first page and $1 for each additional page; uncertified recorded documents are $1 a page; non-recorded, uncertified documents are 15 cents a page; searching records is $8 an hour. The page notes that copy requests by phone end November 2 [21]. Cards carry a 2.35% convenience fee, $2 minimum [19].
  • Statewide law: RCW 36.18.010 sets the county auditor's base fees: $5 for the first page of a recorded document and $1 for each additional page, $3 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $1 a page for noncertified copies and $8 an hour for record searches. Surcharges from other state laws are added on top [22], which is why the county fee above is much higher.

Court records: King County Superior Court Clerk

  • Fees: the Department of Judicial Administration's fee schedule lists certified copies at $5 for the first page and $1 for each additional page, and non-certified copies at 50 cents a page [23].
  • Fees (statewide law): the superior court clerk charges $5 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $2 for each additional seal when a copy is authenticated or exemplified, and 50 cents a page for a copy without a seal. A certificate is $2, and mailed copies cost postage plus a $2 handling fee [24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Washington apostilles come from the Secretary of State, not the county [5]. Vital records must be apostilled by the state that issued them [5].
  • Fee: $15 per document; a document can be more than one page. Pay by check or money order to "Secretary of State" [5][25].
  • Timing: standard processing typically takes 7–10 business days. Expedited processing is $100 extra for each 10 documents and takes 2–3 business days [5].
  • Same day in person: at the Tumwater office or the newer Cheney (Eastern Washington) location, for $150 extra for the first 10 documents and $100 for each additional 10. Cheney offers same-day service only, and visits there are recorded [5]. The expedited and same-day fees both appear on the current apostille page, so both are listed here [5].
  • Cheney office: 960 Washington St., Cheney, WA 99004, open Monday–Wednesday and Friday 9 a.m.–3 p.m. and closed Thursdays. It provides same-day service only and can't receive mail [26].
  • Tumwater office: 243 Israel Rd. SE, Tumwater, WA 98501, open Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. and Friday 8 a.m.–3 p.m. The page says this address "can only receive overnight deliveries by a commercial carrier." The same-day cutoff is 30 minutes before closing. Phone 360-725-0377 [26].
  • By mail: send the request form to PO Box 40228, Olympia, WA 98504-0234 [25]. Documents come back by first-class mail unless you include a prepaid, traceable label [5]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I send a fax for free in Bellevue, WA?

Yes. KCLS libraries let you send a fax for free at Library Document Stations whenever they are open [6].

Where can I apply for a passport in Bellevue, WA?

At King County District Court, 1309 114th Ave. SE (appointment only, new applications only), or the Bel Red Post Office [8][4].

What does a notary cost in Washington?

At most $15 per notarial act, or $25 for a remote notarial act, plus copy costs and any travel fee you agree to in advance (statewide) [9].

Where do I get a marriage license?

From the King County Recorder's Office at 201 S. Jackson St. in Seattle, or by mail. The license is $169, with a 3-day waiting period [1].

Is this the Bellevue, Nebraska guide?

No. This page covers Bellevue, Washington. See the Bellevue, NE guide for Nebraska.
