South Burlington, Vermont document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in South Burlington, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: we didn't find a passport acceptance facility with a South Burlington address; the nearest locator listings are in Burlington city [1]. City Clerk notary is free for South Burlington residents (clerk page also lists business owners; notary page says residents only — both cited) with a 3-signature / 3-document daily cap [2][3]. Library prints are a suggested $0.10 B&W / $0.20 color; scanning is free; the library does not fax [4]. Certified vital copies are $10 [5][6][7].
Counties in Vermont (statewide): Vermont has 14 counties. Land records are kept by each town or city clerk — there is no county registry of deeds. Vital records are issued by town and city clerks and by the Vermont Department of Health Vital Records Office [6][8][9].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • South Burlington Public Library (180 Market Street): suggested donation $0.10 per black-and-white page and $0.20 per color page for printing and copying (cash or card). Scanning is free to email. The library does not offer fax [4].
  • The UPS Store: 150 Dorset St Ste 245, The Blue Mall ((802) 860-7428). Store pages advertise printing, shipping, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailboxes [10][11].
  • FedEx: FedEx's South Burlington page lists 1 location — FedEx at Walgreens, 39 Hinesburg Rd — drop-off and hold for pickup of pre-packaged, pre-labeled shipments only (no print/copy center on that Walgreens page) [12][13]. The same city page's marketing copy mentions "FedEx Office in South Burlington" and passport photos at FedEx Office; the locations list does not name a staffed FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in South Burlington (both cited; last checked Nov 5, 2026). FedEx's official locate search for South Burlington, VT likewise lists no staffed FedEx Office Print & Ship Center in South Burlington—Walgreens and drop boxes only [14]. The nearest FedEx Office is in Burlington at 199 Main St ((802) 658-2561; lists self-service copying, printing, passport photos and shredding) [15].

Notary in South Burlington

  • City Clerk (free for residents): 180 Market Street. Hours Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (closes at noon the day before an election). The Clerk page says notary is free for South Burlington residents and business owners (no more than 3 signatures per day) [2]. The dedicated Notary Services page says service is for South Burlington residents only, free of charge, with a cap of 3 signatures or 3 documents (whichever is less) in a 24-hour period; do not sign before you arrive; bring ID; the office will not notarize I-9s, wills, powers of attorney, or "true copy" of ID documents [3]. Ask which eligibility rule applies before you go. Phone 802-846-4105.
  • The UPS Store: 150 Dorset St Ste 245 advertises notary; fees vary by location. Vermont sets a $30 commission fee for notaries but we didn't find a statutory cap on customer notarial-act charges [11][16][17][18].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Vermont's notary chapter sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary public (26 V.S.A. § 5324). We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on what a Vermont notary may charge a customer for an ordinary notarial act in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Remote and electronic notarial acts need a special endorsement from the Office of Professional Regulation [17][18][19].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

South Burlington City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 180 Market Street. Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Phone 802-846-4105. Any Vermont clerk can issue eligible Vermont birth and death certificates; marriage certified copies are requested from the municipality that issued the license [5][2][6].
  • Fees: $10 per certified copy by mail (include ID for birth/death, a check, and a self-addressed return envelope) [5]. Statewide VDH certified copies are also $10 (plus $2 online) [6][7].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Town or city clerk (statewide): Vermont town and city clerks issue certified copies of vital records; since 2019 clerks can print eligible Vermont birth and death records filed anywhere in the state [6].
  • Vermont Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): 280 State Drive, Waterbury, VT 05671-8300. Certified copies with raised seal are $10 each; online orders add a $2 processing fee [6][7].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified (or notarized, as required) copy from a clerk or VDH, then submit it to the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration for an apostille [23][6].

Marriage licenses (South Burlington City Clerk)

  • Where: 180 Market Street. The office does not process marriage licenses after 4:00 p.m. Both parties should sign the application; at least one must appear [24][2].
  • Fee: $80; check, cash, or credit card (additional 2.8% charge plus site fee) [24].
  • Waiting period: none; the license is valid for 60 days [24][25].
  • State rules (statewide): obtain a marriage license from any Vermont town or city clerk. The license costs $80, there is no waiting period, and it is valid for 60 days. Both parties must sign the application; at least one must sign in front of the clerk. You must be 18 or older [25].

Land records (South Burlington City Clerk)

  • Office: 180 Market Street. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system. South Burlington records only South Burlington property (Burlington is separate) [26][8].
  • Recording: $15 per page (statewide statute and City page); multiple assignments/releases $15 each; Property Transfer Tax Return $15; survey plat 18×24 $25; UCC (not in land records) $25; examination of records $4 an hour [26][9].
  • Copies: research in the office uses state fees of $4 an hour and $1 per page copy [26]. Online land-records access fees (24-hour $20; monthly $60; per-page print $3) are listed on the City page [26]. Certified-copy wording on the City land page focuses on recording; statewide statute sets certified copies of recorded documents at $10.00 per page — confirm at the counter [26][9].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): 32 V.S.A. § 1671 sets $15.00 per page to record most documents at the town or city clerk; certified copies of recorded documents are $10.00 per page; uncertified copies are $1.00 per page (minimum $2.00). A property transfer tax return filing is $15.00; survey plats are $25.00 per sheet size listed in the statute. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration (VSARA) on behalf of the Secretary of State. Physical/drop-box address: 1078 U.S. Route 2, Middlesex; mailing address 1078 U.S. Route 2 - Middlesex, Montpelier, VT 05633-7701 [23].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per apostille or authentication. Do not send cash by mail; credit and debit cards are not accepted. Checks or money orders payable to the VT Secretary of State, in U.S. dollars [23].
  • Mail (statewide): include the VSARA-35 request form, payment, and documents; preliminary review before submitting is encouraged [23].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I get a free notary in South Burlington?

City Clerk, 180 Market Street. Free for South Burlington residents (clerk page also lists business owners; notary page says residents only). Cap of 3 signatures or 3 documents per day; bring ID; do not sign early [2][3].

How much is a birth certificate in South Burlington?

Certified copies are $10 at the City Clerk [5]. VDH statewide is also $10 certified plus $2 online [6][7].

Is there a passport office in South Burlington?

We didn't find one with a South Burlington address in the federal locator. The nearest listed facilities are the Burlington Post Office (11 Elmwood Ave) and Chittenden Superior Court (175 Main Street) [1].

How much can a Vermont notary charge?

26 V.S.A. § 5324 sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary. We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on customer charges for ordinary notarial acts in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [17][18].

Where do I record a deed for South Burlington property?

South Burlington City Clerk land records, 180 Market Street. Recording is $15 per page. Vermont has no county registry of deeds [26][9].
