Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Minot Public Library

  • Prints and copies: 15 cents per page for black and white and 50 cents per page for color. The public copier takes change or dollar bills. Every computer user gets one free black-and-white print per day; other printing is prepaid by adding value to your library card or guest pass at the Circulation Desk [1]. The library announced these prices as part of increases starting August 1, 2026 [2].
  • Print from your device: wireless printing through the Princh service, using printer code 109233 [1].
  • Scan: black-and-white and color scanning to a USB drive, or to a folder the library's computers can reach, is free for all visitors [1].
  • Other: lamination is 50 cents per pouch, letter size or smaller. Roll printing is $2.00 per foot of a 24-inch-wide roll, by request form [1].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax service on the library pages we checked. Call 701-852-1045 to ask.
  • Computers: you need a library card and PIN. Visitors without a card get a free 20-minute guest pass once a day, or a $2.00 daily guest pass. Computers are unavailable 10 minutes before closing [1].
  • Location and hours: 516 2nd Ave SW, Minot, ND 58701, 701-852-1045. Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–8 p.m., Fri–Sat 10 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Sunday [1].

The UPS Store #1423, 1940 S Broadway in Marketplace Food Plaza, (701) 838-7870, lists copies and documents, faxing and scanning, notary, passport photos and shredding [25]. We didn't find prices or store hours on the store page.

Notary in Minot

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Minot Public Library pages we checked. Call 701-852-1045 to ask.
  • The UPS Store: #1423 lists notary service [25]. We didn't find its notary hours or price.
  • Fees (statewide): North Dakota law says a notary "is entitled to charge and receive not more than five dollars per notarial act." A notary may also charge a travel fee, or a technology fee for an electronic or remote notarization, if you agree to it in advance and the notary explains that it's separate from the notarial fee and not required by law [16][17]. That's lower than Wyoming's and Arizona's $10 caps and matches Nebraska's $5 acknowledgment cap. South Dakota and Kansas have no dollar cap.
  • ID (statewide): the Secretary of State lists a valid driver's license, non-driver ID or passport, with a name that matches the document, or a credible witness [17].
  • Remote notarization (statewide): North Dakota allows remote notarization over a recorded audio-video session. With a paper document, only an acknowledgment is allowed and the signer sends the signed document to the notary within 3 days. With an electronic document, oaths, acknowledgments and witnessing are allowed [18].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: North Dakota Vital Records (statewide)

  • Where: the state Division of Vital Records, 600 E. Boulevard Ave., Dept. 325, Bismarck, ND 58505-0250, (701) 328-2360, vitalrec@nd.gov. Order online, by mail or, if needed, by an appointment scheduled in advance. Allow 5–7 business days for processing once a request arrives, not counting return mail [10].
  • Birth fee: $15.00 for each certified copy. The office doesn't issue uncertified copies [11]. State law caps the fee at $15 [27].
  • Death fee: $15.00 for the first copy and $10.00 for each additional copy ordered at the same time. There are three types: a complete record, a "facts of death" copy, and an informational copy that the general public can get [12].
  • Who can order a birth record: the person named, if 16 or older, or the mother or father named on it. Others need a court order or other legal documentation as an authorized representative. A notarized authorization form is available [11].
  • By mail: form SFN 8140 lists $15 per copy by check or money order payable to ND DHHS, with optional FedEx return shipping for $25 or UPS for $30. Certified copies can't be faxed or emailed [13]. Mailed copies usually go out in 3 to 5 business days, and requests with FedEx or UPS return shipping are processed the next business day [11].
  • Online: the state's own secure online ordering application, which it calls its fastest method [11]. We didn't find any online processing fee on the pages we checked.

Marriage licenses and certified marriage copies: Ward County Recorder

  • License fee: $65 in cash; no checks or credit cards [9]. State law sets a license fee of up to $30 plus a $35 supplemental fee for the domestic violence prevention fund (statewide) [19].
  • Rules: both of you must be present with a driver's license or other valid photo ID. The license is valid for 60 days, counting the purchase day as day one. If either of you was divorced, bring a certified copy of the most recent divorce decree from the clerk of court where you divorced; the office keeps it. Applicants 16 or 17 need a signed, notarized parental consent form [9]. Arrive by 4 p.m. [7].
  • Which county (statewide): the license must be issued no more than 60 days before the wedding, by the county where either of you or a parent lives, or where the marriage will take place [19].
  • Certified copies: request by mail with both names and the date of marriage, paid in advance: $8 for marriages filed before Dec. 31, 1994, or $6 for marriages filed after Jan. 1, 1995 [9]. The state's county list gives the Ward County Recorder, PO Box 5005, Minot, ND 58702, (701) 857-6410 [14].

Recorded documents: Ward County Recorder

  • Where: Administration Building, 225 Third St. SE, Minot, ND 58701 (mail to P.O. Box 5005, Minot, ND 58702-5005). Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Phone 701-857-6413, fax 701-857-6414. The office has request forms for recorded documents and recorded marriage licenses [7].
  • Recording (statewide): $20 for 1–6 pages, $65 for more than six pages, plus $3 per page after the first 25 [20].
  • Copies (statewide): certified copies of a recorded document are $5 for the first page and $2 for each additional page; a plain copy is no more than $1 per page [20]. We didn't find a Ward County copy fee list.

Court records: Clerk of District Court

  • Ward County: Ward County Courthouse, 315 3rd St SE, Minot, ND 58701 (mail to P.O. Box 5005, Minot, ND 58702-5005). Courthouse hours are 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Phone (701) 857-6600, fax (701) 857-6623. Public parking is in the lot west of the courthouse entrance [6]. Divorce decree copies come from the clerk in the county where the divorce was granted [15].
  • Fees (statewide): the court fee schedule, effective July 1, 2025, lists $20 for a certified copy of a judgment, order or other paper, and $10 for additional copies of the same document. "There is no per page charge for a certified copy. There is no fee for electronic copies." Plain copies are $.10 per page and outgoing faxes $.25 per page. Civil or criminal record searches are $10 per name [21].
  • Online search (statewide): the court system's Case Search lets you search district court records anywhere in the state by name, citation number or case number. Asking only whether a record exists is an exception to the $10 search fee [22]. We didn't find a fee for the online search on that page.

Apostilles (statewide)

The North Dakota Secretary of State in Bismarck charges $10 per authentication plus $5 per record search, with one search for each different notary signature. Requests are usually processed within 3 business days and returned by first-class mail unless you include a prepaid label. You can stop by during business hours with no appointment needed, though the office encourages calling (701) 328-2900 to make one [23]. For a birth or death record, Vital Records can coordinate the apostille with the Secretary of State if you note "apostille" and the country on your order [24]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [26]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does printing cost at the Minot library?

15 cents per page for black and white and 50 cents per page for color, with one free black-and-white print per computer user each day [1].

Can I get a passport at the Ward County Recorder without an appointment?

Yes. The office takes walk-in applications Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m. and doesn't make appointments. It can't accept renewals by Form DS-82 [8].

What does a notary cost in North Dakota?

No more than $5 per notarial act, plus a travel or technology fee only if you agree to it in advance (statewide) [16].

How much is a marriage license in Minot?

$65, cash only, at the Ward County Recorder. Both of you must be present with photo ID [9].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Minot?

From the North Dakota Division of Vital Records in Bismarck, by mail, online or by appointment, for $15 a copy [10][11].
