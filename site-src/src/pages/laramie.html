Laramie, Wyoming document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Which offices serve Laramie: Laramie is in Albany County, Wyoming (not Laramie County, which is Cheyenne's county). This page covers the Albany County Public Library, Laramie Main Library at 310 S. 8th St., 82070, 307-721-2580 [4]; the Albany County Clerk, 525 Grand Avenue, Suite 202, for recording and marriage licenses [6]; the Albany County Clerk of District Court, Suite 305, for passports [10]; and Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, the state office in Cheyenne that issues birth and death certificates [11][12].
Short version: Copying and printing at the Albany County Public Library is $0.15 a page for black and white, and faxing is $2.00 for the first page [1]. The Clerk of District Court is the only Laramie passport acceptance facility in the State Department's locator data [9]. The library's local resources list says the Albany County Courthouse notarizes free of charge [2]. Birth certificates come from the state office, at $25 each [13].

Notary in Laramie

  • From the library's list: the Albany County Public Library's Local Resources page lists these Laramie notary rates: the Albany County Courthouse, 525 Grand Avenue, "Free of charge" (through a judicial assistant); Wells Fargo, 2127 Grand Ave., "Free except for certifying copies"; UniWyo, 2020 Grand Ave., "Free for members"; Insurance Solutions by Laramie Investment Company, Inc., free for customers, otherwise $3 per document; and UPS, 514 Grand Ave., $10.00 per stamp [2]. We didn't verify these on each provider's own page. Call first.
  • Library: we didn't find a notary service at the Albany County Public Library itself [1][2].
  • Wyoming fee limit (statewide): under W.S. 32-3-126, a notarial officer may charge "not more than ten dollars ($10.00) per notarial act," may charge less or waive the fee, and may add a technology fee for electronic notarization or a travel fee (up to the IRS standard mileage rate) only if you agree to it in advance [15]. The Wyoming Secretary of State's notary page links to this law [14]. This differs from Colorado, where the limit is $15 per document.
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [16]. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Wyoming Vital Statistics Services (state office)

  • Who issues them (statewide): in Wyoming, certified birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates come from the state's Vital Statistics Services, part of the Wyoming Department of Health. Its office is in Cheyenne [11][12]. We didn't find a vital records counter in Laramie or Albany County. This differs from Colorado, where county public health offices issue certificates.
  • In person (Cheyenne): 2300 Capitol Avenue, 1st Floor of the Hathaway Building, Cheyenne. Public hours Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; Friday by appointment only, (307) 777-7591 [12].
  • Fees (statewide): birth, marriage, divorce or stillbirth certificate $25.00 each. Death certificate $25.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy per request [13].
  • Closed records (statewide): "Wyoming is a closed record state." Birth records are closed to the public for 100 years and death, marriage and divorce records for 50 years, so only entitled people (such as the person named, a parent, or immediate family for a death record) can get copies. You need a government-issued photo ID, or a notarized signature on the form [13].
  • By mail: send the form with ID, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Checks or money orders go to "Vital Statistics Services"; cash is accepted but not recommended [13]. The state's How to order page also links an online ordering portal [11]. We couldn't read the portal's fees because it needs JavaScript.

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Albany County Clerk

  • Office: Albany County Clerk, 525 Grand Avenue, Suite 202, Laramie, WY 82070, 307-721-2541. Open Monday–Friday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. [6].
  • Recording: $12.00 for the first page and $3.00 for each additional page; double-sided documents count as one page per side. Record in person, by mail or through an eRecording vendor. Copies in person are $0.25 a page [5]. The fee schedule (effective July 1, 2013) lists certification at $5 per document and a certified copy at $5.25 [7]. This differs from Colorado's flat $43 per document.
  • Marriage licenses: $30. Pay with cash, a personal check or a debit card with a PIN; there's an additional $2 convenience fee for a debit card [8]. Licenses can be used anywhere in Wyoming and expire one year from issue. Return the license within 10 days of the wedding. Certified copies are $5.25 each and are issued only to the couple [18].

More on certified copies: how to get a certified copy.

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. The Clerk of District Court accepts a money order for both passport payments [10]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

We didn't find a City of Laramie or Albany County shred event on the official pages we could read. The UPS Store lists shredding at its Laramie location, without prices [19]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Laramie?

At the Albany County Clerk of District Court, 525 Grand Avenue, Suite 305, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Its fee is $35 [10].

How much does it cost to fax at the Laramie library?

$2.00 for the first page and $1.00 for each subsequent page [1].

Is Laramie in Laramie County?

No. Laramie is in Albany County; its county offices are at 525 Grand Avenue in Laramie [6]. Laramie County is Cheyenne's county.

How much is a marriage license in Albany County?

$30, plus a $2 convenience fee if you pay by debit card [8].
