Court records come from the Eighth Judicial District Court clerk [4]. Nevada apostilles come from the Secretary of State [5].

Notary in North Las Vegas

  • The UPS Store: its North Las Vegas page lists notarizing [11]. We didn't find store notary prices; Nevada's legal maximums are below.
  • Fees (statewide): Nevada law sets the most a notary may charge: $15.00 for taking an acknowledgment, for the first signature of each signer, and $7.50 for each additional signature of each signer; $7.50 for an oath or affirmation; $7.50 for a certified copy; and $15.00 for each signature on a jurat. Fees are payable in advance if demanded [5].
  • Travel fee (statewide): a notary may add a travel fee only if you asked for the travel, the notary explains it's extra and not required by law, and you agree to the hourly rate in advance. The cap is $15 an hour from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. and $30 an hour from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m., with a 2-hour minimum allowed [5].
  • Posted fee table (statewide): a notary who charges must post a table of fees in the office, in type no smaller than 1/2 inch, and can't charge without one [5]. Nevada's $15 acknowledgment fee is higher than Montana's and Wyoming's $10 caps.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Southern Nevada Health District (Clark County)

  • Office: 280 S. Decatur Blvd., Las Vegas, NV 89107. Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; the district says that effective Monday, Oct. 5, the office opens at 8 a.m. [3]. Phone (702) 759-1010, email VitalRecords@snhd.org [8].
  • Fees: $38.00 for the first certificate and $25.00 for each additional copy in the same request, for births and deaths. The first-certificate price includes a $13 registration fee, which is waived only with verified proof of prior payment and isn't refunded [8][9].
  • How to order: online, by mail or dropped off in the office, with valid photo ID of the requester and proof of relationship if required. Mail and drop-off orders take a money order or business check payable to SNHD, not a personal check, and take 2–4 weeks, as do online orders; there's no expedited service. Once you've paid, you can pick the certificate up during business hours; it's held 60 days [8]. The district doesn't offer same-day service for death certificates [9].
  • Records held: Clark County births from 1973 and deaths from 1988 to the present. Older records come from the state office [3].

Birth and death certificates: Nevada Office of Vital Records (statewide)

  • State office: 4150 Technology Way, Suite 104, Carson City, NV 89706, (775) 684-4242, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., excluding state holidays. It no longer accepts faxed documents [15][16].
  • State price: $25 for each certified birth certificate. A certified death certificate is $25 if the death happened in Carson City or Clark, Douglas, Lyon, Mineral or Washoe County, and $22 for any other Nevada county. A record search is $10 for each name; corrections cost $45.00 [16].
  • Local offices: the state says you can also get records from the Southern Nevada Health District (Clark County) or the Washoe County health district, and that "Pricing may vary" there [15]. The state's only online vendor is VitalChek (vitalchek.com, 877-456-5410) [16].

Marriage licenses: Clark County Clerk

  • Marriage License Bureau: 201 E. Clark Avenue, Las Vegas, NV 89101. Open 7 days a week, 8 a.m. to midnight, including all holidays, "no exceptions." Besides licenses it handles vow renewals, fictitious firm names, notary bonds and oaths, marriage certificate purchases (kiosk only) and passport acceptance by appointment [1].
  • Henderson office: 240 S. Water Street, Henderson, NV 89015, Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., closed noon–1 p.m. and holidays. It issues marriage licenses but doesn't accept cash [1].
  • Fees: $102.00 for a marriage license and $20.00 for a certified marriage certificate. Checks and money orders aren't accepted for marriage licenses; card payments carry a 2% plus $1.25 service fee [7].
  • Requirements: both applicants 18 or older with government ID; no waiting period and no blood test [17].
  • Clerk records: plain copies 50 cents a page; certified copies $6.00 plus 50 cents a page; record search 50 cents per name [7]. The Government Center office, 500 S. Grand Central Pkwy., 1st floor, is open Monday–Thursday 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Fridays and holidays, and doesn't issue marriage licenses [1]. Phone (702) 671-0600 [1].

Recorded documents: Clark County Recorder

  • Fees: plain copies $1.00 a page and certification $4.00 a document. Personal checks aren't accepted, and card payments carry a 2% plus $1.25 fee. We didn't find the general per-document recording fee on the fees page [2].
  • Office: 500 S. Grand Central Pkwy., 2nd Floor, Las Vegas, NV 89155 [2]. We didn't find its hours on the pages we checked.
  • Nevada Day: the office is closed Friday, Oct. 30, 2026, and reopens Monday, Nov. 2 [10].
  • Statewide law: county recorders charge $1 a page for copies, $4 to certify a document, and $10 plus $5 (for the state's Account for Aid for Victims of Domestic or Sexual Violence) for a certified copy or abstract of a marriage certificate [18].

Court records: Eighth Judicial District Court Clerk (Clark County)

  • Fees: copies 50 cents a page; certified copies add $3 per document and exemplified copies $6. Certifying a copy you bring is $5 ($9 exemplified). Searches of records before 1990 are 50 cents per year per name [4].
  • How to ask: email recordsrequest@clarkcountycourts.us or write to the Clerk of Court, Attn: Records, 200 East Lewis Avenue, Las Vegas, NV 89155. Copies may be made while you wait if staffing allows; otherwise they can take up to 2 weeks [4].
  • Fees (statewide law): the county clerk or clerk of the court charges 50 cents a page for a copy of any record (unless waived), $3.00 for each certificate under seal, $5.00 to examine and certify a copy you prepared, and 50 cents for each year searched [19].

Apostilles (statewide)

Nevada apostilles come from the Secretary of State, not the county. State law sets the fee at $20 to authenticate a notary's signature on a document for use abroad, as an apostille for Hague Convention countries or a certification for others [5]. The Secretary of State's request form lists 401 North Carson Street, Carson City, NV 89701, 775-684-5708, offers regular, expedited, 24-hour, 4-hour, 2-hour and 1-hour service, and says all fees are per apostille or certification and listed on its fee schedule [20]. We couldn't open the Secretary of State's apostille page or fee schedule from our checker (its firewall blocked us), so we didn't verify the expedite fees or the rules for other documents, such as vital records: nvsos.gov/sos/businesses/apostille. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in North Las Vegas?

The State Department's locator lists the City of North Las Vegas at 2250 Las Vegas Blvd. North, by appointment, and the Main and Meadow Mesa post offices [6].

What does a notary cost in Nevada?

At most $15 for the first signature of each signer on an acknowledgment, $7.50 for each additional signature, and $15 for each signature on a jurat (statewide) [5].

Where do I get a marriage license?

From the Clark County Clerk. The Marriage License Bureau in downtown Las Vegas is open 8 a.m. to midnight every day, and the license is $102 [1][7].

Where do I get a birth certificate in North Las Vegas?

From the Southern Nevada Health District, $38 for the first copy and $25 for each additional copy, or from the state office in Carson City for $25 [8][16].
