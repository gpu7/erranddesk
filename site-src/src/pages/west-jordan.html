West Jordan, Utah document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in West Jordan, Utah, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Utah rules differ from Colorado's and Wyoming's in several places, and we point those out.

Which offices serve West Jordan: West Jordan is in Salt Lake County. Its libraries are branches of The County Library (Salt Lake County Library): West Jordan, 8030 S 1825 W, and Bingham Creek, 4834 W 9000 S [5]. County records come from the Salt Lake County Clerk and Salt Lake County Recorder at 2001 South State Street in Salt Lake City [10][12]. The Salt Lake County Health Department has a vital records office in West Jordan [14]. City records and the city's passport office are at West Jordan City Hall, 8000 S. Redwood Road [7][9].
Short version: West Jordan City Hall takes passport walk-ins Mon–Fri 10 a.m.–5 p.m. and takes photos for $16.12 [7]. The city's fee schedule lists notary service at $5 [8]. Faxing is free at all County Library locations, and printing is 10 cents a page [1][3]. Utah birth certificates are $25.00 for the first copy at the South Redwood Public Health Center in West Jordan [14][15].

Notary in West Jordan

  • West Jordan City: the city's fee schedule (updated June 23, 2026, Resolution 26-021, effective through June 30, 2027) lists "Notary Public Services $5" under the City Recorder [8]. The passport locator data also lists notary service at the city's passport office [6]. City Recorder's office: 8000 South Redwood Rd, 801-569-5116 [9]. Call ahead, since we didn't find notary hours.
  • The UPS Store: the 7103 S Redwood Road store lists notary service [25]. We didn't find its price.
  • Utah fee limits (statewide): under Utah Code 46-1-12, a notary may charge at most $10 per signature for an acknowledgment or jurat, $10 per page for a certified copy, $10 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature, and $10 for each signature witnessing. A remote notarization can cost up to $25. A travel fee, up to the federal mileage rate, is allowed only if the notary explains it and you agree in advance. Notaries must display an English-language fee schedule [19]. Utah's limit is per signature, unlike Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document.
  • New rules (statewide): S.B. 139 took effect May 6, 2026. It requires an unexpired ID as satisfactory evidence of identity, lets a credible witness vouch instead, and requires journals for new and renewing notaries [21]. The Lieutenant Governor's notary site has a "Find a Notary" search [20].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [22]. For other banks, see free notary options.

  • How to pay at City Hall: the application fee goes to the U.S. Department of State by check, money order or certified check, one for each application.   • Minors: the city says children under 16 must be accompanied by both parents or guardians, and 16- and 17-year-olds need one parent or guardian present [7].
  • Post office fees (national): a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [23].
  • Salt Lake County Clerk: another option, in Salt Lake City, by appointment only [10].

Birth, death, marriage and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: South Redwood Public Health Center

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Utah Office of Vital Records and Statistics keeps Utah birth and death records from 1905 to the present. "In person orders can be placed at most Utah Local Health Departments" [17]. Wyoming has only the state office for this; Utah's local counters are closer to Colorado's county public health offices.
  • In person in West Jordan: South Redwood Public Health Center, Vital Records Office, 7971 South 1825 West, West Jordan, Utah 84088, 385-468-5312, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. The county says "We will process your order while you wait" and accepts cash, check, money order, Visa and Mastercard [14].
  • Fees: a birth certificate is $25.00 for the first certified copy and a death certificate is $35.00, with additional copies $10.00 each if ordered at the same time [15]. These match the state fee schedule effective July 1, 2026 (statewide) [16].
  • By mail from the county: add a $1.00 mailing fee, pay by check or money order to the Salt Lake County Health Department. Credit cards aren't accepted for mailed orders [14].
  • Who can order: the county lists the person on the record, parents, a current or surviving spouse, siblings, children, grandparents and grandchildren. Others need legal proof of need [13].
  • State office (statewide): online and mail orders are "approximately 6 weeks out," and third-party vendors are not authorized to order for certificate holders [18].

Marriage records and recorded documents: Salt Lake County

  • Marriage license copies: the Salt Lake County Clerk takes walk-in requests Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. at 2001 South State Street, Suite S2 200, Salt Lake City, for a certified copy of your own license. Copies are $5.00 each, plus $3.00 shipping within the U.S. [11]. We didn't find the license fee on that page.
  • State abstracts (statewide): the state office has marriage and divorce records only from 1978 to 2010 [17]. An abstract is $25.00 [16].
  • Recorder: recording is a flat $40.00 per document. A recorded document copy is $2 per page, and a certified copy is $5 plus $2 per page [12].
  • City records: the West Jordan City Recorder, 8000 South Redwood Rd, 801-569-5116, handles city records requests [9]. The city's fee schedule lists copies at $0.25 per page, document certification at $5 per document, and records requests with the first 15 minutes free [8].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. West Jordan City Hall sells money orders for $2.50 each, but only for passport payments to the U.S. Department of State [7][8]. Questions

Can I walk in for a passport in West Jordan?

Yes. West Jordan City Hall takes passport walk-ins Mon–Fri 10 a.m.–5 p.m., though appointments are prioritized [7].

How much is a notary at West Jordan City Hall?

The city's fee schedule lists notary public services at $5 [8]. Utah's legal limit is $10 per signature (statewide) [19].

Can I send a fax for free in West Jordan?

Yes. The County Library says faxing is free at all its locations, including the West Jordan and Bingham Creek branches [3][5].

Where can I get a birth certificate in West Jordan?

At the South Redwood Public Health Center, 7971 South 1825 West, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. The first copy is $25.00 [14][15].
