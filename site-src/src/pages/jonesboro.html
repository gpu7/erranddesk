Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Craighead County Jonesboro Public Library

  • Printing: 10¢ a page for black and white and 50¢ for color. Adult Services staff in the library's business center can help with the public computers, Wi-Fi and printing [1].
  • Fax: $1.00 a page; ask at the Adult Services desk near the Book Shop [2].
  • Copies and scanning: we didn't find photocopy or scanning prices on the library's pages.
  • Where and when: 315 W. Oak Ave., Jonesboro, AR 72401; 870-935-5133; fax 870-935-7987. Hours listed for the Jonesboro library: Monday–Friday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., closed Sunday [3].
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find a Jonesboro store. The UPS Store's Arkansas location list doesn't include Jonesboro [9].

Notary in Jonesboro

  • Library (free): the library provides free basic notary services [3]. Its pages differ on how to get one: the notary page says to call (870) 935-5133, ext. 9968, for availability and hours [3], while the FAQ says free walk-in notary service is offered every day until 15 minutes before closing [2]. Confirm with the office before you go. Notary service ends 15 minutes before closing [3].
  • What to bring: a physical, valid government-issued photo ID that exactly matches the signer's name, and unsigned, completed documents in English or Spanish with a jurat or acknowledgment. Bring your own witnesses if needed [3].
  • What the library won't notarize: real estate documents (including refinances and quitclaim deeds), wills, I-9 and other employment eligibility or Homeland Security forms, documents in a language other than English, and auto titles without the buyer's name and address [3].
  • Fees (statewide): Arkansas sets no dollar amount. The Secretary of State's 2025 Notary Handbook says Arkansas notaries "are not required to charge for their services"; if a notary does charge, the amount must be reasonable and disclosed to and agreed upon by both you and the notary before the notarial act, citing A.C.A. § 21-6-309. The same rule applies to electronic notaries [10].
  • ID and records (statewide): the handbook says Arkansas law doesn't specify "acceptable" identification and recommends a current ID card with the signer's picture and signature. The law doesn't require notaries to keep a journal, though the handbook recommends one [10].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth, death, marriage and divorce records: Arkansas Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): the Arkansas Department of Health (ADH) charges $12 for the first copy of a birth certificate and $10 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [5]; a death certificate is $10 for the first copy and $8 for each additional copy [14]. Certified copies of marriage and divorce records are $10 per copy [15][16]. If no record is found, state law requires ADH to keep the first-copy fee ($12 for births, $10 for the others) as a search fee [5][14][15].
  • Online and phone (statewide): orders through ADH's online vendor add a $5.00 processing fee and a $1.85 identity verification fee, plus any expedited shipping; ADH says online birth certificate orders take 7–14 business days plus shipping. Phone orders: (866) 209-9482 [5][15].
  • Walk-in (statewide): ADH Vital Records, 4815 West Markham St., Little Rock, AR 72205 (across from War Memorial Stadium), Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed state holidays. Most requests are filled the same day if you arrive by 4:00 p.m. with acceptable ID; non-routine requests such as corrections, court orders and genealogy before 1935 aren't guaranteed same-day. You can also order a birth certificate at a local health unit [5]. Walk-in payment: cash, credit or debit card, or a check or money order to the Arkansas Department of Health (no temporary checks) [5].
  • By mail (statewide): send the application, a check or money order (no cash) and a copy of acceptable ID to ADH Vital Records, Slot 44, 4815 West Markham Street, Little Rock, AR 72205; allow 10–14 days plus mail time [5].

Local health unit in Jonesboro

  • Craighead County LHU: 611 E. Washington Ave., Suite B, Jonesboro, AR 72401; 870-933-4585 [17][18].
  • What to expect: ADH says you can order a birth certificate at a local health unit [5], and the Circuit Clerk's FAQ also points to the Craighead County Health Department [19]. We didn't find hours on the unit listing; call ahead. The Census Geocoder places the unit inside Jonesboro city [11].

Marriage licenses: Craighead County Clerk

  • Where: 511 South Main St., #202, Jonesboro; (870) 933-4520; 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday–Friday, except court-approved holidays [6].
  • Fee: $60.00, by cash or credit/debit card; paying by card adds $1.00 plus 3% [6][7].
  • ID: both people, 18 or older, must show valid government-issued ID with their correct name and date of birth. You don't have to be an Arkansas resident [6].
  • After: the license is valid for 60 days and must be returned to the County Clerk's office where you bought it. A copy of the marriage license certificate is $5.00 [6][7].

Recorded documents and court records: Craighead County Circuit Clerk

  • Where: 511 South Main St., #200, Jonesboro, AR 72401; (870) 933-4530. The Recorder's Office is open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday–Friday; there's also a Lake City office [20][19].
  • Recording: $15.00 for the first page (one side) and $5.00 for each additional page for deeds, mortgages, powers of attorney and most other instruments; military discharges (DD2-14) are free [21][7].
  • Certified copies: certification of a document is $5.00 [21][7]. We didn't find a per-page price for plain or certified copies of court records; call before you go. A copy of a full-size plat is $5.00 [7].
  • Online land records: a one-time Tapestry search is $8.75 per search plus $1.00 per printed page and sales tax [20].
  • Statewide: we didn't find a statewide circuit court copy fee schedule.

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Fee: the Arkansas Secretary of State charges $10 per document for an apostille and $5 per document for a certification. It lists no expedite fee and says it strives to process requests within 24–48 hours of receipt [22]. A 4% convenience fee is added to credit and debit card payments [23].
  • Where to send it (two offices, both in Little Rock): documents with an elected official's seal, such as marriage licenses, divorce decrees and judgments, go to the Elections Division, State Capitol, Room 026, (501) 682-5070 or (800) 482-1127. State agency documents and notarized documents, such as diplomas, transcripts and birth, marriage and death certificates, go to the Business & Commercial Services Division, 1401 West Capitol Ave., Suite 250 (one block east of the Capitol), (501) 682-3409 or (888) 233-0325. You can submit by mail or in person, and either office can help if you have both kinds [22].
  • Which copy: a notarized photocopy of a birth certificate isn't accepted; it must be the original or a certified copy from ADH Vital Records. Immigration, citizenship and U.S. Department of State documents can't be apostilled by the state [22].
  • Payment and return: cash, check or money order to the Arkansas Secretary of State; Business & Commercial Services also takes cards, but the Elections Division doesn't. You don't need a return envelope unless you want express return, which needs a prepaid air bill [22].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is printing at the Jonesboro library?

10¢ a page for black and white and 50¢ for color [1].

Can I fax at the library?

Yes, for $1.00 a page [2].

Where can I get something notarized for free?

At the Craighead County Jonesboro Public Library, for simple documents. Call (870) 935-5133, ext. 9968, to check availability [3].

Where can I apply for a passport in Jonesboro?

The locator lists the Caraway Jonesboro Post Office at 2404 Race St. [4].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Jonesboro?

At the Craighead County LHU or from ADH Vital Records in Little Rock, for $12 for the first copy (statewide fee) [5][17].

How much is a marriage license in Craighead County?

$60, plus $1.00 and 3% if you pay by card [6].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Arkansas Secretary of State in Little Rock, in person or by mail: $10 per apostille (statewide) [22].
