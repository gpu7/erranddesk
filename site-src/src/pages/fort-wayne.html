Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Allen County Public Library

  • Printing: print from any public computer for 10 cents per side, on library paper only. Wi-Fi printing works from any device on the library network with a web browser [2].
  • Copying: color and black-and-white copies at all locations; the page lists 10 cents per side [2].
  • Fax: send faxes from all 14 locations for $1.75 for the first page and $1 for each additional page. A credit or debit card is required, and the machine takes only single-sided, letter-size pages [2].
  • Where: the Main Library is at 900 Library Plaza [2]. The Census Geocoder didn't match that address [8].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 2 locations in Fort Wayne, at 5651 Coventry Lane and 10536 Maysville Rd. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. We didn't find prices: the Coventry Lane store page doesn't list any [10] (store #6864, 5651 Coventry Lane, (260) 755-3435), and The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [11]. The Census Geocoder places both in Fort Wayne city [8].

Notary in Fort Wayne

  • Allen County Public Library: we didn't find a notary service on the library pages we read [2]. A search of the library's site for "notary" on Nov 1, 2026 turned up only genealogy articles, not a notary service [27].
  • The UPS Store: its Fort Wayne page lists notarizing [9]. We didn't find prices.
  • Allen County Recorder: documents you record need a notary for each signature [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): in Indiana, notaries are commissioned by the Secretary of State for eight-year terms. Applicants pay a $5 processing fee, get a $25,000 assurance (bond) and complete the required education and exam [13].
  • Fees (statewide law): Indiana Code caps notary fees at "not more than ten dollars ($10) per signature" for acknowledgments, oaths and affirmations, witnessing a signature, verifications and certifying a copy. Fees for other acts are negotiable. A notary who charges must display a list of fees in advance. A travel fee is allowed up to the federal GSA travel rate. Public officials and their deputies may not charge for notarial acts done as part of official business [13].
  • Remote notarization (statewide law): a remote notary may charge "not more than twenty-five dollars ($25) for each remote notarial act" [13].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Allen County Department of Health (birth and death certificates)

  • Where: walk-in, same-day service at Citizens Square, 200 E. Berry St., third floor, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday. The average wait after you fill out a form is 10 to 20 minutes [3]. The Census Geocoder places the building in Fort Wayne city [8].
  • County fees: $20 per certified birth certificate and $20 per certified death certificate; a non-certified death certificate is $3 [3].
  • State fees, side by side: the Indiana Department of Health lists $10.00 for a birth certificate and $8.00 for a death certificate (statewide) [4]. The county and the state list different prices; check with the office you order from.
  • How to pay: cash, money order, or a debit or credit card, with a minimum $2.50 processing fee per card transaction. Personal checks aren't accepted [3].
  • By mail or online: mail a signed form, a copy of your ID and payment to Vital Records Division, 200 E. Berry St., Suite 360; allow 7 to 10 business days. VitalChek takes online orders for an extra fee [3].
  • ID and eligibility: bring a valid driver's license, state ID or passport. You need a direct and tangible interest in the record, and the department issues certificates only for births and deaths in Allen County. Questions: 260.449.7147 [3].

Birth and death certificates (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): the Indiana Department of Health charges $10.00 for a birth certificate and $4.00 for each extra copy in the same order, and $8.00 for a death certificate and $4.00 for each extra copy. Fees aren't refunded and don't include third-party fees for online or phone orders [4]. Local health departments set their own fees; see the county fees above.
  • In person (statewide): the state office has no walk-in service. To buy a certificate in person, go to the local health department in the county where the birth or death happened [4][16].
  • By mail, online or by phone from the state (statewide): mail State Form 49607 (birth) or 49606 (death) with a check or money order payable to the Indiana Department of Health (no cash). Online orders go through VitalChek, and phone orders through (866) 601-0891, with third-party fees [4].
  • Processing times (statewide): the state lists 2–3 weeks for a standard birth certificate, 6–8 weeks for a long-form birth certificate and 60 days for a death certificate [4]. Its home page says to allow 2 weeks for an order to reach the office and 10–15 business days to process it [16]. The long-form birth certificate is only available from the state [17].
  • Who can order (statewide): you need a direct interest, a documented financial or legal interest, or a close family tie (parent, adult sibling or grandparent) to the person named on the record [17].

Marriage licenses (Allen County Clerk)

  • Marriage licenses (statewide): in Indiana, the county clerk issues marriage licenses. Both of you apply in person at the clerk's office in an Indiana county where one of you lives; couples from out of state apply in the county where they will marry. The fee is $25.00 if one or both of you live in Indiana and $65.00 if neither does. Some offices add a $4.00 document fee, and each certified copy is $4.00. Most counties require cash. The license is valid for 60 days, and you can marry the same day. You'll need ID and your Social Security number [7].
  • Copies (statewide): certified copies of marriage licenses come from the clerk of the county where the license was issued, not from the state health department [18].
  • Allen County Clerk: you can start online, but both of you must finish in person at the Central Services Division, Room 201, Allen County Courthouse, 715 S. Calhoun St., 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Monday–Friday. Allow at least 30 minutes. The fee is $25.00 for Indiana residents and $65.00 for out-of-state residents. The license expires 60 days after it's issued [5]. The clerk's marriage page lists office hours of 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday, with applications taken 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.; marriage license questions: 260-449-7245 or 260-449-7370 [19].
  • How to pay: we didn't find which payment methods the clerk takes for marriage licenses. The clerk's fee page and payments page don't say; its online payments cover only civil, copy, felony and traffic or misdemeanor matters [28][29].
  • Who can apply here: Indiana couples need at least one applicant living in Allen County, with proof of residency. Out-of-state couples can apply only if they marry in Allen County [5].
  • Courthouse rule: phones, cameras and other electronic devices are banned from the courthouse, and the Sheriff may confiscate and destroy them [19].
  • Copies: the clerk's November 2025 marriage license sheet says a certified copy costs an additional $4.00 [5]. Its older fee sheet, effective July 1, 2023, lists "Certification of Copy $3.00 per document" [30], and the clerk's copy page also lists $1.00 a page plus $3.00 per document for certification [20]. The $4.00 figure is the newer one; confirm with the clerk. To order copies, request online, by email or by mail, and pay by check or money order payable to "Clerk of Allen Circuit Court" with a self-addressed stamped envelope. Fees may vary for licenses recorded before 1980 [20].

Recorded documents (Allen County Recorder)

  • Recording fees: the fee schedule on the Recorder's site lists $55.00 for mortgages, $25.00 for all other documents (including one oversize page) and $25.00 for UCCs. It's titled "Indiana County Recorder's Fee Schedule" and dated 2023 in its file name [21].
  • Copies: $1.00 a page (11 by 17 inches or smaller), $5.00 a page if larger, and $5.00 to certify [21]. Certified copies are available in the office or by mail [22].
  • How to record: in person in Room 100 of the Rousseau Centre, 1 E. Main St., 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Monday–Friday (cash, checks except counter checks, or card), by mail to the same address, or through an e-recording vendor. Property transfers also need stamps from the Allen County Assessor and Auditor [23].
  • Format: documents need a notary for each signature, and documents notarized in Indiana need a preparer's statement and a Social Security number redaction statement [12].

Court records (Allen County Clerk)

  • Copy fees: $1.00 a page plus $3.00 per document for certification. Request online, by email or by mail to the Central Services Division, Courthouse Room 201; pay by check or money order. Questions: (260) 449-7890 [20].

Apostilles and authentications (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $2.00 per document from the Indiana Secretary of State. There's no fee for birth and death certificates, adoption documents, or student transcripts and diplomas [24][13]. Pay in person by credit, debit, check or money order, or by mail with a check or money order [24].
  • One certificate (statewide): since July 1, 2022, Indiana uses a single certificate format and no longer tells apostilles and authentications apart. The certificates can't be used inside the United States [24].
  • What it can authenticate (statewide): Indiana vital records only; marriage, divorce, death and birth certificates don't need notarization, but other documents do. A marriage license must first be certified by the current circuit court clerk of the county where the marriage took place. Federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State [24].
  • Where (statewide): there's one office, at 302 W. Washington St., Room E-018, Indianapolis, IN 46204, with no branches. With an appointment it processes up to 10 documents while you wait; without one, up to 5. Phone (317) 234-9768 [24].
  • Fort Wayne note: the Secretary of State's only authentication office is in Indianapolis [24]. For an Allen County marriage license, get the copy certified by the Allen County Clerk first (statewide rule: certification by the circuit court clerk of the county where the marriage took place) [24][20].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [25]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • BBB Shred Day: BBB Serving Northern Indiana's page lists its next Shred Day on Saturday, April 24, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon, at the Allen County War Memorial Coliseum. It suggests a $10 donation per 3 banker-sized boxes, with a limit of 3 boxes per car; binders aren't accepted [26]. The same page also says "We look forward to seeing you at the 2026 Fall Shred Day!" but doesn't list a fall date [26].
  • The UPS Store: its Fort Wayne page lists shredding [9]. We didn't find prices: for the Coventry Lane store, its shredding page says only "Our shredding services are charged at a low cost per pound," with no amount [10].

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Fort Wayne?

At the Allen County Department of Health, Citizens Square, 200 E. Berry St., third floor: $20 per copy, usually in 10 to 20 minutes [3].

Where do I get a marriage license in Fort Wayne?

At the Allen County Clerk's Central Services Division, Room 201 of the Allen County Courthouse: $25.00 for Indiana residents and $65.00 for out-of-state residents [5].

How much does it cost to fax at the Fort Wayne library?

$1.75 for the first page and $1 for each additional page, paid by credit or debit card [2].

How much can an Indiana notary charge?

Up to $10 per signature for common acts such as acknowledgments and oaths, and up to $25 for a remote notarial act (statewide law) [13].

When is the next shred day in Fort Wayne?

BBB Serving Northern Indiana lists Saturday, April 24, 2027, at the Allen County War Memorial Coliseum [26].
