Counties and recording in Hawaii (statewide): Vital records and marriage licenses are issued by the state Department of Health (and licensed marriage agents), not by city clerks. Land records are recorded at the state Bureau of Conveyances in Honolulu under a statewide fee schedule — Hawaii does not use county recorders for deeds [1][2][3].
Short version: East Honolulu is a Honolulu County CDP. The passport locator dump has 0 facilities with an East Honolulu city-name match [4]. Use Oahu DOH marriage agents and Honolulu offices; HSPL print donation 15¢/page [5][6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Hawaii State Public Library System: a print donation of 15¢ per page is appreciated [5]. Photocopies: an enhanced fee of not less than 20¢ per page; color and special paper may cost more — fees are displayed at each library [7].
  • FedEx: there is no separate East Honolulu city listing on local.fedex.com (slug 404). Nearby Honolulu FedEx Office pages publish print prices — see the Honolulu guide once live, or the Honolulu city listing [8].
  • The UPS Store: /hi/east-honolulu returned 404 (not on the Hawaii index of 24 stores) [9]. The UPS Store Hawaii Kai at 7192 Kalanianaole Hwy Ste A-143A, Honolulu 96825, (808) 396-0300, is listed under Honolulu; the Census Geocoder places that address in East Honolulu CDP [10][11].

Notary in East Honolulu, Hawaii

  • City Clerk: City and County of Honolulu (East Honolulu is not a separate municipality), 530 S King St Rm 100, (808) 768-3810. We didn't find a published public notary customer fee; /clerk/notary/ returned 404 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [12].
  • The UPS Store: Hawaii Kai location (geocoded into East Honolulu CDP) advertises notary; fees vary within statewide caps [10][13].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Hawaii commissions notaries through the Attorney General. Application $20, examination $10, commission $100; renewal $120; four-year term; $1,000 surety bond [14][15]. Under HRS §456-17, notaries may charge at most $5 for an acknowledgment (per party signing) or for administration of an oath; remote online notarial acts are capped at $25 [16][17]. A notary may charge less [17].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide)

    • Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): certified birth and marriage certificates are $10 for the first copy and $4 for each additional copy of the same record, plus a $2.50 portal/administration fee per increment of up to five copies [20]. Death certificates use the same $10 / $4 copy fees plus the $2.50 fee [21].
    • Oahu Vital Records office: 201 Merchant St., Suite 100, City Financial Tower, Honolulu. Phone (808) 586-4539 (certificates). Hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (issuance/marriage); closed state holidays [1].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): the Lieutenant Governor authenticates documents; the fee is $3.00 per document [22].

    Marriage licenses

    • State rules (statewide): a marriage or civil union license costs $65.00 ($60 application fee + $5 portal fee), non-refundable. Apply online at emrs.ehawaii.gov, then both applicants meet a licensed agent together in person no more than 30 days before the ceremony [6][23].
    • Where for East Honolulu residents: there is no separate East Honolulu agent heading on the Oct 2026 agent list — use Honolulu / Oahu licensed agents (apply online first) [23][6].

    Land records (Bureau of Conveyances)

    • Bureau of Conveyances office: Kalanimoku Building, 1151 Punchbowl Street, Honolulu. Office hours Monday–Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; recordation windows about 8:01 a.m.–3:29 p.m. (confirm before you go) [2][24].
    • Recording fees (statewide): Bureau of Conveyances Regular System: $41 per document up to 50 pages, or $106 for 51 pages or more. Land Court: $36 (≤50 pages) or $101 (51+); Certificate of Title issuance $50 [2].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): Office of the Lieutenant Governor [22].
    • Fee (statewide): $3.00 per document (apostille or certification) [22].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [25]. Other sellers: money order guide.
