Casper, Wyoming document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Casper, Wyoming, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those. Wyoming rules differ from Colorado's in several places, and we point those out.

Center St., Room 157, which handles recording and marriage licenses [5]; and Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, the state office in Cheyenne that issues birth and death certificates [8][9].
Short version: Black and white printing and copies at the Natrona County Library are $0.20 a page, and its self-serve fax is $1.75 for the first page sent [1]. The library and the Casper post office both take passport applications [3][4]. Wyoming birth certificates are $25 each from the state office in Cheyenne, by mail or in person [8][10]. The county charges $12 for the first page of a recorded document and $3 for each additional page [6].

Notary in Casper

  • Wyoming fee limit (statewide): under W.S. 32-3-126, a notarial officer may charge "not more than ten dollars ($10.00) per notarial act," may charge less or waive the fee, and may add a technology fee for electronic notarization or a travel fee (up to the IRS standard mileage rate) only if you agree to it in advance [12]. The Wyoming Secretary of State's notary page links to this law [11]. This differs from Colorado, where the limit is $15 per document.
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • Credit union (example): WyHy Federal Credit Union says "Notary services are free" [14]. Its Casper branch, 1521 Centennial Ct., Casper, WY 82609, 800.442.2392, lists notary services. Branch hours: Mon, Tue and Thu 8 a.m.–5 p.m., Wed 9 a.m.–5 p.m., Fri 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m., Sat 9 a.m.–noon [15].
  • The UPS Store (example): its Casper page lists notarizing among its services, without prices [19]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $10 per notarial act [12].
  • Library: the State Department's locator data note for the Natrona County Library says "We are no longer having notary services" [4]. We didn't find a current notary page on the library's site.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Wyoming Vital Statistics Services (state office)

  • Who issues them (statewide): in Wyoming, certified birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates come from the state's Vital Statistics Services, part of the Wyoming Department of Health. Its office is in Cheyenne [8][9]. We didn't find a vital records counter in Casper or Natrona County. This differs from Colorado, where county public health offices issue certificates.
  • In person (Cheyenne): 2300 Capitol Avenue, 1st Floor of the Hathaway Building, Cheyenne. Public hours Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; Friday by appointment only, (307) 777-7591 [9].
  • Fees (statewide, from the mail form): birth, marriage, divorce or stillbirth certificate $25.00 each. Death certificate $25.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy per request. A photocopy of an original certificate is $30.00. The 2026 form's footer reads "Form 1a - 2024" [10].
  • Closed records (statewide): "Wyoming is a closed record state." Birth records are closed to the public for 100 years and death, marriage and divorce records for 50 years, so only entitled people (such as the person named, a parent, or immediate family for a death record) can get copies. You need a government-issued photo ID, or a notarized signature on the form [10].
  • By mail: send the form with ID, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. Checks or money orders go to "Vital Statistics Services"; cash is accepted but not recommended [10].
  • Online (statewide): the How to order page's Order Online link goes to the state's own VSS Portal at wyomingvitalrecords.wyo.gov [8]. You need an account, and you must attach an image of a valid government-issued ID. Online orders are paid by credit card only, with a 2.4% convenience fee ($1.00 minimum) on every order. For delivery other than USPS First Class Mail, you can attach a prepaid label you buy from UPS, FedEx or USPS [21]. Per-certificate online fees, processing times and shipping options are behind the portal sign-in; we didn't find them.
  • Payment (statewide): the state FAQ lists money orders, a personal check from the person applying, credit card (online or in-person orders only) and cash (in-person orders only). The office can't take payment over the phone [22].

Recorded documents and marriage licenses: Natrona County Clerk

  • Office: Natrona County Clerk, 200 N. Center St., Room 157, Casper, WY 82601, 307-235-9206. Hours Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. [5].
  • Recording: the clerk's posted fee schedule (effective July 1, 2013, updated 2019) lists $12.00 for the first page of any instrument and $3.00 for each additional page. Copies are $0.25 a page and certifications $5.00. Recording military discharge papers is free [6]. This differs from Colorado's flat $43 per document.
  • Marriage licenses: $30.00; a certified copy of a marriage license is $5.00. Pay with cash or a credit or debit card (a processing fee applies). No refunds [7].
  • Document alerts: the clerk offers free email alerts when a document with your name or legal description is recorded [5].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [18]. The Natrona County Library takes checks or money orders for passport applications [3]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Casper?

At the Natrona County Library, 307 E. 2nd St., by appointment, or at the Casper Post Office, 411 N. Forest Dr., by appointment at usps.com/scheduler [3][4].

How much does it cost to send a fax at the library in Casper?

$1.75 for the first page and $1.00 for each following page. Receiving is $0.20 a page [1].

Can I get a Wyoming birth certificate in Casper?

We didn't find a Casper counter. Order by mail from Wyoming Vital Statistics Services, or go in person to its office in Cheyenne. Each copy is $25 [8][10].

How much is a marriage license in Natrona County?

$30.00, from the Natrona County Clerk [7].
