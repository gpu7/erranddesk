Notary in Lewiston, Maine

  • City Clerk: 27 Pine Street. Notary public services for Lewiston residents with valid government photo ID; also dedimus justice services. Hours (effective Sept. 12, 2024): Mon/Wed/Thu 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; Tuesday 7:45 a.m.–5:30 p.m.; Friday closed. Phone 207-513-3124. We didn't find a published notary customer fee on the Clerk page (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maine charges $50 for a notary public commission that lasts seven years [10]. The Secretary of State's Course of Study says Maine does not set statutory fees for notarial acts; any fee must be disclosed in writing and agreed to before the act [11]. Remote and electronic notarization need separate SOS approval [10].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Lewiston City Clerk (vital records)

    • Where: City Clerk’s Department, 27 Pine St., Lewiston, ME 04240-7297. Phone 207-513-3124; fax 207-777-4621; email cityclerkoffice@lewistonmaine.gov [2][3].
    • Hours note: marriage licenses Mon/Wed/Thu until 3:30 p.m. and Tuesday until 5:15 p.m.; death-certificate orders are not processed Monday–Thursday 12:00–1:00 p.m. [2].
    • Fees: first certified copy $15; each additional certified copy of the same record at the same time $6. Payable to "The City of Lewiston"; Visa/MC/Discover accepted. Mail requests need a self-addressed stamped #10 envelope [3][4].

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

    • City or town clerk (statewide): 30-A M.R.S. § 2652 sets municipal clerk fees at $15 for the first certified copy of a birth, marriage, or death record and $6 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [4].
    • Maine CDC Division of Vital Records (statewide): 220 Capitol Street, Augusta. Certificate fee $15.00 per certificate, $6.00 for each additional copy ordered at the same time; VitalChek online orders add a service fee [6].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified vital record from a clerk or Maine CDC, then request a special certification (apostille or authentication) from the Secretary of State [14][6].

    Marriage licenses (Lewiston City Clerk)

    • Fee: $40 filing fee ($20 per applicant) [5].
    • Hours: arrive by 3:30 p.m. Mon/Wed/Thu and by 5:15 p.m. Tuesday to complete the process [2][15].
    • Waiting period: none; license valid 90 days anywhere in Maine (statewide) [4][16].
    • State rules (statewide): file marriage intentions with a municipal clerk (or the Department). The clerk fee is $40 for recording intentions and issuing a license ($20 each when two licenses are required). There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in Maine. Both parties must usually sign; you must be 18 or older [4][16].

    Land records (Androscoggin County Registry of Deeds)

    • Office: 2 Turner Street, Unit 4, Auburn, ME 04210 (Lewiston is in Androscoggin County). County site phone 207-753-2500; Maine Registries directory lists 207-782-0191 [17][18][7].
    • Recording: statewide private instruments are $40 total ($35 + $5 surcharge under 33 M.R.S. §§ 751–752). We didn't find a current fee schedule on the county deeds page (a 2013 PDF is stale) — confirm with the registry before you go [8][19].
    • Recording fees (statewide statute): 33 M.R.S. § 751 sets a flat $35 per instrument for most private submitters ($25 when the State or a municipality records). 33 M.R.S. § 752 adds a $5 records-preservation surcharge for most non-government recordings, so the usual private total is $40 per instrument. Plans are $45 under § 751 (county practice may list $50 with surcharge—confirm with the registry) [8][19].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): the Maine Secretary of State, Bureau of Corporations, Elections & Commissions. Mail: 101 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0101. Courier: 6 E. Chestnut Street, Augusta. Phone 207-624-7752 [14].
    • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per special certification (apostille or authentication), payable to the Secretary of State. Processing is typically 10–15 business days; call for an appointment when more than 5 documents need certification [14].
    • Mail (statewide): include the request form, documents, and payment; VitalChek orders can be forwarded to this office on request [14].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [20]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Questions

    Where can I get a notary in Lewiston, Maine?

    City Clerk at 27 Pine Street provides notary public services for Lewiston residents with government photo ID [2].

    How much is a birth certificate in Lewiston, Maine?

    $15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional copy of the same record [3].

    Is there a passport office in Lewiston?

    No facility with a Lewiston address appears in the State Department locator. Auburn lists two nearby facilities [1].

    Where do I record a deed for Lewiston property?

    Androscoggin County Registry of Deeds, 2 Turner Street Unit 4, Auburn. Private recording is $40 under the statewide schedule [17][8].
