Notary in Winooski

  • City Clerk (free for everyone): 27 West Allen Street. Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Limited notary public services are free of charge and available to everyone (Winooski residents and visitors from anywhere). Call or email before you go to confirm your document can be notarized (802-655-6410 | clerk@winooskivt.gov). Valid ID required. The office does not support powers of attorney without witnesses, wills, or identity-related documents [2][15].
  • The UPS Store (South Burlington): 150 Dorset St Ste 245 advertises notary; fees vary by location. Vermont sets a $30 commission fee for notaries but we didn't find a statutory cap on customer notarial-act charges [11][16][17][18].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Vermont's notary chapter sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary public (26 V.S.A. § 5324). We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on what a Vermont notary may charge a customer for an ordinary notarial act in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026). Remote and electronic notarial acts need a special endorsement from the Office of Professional Regulation [17][18][19].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Winooski City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: 27 West Allen Street. Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Phone 802-655-6410 [15].
  • Fees: birth and death certified copies are $10 by mail (check to Winooski City Clerk's Office with the application) [5]. Statewide VDH certified copies are also $10 (plus $2 online) [6][7].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Town or city clerk (statewide): Vermont town and city clerks issue certified copies of vital records; since 2019 clerks can print eligible Vermont birth and death records filed anywhere in the state [6].
  • Vermont Department of Health Vital Records (statewide): 280 State Drive, Waterbury, VT 05671-8300. Certified copies with raised seal are $10 each; online orders add a $2 processing fee [6][7].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified (or notarized, as required) copy from a clerk or VDH, then submit it to the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration for an apostille [23][6].

Marriage licenses (Winooski City Clerk)

  • Fee: $80; cash or check. Certified copy of an existing marriage license is $10 [5].
  • Waiting period: none under statewide rules; the license is valid for 60 days [24][5].
  • State rules (statewide): obtain a marriage license from any Vermont town or city clerk. The license costs $80, there is no waiting period, and it is valid for 60 days. Both parties must sign the application; at least one must sign in front of the clerk. You must be 18 or older [24].

Land records (Winooski City Clerk)

  • Office: 27 West Allen Street. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [25][8].
  • Recording: $15 per page; Property Transfer Tax Return $15 per document; survey plots $25 per sheet; examination of records $4 per hour; certified copies $10 per page [25][9].
  • Online index: City Clerk recorded land records search (images from January 3, 2000 forward, with noted exceptions). Searching is free; non-subscriber download/email/print is $3.50 per page [26].
  • Recording fees (statewide statute): 32 V.S.A. § 1671 sets $15.00 per page to record most documents at the town or city clerk; certified copies of recorded documents are $10.00 per page; uncertified copies are $1.00 per page (minimum $2.00). A property transfer tax return filing is $15.00; survey plats are $25.00 per sheet size listed in the statute. Vermont records land by town or city — there is no county recording system [9].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Vermont State Archives & Records Administration (VSARA) on behalf of the Secretary of State. Physical/drop-box address: 1078 U.S. Route 2, Middlesex; mailing address 1078 U.S. Route 2 - Middlesex, Montpelier, VT 05633-7701 [23].
  • Fee (statewide): $10 per apostille or authentication. Do not send cash by mail; credit and debit cards are not accepted. Checks or money orders payable to the VT Secretary of State, in U.S. dollars [23].
  • Mail (statewide): include the VSARA-35 request form, payment, and documents; preliminary review before submitting is encouraged [23].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I get a free notary in Winooski?

City Clerk, 27 West Allen Street. Free for everyone (residents and visitors). Call ahead to confirm your document type; bring ID. The office does not notarize POAs without witnesses, wills, or identity documents [2].

How much is a birth certificate in Winooski?

Certified copies are $10 at the City Clerk [5]. VDH statewide is also $10 certified plus $2 online [6][7].

Is there a passport office in Winooski?

We didn't find one with a Winooski address in the federal locator. The nearest listed facilities are the Burlington Post Office (11 Elmwood Ave) and Chittenden Superior Court (175 Main Street) [1].

How much can a Vermont notary charge?

26 V.S.A. § 5324 sets a $30 commission fee for becoming a notary. We didn't find a statutory dollar cap on customer charges for ordinary notarial acts in Chapter 103 (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [17][18].

Where do I record a deed for Winooski property?

Winooski City Clerk land records, 27 West Allen Street. Recording is $15 per page. Vermont has no county registry of deeds [25][9].
