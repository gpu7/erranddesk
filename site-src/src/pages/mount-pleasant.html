Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Charleston County Public Library

  • Prices (all branches): black-and-white copies or prints $0.10 a page; color $0.25 a page; scanning to email or to a USB flash drive is free [1].
  • Mobile printing: email documents to the library's public printers, then release, pick up and pay for them at your branch, at the same prices [1].
  • Branches in the town: Mt. Pleasant Library, 1133 Mathis Ferry Road, 843-849-6161 [2]; Wando Mount Pleasant Library, 1400 Carolina Park Blvd., 843-805-6888 [3]; and the Village Library, 430 Whilden St., 843-884-9741 [11]. All three list black-and-white and color copies and printing and mobile printing [2][3][11]. The Census Geocoder places all three in Mount Pleasant town [10].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax on the library's printing page or on the three Mount Pleasant branch pages [1][2][3][11]. An old library news post (May 4, 2020) described a public fax service at the Otranto Road Regional Library in North Charleston [12]; we didn't find a current page confirming it.
  • Hours: the Mt. Pleasant and Wando Mount Pleasant libraries are both open Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., and closed Sunday [2][3]. The Village Library's page shows no hours [11]; call 843-884-9741 before you go (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Main Library (downtown Charleston): the county's Main Library closed Aug. 1 for the last phase of its renovation, expected to take about 18 months; we didn't find a reopening date. Its temporary home, Main at Morrison, 1142 Morrison Drive, Charleston, 843-805-6930, has print and copy services [13]. Its hours are Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., closed Sunday [13] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Register of Deeds copies (Charleston County): 50 cents a page for deeds and 50 cents or $1 for plats, depending on size. You can download copies from the Register of Deeds website at no cost [14].
  • The UPS Store: its Mount Pleasant page lists 3 locations (1000 Johnnie Dodds Blvd, 1150 Hungryneck Blvd and 3022 S Morgan Point Rd) and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [15]. The Census Geocoder places all 3 in Mount Pleasant town [10]. Prices aren't listed; the page says products, services, pricing and hours may vary by location [15], so call the store for prices (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Notary in Mount Pleasant

  • Library (by appointment): the Mt. Pleasant Library page lists "Notary Available by Appointment" [2] and the Wando Mount Pleasant Library page lists "Notaries by appointment" [3]. The library's 2022 notary article lists both branches [16]. The Village Library page doesn't list notary [11], and the 2022 article doesn't list the Village Library [16]. We didn't find a library notary fee.
  • Before you go: the 2022 library article says notary service is by appointment only and subject to availability; you book by calling the branch. Bring all documents, ID, signers and witnesses, and don't sign in advance. The library's notaries can decline to notarize a document for any reason [16].
  • The UPS Store: its Mount Pleasant page lists notarizing [15]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store. Notary rules vary by state [17].
  • How notaries are appointed (statewide): the Governor appoints South Carolina notaries for a ten-year term, and applicants must be registered voters [18]. The Secretary of State commissions notaries and keeps their oaths on file. You apply on a paper form mailed to your county legislative delegation office, or to the House of Representatives if your county has none [19]. The fee is $25.00, payable to the Secretary of State, plus a $1.43 SC.GOV service fee if you pay by card [20]. A notary must report a name or address change within 45 days [19].
  • Fees (statewide; two laws differ): under § 26-1-100 (2014), a notary can charge at most $5 per signature for an acknowledgment, jurat or signature witnessing, $5 per person for an oath or affirmation without a signature, and $5 per certificate for a verification of fact. A notary who charges must post or show an English fee schedule, may charge a travel fee only if you agree on it in advance, and doesn't have to charge at all [18]. The Secretary of State's manual also says up to $5.00 [21]. An older section, § 8-21-140 (1898), still lists notary fees of 50 cents for every notarial certificate with seal and 25 cents for administering an oath for an affidavit [22]. We didn't find an official note on how the two fit together. The $5 limit is the one the Secretary of State cites.
  • No certified photocopies (statewide): a South Carolina notary can't certify or authenticate a photograph or photocopy [18]. Notaries also can't advertise as a "notario publico" [21].
  • Electronic notarization (statewide): the South Carolina Electronic Notary Public Act was signed May 18, 2021. Electronic notarization (IPEN) still requires the notary and the signer to be in each other's presence [19].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates (state vital records)

  • Where: we didn't find a state vital records office in Mount Pleasant. The nearest listed office is the Charleston County Vital Records office at the Teddie E. Pryor, Sr. Social Services Hub, 3685 Rivers Ave., North Charleston, SC 29405, phone (843) 953-0032 [5]. There's no separate town or county fee; state fees apply.

Birth and death certificates from the state (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): a records search is $12 and includes one certified copy; an expedited search is $17, because a $5 expedite fee has applied since October 1, 2023 to services that take 10 business days or less. Additional copies of the same record ordered at the same time are $3 each, and the special filing fee for an amended certificate is $15 [25].
  • In person (statewide): birth and death certificates are usually ready in 30–45 minutes at the $17 expedited fee, paid with exact cash, a money order or a cashier's check [6][26]. The fee page also lists credit and debit cards and cash (no bills over $50) for on-site service [25]. Regional offices issue long-form birth certificates [6] and death certificates from 2005 on [26]. Since January 1, 2026, all Vital Records offices are open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. and Wednesday 9:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [5].
  • By mail or drop-off (statewide): mail takes about 4 weeks at the $12 fee, to S.C. Department of Public Health, Vital Records Section, P.O. Box 2046, West Columbia, SC 29171, paid by money order or cashier's check. Drop-offs are accepted only at the state office in Richland County (2600 Bull St., Columbia): $12 for about 4 weeks or $17 for 5 business days or less [6][26][25].
  • Online or by phone (statewide): through GoCertificates, $17 plus $3 for each additional copy and an $8.70 processing fee, or VitalChek online, $17 plus an $8.75 processing fee; by phone, VitalChek charges $12.85. Processing takes 5–7 business days [6][26].
  • Amendments (statewide): since Nov. 17, 2025, the Richland County office (formerly called the State Office) no longer does same-day amendments; most in-person amendment requests take 1–2 business days [25].
  • Divorce reports (statewide): DPH has certified copies of the Report of Divorce for divorces from July 1962 to December 2025, by mail only for now: in-person requests are currently unavailable [27]. The divorce decree itself comes from the county Clerk of Court [28].

Marriage licenses (Charleston County Probate Court)

  • State law (statewide): you file a written application with the probate judge at least 24 hours before the license is issued (in Darlington and Georgetown counties, with the clerk of court instead) [29]. A $20 fee for the Domestic Violence Fund is added to each license, and couples who finish a qualifying premarital preparation course can get a one-time $50 state income tax credit [29].
  • Older copies from the state (statewide; the page gives two date ranges): DPH says its marriage records cover July 1950 to December 2025, and in-person requests are currently unavailable. The same page later says the Columbia office has July 1950 to December 2018, and that records before July 1950 or after December 2018 come from the probate office in the county that issued the license [30]. Mail requests are $12 with one certified copy and $3 for each additional copy [30].
  • Charleston County: you apply through the Live Online Virtual E-Application (LOVE). The cost is $70 by credit card plus a transaction fee, and it is non-refundable. The license is emailed to you to print [4]. Certified copies are $5.00 a copy by credit card plus a transaction fee. The office is at the Judicial Center, 100 Broad St., Suite 469, Charleston, phone (843) 958-5183, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [4].
  • Who can marry you: Charleston County's page says a South Carolina notary can perform wedding ceremonies, along with ordained ministers, Jewish rabbis and officers authorized to administer oaths [4].

Recorded documents (Charleston County Register of Deeds)

  • State law (statewide, uniform since 2019): under § 8-21-310, registers of deeds collect $15 to record a deed and $25 for a mortgage, plat, lease, easement, power of attorney or other document affecting real estate. A power of attorney is free with deployment orders to a combat zone. Separate acknowledgements or affidavits not attached to another document are $10 [22]. The county's posted fees follow; if they differ from the statute, ask the office which applies.
  • Charleston County fees: the Register of Deeds' fee list (as of August 1, 2019) shows $15.00 to record a deed, plus deed tax of $1.30 state and $0.55 county for each $500 of consideration; $25.00 for a power of attorney or a plat; and $10.00 for an affidavit [31].
  • Where and when: County Office Building, 101 Meeting St., Suite 200, Charleston, phone (843) 958-4800. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m., but walk-in documents aren't recorded in person after 4:30 p.m. The office provides certified true copies of documents on record [32].
  • Copies: 50 cents a page for deeds and 50 cents or $1 for plats; free downloads from the website. Older records mean a visit to use the microfilm, or a written request [14].

Court record copies (Charleston County Clerk of Court)

  • State law (statewide): § 8-21-310 sets uniform clerk fees, including $10 for an official certificate under the seal of the court and $10 for filing a notary commission. It sets $100 for filing a first complaint [22], and § 14-1-204(B)(1) adds a $50 fee to that, so the total is $150 [22][33]. The county's posted fees follow; if they differ from the statute, ask the Clerk which applies.
  • Charleston County fees: copies (xerox, computer or microfilm) $0.10 each; exemplification $10.00; record retrieval $4.00 per record; a certified copy of a DD214 is free. The Clerk lists $150.00 for a common pleas or domestic relations filing [34]. That matches state law: $100 under § 8-21-310(C)(1) [22] plus $50 under § 14-1-204(B)(1) [33].
  • Where: Common Pleas and General Sessions, 100 Broad Street, Suite 106, Charleston, phone (843) 958-5000; Family Court, 100 Broad Street, Suite 143, phone (843) 958-4400. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m. [35].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the South Carolina Secretary of State, in Columbia. It certifies documents signed by South Carolina officials and documents notarized by a commissioned South Carolina notary, but only documents that will go to a foreign country. The Secretary of State has a separate page for countries outside the Hague Convention [36].
  • Fee (statewide): $5.00 per document, not per page, by cash, personal, business or cashier's check, or money order [37]. Checks and money orders go to the Secretary of State's Office [36].
  • How to send (statewide): the Authentication Cover Letter, a self-addressed stamped return envelope, payment and the documents, by mail, UPS or FedEx, or by hand between 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m., to SC Secretary of State's Office, Attn: Authentications, 1205 Pendleton Street, Suite 525, Columbia, SC 29201 [36]. Phone (803) 734-2512 [37]. Foreign-language documents need an English translation signed by the translator, with the translator's signature notarized [36].
  • Vital records (statewide): photocopies aren't accepted; records must be certified by the issuing agency. Birth and death certificates must come from the state Vital Records office; marriage licenses from the county Probate Court or the state; divorce decrees from the county Clerk of Court or the state [28]. That page still calls the state office DHEC; vital records are now run by the South Carolina Department of Public Health (DPH) [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [38]. Other sellers: money order guide.

The flyer shows no year, and Nov. 16, 2026 is a Monday, so it is likely a past event. We didn't find a 2026 date.
  • State events (statewide, past): the South Carolina Department of Consumer Affairs listed free shred events on Oct. 19, 22 and 29, 2026, none in Mount Pleasant and all now past [41].
  • The UPS Store: its Mount Pleasant page lists shredding [15]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate near Mount Pleasant?

At the state's Charleston County Vital Records office, 3685 Rivers Ave., North Charleston [5]. In person it's $17 with one certified copy; by mail it's $12 and takes about 4 weeks [6].

How much does it cost to print at the Mount Pleasant library?

$0.10 a page for black-and-white and $0.25 for color at all Charleston County Public Library branches; scanning is free [1].

How much is a marriage license?

$70 by credit card plus a transaction fee, through Charleston County's online application [4]. State law requires a 24-hour wait [29].

Where can I apply for a passport in Mount Pleasant?

The State Department's locator lists the Mount Pleasant Main Post Office, 1050 Johnnie Dodds Blvd. [7].

Can I get something notarized at a Mount Pleasant library?

The Mt. Pleasant Library page lists "Notary Available by Appointment," and the Wando Mount Pleasant Library page lists "Notaries by appointment." The Village Library page doesn't list notary. We didn't find a library notary fee [2][3][11].
