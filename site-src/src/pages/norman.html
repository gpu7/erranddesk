Norman, Oklahoma document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Norman, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Which offices serve Norman: Norman's public libraries are part of the Pioneer Library System [1]. Norman is the county seat of Cleveland County: the Cleveland County Court Clerk issues marriage licenses and sells copies of court records, and the Cleveland County Clerk records deeds, both at the courthouse complex at 201 S. Jones Ave. [2][3][4]. Birth and death certificates come from the Oklahoma State Department of Health, whose nearest Will Call pickup is in Oklahoma City [5], and apostilles from the Oklahoma Secretary of State, also in Oklahoma City [6].
Short version: Pioneer Library System prints and copies are 15 cents a page in black and white and 30 cents in color; sending a fax is free, received faxes are 15 cents a page and scanning is free [7]. The passport locator lists 3 Norman facilities: 2 post offices and OU Passport on the University of Oklahoma campus [8]. Marriage licenses are $50, or $5 with an original premarital counseling certificate [2]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [9], and apostilles are $25.00 at the Secretary of State [6].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Pioneer Library System

  • Prices: black-and-white computer prints and photocopies are 15 cents a page; color prints and copies are 30 cents a page. Faxes are free to send and 15 cents a page received. Scanning to USB or email is free. You can also print wirelessly from your device or from home [7].
  • Norman locations: the system's locations page lists three in Norman [1]:
    • Norman East, 3051 E Alameda St, Norman, OK 73071, 405.217.0770: Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–9 p.m., Friday 9 a.m.–8 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., Sunday 1–6 p.m.
    • Norman West, 300 Norman Center Ct, Norman, OK 73072, 405.701.2644: same hours as Norman East.
    • Library Lab, 121 S Santa Fe, Suite 119, Norman, OK 73069, 405.291.0551: Monday–Friday 10 a.m.–7 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–5 p.m., Sunday 1–6 p.m.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 1 location in Norman: 3334 W Main St, (405) 364-1109. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [10]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [11].

Notary in Norman

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Pioneer Library System's services page [12]. Call your library to ask.
  • The UPS Store: its Norman page lists notarizing [10]. We didn't find store notary prices; Oklahoma's legal maximum is below.
  • Fee cap (statewide): under 49 O.S. § 5, the most an Oklahoma notary may charge is $5.00 for each notarial act, and notarizing an official absentee ballot affidavit is free [9]. The Secretary of State's Notary Public Guide (dated 12/2025) gives the same $5.00 maximum and the same absentee ballot rule [13].
  • Remote online notarization (statewide): 49 O.S. § 209 lets a notary charge up to $25.00 for a remote online notarial act [14].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Oklahoma State Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees: OSDH Vital Records charges $15.00 per certified birth or death certificate. Phone and online orders add a $5.00 convenience fee for Oklahoma residents or $12.95 for non-residents [18]; the FAQ puts phone and online orders at $20.00 for the first copy and $15 for each additional copy [18].
  • No walk-ins: the Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins. Online orders (through VitalChek) and phone orders at (405) 426-8880 can be picked up at "Will Call" in Oklahoma City, Tulsa or McAlester, usually in about 2 business days; bring a photo ID and the order confirmation [5][19]. Will Call hours are 12:00–4:45 p.m., Monday–Friday [18].
  • By mail: send the application, a photo ID and a check or money order payable to OSDH to Vital Records Service, Oklahoma State Department of Health, PO Box 248964, Oklahoma City, OK 73124-8964 [18][20]. OSDH lists about 4–6 weeks for mail orders [5], and its death certificate page says mail orders may take 6 weeks [19].
  • Marriage and divorce records: OSDH points these to the county court clerk where the license or divorce was filed [5]; the county block below has the local office.

Marriage licenses: Cleveland County Court Clerk

  • Fee: $50, or $5 if you show an original certificate of successful completion of a premarital counseling program under 43 O.S. § 5.1. Copies, faxes and emailed certificates aren't accepted [2].
  • How: Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., excluding holidays; arrive early. Bring proof of age, such as a driver's license, birth certificate, passport or military ID. There's no waiting period unless an applicant is under 18 (then 72 hours), and no blood test. Cleveland County judges don't perform marriage ceremonies [2].

Court records and marriage license copies: Cleveland County Court Clerk

  • Fees: copies are $1.00 for the first page and 50 cents for each additional page; certification is 50 cents per document; a certified marriage license is $2.00; an authentication certificate is $5.00 [3].
  • How: fill out the Request for Records form. In person, go to the first floor (1E) of the Cleveland County Courthouse, entering through the west door of the complex at 201 S. Jones Ave., Norman. By mail, send it to Cleveland County Court Clerk, 200 S. Peters Ave., Norman, OK 73069. Pay by check or money order, or by credit card in person. Requests are completed within 10 business days of payment, and certified copies can't be emailed. Questions: 405-321-6402 [3].

Recorded documents: Cleveland County Clerk

  • Recording fees: the county's fee schedule lists $18.00 for the first page of a real estate document, saying it "includes $5.00 preservation fee," and $2.00 for each additional page [21]. State law (28 O.S. § 32) sets $8.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, plus a separate $10.00 preservation fee for each instrument [22]. The $18.00 total matches $8.00 plus $10.00, but the county's $5.00 preservation figure differs from the statute's $10.00. The county says its prices are for estimating and to call (405) 366-0240 for final pricing [21].
  • Copies: $1.00 a page, $1.00 to certify copied documents and $5.00 for blue line copies of plats. Recording a military discharge (DD-214) is free [21].
  • Where: 201 S. Jones, Suite 210, Norman, OK 73069, (405) 366-0240, Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m., open through lunch [4].
  • County clerk fees (statewide): 28 O.S. § 32 sets county clerk recording fees of $8.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, plus a $10.00 preservation fee per instrument, and $1.00 a page for copies and $1.00 a page to certify them [22].

Court clerk copy fees (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): under 28 O.S. § 31, a court clerk charges $1.00 for the first page of a copy of a record and $0.50 for each additional page, $0.50 to certify an instrument and $5.00 to authenticate court records. Recording a marriage license is $5.00 with a premarital counseling certificate (43 O.S. § 5.1) or $50.00 without one [23]. A judge or justice may also collect a $10.00 marriage ceremony fee on top of that (28 O.S. § 56) [24].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Oklahoma apostilles and authentications come from the Secretary of State, not the county. They can be attached to an original notarized document or a certified copy of a recordable document, such as an Oklahoma birth certificate, marriage license or divorce decree [6].
  • Fee: $25.00 per apostille (per notarial act or certified document), or $10.00 per apostille for international adoption proceedings; an authentication is $20.00. Credit card payments add a 4% convenience fee [6].
  • In person: 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, (405) 521-4211, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. In person you can pay by cash, cashier's check, money order, credit card or business check [6].
  • By mail: include a cover letter naming the country, a phone number or email address and the return mailing address, and pay by certified cashier's check, money order or business check payable to Oklahoma Secretary of State [6]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [25]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I fax at the library in Norman?

Yes. Pioneer Library System faxes are free to send and 15 cents a page received [7].

How much does printing cost at the library?

15 cents a page in black and white and 30 cents in color, for prints and copies; scanning is free [7].

Where can I get something notarized in Norman?

The UPS Store's Norman page lists notarizing [10]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [9].

Where can I apply for a passport in Norman?

The locator lists the Sooner Station and Norman Main post offices and OU Passport [8].

How much is a marriage license in Cleveland County?

$50, or $5 with an original premarital counseling certificate, at the Court Clerk [2].

Can I walk in for a birth certificate?

No. OSDH Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins; order online or by phone and pick up at Will Call in Oklahoma City, or order by mail (statewide) [5].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Oklahoma Secretary of State, 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, for $25.00 per apostille (statewide) [6].
