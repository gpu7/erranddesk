Broken Arrow, Oklahoma document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Broken Arrow, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

This page is general information. Hours and fees change, so call or check the source before you go.
Two counties: Broken Arrow is in Tulsa County, and parts of the city are in Wagoner County; the city's floodplain page provides FEMA flood maps for both Tulsa County and Wagoner County incorporated areas [1]. Deeds are recorded by the clerk of the county where the property is, so this guide covers both county clerks and both court clerks.
Which offices serve Broken Arrow: Broken Arrow's two public libraries are part of the Tulsa City-County Library [2][3]. The Tulsa County Court Clerk runs a satellite office at 123 N. Main Street that issues marriage licenses and copies, with Saturday hours [4]. The Tulsa County Clerk in Tulsa [5] and the Wagoner County Clerk in Wagoner [6] record deeds. Birth and death certificates come from the Oklahoma State Department of Health, which has a Will Call pickup in Tulsa [7], and apostilles from the Oklahoma Secretary of State in Oklahoma City [8].
Short version: Tulsa City-County Library copies and prints are 10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color, faxes are 50 cents a page and scanning is free [9]. Libraries are cashless, and cardholders get $3 a month of free printing, copying or faxing [10]. The passport locator lists 1 Broken Arrow facility, the Main Post Office [11]. Marriage licenses are $50, or $5 with a premarital counseling certificate, at the Tulsa County Court Clerk's Broken Arrow satellite (cards only) [4]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [12], and apostilles are $25.00 at the Secretary of State [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Tulsa City-County Library

  • Business Center: available at all TCCL locations to copy, fax, print and scan. Copies and prints are 10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color; faxes are 50 cents a page; scanning to email or a USB drive is free, and USB drives are $8 [9].
  • Paying: all TCCL locations are cashless; the card readers take credit, debit and prepaid cards and Apple and Google Pay. Every TCCL cardholder gets a $3 allowance each month for free printing, copying and faxing, which doesn't carry over [10].
  • Wireless printing: send a job from your own device or from home, then release it at a print station at any location within 24 hours. A guest name or library card number is needed [13].
  • Broken Arrow Library: 300 W. Broadway Ave., Broken Arrow, OK 74012, 918-549-7323. Hours: Sunday closed, Monday–Thursday 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday 10 a.m.–6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–5 p.m. Its page lists wireless printing and public fax, scan and copy [2].
  • South Broken Arrow Library: 3600 S. Chestnut Ave., Broken Arrow, OK 74011, 918-549-7323. Same hours. Its page lists a Business Center, wireless printing and public fax, scan and copy [3].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 3 locations in Broken Arrow: 1560 W Tucson St, (539) 367-1144; 2608 West Kenosha, (918) 251-9116; and 1216 E Kenosha, (918) 259-9802. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [14]. We didn't find prices on the store pages; The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [15].

Notary in Broken Arrow

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Tulsa City-County Library's library services page [16]. Call 918-549-7323 to ask.
  • The UPS Store: its Broken Arrow page lists notarizing [14]. We didn't find store notary prices; Oklahoma's legal maximum is below.
  • Fee cap (statewide): under 49 O.S. § 5, the most an Oklahoma notary may charge is $5.00 for each notarial act, and notarizing an official absentee ballot affidavit is free [12]. The Secretary of State's Notary Public Guide (dated 12/2025) gives the same $5.00 maximum and the same absentee ballot rule [17].
  • Remote online notarization (statewide): 49 O.S. § 209 lets a notary charge up to $25.00 for a remote online notarial act [18].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Oklahoma State Department of Health (statewide)

  • Fees: OSDH Vital Records charges $15.00 per certified birth or death certificate. Phone and online orders add a $5.00 convenience fee for Oklahoma residents or $12.95 for non-residents [21]; the FAQ puts phone and online orders at $20.00 for the first copy and $15 for each additional copy [21].
  • No walk-ins: the Vital Records lobbies aren't open to walk-ins. Online orders (through VitalChek) and phone orders at (405) 426-8880 can be picked up at "Will Call" in Oklahoma City, Tulsa or McAlester, usually in about 2 business days; bring a photo ID and the order confirmation [22][23]. Will Call hours are 12:00–4:45 p.m., Monday–Friday [21].
  • By mail: send the application, a photo ID and a check or money order payable to OSDH to Vital Records Service, Oklahoma State Department of Health, PO Box 248964, Oklahoma City, OK 73124-8964 [21][24]. OSDH lists about 4–6 weeks for mail orders [22], and its death certificate page says mail orders may take 6 weeks [23].
  • Marriage and divorce records: OSDH points these to the county court clerk where the license or divorce was filed [22]; the county block below has the local office.
  • Will Call in Tulsa: the Oklahoma State Department of Health's Tulsa Division of Vital Records offers Will Call pickup only, at the James O. Goodwin Health Center, 5051 S. 129th East Ave., Tulsa, OK 74134, 12:00–4:45 p.m. Monday–Friday except state holidays. Records must be ordered in advance online or by phone; same-day service is no longer available. Inquiries: (405) 426-8880 [7].

Marriage licenses: Tulsa County Court Clerk, Broken Arrow satellite

  • Fee: $50, reduced to $5.00 if you present an original signed certificate of completion of premarital counseling showing at least 4 hours. All fees must be paid by credit or debit card; no checks or cash [4].
  • How: both applicants must appear in person together with government-issued photo ID. Proof of age can be a state-issued ID, a birth certificate plus photo ID, or a passport. There's no residency requirement and no blood test; the license is issued while you wait unless an applicant is under 18 (then a 72-hour wait) [4].
  • Where and when: 123 N. Main Street, Broken Arrow, OK 74012, 918-596-8991. Open 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Monday–Friday, but marriage license applications aren't processed after 4:30 p.m.; also open Saturday 9 a.m.–1 p.m. [4].
  • Copies: the satellite provides copies of previously issued marriage licenses and divorce decrees: a $5.00 search fee plus $1.50 for each certified copy [4].

Marriage licenses: Wagoner County Court Clerk

  • Fee: $50, payable in cash, reduced to $5 with a certificate showing at least 4 hours of premarital counseling under 43 O.S. § 5.1 [25].
  • Where and when: first floor, Wagoner County Courthouse, 307 E Cherokee, Wagoner, OK 74467. Both applicants must be present, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday; the county asks you to arrive by 4 p.m. [25]. The Court Clerk's office is open Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and stays open through the lunch hour; phone 918-485-4508 [26].
  • Copies: visit the Court Clerk's Office 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday [25], or mail the names on the license, the date of marriage, a $2 check or money order made to WC.C.C. and a self-addressed stamped envelope to Wagoner County Court Clerk, P.O. Box 249, Wagoner, OK 74467 [26].

Recorded documents: Tulsa County Clerk

  • Recording fees: $18.00 for the first page of a deed or other recorded instrument and $2.00 for each additional page; a cover sheet is $2.00 [5].
  • Copies: $1.00 a page; a certified copy of an instrument is $1.00 plus $1.00 for each additional page; plat copies are $5.00. Filing or copying a military discharge (DD214) is free [5].
  • Where and how: Tulsa County Headquarters, 218 W. 6th St., 7th Floor, Tulsa, 918-596-5801. Documents are accepted for recording Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. [5]. Credit cards are accepted with a 2½ percent convenience fee [27].

Recorded documents: Wagoner County Clerk

  • Recording fees: $18 for the first page of a deed, mortgage or other instrument (includes the $10 preservation fee) and $2 for each additional page [28].
  • Copies and searches: $1 a page, $2 a page certified; searches are $3; faxes are $1 for the first page and 25 cents for each additional page. Four public view stations let you search and print land records, and records filed since 1995 are online [28].
  • Where and when: 307 E Cherokee Street, Wagoner, OK 74467, 918-485-2216 [6]. Land records are accepted for filing 8 a.m.–3:45 p.m.; plats and mechanics liens 8 a.m.–2:30 p.m. Documents brought in later are filed the next working day [28].
  • County clerk fees (statewide): 28 O.S. § 32 sets county clerk recording fees of $8.00 for the first page and $2.00 for each additional page, plus a $10.00 preservation fee per instrument, and $1.00 a page for copies and $1.00 a page to certify them [29].

Court clerk copy fees (statewide)

  • Fees (statewide): under 28 O.S. § 31, a court clerk charges $1.00 for the first page of a copy of a record and $0.50 for each additional page, $0.50 to certify an instrument and $5.00 to authenticate court records. Recording a marriage license is $5.00 with a premarital counseling certificate (43 O.S. § 5.1) or $50.00 without one [30]. A judge or justice may also collect a $10.00 marriage ceremony fee on top of that (28 O.S. § 56) [31].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Oklahoma apostilles and authentications come from the Secretary of State, not the county. They can be attached to an original notarized document or a certified copy of a recordable document, such as an Oklahoma birth certificate, marriage license or divorce decree [8].
  • Fee: $25.00 per apostille (per notarial act or certified document), or $10.00 per apostille for international adoption proceedings; an authentication is $20.00. Credit card payments add a 4% convenience fee [8].
  • In person: 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, (405) 521-4211, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. In person you can pay by cash, cashier's check, money order, credit card or business check [8].
  • By mail: include a cover letter naming the country, a phone number or email address and the return mailing address, and pay by certified cashier's check, money order or business check payable to Oklahoma Secretary of State [8]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [32]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Can I fax at the library in Broken Arrow?

Yes. Both Broken Arrow libraries list public fax, scan and copy [2][3]. Faxes are 50 cents a page [9], paid by card; locations are cashless [10].

How much does printing cost at the library?

10 cents a page in black and white and 25 cents in color, and cardholders get $3 a month free [9][10].

Where can I get something notarized in Broken Arrow?

The UPS Store's Broken Arrow page lists notarizing [14]. Oklahoma notaries may charge at most $5.00 per act (statewide) [12].

Where can I apply for a passport in Broken Arrow?

The locator lists the Broken Arrow Main Post Office, 1701 S. Main St. [11].

Can I get a marriage license in Broken Arrow on a Saturday?

Yes. The Tulsa County Court Clerk's Broken Arrow satellite is open Saturday 9 a.m.–1 p.m., and it takes credit or debit cards only [4].

Which county records my deed?

The county where the property is. Broken Arrow property may be in Tulsa County or Wagoner County; both list $18 for the first page [5][28].

Where do I get an apostille?

From the Oklahoma Secretary of State, 421 NW 13th St, Suite 210, Oklahoma City, in person or by mail, for $25.00 per apostille (statewide) [8].
