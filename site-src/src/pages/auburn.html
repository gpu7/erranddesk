Auburn, Maine document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Auburn, Maine, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: the locator lists 2 passport acceptance facilities in Auburn (Androscoggin Probate Court and Central Maine Community College) [1]. City Clerk lists Notary services at 60 Court Street [2]. Certified vital copies $15 / $6; marriage license $40 [3][4].
Counties in Maine (statewide): Maine has 16 counties. Land records are kept by each county's Registry of Deeds (not by town clerks). Vital records are issued by city and town clerks and by the Maine CDC Division of Vital Records [5][6][7].

Notary in Auburn, Maine

  • City Clerk / Tax Division: 60 Court Street, 1st Floor. Phone 207-333-6601. Hours Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (marriage licenses until 3:30 p.m.). Clerk page lists Notary. We didn't find a published notary customer fee (last checked Nov 5, 2026) [2].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Maine charges $50 for a notary public commission that lasts seven years [9]. The Secretary of State's Course of Study says Maine does not set statutory fees for notarial acts; any fee must be disclosed in writing and agreed to before the act [10]. Remote and electronic notarization need separate SOS approval [9].

    Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

    Auburn City Clerk (vital records)

    • Where and when: 60 Court Street, 1st Floor. Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; marriage licenses until 3:30 p.m. Phone 207-333-6601 [2].
    • Fees: $15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional copy of the same record; marriage license $40 (valid 90 days) [3][4].

    Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

    • City or town clerk (statewide): 30-A M.R.S. § 2652 sets municipal clerk fees at $15 for the first certified copy of a birth, marriage, or death record and $6 for each additional copy of the same record ordered at the same time [4].
    • Maine CDC Division of Vital Records (statewide): 220 Capitol Street, Augusta. Certificate fee $15.00 per certificate, $6.00 for each additional copy ordered at the same time; VitalChek online orders add a service fee [5].
    • Need an apostille? (statewide): get a certified vital record from a clerk or Maine CDC, then request a special certification (apostille or authentication) from the Secretary of State [14][5].

    Marriage licenses (Auburn City Clerk)

    • Fee: $40 [3].
    • Hours: licenses processed until 3:30 p.m. [2].
    • Waiting period: none; license valid 90 days anywhere in Maine (statewide) [4][15].
    • State rules (statewide): file marriage intentions with a municipal clerk (or the Department). The clerk fee is $40 for recording intentions and issuing a license ($20 each when two licenses are required). There is no waiting period; the license is valid for 90 days anywhere in Maine. Both parties must usually sign; you must be 18 or older [4][15].

    Land records (Androscoggin County Registry of Deeds)

    • Office: 2 Turner Street, Unit 4, Auburn, ME 04210. County site phone 207-753-2500; Maine Registries directory lists 207-782-0191. Auburn is in Androscoggin County [16][17][6].
    • Recording: statewide private instruments $40 total ($35 + $5 surcharge). County fee PDF from 2013 is stale — confirm with the registry before you go [7][18].
    • Recording fees (statewide statute): 33 M.R.S. § 751 sets a flat $35 per instrument for most private submitters ($25 when the State or a municipality records). 33 M.R.S. § 752 adds a $5 records-preservation surcharge for most non-government recordings, so the usual private total is $40 per instrument. Plans are $45 under § 751 (county practice may list $50 with surcharge—confirm with the registry) [7][18].

    Apostilles (statewide)

    • Who issues them (statewide): the Maine Secretary of State, Bureau of Corporations, Elections & Commissions. Mail: 101 State House Station, Augusta ME 04333-0101. Courier: 6 E. Chestnut Street, Augusta. Phone 207-624-7752 [14].
    • Fee (statewide): $10.00 per special certification (apostille or authentication), payable to the Secretary of State. Processing is typically 10–15 business days; call for an appointment when more than 5 documents need certification [14].
    • Mail (statewide): include the request form, documents, and payment; VitalChek orders can be forwarded to this office on request [14].

    Money orders

    USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [19]. Other sellers: money order guide.

    Questions

    Where can I get a notary in Auburn, Maine?

    City Clerk at 60 Court Street lists Notary; phone 207-333-6601 [2].

    How much is a birth certificate in Auburn, Maine?

    $15 for the first certified copy and $6 for each additional copy [3].

    Where are Auburn passport offices?

    Androscoggin Probate Court (2 Turner Street) and Central Maine Community College (1250 Turner St) appear in the locator [1].

    Where do I record a deed for Auburn property?

    Androscoggin County Registry of Deeds, 2 Turner Street Unit 4, Auburn. Private recording is $40 under the statewide schedule [16][7].
