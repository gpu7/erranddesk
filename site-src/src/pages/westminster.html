Westminster, Colorado document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Westminster, Colorado, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Which offices serve Westminster: Westminster is split between two counties. The City Clerk's office says the city "coordinates with both Jefferson and Adams counties" on elections [6], and Adams County lists "parts of" Westminster among the communities it serves [9]. The city runs its own library, Westminster Public Library, with two branches, College Hill and Irving Street [3]. This page covers those branches, not Anythink or Jefferson County Public Library. For county offices, which one you use depends on which county you live in or where the record was filed. Search results for Westminster, California, Maryland and London also turn up, so check that a result says Colorado.
Short version: Westminster Public Library gives you 25 free printed pages a day, plus free local and toll-free faxing and free scanning to a flash drive [1]. Westminster City Hall takes passport applications by appointment [5]. The Adams County Health Department issues birth certificates for any Colorado county [11].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax: Westminster Public Library

ServiceWhat Westminster Public Library says
Computer printsBlack and color printing from library computers. Up to 25 pages a day are free. After that, $.10 per page for black and $.25 per page for color. Add funds at a Circulation desk before printing more [1].
Remote printingPrint from a home computer or mobile device through the remote printing portal, with your library card and PIN or as a "Guest User." Jobs stay on the server for 7 days. Pay in advance at the Circulation desk for jobs over 25 pages [1].
Photocopies10 cents per page for black and white and 25 cents for color. Double-sided copies are 20 cents per page for black and 50 cents for color [1].
FaxBoth branches offer faxing. Local and toll-free faxes are free [1]. Prices for other faxes, and whether you can receive faxes, aren't stated.
ScanFree scanning to a flash drive at both branches. Ask a staff member for details [1].

Westminster Public Library branches:

  • College Hill Library, 3705 W 112th Avenue, Westminster, CO 80031, phone 303-658-2700. It's a joint public and academic library at the west end of the Front Range Community College campus, open to everyone [2].
  • Irving Street Library, 7392 Irving Street, Westminster, CO 80030, phone 303-658-2300. A smaller branch next to Irving Street Park in south Westminster [2].

Both list the same hours: Mon–Wed 9 a.m.–8 p.m., Thu–Fri 9 a.m.–5 p.m. and Sat–Sun 1–5 p.m. Remaining 2026 closures listed: Nov. 11, Nov. 26 and 27, and Dec. 24, 25 and 31. On Nov. 25 they close at 3 p.m. [2]. Compare printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Westminster

  • City of Westminster: the State Department's locator listing for the City of Westminster includes the comment "NOTARY SERVICES AVAILABLE" [4]. The city's Passports and City Clerk pages don't mention a notary service [5][6], so call City Hall at 303-658-2400 to confirm before you go.
  • Colorado fee limit (statewide): "the maximum fee allowed for a notarization is $15 per document." The maximum for an electronic or remote notarization is $25. Notaries must tell you all charges before the notarial act [17].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [18]. For other banks, see free notary options.
  • The UPS Store (example store): the store at 10343 Federal Blvd., Unit J (104th and Federal, King Soopers Shopping Center) says it has a commissioned notary public on staff. It lists notary hours as "Available 7 Days." Phone (303) 469-2200. Its page doesn't post a price [19]. Under state law the fee can't exceed $15 per document in person [17].
  • Westminster Public Library: we didn't find a notary service on the library's technology and document services page as of Oct 28, 2026 [1].

Passports in Westminster

The U.S. Department of State's Passport Acceptance Facility Locator lists two acceptance facilities in Westminster, Colorado [4]:

FacilityAddress and phonePassport hours listedPhotos
City of Westminster (City Hall)4800 W. 92nd Ave., Westminster, CO 80031. 303-658-2400Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m., closed noon–1 p.m., by appointmentListed as on site
Westminster Post Office (USPS)3051 W. 105th Ave., Westminster, CO 80031. 303-464-9941Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–4 p.m., closed 12:30–1:30 p.m.; Sat 10:30 a.m.–12:45 p.m.; by appointmentListed as on site
  • City Hall services: new passport applications (DS-11) for adults and children, and renewals for children. Appointments are required; call 303-658-2400 or use the city's request form. Appointments are available with a Spanish-speaking passport agent. Adults renewing on Form DS-82 can apply online or by mail without an acceptance agent [5].
  • City Hall payment: money orders or personal checks only; no starter or temporary checks, no cash and no credit cards. Fees are split into two payments. The city's page lists $130 (adult) or $100 (child 15 and under) to the U.S. Department of State, plus $35 to the City of Westminster [5].
  • City Hall photos: a self-service kiosk in the City Hall lobby takes passport photos during business hours for $15, credit or debit card only. The city says it isn't recommended for infants [5].
  • What to bring to City Hall: a completed DS-11 in black ink, unsigned until the agent gives the oath and printed single-sided. Also proof of U.S. citizenship (photocopies aren't accepted), one passport photo, a state-issued photo ID for anyone 16 and older, and a copy of the ID with front and back on one side of the paper. All applicants must be present, including children [5].
  • Post Office: the locator says to schedule at www.usps.com/scheduler [4]. USPS fees are national: a $35.00 acceptance fee and a $15.00 photo fee "if needed" [20].
  • County clerks: the Adams County Clerk and Recorder (Brighton) and the Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (Golden) also take applications by appointment. See our Thornton and Arvada guides for their details.

Birth, death and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Adams County Health Department: it "issues birth and death certificates for any Colorado county" [11]. The Vital Records office is at 7190 Colorado Blvd., Suite 170, Commerce City, CO 80022, 720.200.1401. It's open Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and closed for lunch 12–1 p.m. [12]. In-person visits are by appointment. Cash, check, money order or credit card are accepted. If the record is available, certificates are issued the same day. You can also order by email, by mail or online [11].
  • Jefferson County Public Health, Vital Records: 800 Jefferson County Parkway, Suite 1300, Golden, CO 80401, phone 303-271-6450. Open Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–2 p.m., excluding holidays. It issues birth certificates for any birth in Colorado from 1903 to the present. Card payments carry a non-refundable 2.25% service fee [15].
  • Fee (statewide): $25 for the first birth or death certificate and $20 for each additional copy ordered at the same time, effective Jan. 1, 2026. Jefferson County Public Health says these fees are set at the state level [15]. Mesa County Public Health says the Colorado Office of the State Registrar of Vital Statistics sets the fee schedule statewide [13].

Recorded documents: the county where the document is recorded

  • Adams County Clerk and Recorder: recording is $43.00 per document. Regular copies are $0.25 per page. Certified copies are $0.25 per page plus $1.00 per document for certification. A credit card fee of 3% applies [14].
  • Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder: recording is $43 per letter or legal-size document. Digital copies are free. Paper requests under 20 pages are free; 20 pages or more cost $5.00 plus $0.25 per additional page. Certification is $1.00 per document [16].

Motor vehicle: Adams County offices in Westminster

  • The Adams County Clerk and Recorder lists two motor vehicle offices in Westminster: North Pecos, 12200 Pecos St., Westminster, CO 80234, and Westminster, 8452 Federal Blvd., Westminster, CO 80031. Lobby hours are Mon, Tue, Thu and Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m. and Wed 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m., except holidays. Phone 720.523.6010 [10].

Money orders (national USPS fees)

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Westminster City Hall accepts money orders for passport payments [5]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

The City of Westminster's 2026 Shred-A-Thon with Metro Denver Crime Stoppers was on Saturday, May 16, 8–11 a.m., at the Public Safety Center, 9110 Yates St. It was free, with donations encouraged. The limit was 3 bags or boxes per person, for individuals only, and you didn't need to be a Westminster resident [7]. That date has passed. The city calendar lists no later shred event as of Oct 28, 2026 [8]. For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Is printing free at the library in Westminster?

Up to 25 pages a day are free at Westminster Public Library. After that, black prints are $.10 a page and color prints are $.25 [1].

Is Westminster's library part of Anythink or Jeffco?

Westminster has its own library, Westminster Public Library, with College Hill and Irving Street branches and kiosks at city recreation centers. Its contact is the city's Parks, Recreation & Libraries office at City Hall [1][3].

Where can I apply for a passport in Westminster?

Westminster City Hall, 4800 W. 92nd Ave., and the Westminster Post Office, 3051 W. 105th Ave. Both are by appointment [4][5].

Can I pay for a passport with a card at Westminster City Hall?

No. The city takes money orders or personal checks only, with no cash or credit cards. The photo kiosk is the exception: it takes credit or debit cards only [5].
