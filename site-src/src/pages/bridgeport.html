Bridgeport, Connecticut document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Bridgeport, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Not affiliated, not legal advice. Errand Desk isn't affiliated with the City of Bridgeport, the Bridgeport Public Library, the State of Connecticut, USPS, FedEx, The UPS Store or the U.S. Department of State. Fees change; confirm with the office before you go.
Short version: 3 passport acceptance facilities are inside the city: the main library and 2 post offices [1][2]. Bridgeport Public Library notarizes free at every location [3]. A marriage license is $50, with a 48-hour wait since Oct. 1, 2026 [4][5]. A certified birth certificate from Bridgeport Vital Records is $20 [6].
No county clerk in Connecticut (statewide): Connecticut abolished county government effective Oct. 1, 1960, and counties remain only as geographic areas [7][8]. Town clerks keep land records, and each town's registrar of vital statistics issues birth, death and marriage records.

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Bridgeport Public Library printing: 15¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color. You can send jobs from a laptop or phone and pick them up at the Burroughs-Saden, Beardsley, Black Rock, Newfield or North library [9].
  • Library fax and copiers: we didn't find fax or copier details on the library pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • FedEx: FedEx's Bridgeport page lists 3 locations: FedEx at Walgreens at 1000 Park Ave. and 4083 Main St., and a FedEx Authorized ShipCenter at 1019 Main St. None is a FedEx Office store (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Connecticut city list doesn't include Bridgeport, so we count 0 stores in the city (last checked Nov 4, 2026) [11].

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Bridgeport

  • Library (free): notary service is free, first come, first served and walk-in at all Bridgeport Public Library locations. Bring a photo ID and a second current ID with your signature. The library notarizes only documents written in English [3].
  • Vital Records: the office lists notary service at $5 per notarized signature [12].
  • Town Clerk: the Town Clerk at 45 Lyon Terrace handles notary registrations, 203-576-7208 [13].
  • Notary fees (statewide): since July 1, 2026, a Connecticut notary may charge up to $10.00 per notarial act, plus travel at no more than the IRS business mileage rate. Public Act 26-81 raised the fee from $5.00 [14][15].
  • Sources disagree (statewide): the General Assembly's compiled statute page for §3-95, revised to January 1, 2026, still shows the old cap of $5.00 plus 35¢ a mile [16]. The Secretary of the State's pages give the $10.00 fee in effect since July 1, 2026 [14][15]. We show the newer figure.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Bridgeport Vital Records (registrar of vital statistics)

  • Where: 999 Broad St., Bridgeport, CT 06604. Open 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Monday–Friday, closed noon–1 p.m. Phone 203-576-7476 [20].
  • Fees: certified birth certificate $20; a pocket-sized copy, which isn't a legal document, is $15 [6]. Marriage and death certificates are $20 each [12].
  • Paying: cash, a money order payable to Bridgeport Vital Records, or Discover, Visa or Mastercard, with a $2 card fee. No personal checks [12]. Online orders go through VitalChek for an extra fee [6].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • Where (statewide): from the vital records office of the town where the event happened, or from the State Vital Records Office. Town requests are generally faster [21].
  • Fees (statewide): town-issued birth, death, marriage and civil union certificates are $20 per copy. State-issued birth certificates are $30 per copy [21][22].
  • Paying a town (statewide): by mail, towns take a money order payable to the town; personal checks aren't accepted. Online orders go through VitalChek, which adds fees [21].

Marriage licenses (Bridgeport Vital Records)

  • Fee and wait: $50. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the registrar issues the license at least 48 hours after you complete the application at an appointment. Applications aren't accepted after 4 p.m. [4].
  • The city's page disagrees with itself: another line on the same page still says the license "will be issued immediately" once requirements are met [4]. The state's 48-hour rule applies (statewide) [23].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at the vital records office of the town where the wedding will be. The fee is $50.00. Since Oct. 1, 2026, the town can't issue the license until 48 hours after the second person signs the application. The license is good for 65 days [5][23].

Land records (Bridgeport Town Clerk)

  • Where: 45 Lyon Terrace, Room 122. Open 9 a.m.–4 p.m. Monday–Friday; Friday recordings are 9 a.m.–noon only. Phone 203-576-7208 [13].
  • Copy fees (statewide law): CGS §7-34a sets town clerk copies at $1 per page and $2 to certify a copy. Recording is $10 for the first page and $5 for each extra page, plus $10 and $50 per-document state surcharges [24].
  • Bridgeport's own fee page: we didn't find a posted copy fee schedule on the Town Clerk page (last checked Nov 4, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the Connecticut Secretary of the State. All orders must be placed online at sots.service.ct.gov/apostille; paper order forms are no longer accepted [25].
  • Fee (statewide): $40 per document, or $15 for documents for a child adoption. Expedited service is $50 more per document [26][16]. These figures come from the SOTS order form and the statute; the online-ordering page we read doesn't list fees.
  • Delivery (statewide): paper documents go by courier to 165 Capitol Avenue, Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06106, or by mail to P.O. Box 150470, Hartford, CT 06115-0470. E-apostilles began July 6, 2026, but vital records still need a paper apostille. E-apostille and expedited orders are processed within 24 hours [25].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [27]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Bridgeport?

At the Burroughs-Saden Main Library (by appointment), the main post office on Middle Street or the Bayview post office [1][2][17].

Is there a free notary in Bridgeport?

Yes. Bridgeport Public Library notarizes free at all locations, walk-in [3].

How much is a marriage license in Bridgeport?

$50, with at least 48 hours between the application and the license since Oct. 1, 2026 [4][5].

How much does a Connecticut notary charge?

Up to $10.00 per act since July 1, 2026, plus mileage (statewide) [14][15].
