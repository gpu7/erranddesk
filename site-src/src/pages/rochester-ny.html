Rochester, New York document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Short version: 5 passport acceptance facilities are inside the city, including the Monroe County Clerk and the City of Rochester [1][2]. Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Central Library printing: 25¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color, cash only. You can send jobs from your own device and pick them up within 72 hours [7].
  • Copy, scan and fax: the Central Library, 115 South Ave., lists a copier/scanner/fax machine [6]. We didn't find copy or fax prices on the library pages we read (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Rochester page lists 7 locations offering printing, shredding, notarizing and faxing [8]. Prices aren't listed online.
  • FedEx Office: we didn't find a FedEx Office Print & Ship Center inside city limits. The Rochester-area ones FedEx lists, at 1906 Monroe Ave and 941 Jefferson Rd, are outside the city [9][2].

Notary in Rochester

  • Library: Rochester Public Library lists notary service at the Arnett (585) 428-8214, Central (585) 428-8020, Monday–Friday 9–4, Maplewood (585) 428-8220, Monroe (585) 428-8202 and Winton (585) 428-8204 branches. Call ahead and bring an unexpired government photo ID [10]. We didn't find a fee on its page.
  • The UPS Store: its Rochester page lists notarizing [8]. Notary rules and fees vary by state [11].
  • Notary fees (statewide): a New York notary may charge $2.00 for an oath or affirmation, or for taking an acknowledgment or proof of execution [12]. Executive Law §136 sets $2 for an acknowledgment by one person and $2 for each additional person, plus $2 for each witness sworn [13]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [12]. The Westchester County Clerk's notary FAQ gives the same $2.00 fee [14].

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Birth and death certificates (Monroe County Vital Records, countywide)

  • Where: 740 East Henrietta Rd., by appointment only. There are no walk-ins and no same-day appointments [3].
  • Fee: $30 per certificate in person or by mail. Online and phone orders add a $10 handling fee, so a standard order is $40 [3][17].
  • State alternative: the New York State Department of Health also issues certificates for births outside New York City, for $30 by mail (statewide) [18].

Marriage licenses (Rochester City Clerk)

  • Where and when: in person at the City Clerk's Office, 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Bring a certified birth certificate with a raised seal and a current government photo ID [4].
  • Fee and wait: $40. The license can't be used for 24 hours and must be used within 60 days. A duplicate is $10, and a City Hall civil ceremony is $75 [4].
  • Compared with New York City: the statewide fee outside NYC is $40 [19]; the NYC City Clerk charges $35 [20].

Deeds and land records (Monroe County Clerk, countywide)

  • Copies: 65¢ a page with a $1.30 minimum. A certified copy is $5 for up to 4 pages plus $1.25 for each additional page, and searches add $5 for every 2 years searched [21].
  • Recording a deed: $50 for a one-page deed plus $5 for each additional page, before state forms and transfer tax [21].
  • Payment: cash, check, Visa and Mastercard [21].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the New York State Department of State, for $10 per document. You can apply by mail or walk in at its offices in New York City, Albany, Binghamton, Buffalo and Utica. Drop-off is offered only in Albany and New York City, and same-day walk-in service is limited to 10 documents [22].
  • By mail (statewide): NY Department of State, Division of Licensing Services, Apostille and Authentication Unit, PO Box 22001, Albany, NY 12201-2001. Express carriers use the physical address instead. Phone 518-474-4429 [22].
  • State vital records (statewide): the state Department of Health can forward a state-issued certificate to the Department of State if you order by mail with expedited processing ($45.00 per copy) and enclose a separate $10.00 check or money order per certificate, payable to the Department of State [23].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Past event (outside the city): the Town of Brighton's semi-annual document shredding and electronics recycling event was Saturday, Oct. 3, 2026, 9 a.m.–noon, at 1941 Elmwood Ave. [25].
  • City of Rochester: we didn't find a city-run shred event on official pages (last checked Nov 4, 2026).
  • Monroe County ecopark (countywide): takes presorted documents from Monroe County households and holds them in locked bins for off-site shredding. It isn't certified document destruction, and papers from businesses or home offices are rejected [26].
  • The UPS Store: its Rochester page lists shredding [8].

For paid per-pound shredding, see where to shred documents.

Questions

Where can I apply for a passport in Rochester, NY?

At the Monroe County Clerk, the City of Rochester, or 3 post offices inside the city [1][2].

How much is a birth certificate in Rochester, NY?

$30 from Monroe County Vital Records, by appointment, or $40 for a standard online order [3][17].

How much is a marriage license in Rochester, NY?

$40, with a 24-hour wait before the ceremony [4].

How much does a New York notary charge?

$2 per oath, affirmation or acknowledgment, under Executive Law §136 (statewide) [5]. An electronic notary may charge up to $25.00 per electronic notarial act [12].

How much does printing cost at the Rochester library?

Central Library printing is 25¢ a page in black and white and 50¢ in color, cash only. We didn't find copy or fax prices on the library pages we read [7].
