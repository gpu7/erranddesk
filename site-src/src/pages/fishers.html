Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Hamilton East Public Library (HEPL), Fishers branch

  • Printing: 10¢ per single-sided black-and-white sheet and 25¢ per single-sided color sheet, in the computer lab [1]. The fee list says computer printouts are pre-paid and the list may change without notice [11].
  • Wireless printing: same prices. Send the job at print.princh.com with the Fishers Library printer ID 107090, then pay and pick it up at a Print Release Terminal within 24 hours [12].
  • Copying and scanning: black-and-white copies are 10¢ and color copies are 25¢ per single-sided sheet. The copiers can scan to email or a flash drive [1].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax service on the HEPL pages we checked; a search of its site for "fax" turned up only unrelated results [1][11][12][31]. That isn't the same as saying there is none; call the library at 317-579-0300 [11].
  • Where and when: 5 Municipal Drive; 317-579-0300. Hours are Monday–Thursday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday–Saturday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sunday noon to 6 p.m. [13]. The Census Geocoder places the branch in Fishers city [8].
  • The UPS Store: it lists 2 locations in Fishers, at 11650 Olio Rd, Ste 1000 (Fishers East/Geist) and 11722 Allisonville Rd. Its city page says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [14]. We didn't find prices: the Olio Rd store pages don't list any [15][16] (store #5100, 11650 Olio Rd, Ste 1000, (317) 578-0094), and The UPS Store's site says products, services, prices and hours may vary by location [17]. The Census Geocoder places the Olio Rd store in Fishers city; it didn't match the Allisonville Rd address, so we couldn't confirm that store is inside city limits [14][8].

Notary in Fishers

  • Fishers City Clerk: the clerk's page lists, among the clerk's duties, "administering oaths when necessary, taking depositions, taking acknowledgment of instruments that are required by statute to be acknowledged, without charging a fee." It doesn't describe a walk-in notary service, so call before you go: 3 Municipal Drive, 317-595-3111, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [18].
  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the HEPL pages we checked, and a search of its site for "notary" returned "No Results Found" [1][13][32]. Call the library at 317-579-0300 to ask [11].
  • The UPS Store Fishers East/Geist: 11650 Olio Rd, Suite 1000, says it has a commissioned notary public; bring a valid government-issued photo ID and complete, unsigned documents [16]. We didn't find prices.
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): in Indiana, notaries are commissioned by the Secretary of State for eight-year terms. Applicants pay a $5 processing fee, get a $25,000 assurance (bond) and complete the required education and exam [19].
  • Fees (statewide law): Indiana Code caps notary fees at "not more than ten dollars ($10) per signature" for acknowledgments, oaths and affirmations, witnessing a signature, verifications and certifying a copy. Fees for other acts are negotiable. A notary who charges must display a list of fees in advance. A travel fee is allowed up to the federal GSA travel rate. Public officials and their deputies may not charge for notarial acts done as part of official business [19].
  • Remote notarization (statewide law): a remote notary may charge "not more than twenty-five dollars ($25) for each remote notarial act" [19].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Fishers Health Department (birth and death certificates)

  • Which office: Hamilton County says the City of Fishers Health Department handles vital records for events in Fishers after April 24, 2020, and that certificates recorded before that date are at the Hamilton County Health Department [7]. The Fishers Health Department says it provides records for anyone born or who died in Indiana from 1907 to the present [2].
  • How to order: birth certificates are online requests only; death certificates can be requested online or in person. Bring or upload one current primary ID or two accepted secondary documents; expired documents aren't accepted [2].
  • Fees: $15 for a birth certificate; $15 for a death certificate plus the coroner's continuing education fee, which is $2.50 from July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2028. A records search is $15. Certified mail is $12; standard USPS shipping is free. You pay online when you request [2].
  • How long: online requests are reviewed Monday–Friday, and approval emails usually arrive within 1–2 business days, not counting delivery. You can choose pickup, standard mail or certified mail [2].
  • Where: Vital Records Office, 3 Municipal Drive [2]. The Census Geocoder places it in Fishers city [8].
  • Phone numbers: the department's contact page lists Vital Records at 317-567-5045, option 2 [35]. Its certificate request page gives 317-526-7102 for questions, if you can't pay online, to arrange pickup and for burial transit permits [2]. Hamilton County's page also gives (317) 567-5045 for the City of Fishers Health Department [7].
  • Hours, side by side: the certificate request page lists pickup hours of Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:15 p.m. [2]; the contact page lists 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [35]. Call before you go.
  • Records before April 24, 2020 (Hamilton County): the Hamilton County Health Department, 18030 Foundation Drive, Noblesville (317-776-8500), issues certificates Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. [7]. It charges $15.00 for a birth certificate and $17.50 for a death certificate; online orders add a nonrefundable $5 fee [22].
  • State fees, side by side: the Indiana Department of Health lists $10.00 for a birth certificate and $8.00 for a death certificate (statewide) [10]. The city, county and state list different prices; check with the office you order from.

Birth and death certificates (statewide)

  • State fees (statewide): the Indiana Department of Health charges $10.00 for a birth certificate and $4.00 for each extra copy in the same order, and $8.00 for a death certificate and $4.00 for each extra copy. Fees aren't refunded and don't include third-party fees for online or phone orders [10]. Local health departments set their own fees; see the county fees above.
  • In person (statewide): the state office has no walk-in service. To buy a certificate in person, go to the local health department in the county where the birth or death happened [10][23].
  • By mail, online or by phone from the state (statewide): mail State Form 49607 (birth) or 49606 (death) with a check or money order payable to the Indiana Department of Health (no cash). Online orders go through VitalChek, and phone orders through (866) 601-0891, with third-party fees [10].
  • Processing times (statewide): the state lists 2–3 weeks for a standard birth certificate, 6–8 weeks for a long-form birth certificate and 60 days for a death certificate [10]. Its home page says to allow 2 weeks for an order to reach the office and 10–15 business days to process it [23]. The long-form birth certificate is only available from the state [24].
  • Who can order (statewide): you need a direct interest, a documented financial or legal interest, or a close family tie (parent, adult sibling or grandparent) to the person named on the record [24].

Marriage licenses (Hamilton County Clerk)

  • Marriage licenses (statewide): in Indiana, the county clerk issues marriage licenses. Both of you apply in person at the clerk's office in an Indiana county where one of you lives; couples from out of state apply in the county where they will marry. The fee is $25.00 if one or both of you live in Indiana and $65.00 if neither does. Some offices add a $4.00 document fee, and each certified copy is $4.00. Most counties require cash. The license is valid for 60 days, and you can marry the same day. You'll need ID and your Social Security number [9].
  • Copies (statewide): certified copies of marriage licenses come from the clerk of the county where the license was issued, not from the state health department [25].
  • Hamilton County Clerk: fill out the online application, then book an application meeting. Meetings are by appointment only, online or in person at the clerk's office in Noblesville, and both of you must attend the same meeting [4]. The office is at 1 Hamilton County Square, Suite 106, Noblesville; 317-776-9629; marriage applications 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Monday–Friday [4].
  • Fees: $25.00 for Indiana residents and $65.00 for out-of-state residents (valid only for ceremonies in Hamilton County), cash or credit card. Both include 2 certified copies. The license expires 60 days after it's issued [4].
  • Where to apply: if one of you lives in Hamilton County and the other in another Indiana county, you can apply in either county. If you both live out of state, apply in the county where you'll marry [26].

Recorded documents (Hamilton County Recorder)

  • Recording fees: $55.00 for mortgages and $25.00 for deeds and all other instruments (one oversize page included; $5.00 for each additional page larger than 8½ by 14 inches) [27].
  • Copies: $1.00 a page (11 by 17 inches or smaller), $5.00 a page if larger, and $5.00 to certify a document. Card payments add $0.40 plus 1.96% [27].
  • Where: 33 N 9th Street, Suite 309, Noblesville; 317-776-9618. The recording deadline is 4:15 p.m. [5].

Court records (Fishers City Court and Hamilton County Clerk)

  • Fishers City Court: 3 Municipal Drive, Fishers, IN 46038; 317-595-3130; Monday–Friday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. We didn't find a copy or certified-copy fee on its pages [33].
  • Hamilton County: the Clerk's Office provides copies of documents related to immigration, marriage, divorce, expungements, estates, wills and criminal records through an online request form. It says there are small fees outlined in the form and that "Actual fees will be provided at the time of payment." We didn't find a court-record copy fee on the Hamilton County Clerk's pages we checked [34].

Apostilles and authentications (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $2.00 per document from the Indiana Secretary of State. There's no fee for birth and death certificates, adoption documents, or student transcripts and diplomas [28][19]. Pay in person by credit, debit, check or money order, or by mail with a check or money order [28].
  • One certificate (statewide): since July 1, 2022, Indiana uses a single certificate format and no longer tells apostilles and authentications apart. The certificates can't be used inside the United States [28].
  • What it can authenticate (statewide): Indiana vital records only; marriage, divorce, death and birth certificates don't need notarization, but other documents do. A marriage license must first be certified by the current circuit court clerk of the county where the marriage took place. Federal documents go to the U.S. Department of State [28].
  • Where (statewide): there's one office, at 302 W. Washington St., Room E-018, Indianapolis, IN 46204, with no branches. With an appointment it processes up to 10 documents while you wait; without one, up to 5. Phone (317) 234-9768 [28].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [29]. Other sellers: money order guide.

  • The UPS Store: its Fishers page lists shredding [14]. We didn't find prices.

Questions

Where do I get a birth certificate in Fishers?

From the Fishers Health Department, online only: $15 [2]. For Fishers births before April 24, 2020, Hamilton County says to use the Hamilton County Health Department [7].

How much is printing at the Fishers library?

10¢ per single-sided black-and-white sheet and 25¢ for color [1].

When is the next free shred day in Fishers?

Saturday, November 7, 2026, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Billericay Park, for Fishers residents [30].

How much is a marriage license in Fishers?

$25.00 for Indiana residents or $65.00 for out-of-state residents at the Hamilton County Clerk, by appointment [4].

How much can an Indiana notary charge?

Up to $10 per signature for common acts such as acknowledgments and oaths, and up to $25 for a remote notarial act (statewide law) [19].
