Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Maricopa County Library District libraries in Gilbert

  • Printing: $0.25 a page for black and white and $0.75 a page for color. Pay with small bills ($5 or $1), coins, or a credit or debit card. You can print from a library computer, your own device or a USB drive, and a free guest account works if you don't have a card. Library computers are free with a card or guest pass [1].
  • Copying, scanning and fax: we didn't find these services or prices on the district's printing and services pages.
  • Southeast Regional Library: 775 N Greenfield Rd, Gilbert, AZ 85234, 602-652-3000. Mon–Thu 10 a.m.–9 p.m., Fri–Sat 10 a.m.–5 p.m., Sun 1–5 p.m. The library says it is "undergoing improvements over the next several months," with construction and temporary fencing [2].
  • Perry Library: 1965 E Queen Creek Rd, Gilbert, AZ 85297, 602-652-3000. Mon–Thu 10 a.m.–7 p.m., Fri–Sat 10 a.m.–4 p.m., closed Sunday [2].

The UPS Store

The UPS Store #6945, 1515 N Gilbert Rd, Suite D107, Gilbert, (480) 632-2483, lists printing and copying, mobile printing, fax (send and receive), notary, passport and ID photos, and shredding. Its page lists hours of Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–6:30 p.m., Sat 10 a.m.–3:30 p.m. and Sun 10 a.m.–3 p.m. [15]. We didn't find prices on the store's page.

Notary in Gilbert

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Maricopa County Library District's services page.
  • The UPS Store: the 1515 N Gilbert Rd store lists notary service [15]. We didn't find its notary price or separate notary hours.
  • Fees (statewide): the Arizona Secretary of State says notary fees run from "No charge up to a maximum of $10.00 per notarial act," including electronic and remote online notarizations. A notary may not charge more for an electronic or remote notarization [11]. Arizona law also allows mileage and per diem up to the rates for state employees [12]. New Mexico caps most notary fees at $5 and Nebraska's acknowledgment cap is $5; Utah's is $10 per signature, Wyoming's $10 per notarial act and Colorado's $15 per document, and Kansas has no dollar cap.
  • What Arizona notaries do (statewide): only acknowledgments, jurats, copy certifications, and oaths and affirmations [11].
  • Thumbprint (statewide): since September 12, 2026 (SB 1479), a notary must take your right thumbprint in the notary journal for deeds, quitclaim deeds, deeds of trust, other documents affecting real property and powers of attorney [11].
  • Banks (national policy): Bank of America says notary service is available "at no cost to our customers" and "in many of our financial centers." Schedule an appointment. All signers must be present [13]. We didn't check which Gilbert branches offer it. For other banks, see free notary options.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Maricopa County Office of Vital Registration

  • Nearest office: the county has no office in Gilbert. Its nearest is the East Valley office at 331 E Coury Ave, Mesa, AZ 85210. The county lists 602-506-6805 and hours of Mon, Tue, Thu and Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. and Wed 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; arrive by 4 p.m. Other offices are in Phoenix, Peoria, Glendale and Goodyear [9].
  • Fees: $20 per certified copy, and $30 for corrections [9].
  • Same day: in person, you can often leave with the certificate the same day if your application is eligible. By mail (PO Box 2111, Phoenix, AZ 85001) it takes about a week. VitalChek takes online orders for extra fees [9].
  • Who can order (statewide): Arizona is a "closed record" state, so vital records aren't public. For a birth certificate you generally must be 18 or older and be the person named or another eligible applicant, such as a parent named on the certificate [10].
  • State office (statewide): the ADHS Bureau of Vital Records charges the same $20 per certificate under A.A.C. R9-19-105. Mail applications take about five business days on average. ADHS sends in-person customers to their county office for same-day service [10].

Marriage licenses: Clerk of the Superior Court

  • Fee: $98, by cash, money order, debit or credit card [6].
  • Where: Southeast Regional, 222 E Javelina Ave, Mesa 85210, plus Downtown and Northeast Regional in Phoenix and Northwest Regional in Surprise. Appointments are recommended, and walk-ins are accepted [6].
  • Rules: both people must appear with photo ID. The license is issued the same day and is valid for 1 year. An online application is open to Arizona residents only [6].
  • Certified copies: $43.50 by mail, or $35.50 if you send a self-addressed stamped envelope [6].

Recorded documents: Maricopa County Recorder

  • Where: the Recorder's office is at 301 W Jefferson St, Suite 200, Phoenix, AZ 85003, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. A recording kiosk is in Gilbert, at Southeast Regional Library, 775 N Greenfield Rd, Gilbert, AZ 85234, Mon–Fri 10 a.m.–4 p.m. Kiosks take credit cards only [8].
  • Recording: the standard fee is $30.00 per document, with some exceptions. At the Phoenix office your original is returned right away; by mail, allow 2–4 weeks [8].
  • Copies: official copies are $1.00 a page. Certified copies are $1.00 a page plus $3.00 per document [8].

Court records: Clerk of the Superior Court

  • In person: the Southeast Regional facility, 222 E Javelina, Mesa, is one of the Clerk's records locations, open Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Pay by money order, debit or credit card, or cash; personal checks aren't accepted [7].
  • Fees: copies are $0.50 a page, and certifying any document is $35.00. Research fees may apply. You can inspect public records free at the same locations [7].
  • Online: the Clerk takes requests through its Online Records Request Form. Its eAccess online records system charges fees [7].

State ID cards: Arizona MVD (statewide)

A standard Arizona ID card is $12, and it's free if you're 65 or older. Some people on SSI may also qualify for a free card. Free cards don't apply to the Travel ID. Apply at any MVD or Authorized Third Party office, starting at AZMVDNow.gov [14]. A Travel ID or driver license, original or renewal, is $25 [14].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [16]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • Free town event: Gilbert's Mobile Drop-off Household Hazardous Waste & Recycling Event is January 9, 2027, 7 a.m.–1 p.m., at Gilbert Town Hall, 50 E. Civic Center Drive. It includes the Gilbert Police Department's Annual Document Shredding Event, with free, secure shredding. It is limited to Gilbert residents with a current utility bill and matching ID [17].
  • The UPS Store: the 1515 N Gilbert Rd store lists shredding [15]. We didn't find its price.

Questions

How much is printing at the library in Gilbert?

$0.25 a page in black and white and $0.75 in color at Maricopa County Library District libraries [1].

Can I get a passport in Gilbert?

The State Department's locator lists no passport acceptance facility in Gilbert. It lists two in Mesa and two in Chandler [3].

Where can I get a birth certificate near Gilbert?

At the Maricopa County Office of Vital Registration's East Valley office, 331 E Coury Ave, Mesa. It's $20 a copy [9].

Can I record a deed in Gilbert?

Yes, at the Recorder's kiosk in Southeast Regional Library, 775 N Greenfield Rd. Kiosks take credit cards only, and recording is $30.00 per document [8].

What does a notary cost in Arizona?

No more than $10.00 per notarial act, including electronic and remote notarizations (statewide) [11].
