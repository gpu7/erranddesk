Which offices serve Tacoma: the Pierce County Auditor issues marriage licenses, records documents and takes passport applications at 2401 S. 35th St., Room 200 [1][2]. The Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department issues birth and death certificates [3]. Apostilles come from the Washington Secretary of State [4].
Short version: Tacoma Public Library cardholders get $5.00 of free prints and copies a week, then pay 10 cents a black-and-white page, and USB scanning is free [5]. The locator lists 4 Tacoma passport facilities: the Pierce County Auditor and three post offices [6]. Washington notaries may charge at most $15 per notarial act (statewide) [7]. A Pierce County marriage license is $172 [8]. Walk-in birth and death certificates start at $39 at the health department [3].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Tacoma Public Library

  • Free credit: library cardholders get a weekly print and copy credit of $5.00 [5].
  • Prices: prints and copies are 10 cents a page in black and white and 20 cents a page in color, paid by cash or credit or debit card. Card payments for copies are capped at 99 copies a transaction [5].
  • Remote printing: send documents from your own device through the ePRINTit web page at tacoma.eprintitsaas.com/public/upload, then pick them up at any location with your card number and PIN. First-time users ask staff to release the prints [5].
  • Scanning: every location has a print, scan and copy device. Scanning to a USB drive is free; you can borrow a flash drive or buy one for $5.00. Scanning straight to email isn't available [5].
  • Fax: we didn't find fax service on the Tacoma Public Library printing page.
  • The UPS Store: it lists 4 locations in Tacoma: 1625 E 72nd St., Ste. 700, (253) 328-4819; 2522 N Proctor, (253) 756-6650; 2661 N Pearl St, (253) 761-0790; and 2602 S 38th St., Ste. A, (253) 473-1361. Its city page says they offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [9]. We didn't find prices on the store pages.

Notary in Tacoma

  • Library: we didn't find notary service on the Tacoma Public Library pages we checked.
  • The UPS Store: its Tacoma page lists notarizing [9]. We didn't find store notary prices; Washington's legal maximum is below.
  • Fees (statewide): Washington caps a notary's fee at $15 for witnessing or attesting a signature, taking an acknowledgment or a verification on oath or affirmation, certifying or attesting a copy, administering an oath or affirmation, or certifying that an event occurred. A remote notarial act can cost up to $25 [7][10]. A notary need not charge at all [11].
  • Extras (statewide): a notary may charge the actual cost of copies, and may charge a travel fee only if you agree to it in advance and are told it isn't required by law [7]. Washington's $15 cap matches Nevada's and is three times Idaho's $5.

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates: Washington Department of Health (statewide)

  • State price: state law sets $25 for each certified or informational copy, whether it comes from the Department of Health (DOH) or a local registrar, and allows an extra fee for electronic payment [14].
  • Online or phone (VitalChek): from $40.50 a copy ($25 certificate, $8.50 VitalChek fee and $7 DOH processing fee), plus $3 if you choose online identity verification. DOH says these orders ship 3–7 business days after it receives them [15].
  • Mail: from $25 a copy, by check or money order to DOH, PO Box 9709, Olympia, WA 98507 [15][16]. The DOH page gives two timelines: shipped within 6–8 weeks in one place, received within 8–10 weeks in another [15].
  • In person: DOH partners with local health departments, which may charge an extra fee; most of those orders are filled the same day [15]. The local office for this city is below.
  • Extras: the state order form lists heirloom birth certificates at $50 and an apostille at $15 [16].

Birth and death certificates in person: Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department

  • Where: 3629 S. D St., Tacoma, WA 98418. Walk in Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (9:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. on the last Tuesday of the month); no appointment is needed. Phone (253) 649-1401 [3].
  • Price: walk-in orders start at $39 (the $25 certificate plus a $14 processing and expedite fee), with an extra fee for cards; the average wait is 20–30 minutes. Online orders through VitalChek start at $48.50 and ship within 3 business days. Mail orders start at $33 and ship within 1–2 weeks [3].
  • Not here: Auditor services, including passports, are no longer offered at the Health Department [3].

Marriage licenses: Pierce County Auditor

  • Where: 2401 S. 35th St., Room 200, Tacoma, WA 98409, Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Appointments are recommended. You can also apply by mail with a notarized application. Phone 253-798-7435 [1].
  • Fee: $172, paid by cash, money order, check or card. Certified copies are $3 and non-certified copies $1 [8].
  • Timing: Washington has a mandatory 3-day waiting period that can't be waived; it starts when you buy the license, and the license is valid for 60 days from issuance [1].

Recorded documents: Pierce County Auditor

  • County fees: $303.50 for the first page of a standard document and $1 for each additional page [17].
  • Statewide law: RCW 36.18.010 sets the county auditor's base fees: $5 for the first page of a recorded document and $1 for each additional page, $3 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $1 a page for noncertified copies and $8 an hour for record searches. Surcharges from other state laws are added on top [18], which is why the county fee above is much higher.

Court records (statewide fees)

  • Local page: we didn't find the Pierce County Clerk's own copy-fee page, so only the statewide fees are listed here.
  • Fees (statewide law): the superior court clerk charges $5 for the first page of a certified copy and $1 for each additional page, $2 for each additional seal when a copy is authenticated or exemplified, and 50 cents a page for a copy without a seal. A certificate is $2, and mailed copies cost postage plus a $2 handling fee [19].

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who: Washington apostilles come from the Secretary of State, not the county [4]. Vital records must be apostilled by the state that issued them [4].
  • Fee: $15 per document; a document can be more than one page. Pay by check or money order to "Secretary of State" [4][20].
  • Timing: standard processing typically takes 7–10 business days. Expedited processing is $100 extra for each 10 documents and takes 2–3 business days [4].
  • Same day in person: at the Tumwater office or the newer Cheney (Eastern Washington) location, for $150 extra for the first 10 documents and $100 for each additional 10. Cheney offers same-day service only, and visits there are recorded [4]. We didn't find street addresses on the apostille page.
  • By mail: send the request form to PO Box 40228, Olympia, WA 98504-0234 [20]. Documents come back by first-class mail unless you include a prepaid, traceable label [4]. See the apostille guide.

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [21]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much does printing cost at Tacoma Public Library?

10 cents a black-and-white page and 20 cents a color page, after a weekly $5.00 credit for cardholders [5].

Where can I apply for a passport in Tacoma?

At the Pierce County Auditor's Office (appointment only) or at the Downtown Station, University Place or Lincoln post offices [6][2].

What does a notary cost in Washington?

At most $15 per notarial act, or $25 for a remote notarial act, plus copy costs and any travel fee you agree to in advance (statewide) [7].

Where do I get a marriage license?

From the Pierce County Auditor, 2401 S. 35th St., Room 200. The license is $172, and Washington's 3-day waiting period can't be waived [8][1].

Where do I get a birth certificate in Tacoma?

Walk in at the Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department, 3629 S. D St.; orders start at $39 and the average wait is 20–30 minutes [3].
