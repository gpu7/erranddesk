Two clerks: Morgantown is in Monongalia County [4][5]. The county clerk records and issues birth, death and marriage certificates and records documents [6][7]; the circuit clerk, at the Justice Center, handles court records [8].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

Morgantown Public Library (373 Spruce Street)

  • Printing and copies: 10 cents per black-and-white page, 25 cents per color page [1][9].
  • Fax: $1 a page, up to $5; no additional fees past 5 pages [1][9].
  • Scanning: we didn't find a scanning price on the library FAQ or Services page (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Phone: 304-291-7425 [1]. The Census Geocoder places the library in Morgantown city [10].
  • The UPS Store: its Morgantown page lists 1 location, 364 Patteson Dr, (304) 599-0001, and says its stores offer packing, shipping, printing, shredding, notarizing, faxing and mailbox services [11]. The Census Geocoder places it in Morgantown city [10]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.

See printing prices and fax prices.

Notary in Morgantown

  • Library: the FAQ says notary services are offered at Morgantown and recommends calling ahead to make sure a licensed notary is available [1]. We didn't confirm a fee on a current library page (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: its Morgantown page lists notarizing [11]. Notary rules vary by state [12].
  • How notaries are commissioned (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State commissions notaries. The application or recommission fee is $52, waived for state and local government notaries; a commission is good for five years [13][14]. No fee is charged to add e-notarization authorization [13].
  • Fees (statewide): under W. Va. Code §39-4-30, a notary may charge up to $10.00 for each signature notarized, up to $10.00 per 8½ × 11 page for certifying a copy (the notary keeps a copy on file), and up to $10.00 for any other notarial act [15].
  • Copy certification (statewide): if no other office can certify a document, a West Virginia notary can certify a true copy after comparing it with the original and keeping a copy [16][15]. Vital records, court orders and recorded deeds still come from the office that holds them [16].

Birth, death, marriage, court and recorded-document copies

Birth and death certificates

  • Monongalia County Clerk: issues birth, marriage and death certificates for the county; mail-in applications are on its Forms page [6]. We didn't find a county certificate fee online; state law sets $5 for a county clerk's certified copy [19].

Birth, death and marriage certificates from the state (statewide)

  • Fee (statewide): $12 per certified copy from the state Vital Registration Office; the fee includes a nonrefundable search. If the record isn't found, staff search the year given plus the year before and after; more searches are $12 per three-year period [20].
  • In person (statewide): same-day walk-in service at 350 Capitol Street, Room 165, Charleston, WV 25301-3701. Cash, check and money order are accepted; cards at the walk-up counter add a 4% surcharge. Expect a wait of at least 15 minutes, longer from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. [20].
  • By mail or online (statewide): mail the form with a color copy of your ID and a check or money order (no cards by mail). Online orders go through third-party vendors such as VitalChek; the state gets $12 a copy whatever the vendor charges [20].
  • County clerk copies (statewide law): W. Va. Code lets the county clerk charge $5 for a certified copy of a birth certificate, death certificate or marriage license it holds [19]. Check with the county clerk which records it has.
  • Divorce records (statewide): a divorce decree comes from the circuit clerk of the court that issued it, not from Vital Registration [16].

Marriage licenses (Monongalia County Clerk)

  • Monongalia County fees: $57, or $37 with signed documentation of premarital counseling. Cash, money order or card (2.4% or $1.00 minimum card fee); no checks [2]. These are $2 more than the $55 and $35 set in state code [19]; we show both and haven't confirmed what accounts for the difference. Pay the county's posted fee.
  • How: by appointment, Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–3:30 p.m., at the Courthouse, 243 High St. Rm 123. The license must be used within 60 days and only for a ceremony in West Virginia [2].
  • State law (statewide): every West Virginia marriage needs a license from a county clerk (the clerk of the county commission) [21]. The age of consent is 18; 16- and 17-year-olds need written consent under the statute's conditions [22]. The statutory fee is $55, or $35 if you show a premarital education course completion certificate [19].

Recorded documents (Monongalia County Clerk)

  • Who records deeds (statewide): in West Virginia, the county clerk (clerk of the county commission) records deeds; the circuit clerk handles court records [19][16]. Recording a deed or trust deed is $30; most other documents are $10; documents over five pages add $1 per extra page. Copies are $1.50 (plus $1 per page over two), and attaching the clerk's seal is $1 (§59-1-10) [19].
  • Monongalia County: the clerk's site has a Document Recording Calculator and notes that a grantee signature is now required on certain deeds. Office hours are Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m., (304) 291-7230 [7].

Court records and divorce decrees (Monongalia County Circuit Clerk)

  • Circuit clerk fees (statewide): W. Va. Code §59-1-11 sets $1 a page for a copy the circuit clerk makes to go out of the office, $135 to file for divorce, separate maintenance or annulment, and $200 for most other civil filings [23] (last checked Nov 3, 2026).
  • Monongalia County: 75 High St, Justice Center, (304) 291-7240 [8]. The circuit clerk's own website timed out when we checked, so we couldn't confirm its hours, payment rules or copy prices from an official source (last checked Nov 3, 2026).

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): the West Virginia Secretary of State, at the State Capitol Building in Charleston. It issues Apostille Certificates for countries in the Hague Apostille Convention and Authentication Certificates for other countries [16].
  • What it certifies (statewide): only documents attested by a proper West Virginia officer, such as a notary public, county clerk or the State Registrar. Recorded deeds and marriage certificates need the county clerk's certified copy; court orders and divorce decrees need the circuit clerk's [16].
  • Fee and hours (statewide): $10 per certification; if several documents are certified by the same official, the first is $10 and each additional one is $5. In-person service runs 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday–Friday [16].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [24]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Shredding

  • The UPS Store: its Morgantown page lists shredding [11]. Prices aren't listed online; call the store.
  • Free events: the Monongalia County Solid Waste Authority's free paper shredding event was Saturday, April 11, 2026 (past), and its page says the fall date is to be determined [25]. We found no upcoming free shred event as of Nov 3, 2026.
