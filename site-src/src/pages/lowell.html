Lowell, Massachusetts document errands: printing, fax, notary, passports and certified copies (2026)

Where to print, fax, notarize, apply for a passport and get certified copies in Lowell, using only official and provider pages. Each fact links to its source. Some facts are statewide or national rather than local. We label those.

Short version: the locator lists 1 passport acceptance facility in Lowell (Lowell Post Office) [1]. Pollard Memorial Library prints from 15¢ a page [2]. Birth, death and marriage certificates are $20 ($25 online) under the clerk fee schedule adopted April 28, 2026 [3][4]. Marriage license fee is $50 [5]. Massachusetts does not cap ordinary notary fees (statewide) [6].
Counties in Massachusetts (statewide): Census geography still uses counties, but Berkshire, Essex, Franklin, Hampden, Hampshire, Middlesex and Worcester counties are no longer legal governmental units [7]. Land records are kept by county Registries of Deeds under the Secretary of the Commonwealth. Suffolk County and Boston city are governmentally consolidated [7].

Printing, copying, scanning and fax

  • Pollard Memorial Library (401 Merrimack St.): copies and black-and-white printing are 15¢ a page; color printing is 50¢ a page on Floor 2. Scanning is free. Fax is $1.75 for the first U.S. sheet and $1.00 each additional ($3.95/$3.50 international); credit/debit only for fax. The library does not receive faxes [2].
  • FedEx: FedEx's Lowell page lists 2 locations, both FedEx at Walgreens (1161 Bridge St. and 54 Plain St.). No FedEx Office Print & Ship Center is listed for Lowell [8].
  • The UPS Store: we didn't find a The UPS Store city locator page for Lowell, MA (last checked Nov 5, 2026). The nearest official city page is Chelmsford (119 Drum Hill Rd., (978) 454-4700)—a separate town [9].

Notary in Lowell

  • City Clerk: notary fee is $5.00 per notary signature under the clerk fee schedule adopted April 28, 2026 [3][4]. Confirm at Room 31 [10].
  • Library: we didn't find a Pollard Memorial Library notary page (last checked Nov 5, 2026).
  • The UPS Store: notary rules and fees vary by state [11]. See Chelmsford above if you use that store [9].
  • Notary fees (statewide): Massachusetts does not set a maximum fee for ordinary notarial acts such as acknowledgments and jurats. G.L. c. 262, § 41's $1.25 figure applies only to "noting" a dishonored negotiable instrument [12]. The Supreme Judicial Court held that cap does not limit fees for other notarial acts [6]. Ask the fee before you sit down.

Birth, death, marriage and land record copies

Lowell City Clerk (vital records)

  • Where and when: City Hall, 375 Merrimack St., 1st Floor Room 31. Monday/Wednesday/Thursday 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; Tuesday 8 a.m.–8 p.m.; Friday 8 a.m.–noon. Phone 978-674-4161 [16][10].
  • Fees: birth, death and marriage certificates are $20 each; online orders are $25. Fee schedule adopted by City Council April 28, 2026 [3][4]. Birth and marriage mail/online requests need a valid photo ID [16].

Birth, death and marriage copies (statewide rules)

  • City or town clerk (statewide): you can usually get a certified copy from the city or town where the event happened (or where marriage intentions were filed). Fees vary by city or town [17].
  • State Registry of Vital Records and Statistics (statewide): 150 Mt. Vernon St., 1st Floor, Dorchester. Counter hours Monday–Friday 8:45 a.m.–4:45 p.m. State fees: $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54 for the first copy ordered online or by phone ($42 for each additional copy in that order) [18].
  • Need an apostille? (statewide): bring a certified copy with an original signature of a Massachusetts notary, city/town clerk, court clerk or Registrar of Vital Records to the Secretary of the Commonwealth [19].

Marriage licenses (Lowell City Clerk)

  • How: both of you apply in person at Room 31 with photo ID [5].
  • Fee: $50; checks, money orders or cash to "City of Lowell" [5][3].
  • Waiting period: 3-day wait; license valid 60 days and only in Massachusetts [5].
  • State rules (statewide): both of you apply in person at any Massachusetts city or town clerk. There is a 3-day waiting period; the license is valid for 60 days and only inside Massachusetts. You must both be 18 or older. License fees vary by town [17].

Land records (Middlesex North Registry of Deeds)

  • Office: 370 Jackson St., Lowell, MA 01852. Office 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.; recording 8:30 a.m.–3:45 p.m. Monday–Friday. Phone (978) 322-9000 [20].
  • Recording fees: deed $155, mortgage $205, mortgage discharge $105, declaration of homestead $35. Cash or check to Middlesex North Registry of Deeds or Commonwealth of Massachusetts [21].
  • Copies: certified copies $1 per page [21].
  • Recording fees (county registries): each Registry of Deeds posts its own fee schedule (often a flat amount per document). See the city section for that county's posted fees.

Apostilles (statewide)

  • Who issues them (statewide): only the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth. Walk-in at Commissions Section, One Ashburton Place, Room 1719, Boston, or regional offices in Springfield and Fall River [19][22].
  • Fee (statewide): $6.00 per document; check or money order payable to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts [19][22].
  • Mail (statewide): mailed requests take about two to three weeks; include the destination country and a prepaid return envelope [19].
  • Counter limit (statewide): up to three documents per customer at the Boston counter; four or more are ready the next business day after 3 p.m. [19].

Money orders

USPS money order fees are national: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00, and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Pay with cash or a debit card, not a credit card. The limit is $1,000 per domestic money order [23]. Other sellers: money order guide.

Questions

How much is a birth certificate in Lowell?

$20 per certificate, or $25 online, under the clerk fee schedule adopted April 28, 2026 [3][4]. Statewide RVRS copies are $20 in person, $32 by mail, or $54/$42 online or by phone [18].

Where can I get a notary in Lowell?

City Clerk notary is $5 per signature [3]. The UPS Store also offers notary; the nearest official city locator page is Chelmsford [9][11].

How much can a Massachusetts notary charge?

There is no statewide maximum for ordinary notarial acts. The $1.25 figure in G.L. c. 262, § 41 applies only to "noting" a dishonored instrument (statewide) [12][6]. Ask the fee first.

Can I get an apostille on a Lowell birth certificate?

Bring a certified copy with an original clerk or RVRS signature to the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The apostille is $6 per document (statewide) [19][22].
